Let $$ \begin{align*} C_0 ={}& A_0, \\ C_n ={}& A_n \setminus \bigcup_{j=0}^{n-1} A_j \end{align*} $$ which satisfies $\bigcup_{j=0}^{n} A_j = \bigsqcup_{j=0}^{n} C_j$ and $\bigcup_{j=0}^{\infty} A_j = \bigsqcup_{j=0}^{\infty} C_j$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} μ\left(\bigcup_{j=0}^{\infty} A_j\right) = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} μ(C_j) \leq \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} μ(A_j). \end{align*} $$
