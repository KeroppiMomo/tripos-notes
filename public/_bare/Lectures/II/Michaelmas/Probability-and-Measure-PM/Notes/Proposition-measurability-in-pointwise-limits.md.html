Let $\{f_n: (E, \mathcal{A})\to (\overline{\R}, \mathcal{B}(\overline{\R}))\}_{n\in \N}$ be a sequence of measurable functions.

  1. The functions $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{n\in \N} f_n, \quad \inf_{n\in \N} f_n, \quad \limsup_{n\to\infty} f_n, \quad \liminf_{n\to \infty} f_n \end{align*} $$ are measurable.

  2. In particular, if $f_n$ converges pointwise to $f: E\to \overline{\R}$, then $f$ is measurable.

  3. For any such $f_n$, the set $$ \begin{align*} \sb{x\in E}{\text{$f_n(x)$ has a limit as $n\to \infty$}} \end{align*} $$ is measurable.
