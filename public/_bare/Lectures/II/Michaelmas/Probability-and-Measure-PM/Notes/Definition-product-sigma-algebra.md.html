Let $(E_1, \mathcal{A}_1)$ and $(E_2, \mathcal{A}_2)$ be measurable spaces.

The product $σ$-algebra $\mathcal{A}_1 \otimes \mathcal{A}_2$ on $E_1 \times E_2$ is $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{A}_1 \otimes \mathcal{A}_2 = σ\{ A_1 \times A_2 \ |\ A_1 \in \mathcal{A}_1, A_2 \in \mathcal{A}_2\}. \end{align*} $$
