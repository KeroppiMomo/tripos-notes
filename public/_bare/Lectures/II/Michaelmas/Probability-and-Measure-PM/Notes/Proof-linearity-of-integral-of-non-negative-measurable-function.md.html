Pick sequences of non-negative simple functions $(f_n)$ such that $f_n \nearrow f$ and $(g_n)$ such that $g_n \nearrow g$.

Then $af_n + bg_n \nearrow af + bg$, so apply the monotone convergence theorem and linearity of integrals of simple functions to conclude $$ \begin{align*} \int af + bg \d μ ={}& \limup_{n\to \infty} \int af_n + bg_n \d μ \\ ={}& \limup_{n\to \infty} \biggl(a \int f_n \d μ+ b \int g_n \d μ\biggr) \\ ={}& a \limup_{n\to \infty} \int f_n \d μ + b \limup_{n\to \infty} \int g_n \d μ \\ ={}& a \int f \d μ + b \int g \d μ. \end{align*} $$
