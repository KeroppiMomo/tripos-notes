Let $f: (E, \mathcal{A}) \to (F, \mathcal{B})$ be a measurable function, and $μ$ is a non-negative measure on $(E,\mathcal{A})$.

The image of $μ$ by $f$, denoted $f_\astμ$ or $f(μ)$ is the non-negative measure on $(F,\mathcal{B})$ given by $$ \begin{align*} f_\astμ(B) = μ(f\inv(B)), \qquad \forall B\in \mathcal{B}. \end{align*} $$
