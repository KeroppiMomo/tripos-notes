Let $(E,\mathcal{A},μ)$ be a measure space, and $f,g: E \to [0,\infty]$ be non-negative measurable functions.

  1. If $\int f \d μ < \infty$, then $f < \infty$ almost everywhere.

  2. $\int f \d μ = 0$ iff $f = 0$ almost everywhere.

  3. If $f=g$ almost everywhere, then $\int f \d μ = \int g \d μ$.
