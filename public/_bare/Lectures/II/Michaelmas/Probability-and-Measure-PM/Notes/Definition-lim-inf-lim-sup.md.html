Let $(a_n)_{n\geq 0}$ be a sequence in $\overline{\R}$. Define $$ \begin{align*} \limsup_{n\to \infty} a_n &:= \limdown_{n\to \infty} \sup_{k\geq n} a_k = \inf_{n\geq 0} \sup_{k\geq n} a_k, \\ \liminf_{n\to \infty} a_n &:= \limup_{n\to \infty} \inf_{k\geq n} a_k = \sup_{n\geq 0} \inf_{k\geq n} a_k. \end{align*} $$
