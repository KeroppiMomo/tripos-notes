Let $E$ be a set.

For any subset $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(E)$, the monotone class generated by $C$ is the smallest monotone class on $E$ containing $\mathcal{C}$: $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{C}) = \bigcup_{\substack{\mathcal{M} \text{ monotone class}\\ \mathcal{C}\subseteq \mathcal{M}}} \mathcal{M}. \end{align*} $$
