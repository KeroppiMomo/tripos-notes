Let $F_n = \inf_{k\geq n} f_k$. By definition $\liminf_{n\to \infty} f_n = \limup_{n\to \infty} F_n$, so by the monotone convergence theorem, $$ \begin{align*} \int \liminf_{n\to \infty} f_n \d μ = \limup_{n\to \infty} \int F_n \d μ. \end{align*} $$

Note for $k\geq n$, $$ \begin{align*} F_n \leq{}& f_k \\ \implies \int F_n \d μ \leq{} & \int f_k \d μ \\ \implies \int F_n \d μ \leq{} & \inf_{k\geq n} \int f_k \d μ. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \int \liminf_{n\to \infty} f_n \d μ \leq \limup_{n\to \infty} \inf_{k\geq n} \int f_k \d μ = \liminf_{n\to \infty} \int f_n \d μ. \end{align*} $$
