Extending a Galois extension to obtain a solvable Galois group

Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension, $a\in L^\times$ and $n \in \Z_{\geq 1}$. We claim there is a finite extension $M/L/K$ such that

  • $μ_n$${}\subseteq M$ and $\sqrt[n]{a}\in M$;

  • $M/K$ is Galois; and

  • $\Gal(M/L)$ is solvable.

Indeed, let $\Gal(L/K) = \set{σ_1, \ldots, σ_m}$ and take $$ \begin{align*} f(X) := \prod_{i=1}^{m} (X^n - σ_i(a)). \end{align*} $$ Since $σ_j(f) = f$ for all $σ_j\in \Gal(L/K)$, we have $f\in (L^{\Gal(L/K)})[X] = K[X]$.

Take $M$ to be the splitting field of $f$ over $L$, so $$ \begin{align*} M = L(μ_n, \sqrt[n]{σ_1(a)}, \ldots, \sqrt[n]{σ_m(a)}). \end{align*} $$ $M$ is the compositum of $L$ and the splitting field of $f$ over $K$, both of which are Galois over $K$ (noting $\op{char}(K) = 0$ so any splitting field is a Galois extension). Therefore $M$ is Galois over $K$.

Note $M$ is obtained as a series of cyclotomic and Kummer extensions $$ \begin{align*} L_0 &:= L, \\ L_1 &:= L_0(μ_n), \\ L_i &:= L_{i-1}(\sqrt[n]{σ_{i-1}(α)}). \end{align*} $$ Therefore $L_i/L_{i-1}$ is Galois and $\Gal(M/L_{i-1}) / \Gal(M/L_i)$${}\isom{}$$\Gal(L_i/L_{i-1})$ is abelian. Hence $$ \begin{align*} 1 = \Gal(M/L_{m+1}) \subseteq \Gal(M/L_{m}) \subseteq \ldots \subseteq \Gal(M/L_1) \subseteq \Gal(M/L_0) = \Gal(M/L) \end{align*} $$ shows $\Gal(M/L)$ is solvable.

Repeating

Back to the theorem: let $f$ be solvable by radicals over $K$, i.e. there exist fields $$ \begin{align*} K_0 = K \subseteq K_1 \subseteq \ldots \subseteq K_m \end{align*} $$ such that $f$ has a root in $K_m$ and $K_i = K_{i-1}(a_i)$ where $a_i^{n_i}\in K_{i-1}$.

Set $K_0' = K_0 = K$ and apply the above construction repeatedly (with $L = K_{i-1}'$, $a = a_i$, $n = n_i$) to obtain fields $$ \begin{align*} K_0' = K \subseteq K_1' \subseteq \ldots \subseteq K_m' \end{align*} $$ such that for all $i=1,\ldots,m$

  • $K_i' \supseteq K_i$;

  • $K_i'/K$ is Galois; and

  • $\Gal(K_i'/K_{i-1}')$ is solvable.

Since $\Gal(K_i'/K) /\Gal(K_i'/K_{i-1}') \isom \Gal(K_{i-1}'/K)$, induct (using the fact that if $N$ and $G/N$ are solvable, then $G$ is solvable) to show $\Gal(K_m'/K)$ is solvable.

Finally, since $f$ has a root in $K_m\subseteq K_{m}'$, by normality of $K_m'$, $f$ splits into linear factors in $K_m'$, so $K_m'$ contains a splitting field $L$ of $f$ over $K$. Hence $\Gal(f/K) = \Gal(L/K)\isom \Gal(K_m'/K) / \Gal(K_m'/L)$ is a quotient of the solvable group $\Gal(K_m'/K)$, hence solvable.
