Let $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_m}$ be a $L$-basis for $M$ and $\set{w_1,\ldots, w_n}$ be a $K$-basis for $L$. From the proof of the tower law, $\set{v_1 w_1, \ldots, v_1 w_n, \ldots, v_m w_1, \ldots, v_m w_n}$ is a $K$-basis for $M$.

If $A$ is the $n\times n$ matrix representing $L\to L: x \mapsto αx$, then $$ \begin{align*} B = \pmat{A \\ &\ddots \\ &&A} \end{align*} $$ is the $mn\times mn$ matrix representing $M\to M: x \mapsto αx$.

Thus $\tr_{M/K}(α) = m \tr(A)$ and $N_{M/K}(α) = (\det A)^m$.
