Let $K(ξ_n)/K$ be a cyclotomic extension.

  1. There is an injective group homomorphism, known as the cyclotomic character, $$ \begin{align*} χ: \Gal(K(ξ_n)/K) &\hookrightarrow (\Z/n\Z)^\times \\ \end{align*} $$ given by $$ \begin{align*} σ(ξ) = ξ^{χ(σ)} \end{align*} $$ for all $n$-th roots of unity $ξ\in μ_n$, where $(\Z/n\Z)^\times = \sb{a\in \Z/n\Z}{\op{gcd}(a,n) = 1}$ has order $ϕ$$(n)$.

  2. If $K = \mathbb{F}_p$, then $$ \begin{align*} [\mathbb{F}_p(ξ_n) : \mathbb{F}_p] = \text{order of $p$ in $(\Z/n\Z)^\times$} \end{align*} $$ and $\Gal$$(\mathbb{F}_p(ξ_n)/\mathbb{F}_p)$ is cyclic.

  3. If $K = \Q$, then $χ$ is an isomorphism, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \Gal(\Q(ξ_n)/\Q) \isom (\Z/n\Z)^\times. \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} [\Q(ξ_n):\Q] = ϕ(n). \end{align*} $$
