Expand to see $$ \begin{align*} β_1 - β_2 ={}& (α_1 - α_4)(α_3 - α_2), \\ β_2 - β_3 ={}& (α_1 - α_2)(α_4 - α_3), \\ β_3 - β_1 ={}& (α_1 - α_3)(α_2 - α_4). \end{align*} $$ Thus $$ \begin{align*} \op{Disc}(g) = \op{Disc}(f). \end{align*} $$

$f$ is separable $\iff$ $\op{Disc}(f) = 0$ $\iff$ $\op{Disc}(g) = 0$ $\iff$ $g$ is separable.
