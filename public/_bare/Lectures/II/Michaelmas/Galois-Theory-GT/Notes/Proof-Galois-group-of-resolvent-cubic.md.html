Let $M$ be a splitting field of $f$ over $K$, and let $α_1, \ldots, α_4$ be the roots of $f$ in $M$. Then $L = K(β_1, β_2, β_3)$ is a splitting field of $g$ over $K$.

Note the restriction $\Gal(M/K) \to \Gal(L/K)$ sends $σ\in \Gal(f/K)$ to $π(σ)\in \Gal(g/K)$, because $β_1, β_2, β_3$ correspond to elements of $V \setminus \set{\id}$.

Since $L/K$ is Galois, the restriction map is surjective, so $π(\Gal(f/K)) = \Gal(g/K)$.
