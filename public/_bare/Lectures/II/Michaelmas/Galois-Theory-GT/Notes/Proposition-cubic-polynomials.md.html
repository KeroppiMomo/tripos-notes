Let $K$ be a field and $f(X) = X^3 + aX^2 + bX + c \in K[X]$ be a cubic polynomial.

  1. If $\op{char}(K)\neq 2$ and $f$ is irreducible and separable, then

    • $\Gal$$(f/K)\isom A_3$ if $\op{Disc}(f)$ is a square in $K$; and

    • $\Gal(f/K)\isom S_3$ otherwise.

    Proof: the only transitive subgroups of $S_3$ are $A_3$ and $S_3$. Then apply the discriminant criterion.

  2. Make a substitution $X\mapsto X-\frac{a}{3}$ to assume $f$ is a depressed cubic $$ \begin{align*} f(X) = X^3 + bX + c. \end{align*} $$

    Letting $α, β, γ$ be the roots of $f$ and $ω = e^{2πi/3}$, then $$ \begin{align*} 0 ={}& α + β + γ, \\ u :={}& α + ωβ + ω^2 γ, \\ v :={}& α + ω^2 β + ωγ \end{align*} $$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} u^3 + v^3 &= -27c, & uv &= -3b \end{align*} $$ so $u^3$ and $v^3$ are the roots of $$ \begin{align*} X^2 + 27cX - 27b^3 = 0. \end{align*} $$

    Solving for $u^3,v^3$, taking their cube roots, and solving for $α,β,γ$ gives the roots of $f$.
