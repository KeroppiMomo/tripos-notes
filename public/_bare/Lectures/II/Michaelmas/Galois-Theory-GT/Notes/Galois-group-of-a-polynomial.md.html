Definition (symmetric polynomial) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring.

A polynomial $f\in R[X_1, \ldots, X_n]$ is symmetric if for all permutation $σ\in S_n$, $$ \begin{align*} f(X_{σ(1)}, \ldots, X_{σ(n)}) = f(X_1, \ldots, X_n). \end{align*} $$

Write $R[X_1, \ldots, X_n]^{S_n}$ to denote all symmetric polynomials.

Theorem (fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials) open_in_new

The elementary symmetric polynomials in $n$ variables are $s_1, \ldots, s_n$ given by $$ \begin{align*} s_r(X_1, \ldots, X_n) = \sum_{1\leq i_1 < \ldots < i_r \leq n} X_{i_1} \ldots X_{i_r}. \end{align*} $$ Equivalently, they satisfy $$ \begin{align*} \prod_{i=1}^{n} (X_i + T) = T^n + s_1 T^{n-1} + \ldots + s_{n-1} T + s_n. \end{align*} $$

There is a ring isomorphism given by $$ \begin{align*} R[Y_1, \ldots, Y_n] &\to R[X_1, \ldots, X_n]^{S_n} \\ Y_i &\mapsto s_i(X_1, \ldots, X_n). \end{align*} $$

Definition (Galois group of polynomial) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field.

For a separable polynomial $f\in K[X]$, let $L = K(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$ be the its splitting field of $f$ over $K$ and $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ be the roots of $f$ in $L$ (so $\deg f = n$). The Galois group $\Gal(L/K)$ acts on $\set{α_1, \ldots, α_n}$ faithfully, determining an injective group homomorphism $\Gal(L/K) \hookrightarrow S_n$.

The Galois group $\Gal(f/K) \leq S_n$ of $f$ over $K$ is the image of the homomorphism, defined up to conjugacy.

$f$ is irreducible iff $\Gal(f/K)$ is transitive. (Proof)

Definition (discriminant) open_in_new

Let $f\in K[X]$ be a monic separable polynomial, and let $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ be its roots in some splitting field.

Define $$ \begin{align*} Δ &:= \prod_{i<j} (α_i - α_j), \\ \op{Disc}(f) &:= Δ^2 = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2} \prod_{i\neq j} (α_i - α_j). \end{align*} $$ $\op{Disc}(f)$ is called the discriminant of $f$.

$\op{Disc}(f) \in K$. (Proof)

We have a formula $$ \begin{align*} \op{Disc}(X^n + bX + c) ={}& (-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\bigl((1-n)^{n-1} b^n + n^n c^{n-1}\bigr). \end{align*} $$ (sheet 3 Q7). In particular for $n=2,3$, $$ \begin{align*} \op{Disc}(X^2 + bX + c) &= b^2 - 4c, \\ \op{Disc}(X^3 + bX + c) &= -4b^3 - 27c. \end{align*} $$

Lemma (Galois group in alternating group) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field and $f\in K[X]$ be a separable polynomial.

If $\op{char}(K)\neq 2$, then the fixed field of $\Gal$$(f/K) \cap A_n$ is $K($$Δ$$)$.

Moreover, $\Gal(f/K)\subseteq A_n$ iff $\op{Disc}(f)$ is a square in $K$.

Definition (resolvent cubic) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field and $f\in k[X]$ be a monic quartic (degree 4) polynomial.

Letting $α_1, α_2, α_3, α_4$ be the roots of $f$ in some splitting field, define $$ \begin{align*} β_1 ={}&(α_1 + α_2)(α_3 + α_4) \\ β_2 ={}& (α_1 + α_3)(α_2 + α_4) \\ β_3 ={}& (α_1 + α_4)(α_2 + α_3). \end{align*} $$

The resolvent cubic of $f$ is $$ \begin{align*} g(X) = \prod_{i=1}^{3} (X - β_i). \end{align*} $$

$g\in k[X]$. (Proof)

$\op{Disc}$$(f) = \op{Disc}(g)$. In particular, $f$ is separable iff $g$ is separable. (Proof)

For a depressed quartic $f(X) = X^4 + pX^2 + qX + r$, $$ \begin{align*} g(X) = X^3 - 2pX^2 + (p^2 - 4r)X + q^2. \end{align*} $$

Let $S_4$ act on $V \setminus \set{\id} = \set{(12)(34), (13)(24), (14)(23)}$ by conjugation, and let $π: S_4 \to S_3$ be the corresponding homomorphism (surjective and $\ker π = V$). Then if $f$ is separable, then $$ \begin{align*} π(\Gal(f/K)) = \Gal(g/K). \end{align*} $$ (Proof)

Lemma (Galois group over F_p) open_in_new

Let $f\in \mathbb{F}_p[X]$ be a separable polynomial.

If $f$ has irreducible factors of degree $n_1, \ldots, n_r$ (so $\deg f = \sum_{i=1}^{r} n_i$), then $\Gal$$(f/\mathbb{F}_p)$ is cyclic, generated by an element of cycle type $(n_1, \ldots, n_r)$.
