It is clear that $ϕ(0) = 0$, $ϕ(1) = 1$ and $ϕ(xy) = (xy)^p = x^p y^p = ϕ(x) ϕ(y)$.

Since $p$ is prime, $p \mid \pmat{p\\ i}$. Using the binomial theorem, $$ \begin{align*} ϕ(x + y) = x^p + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} \pmat{p \\ i} x^{p-i} y^i + y^p = x^p + y^p = ϕ(x) + ϕ(y). \end{align*} $$

Moreover by Fermat's little theorem, $x^p = x$ for $x\in \mathbb{F}_p$.
