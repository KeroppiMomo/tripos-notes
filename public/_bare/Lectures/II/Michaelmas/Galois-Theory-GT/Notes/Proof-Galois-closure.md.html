By the primitive element theorem, $L = K(α)$ for some $α\in L$. Let $f$ be the minimal polynomial of $α$ over $K$. $L/K$ is separable implies $f$ is separable.

Let $M$ be splitting field of $f$ over $L$. Since $L = K(α)$, where $α$ is a root of $f$, it follows that $M$ is a splitting field of $f$ over $K$. Theorem 6.2 shows $M/K$ is Galois.

Let $M'$ as in 2. As $α\in M'$ and $M'/K$ is Galois, in particular normal, $f$ splits into linear factors in $M'$, hence $M = M'$.
