$α + β + γ = 0$ because the $X^2$ coefficient is zero.

Either expand $u^3 + v^3$ and $uv$, or note $u^3+v^3$ and $uv$ are symmetric polynomials in $α,β,γ$. Indeed we check generators $(α\mapsto β, β\mapsto γ, γ\mapsto α)$ and $(β\mapsto γ, γ\mapsto β)$ of the permutation group $S_3$:

  1. Under the 3-cycle, $u \mapsto ω^2 u$ and $v\mapsto ωv$.

  2. Under the 2-cycle, $u \mapsto v$ and $v \mapsto u$.

Therefore by the fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials, they are polynomials in the elementary symmetric polynomials. Because $s_1(α, β, γ) = 0$, $s_2(α, β, γ) = b$, $s_3(α, β, γ) = -c$, we can write $$ \begin{align*} u^3 + v^3 = -λc, \quad uv = μb \end{align*} $$ as $u^3+v^3$ has degree 3 and $uv$ has degree 2. Substituting any value of $(α,β,γ)$ gives $λ = 27$ and $μ = -3$.
