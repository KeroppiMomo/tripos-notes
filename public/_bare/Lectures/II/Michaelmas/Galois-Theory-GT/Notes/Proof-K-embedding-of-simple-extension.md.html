Well-definedness

For any $σ$-embedding $τ: L\to M$, note $σ(f)(τ(α)) = τ(f)(τ(α)) = τ(f(α)) = τ(0) = 0$, so $τ(α)$ is indeed a root of $σ(f)$.

Injectivity

Notice the value $τ(α)$ uniquely determines a $σ$-embedding $τ$: $$ \begin{align*} τ\left(\sum_{i=0}^{|\deg f-1|} a_i α^i\right) = \sum_{i=0}^{|\deg f-1|} a_i τ(α)^i. \end{align*} $$

Surjectivity

Evaluating at $α$ gives a $K$-isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: K[X]/(f) &\xrightarrow{\sim} L \\ X + (f) &\mapsto α. \end{align*} $$

Let $β\in M$ be a root of $σ(f)$ in $M$. Since $σ(f)$ is irreducible over $σ(K)$, evaluating at $β$ gives a $σ(K)$-embedding $$ \begin{align*} ψ: σ(K)[X]/(σ(f)) &\xrightarrow{\sim} M \\ X + (σ(f)) &\mapsto β. \end{align*} $$

Composing yields a $K$-isomorphism $$ \begin{alignat}{2} ψ\circ σ \circ ϕ\inv: \qquad L &\to K[X]/(f) &&\to σ(K)[X]/(σ(f)) &&\to M \\ α &\mapsto X + (f) &&\mapsto X + (σ(f)) &&\mapsto β \end{alignat} $$ as required.
