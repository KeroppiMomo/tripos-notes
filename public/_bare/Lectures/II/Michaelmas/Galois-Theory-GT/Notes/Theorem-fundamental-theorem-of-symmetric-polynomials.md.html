The elementary symmetric polynomials in $n$ variables are $s_1, \ldots, s_n$ given by $$ \begin{align*} s_r(X_1, \ldots, X_n) = \sum_{1\leq i_1 < \ldots < i_r \leq n} X_{i_1} \ldots X_{i_r}. \end{align*} $$ Equivalently, they satisfy $$ \begin{align*} \prod_{i=1}^{n} (X_i + T) = T^n + s_1 T^{n-1} + \ldots + s_{n-1} T + s_n. \end{align*} $$

There is a ring isomorphism given by $$ \begin{align*} R[Y_1, \ldots, Y_n] &\to R[X_1, \ldots, X_n]^{S_n} \\ Y_i &\mapsto s_i(X_1, \ldots, X_n). \end{align*} $$
