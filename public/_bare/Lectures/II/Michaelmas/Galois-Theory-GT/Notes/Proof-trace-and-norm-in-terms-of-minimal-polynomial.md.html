It suffices to show $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{K(α)/K}(α) &= -a_{n-1}, & N_{K(α)/K} &= (-1)^n a_0. \end{align*} $$

If $A$ represents $m_α$ with respect to the basis $1, α, \ldots, α^{n-1}$, then $$ \begin{align*} A = \pmat{0 &&&& -a_0\\ 1 & 0 &&& - a_1\\ & 1 & \ddots && \vdots\\ && \ddots & 0 & -a_{n-2}\\ &&&1 & -a_{n-1}}. \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{L/K}(α) ={}& \tr(A) = -a_{n-1}, \\ N_{L/K}(α) ={}& \det(A) = (-1)^n a_0. \end{align*} $$
