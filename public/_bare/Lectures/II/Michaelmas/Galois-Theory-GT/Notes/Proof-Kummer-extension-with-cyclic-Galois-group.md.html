Let $L/K$ be Galois and $\Gal(L/K) = \langle σ\rangle\isom C_n$. Fix a primitive $n$-th root of unity $ξ_n\in K$.

By linear independence of field embeddings, there exists $x\in L$ such that $$ \begin{align*} α := \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} ξ_n^i \, σ^i(x) \neq 0. \end{align*} $$ Note $$ \begin{align*} σ(α) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} ξ_n^i σ^{i+1}(x) = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} ξ_n^{n-1} σ^i(x) = ξ_n^{-1} α \end{align*} $$ since $σ^n = \id$.

Hence if $f$ is the minimal polynomial of $α$ over $K$, because $τ\in \Gal(L/K)$ sends roots of $f$ to roots of $f$, $f$ has at least $n$ distinct roots $α, ξ_n α, \ldots, ξ_{n}^{n-1} α$. As $[L:K] = n$, we have $L = K(α)$.

Finally $σ(α^n) = (ξ_n\inv)^n α^n = α^n$, so $α^n\in L^{\Gal(L/K)} = K$. Thus $f(X) = X^n - α^n \in K[X]$.
