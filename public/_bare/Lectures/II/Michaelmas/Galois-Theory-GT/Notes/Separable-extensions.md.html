Lemma (GCD of polynomial is independent of base field) open_in_new

Let $L/K$ be a field extension.

For $f,g\in K[X]^\times$, their greatest common divisor is the same in $K[X]$ and in $L[X]$.

Proof: the Euclidean algorithm gives the same answer.

Definition (separable polynomial, extension) open_in_new

Let $L/K$ be a field extension.

A polynomial $f\in k[X]$ is separable if it splits into distinct linear factors over some (equivalently all) splitting fields of $f$.

An element $α\in L$ is separable over $K$ if $α$ is algebraic over $K$, and its minimal polynomial is separable over $K$.

$L/K$ is separable if every $α\in L$ is separable over $K$.

If $L/K$ is separable, then $L/K$ is algebraic.

$f$ is separable iff $\op{gcd}$$(f,f') = 1$. Note $\op{gcd}$ is independent of which base field you take. (Proof)

If $f$ is irreducible, then $f$ is separable iff $\op{char}K =: p >0$ and $f(X) = g(X^p)$ for some $g\in k[X]$. (Proof)

Theorem (embedding of adjoined extension and degree) open_in_new

Let $L/K$ be a finite extension, and $σ: K\hookrightarrow M$ be a field embedding.

Then $\#$${}\op{Hom}_σ$$(L, M) \leq{}$$[L:K]$.

Moreover, let $L = K(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$ and $f_i$ be the minimal polynomial of $α_i$ over $K$. Equality is achieved iff all $σ(f_i)$ split into distinct linear factors over $M$.

Proof

If $M$ is a field extension of $K$ such that $f_1, \ldots, f_n$ all split into linear factors over $M$ (such as the algebraic closure of $K$), then the following are equivalent:

  1. $L/K$ is separable;

  2. $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ are separable;

  3. $\#\op{Hom}_K(L,M) = [L:K]$.

(Proof)
