Theorem (Artin, Galois over fixed field) open_in_new

Let $L$ be a field.

For any finite subgroup $L \subseteq \Aut(L)$ of automorphisms, $L/L^G$ is finite and Galois and $$ \begin{align*} \Gal(L/L^G) = G. \end{align*} $$

In particular, $[L:L^G] = \# G$.

Proof

To compute $L^G$, guess some elements $α_1, \ldots, α_n\in L^G$, compute $[L:L(α_1, \ldots, α_n)]$, and verify it equals $\#G$.
