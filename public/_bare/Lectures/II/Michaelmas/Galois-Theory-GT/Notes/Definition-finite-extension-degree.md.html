Let $L/K$ be a field extension. Then $L$ is a $K$-vector space with the same operations $+$ and $\times$.

We say $L/K$ is finite if $L$ is a finite-dimensional $K$-vector space, in which case the degree $[L:K]$ of $L/K$ is the dimension.

Otherwise $L/K$ is infinite and $[L:K] = \infty$.

Call $L/K$ quadratic, cubic etc if $[L:K] = 2,3$ etc.
