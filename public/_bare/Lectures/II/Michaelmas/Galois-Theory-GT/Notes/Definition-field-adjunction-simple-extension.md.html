Let $L/K$ be a field extension, and $α_1, \ldots, α_n\in L$.

  • Define $K[α_1, \ldots, α_n] = \sb{f(α_1, \ldots, α_n)}{f\in K[X_1, \ldots, X_n]}$ as the smallest subring of $L$ containing $K$ and $α_1, \ldots, α_n$.

    It is the image of the evaluation homomorphism $K[X_1, \ldots, X_n] \to L: X_i \mapsto α_i$.

  • Define $K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) = \sb{\frac{f(α_1, \ldots, α_n)}{g(α_1, \ldots, α_n)}}{f,g\in K[X_1, \ldots, X_n]}$ as the smallest subfield of $L$ containing $K$ and $α_1, \ldots, α_n$.

    It is the field of fractions of $K[α_1, \ldots, α_n]$.

  • $L/K$ is simple if $L = K(α)$ for some $α\in L$.
