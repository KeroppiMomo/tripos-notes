Let $L/K$ be a field extension.

An automorphism of $L$ is a bijective ring homomorphism $L\to L$. The automorphism group $\Aut(L)$ is the set of all automorphisms of $L$, with the group operation being composition.

A $K$-automorphism of $L$ is an automorphism $σ: L\to L$ such that $L|_K = \id_K$. They form a subgroup $\Aut(L/K)$ of $\Aut(L)$.

Check the inverse of a bijective field homomorphism is a field homomorphism.

$\Aut(L) = \Aut(L/K)$ for $K$ the prime subfield $\Q$ or $\mathbb{F}_p$ of $L$.

If $L/K$ is finite, then $\Aut(L/K) = \op{Hom}_K(L,L)$ because any embedding $L\to L$ is surjective by rank-nullity. Therefore $\# \Aut(L/K) \leq [L:K]$.
