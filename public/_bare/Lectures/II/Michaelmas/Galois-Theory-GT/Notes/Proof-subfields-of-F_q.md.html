Subgroups of $G := \Gal(\mathbb{F}_{p^n}/\mathbb{F}_p) = \langle ϕ\rangle \isom C_{n}$ (where $ϕ: x \mapsto x^p$ is the Frobenius map) are precisely $H = \langle ϕ^m\rangle \isom C_{p^{n/m}}$ for $m\mid n$. By correspondence, subfields of $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ are precisely $K = (\mathbb{F}_{p^n})^{H}$. Since $[K:\mathbb{F}_p] = [\mathbb{F}_{p^n} : \mathbb{F}_p] / [\mathbb{F}_{p^n}: K] = \#G /\#H = m$, we have $\#K = p^m$.
