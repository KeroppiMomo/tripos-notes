Suppose $\#\op{Hom}_K(L,M) = [L:K]$, so $f_1, \ldots, f_n$ splits into distinct linear factors in $M$. For any $β\in L$, because $L = K(α_1, \ldots, α_n, β)$ still satisfies $\# \op{Hom}_K(L,M) = [L:K]$, theorem implies the minimal polynomial of $β$ splits into distinct linear factors over $M$, i.e. $β$ is separable.
