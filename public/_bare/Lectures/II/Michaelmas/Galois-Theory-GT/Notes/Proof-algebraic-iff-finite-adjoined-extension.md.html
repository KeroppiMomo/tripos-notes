The case $n=1$ is clear from remarks here.

$[K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) : K] < \infty$ $\implies$ $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ are algebraic

Since $K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) / K(α_i) / K$, we see $K(α_i) / K$ is finite, so $α_i$ is algebraic.

$α_1, \ldots, α_n$ are algebraic $\implies$ $[K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) : K] < \infty$

Induct on $n$: since $α_n$ is algebraic over $K(α_1, \ldots, α_{n-1})$, we get that $[K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) : K(α_1, \ldots, α_{n-1})] < \infty$. Thus by the tower law, $[K(α_1, \ldots, α_n): K] = [K(α_1, \ldots, α_n) : K(α_1, \ldots, α_{n-1})] [K(α_1, \ldots, α_{n-1}) : K] < \infty$.
