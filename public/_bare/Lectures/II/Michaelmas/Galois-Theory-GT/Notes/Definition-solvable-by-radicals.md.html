Let $K$ be a field.

An irreducible polynomial $f\in K[X]$ is solvable by radicals over $K$ if there exist fields $$ \begin{align*} K = K_0 \subseteq K_1 \subseteq \ldots \subseteq K_m \end{align*} $$ such that

  • $f$ has a root in $K_m$; and

  • $K_i ={}$$K_{i-1}(α_i)$ for some $α_i^{n_i} \in K_{i-1}$, $n_i\geq 1$ for all $i=1,\ldots,m$.
