Let $L$ be a splitting field of $f$ over $K$.

Since $\sign(σ)$ is the number of inversions of $σ\in S_n$, for $σ\in \Gal(f/K)$ $$ \begin{align*} σ(Δ) = \prod_{i<j} (X_{σ(i)} - X_{σ(j)}) = \sign(σ) \prod_{i<j} (X_i - X_j) = \sign(σ) Δ. \end{align*} $$ Since $f$ is separable, $Δ\neq 0$. Because $\op{char}(K)\neq 2$, $Δ\neq -Δ$.

Hence $$ \begin{align*} \Gal(f/K) \cap A_n ={}& \sb{σ\in \Gal(f/K)}{\sign(σ) = +1} \\ ={}& \sb{σ\in \Gal(f/K)}{σ(Δ) = Δ} = \Gal(f/K(Δ)). \end{align*} $$ Thus by Galois correspondence $$ \begin{align*} L^{\Gal(f/K)\cap A_n} = K(Δ). \end{align*} $$

Therefore $\Gal(f/K) \subseteq A_n$ $\iff$ $\Gal(f/K)\cap A_n = \Gal(f/K)$ $\iff$ $K(Δ) = L^{\Gal(f/K)\cap A_n} = L^{\Gal(f/K)} = K$ $\iff$ $Δ\in K$ $\iff$ $\op{Disc}(f)$ is a square in $K$.
