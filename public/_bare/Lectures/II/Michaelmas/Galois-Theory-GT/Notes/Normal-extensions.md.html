Definition (normal extension) open_in_new

A field extension $L/K$ is normal if it is algebraic, and for all $x\in L$, the minimal polynomial of $x$ over $K$ splits into linear factors over $L$.

Let $M/L/K$ be extensions and $L/K$ be normal. Then any $K$-automorphism of $M$ restricts to a $K$-automorphism of $L$. (Proof)

Theorem (finite normal extension) open_in_new

Let $L/K$ be a finite extension.

$L/K$ is normal iff $L$ is the splitting field of some (possibly reducible) polynomial in $K[X]$ over $K$.

Proof

Given any finite extension $L = K(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$ of $K$ with $f_i$ the minimal polynomial of $α_i$, the normal closure of $L/K$ is any splitting field $M$ of $\prod_{i=1}^{n} f_i$ over $L$, which is the unique extension (up to isomorphism) satisfying that if $M/M'/L$ where $M'$ is normal, then $M' = M$.
