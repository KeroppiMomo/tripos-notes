We may assume $M = L(α)$.

Let $f$ be the minimal polynomial of $α$ over $L$, say $f(X) = X^n + a_{n-1} X^{n-1} + \ldots + a_1 X + a_0\in L[X]$.

Say $L/K$ has basis $v_1, \ldots, v_m$, and $M/L$ has basis $1, α, \ldots, α^{n-1}$.

Let $$ \begin{align*} B = \pmat{0 &&&& -A_0\\ I & 0 &&& - A_1\\ & I & \ddots && \vdots\\ && \ddots & 0 & -A_{n-2}\\ &&&I & -A_{n-1}} \end{align*} $$ where $A_i$ is $m\times m$, $B$ is $mn \times mn$, and $A_i$ represents $m_{a_i}$ with respect to $v_1, \ldots, v_m$.

Then $B$ represents $m_α$ with respect to $\sb{v_i α^{j-1}}{1\leq i\leq m, 1\leq j\leq n}$.

We compute $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{M/K}(α) ={}& \tr(B) \\ ={}& -\tr(A_{n-1}) \\ ={}& \tr_{L/K}(-a_{n-1}) \\ ={}& \tr_{L/K}(\tr_{M/L}(α)), \\ N_{M/K}(α) ={}& \det(B) \\ ={}& (-1)^{mn} \det(A_0) \\ ={}& N_{L/K}((-1)^n a_0) \\ ={}& N_{L/K}(N_{M/L}(α)). \end{align*} $$
