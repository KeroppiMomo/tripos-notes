Note $\op{Disc}(f)$ is a symmetric polynomial in $α_1, \ldots, α_n$. Therefore by the fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials, it can be written as a polynomial of $s_1(α_1, \ldots, α_n), \ldots, s_n(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$, where $$ \begin{align*} f(X) &= \prod_{i=1}^{n} (X - α_i) \\ &= X^n - s_1(α_1,\ldots, α_n) X^{n-1} + s_2(α_1, \ldots, α_n) X^{n-2} - \ldots + (-1)^n s_n(α_1, \ldots, α_n). \end{align*} $$

Because $s_r(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$ are coefficients of $f$, they are in $K$.
