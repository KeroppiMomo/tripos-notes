Definition (trace, norm) open_in_new

Let $L/K$ be a finite field extension.

For $α\in L$, let $m_α: L\to L: x \mapsto αx$ be the $K$-linear map given by multiplication by $α$.

The trace and norm maps are $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{L/K}: L&\to K, &N_{L/K}: L&\to K \\ α &\mapsto \tr(m_α), & α &\mapsto \det(m_α). \end{align*} $$ ($\tr$, $\det$)

$\tr_{L/K}$ is $K$-linear.

$N_{L/K}$ is multiplicative, i.e. $N_{L/K}(αβ) = N_{L/K}(α) \cdot N_{L/K}(β)$.

$N_{L/K}(α) = 0$ $\iff$ $m_α$ is singular $\iff$ $α = 0$.

If $M/L/K$ and $α\in L$, then $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{M/K}(α) &= [M:L] \tr_{L/K}(α), & N_{M/K}(α) &= (N_{L/K}(α))^{[M:L]}. \end{align*} $$ In particular, if $α\in K$, then $\tr_{L/K}(α) = [L:K]α$ and $N_{L/K}(α) = α^{[L:K]}$. (Proof)

If $α\in L$ has a minimal polynomial $f(X) = X^n + a_{n-1} X^{n-1} + \ldots + a_1 X + a_0\in K[X]$ over $K$, then $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{L/K}(α) &= -[L:K(α)] a_{n-1} = [L:K(α)] \cdot (\text{sum of roots of $f$}), \\ N_{L/K}(α) &= ((-1)^n a_0)^{[L:K(α)]} = (\text{product of roots of $f$})^{[L:K(α)]}. \end{align*} $$ (Proof)

Trace and norm are transitive, i.e. if $M/L/K$ and $α\in M$, then $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{M/K}(α) ={}& \tr_{L/K}(\tr_{M/L}(α)), & N_{M/K}(α) ={}& N_{L/K}(N_{M/L}(α)). \end{align*} $$ (Proof)

Theorem (trace and norm in terms of embeddings) open_in_new

Let $M/L/K$ be field extensions, with $L/K$ finite separable and $M/K$ normal. Let $\{σ_1, \ldots, σ_d\}=\Hom_K(L,M)$ (so $[L:K] = d$). Then for all $α\in L$, $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{L/K}(α) &= \sum_{i=1}^{d} σ_i(α), & N_{L/K}(α) &= \prod_{i=1}^{d} σ_i(α). \end{align*} $$ ($\tr$, $N$)
