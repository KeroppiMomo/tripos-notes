Let $f$ be a monic and separable polynomial in $\Z[X]$, and $p$ be a prime number.

If the reduction $\bar f\in \mathbb{F}_p[X]$ mod $p$ of $f$ is separable, then $$ \begin{align*} \Gal(\bar f/\mathbb{F}_p) \subseteq \Gal(f/\Q) \end{align*} $$ as subgroups of $S_{\deg f}$ up to conjugacy. ($\Gal$)
