Definition (splitting field) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field and $f\in K[X]^\times$ be a non-zero polynomial.

An extension $L/K$ is a splitting field of $f$ over $K$ if

  1. $f$ splits into linear factors in $L$; and

  2. $L ={}$$K(α_1, \ldots, α_n)$ where $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ are the roots of $f$ in $L$.

Therefore splitting fields are finite extensions.

Theorem (existence of splitting field) open_in_new

For any field $K$ and $f\in K[X]^\times$, there exists a splitting field of $f$ over $K$.

Proof

Theorem (uniqueness of splitting field) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field and $f\in K[X]^\times$.

Let $L$ be a splitting field of $f$ over $K$, and $σ: K\hookrightarrow M$ be a field embedding where $σ(f)$ splits into linear factors in $M$.

  1. There exists a $σ$-embedding $L\hookrightarrow M$.

  2. If $M$ is a splitting field of $σ(f)$ over $σ(M)$, then any $σ$-embedding $L \to M$ is an isomorphism.

Proof
