Existence of $σ$-embedding $L\hookrightarrow M$

Apply Zorn's Lemma to the partial order $$ \begin{align*} (S = \set{\text{$σ$-embedding $F\hookrightarrow M$ where $L/F/K$}}, \subseteq) \end{align*} $$ (where we compare two $σ$-embeddings $τ_1, τ_2$ in the set-theoretic way: $τ_1\subseteq τ_2$ iff $\op{dom}(τ_1) \subseteq \op{dom}(τ_2)$ and $τ_1 = τ_2|_{\op{dom(τ_1)}}$).

$S$ is non-empty as $σ\in S$.

If $T$ is a chain of $S$, we take $σ$ as an upper bound if $T$ is empty. Otherwise, let $$ \begin{align*} ϕ := \bigcup_{τ\in T} τ: \quad \bigcup_{τ\in T} \op{dom}(τ) &\to M \\ x &\mapsto τ(x) &\text{if $x\in \op{dom}(τ)$}, \end{align*} $$ which is a well-defined $σ$-embedding. This is an upper bound of $T$.

Therefore Zorn's lemma provides a maximal element $τ: F\hookrightarrow M$. We claim $F = L$. Otherwise, for any $α\in L\setminus F$, which is algebraic over $K$, let $f\in K[X]$ be minimal polynomial of $α$. As $M$ is algebraically closed, pick a root $β\in M$ of $f$ in $M$. Then there exists a $τ$-embedding $$ \begin{align*} F(α) &\hookrightarrow M \\ α &\mapsto β, \end{align*} $$ which extends $τ$, contradicting maximality of $τ$.

$L,M$ are algebraic closures

Let $τ: L\hookrightarrow M$ be a $σ$-embedding. Then we have a tower $M / τ(L) / K$ of extensions. Since $M/K$ is algebraic, so is $M/τ(L)$. But $τ(L)\isom L$ is algebraically closed, so $M = τ(L)$. Thus $τ$ is surjective, hence an isomorphism.
