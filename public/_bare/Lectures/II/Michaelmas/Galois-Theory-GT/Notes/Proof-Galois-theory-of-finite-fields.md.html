Let $\# K = q = p^n$ and $ϕ^n: x \mapsto x^q$. Let $G \subseteq \Aut(L/K)$ be the subgroup generated by $ϕ^n$. Then $$ \begin{align*} L^G = L^{ϕ^n} = \sb{α\in L}{α^q = α} = K, \end{align*} $$ because all $q$-many elements of $K$ satisfies $X^q - X$ and $X^q - X$ has at most $q$ roots. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} K \subseteq L^{\Aut(L/K)} \subseteq L^G = K. \end{align*} $$ Hence $L^{\Aut(L/K)} = K$, so $L/K$ is Galois, and by correspondence, $G = \Aut(L/K)$.
