In a splitting field $L$, $$ \begin{align*} f(X) = \prod_{i=1}^{n} (X-α_i)^{m_i} \end{align*} $$ for distinct roots $α_1, \ldots, α_n\in L$ and $m_i\geq 1$ for all $i$. Note its formal derivative is $$ \begin{align*} f'(X) = \sum_{i=1}^{m} m_i(X - α_i)^{m_i-1} \prod_{\substack{j=1: \\j\neq i}}^{m} (X-α_j)^{m_j}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $α_i$ is a simple root (i.e. $m_i=1$) iff $f'(α_i)\neq 0$, which is equivalent to $(X-α_i)\not\mid f'$. Hence $f$ is separable iff $(X-α_i)\not\mid f'$ for all $i$, equivalently $\op{gcd}(f, f') = 1$.
