Suppose any non-constant polynomial in $K[X]$ splits in $L$. We use the formulation that $L$ is algebraically closed iff any algebraic extension $M$ over $L$ has $M=L$. Indeed if $M$ is algebraic over $L$, then $M$ is algebraic over $K$. Therefore for any $α\in M$, its minimal polynomial $f\in K[X]$ over $K$ splits in $L$, so $α\in L$.

The other direction is trivial.
