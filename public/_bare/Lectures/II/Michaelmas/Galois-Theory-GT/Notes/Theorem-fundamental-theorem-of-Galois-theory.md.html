Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension.

There are inclusion-reversing bijections $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{intermediate subfield }L/F/K} &\leftrightarrow \set{\text{subgroups of $\Gal(L/K)$}} \\ \sb{\text{intermediate subfield $L/F/K$}}{\text{$F/K$ is Galois}} &\leftrightarrow \set{\text{normal subgroups of $\Gal(L/K)$}} \\ F &\mapsto \Gal(L/F) \\ L^H &\mapsfrom H. \end{align*} $$

For any intermediate subfield $F$, $L/F$ is a Galois extension. The following are equivalent:

  1. $F/K$ is Galois;

  2. $σ(F) = F$ for all $σ\in \Gal(L/K)$;

  3. $\Gal(L/F)$ is a normal subgroup of $\Gal(L/K)$.

In this case, restriction induces a group isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} {\Gal(L/K)}\; /\; {\Gal(L/F)} &\xrightarrow{\sim} \Gal(F/K) \\ σ + \Gal(L/F) &\mapsto σ|_F. \end{align*} $$
