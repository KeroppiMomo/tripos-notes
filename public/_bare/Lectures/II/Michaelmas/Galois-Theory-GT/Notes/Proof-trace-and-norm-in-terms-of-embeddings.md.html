Let $f\in K[X]$ be the separable minimal polynomial of $α$ over $K$. It has roots $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ in $M$.

Recall there is a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \Hom_K(K(α), M) &\leftrightarrow \set{α_1, \ldots, α_n} \\ τ &\mapsto τ(α). \end{align*} $$ Moreover, each $K$-embedding $K(α)\hookrightarrow M$ extends to $L\hookrightarrow M$ in exactly $[L:K(α)]$ ways. In other words, for each $α_j$, $σ_i(α) = α_j$ for exactly $[L:K(α)]$-many $i$'s.

Therefore using the formula of trace and norm in terms of the minimal polynomial, $$ \begin{align*} \tr_{L/K} ={}& [L:K(α)] \sum_{j=1}^{n} α_j = \sum_{i=1}^d σ_i(α), \\ N_{L/K} ={}& \left(\prod_{j=1}^{n} α_j\right)^{[L:K(α)]} = \prod_{i=1}^d σ_i(α). \end{align*} $$
