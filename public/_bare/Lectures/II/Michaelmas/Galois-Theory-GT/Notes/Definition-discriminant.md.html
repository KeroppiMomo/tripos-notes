Let $f\in K[X]$ be a monic separable polynomial, and let $α_1, \ldots, α_n$ be its roots in some splitting field.

Define $$ \begin{align*} Δ &:= \prod_{i<j} (α_i - α_j), \\ \op{Disc}(f) &:= Δ^2 = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2} \prod_{i\neq j} (α_i - α_j). \end{align*} $$ $\op{Disc}(f)$ is called the discriminant of $f$.

$\op{Disc}(f) \in K$. (Proof)

We have a formula $$ \begin{align*} \op{Disc}(X^n + bX + c) ={}& (-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\bigl((1-n)^{n-1} b^n + n^n c^{n-1}\bigr). \end{align*} $$ (sheet 3 Q7). In particular for $n=2,3$, $$ \begin{align*} \op{Disc}(X^2 + bX + c) &= b^2 - 4c, \\ \op{Disc}(X^3 + bX + c) &= -4b^3 - 27c. \end{align*} $$
