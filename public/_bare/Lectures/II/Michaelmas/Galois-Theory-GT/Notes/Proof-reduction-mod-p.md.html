Let $K$ be a field, and $f\in K[X]$ be monic and separable of degree $n$, with splitting field $L$ and roots $α_1, \ldots, α_n\in L$.

Let $$ \begin{align*} F(T_1, \ldots, T_n, X) = \prod_{σ\in S_n} (X - (α_1 T_{σ(1)} + \ldots + α_n T_{σ(n)})). \end{align*} $$ Then $F(T_1, \ldots, T_n, X) \in K[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$, since coefficients in $L$ are fixed by $\Gal(L/K)$.

Define an action on $S_n$ on $K[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$ by setting $$ \begin{align*} (σ * h)(T_1 ,\ldots, T_n , X) = h(T_{σ(1)}, \ldots, T_{σ(n)}, X). \end{align*} $$ Note that $σ*F = F$ for all $σ\in S_n$.

Lemma 9.8

Let $F_1\in K[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$ be an irreducible factor of $F$.

Then $\Gal(f/K) \subseteq S_n$ is conjugate to $\op{Stab}(F_1) = \sb{τ\in S_n}{τ*F_1 = F_1}$.

Proof. Since $S_n$ acts transitively on $(X - (α_1 T_{σ(1)} + \ldots + α_n T_{σ(n)}))$, it acts transitively on the irreducible factors of $F$, so replacing $F_1$ by $τ * F_1$ for some suitable $τ\in S_n$, we may assume it has a factors $X - (α_1T_1 + \ldots + α_nT_n)$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} \prod_{σ\in G} (X - (α_{σ(1)}T_1 + \ldots + α_{σ(n)}T_n)) \end{align*} $$ has coefficients in $K$ (as they are invariant under $G$), and divides $F_1$ (c.f. Remark 6.3), and hence is equal to $F_1$, since $F_1$ is irreducible.

For $τ\in S_n$, we have $$ \begin{align*} τ * F_1 ={}& \prod_{σ\in G} (X - (α_{σ(1)}T_{τ(1)} + \ldots + α_{σ(n)} T_{τ(n)})) \\ ={}& \prod_{σ\in G} (X - (α_{στ\inv(1)} T_1 + \ldots + α_{στ\inv(n)} T_n)) &&\text{reordering terms} \\ ={}& \prod_{σ\in Gτ\inv} (X - (α_{σ(1)}T_1 + \ldots + α_{σ(n)}T_n)) \end{align*} $$

Therefore $τ * F_1 = F_1 \iff G = Gτ\inv \iff τ\in G$. $$ \begin{align*} \square \end{align*} $$

Proof of Theorem 9.6. By the symmetric polynomial theorem, the coefficients of $F$ are $\Z$-coefficients polynomials in the coefficients of $f$, so if $f\in \Z[X]$, then $F\in \Z[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$.

Let $\bar f\in \mathbb{F}_p[X]$ and $\bar F\in \mathbb{F}_p[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$ be polynomials obtained by reducing mod $p$. Then $\bar F$ can also be constructed from $\bar f$ in the same way as $F$ from $f$.

Write into distinct irreducible factors $F = F_1 \ldots F_s$ with $F_i\in \Z[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$ (also irreducible in $\Q[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$) and $\bar F = Φ_1 \ldots Φ_t$ with $\mathbb{F}_p[T_1, \ldots, T_n, X]$.

WLOG $Φ_1 \mid \bar F_1$ (so $Φ_1 \not\mid \bar F_i$ for $2\leq i\leq s$). Then $\op{Stab}(Φ_1) = \sb{τ\in S_n}{τ * ϕ_{1} = ϕ_1} \subseteq \sb{τ\in S_n}{τ * F_1 = F_1} = \op{Stab}(F_1)$. Lemma 9.8 then shows up to conjugacy, $\Gal(F/\mathbb{F}_p) \subseteq \Gal(f/\Q)$.
