Let $α = \sqrt[n]{a}$.

Group injection

Any $σ\in \Gal(K(α)/K)$ sends roots of $X^n - a$ to roots of $X^n - a$, so $σ(α) = ξ_n^b α$ for some $b\in \Z/n\Z$. Therefore $σ(α)/α\in μ_n$.

For $σ,τ\in \Gal(K(α)/K)$ with $σ(α) = ξ_n^b α$ and $τ(α) = ξ_n^c α$, we compute $$ \begin{align*} (στ)(α) = σ(ξ_n^c α) = ξ_n^c σ(α) = ξ_n^b ξ_n^c \sqrt[n]{a} \end{align*} $$ because $ξ_n\in K$. Hence $θ$ is a group homomorphism.

Since any $K$-automorphism of $K(α)$ is uniquely determined by its action on $α$, $θ$ is injective.

Degree

For $m\geq 1$, $$ \begin{align*} α^m \in K = K(α)^{\Gal(K(α)/K)} \iff{}& σ(α^m) = α^m && \forall σ\in \Gal(K(α)/K) \\ \iff{}& θ(σ)^m = 1 &&\forall σ\in \Gal(K(α)/K) \\ \iff{}& \im θ \leq μ_m \\ \iff{}& \#\Gal(K(α)/K) = \#\imθ \mid m \end{align*} $$ so $[K(α) : K ]= \# \Gal(K(α)/K)$ is the least $m$ such that $α^m\in K$.

Reducibility of $X^n - a$

$$ \begin{align*} X^n - a \text{ is irreducible} &\iff \# \Gal(K(α)/K) = n \\ &\iff α^m\not\in K &&\forall m\mid n,\; m<n \\ &\iff \text{$a$ is not a $d$-th power in $K$} &&\forall d \mid n,\; d>1 \end{align*} $$ where $d = n/m$.
