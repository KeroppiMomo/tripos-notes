Let $\SL_2(\mathbb{F}_q) = \sb{\pmat{a&b\\c&d}}{a,b,c,d\in \mathbb{F}_q, \; ad - bc = 1}$, the special linear group over $\mathbb{F}_q$. Suppose $q$ is odd.

  1. Let $ε$ be a non-square in $\mathbb{F}_q$, and let $\sqrt{ε}$ be a square root of $ε$ in $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$. Then $$ \begin{alignat}{3} \mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times &&&\to \GL_2(\mathbb{F}_q) &&\xrightarrow{\det} \mathbb{F}_q^\times \\ a + b\sqrt{ε} &&&\mapsto \pmat{a & bε \\ b & a} &&\mapsto a^2 - b^2ε \end{alignat} $$ is a surjective group homomorphism. Moreover the non-split torus $T_{ns} := \sb{\pmat{a & bε \\ b & a}}{a,b\in \mathbb{F}_q,\; a^2 - b^2ε = 1} \leq \SL_2(\mathbb{F}_q)$ is cyclic of order $q+1$. (Proof)
