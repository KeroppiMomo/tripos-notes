The partition of $G$ into double cosets (orbits of $H\times K \actson G$) $$ \begin{align*} G = \bigsqcup_{HwK\in H\backslash G/K} HwK \end{align*} $$ results in the decomposition $$ \begin{align*} \C[G] = \bigoplus_{HwK\in H\backslash G/K} \C[HwK] \end{align*} $$ into $(H\times K)$-invariant subspaces. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \Hom_H(\C[G], V) = \bigoplus_{KwH\in K\backslash G/H} \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V) \end{align*} $$ as $K$-spaces. It suffices to show there is a $K$-isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} ψ: \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V) \xrightarrow{\sim} \Hom_{K \cap w\inv Hw}(\C[K], \prescript{w\inv}{}V). \end{align*} $$

Explicitly write $ρ: H\to \GL(V)$ for the $H$-action on $V$. Define $$ \begin{align*} ψ: θ \mapsto (e_k \mapsto θ(e_{wk})) \end{align*} $$ and extend linearly.

  • $ψ$ is well-defined: we need to show $ψ(θ)$ is $(K\cap w\inv H w)$-linear for $θ\in \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V)$. Let $w\inv h w = K \cap w\inv H w$, so $h \in wKw\inv \cap H$. $$ \begin{align*} ψ(θ)(w\inv hw \,e_k) ={}& θ(e_{hwk}) \\ ={}& ρ(h)\, θ(e_{wk}) &&\text{$θ$ is $H$-linear} \\ ={}& \prescript{w\inv}{}ρ(w\inv h w)\, ψ(θ)(e_k). \end{align*} $$

  • $ψ$ is clearly $\C$-linear. To show $ψ$ is $K$-linear, let $k'\in K$ and $θ\in \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V)$. $$ \begin{align*} ψ(k' * θ)(e_k) ={}& (k'*θ)(e_{wk}) \\ ={}& θ(e_{wkk'}) \\ ={}& ψ(θ)(e_{kk'}) \\ ={}& (k' * ψ(θ))(e_k) \end{align*} $$ so $ψ(k'*θ) = k'*ψ(θ)$.

Construct its inverse: $$ \begin{align*} \bar ψ: \Hom_{K\cap w\inv Hw}(\C[K], \prescript{w\inv}{}V) &\to \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V) \\ ϕ &\mapsto (e_{hwk} \mapsto ρ(h)\,ϕ(e_k)). \end{align*} $$

  • $\bar ψ(ϕ)$ is well-defined: if $hwk = h'wk'$, then $$ \begin{align*} ρ(h') \,ϕ(e_{k'}) ={}& ρ(h') \, ϕ(e_{w\inv h'\inv h w k}) \\ ={}& ρ(h') \, \prescript{w\inv}{}ρ(w\inv h'\inv h w) ϕ(e_k) \\ ={}& ρ(h') ρ(h'\inv h) ϕ(e_k) \\ ={}& ρ(h)\, ϕ(e_k). \end{align*} $$

  • $\bar ψ$ is well-defined: we need to check $\bar ψ(ϕ)$ is $H$-linear. $$ \begin{align*} \bar ψ(ϕ)(h' \, e_{hwk}) = ρ(h'h) \, ϕ(e_k) = ρ(h') ρ(h) ϕ(e_k) = ρ(h')\, \bar ψ(ϕ)(e_{hwk}). \end{align*} $$

  • $ψ\circ \bar ψ = \id$: given $ϕ\in \Hom_{K\cap w\inv H w}(\C[K], \prescript{w\inv}{}V)$, $$ \begin{align*} ψ\circ \bar ψ(ϕ)(e_k) = \bar ψ(ϕ)(e_{wk}) = ϕ(e_k). \end{align*} $$

  • $\bar ψ \circ ψ = \id$: given $θ\in \Hom_H(\C[HwK], V)$, $$ \begin{align*} \bar ψ\circ ψ(θ)(e_{hwk}) = ρ(h) ψ(θ)(e_k) = ρ(h)\,θ(e_{wk}) = θ(e_{hwk}). \end{align*} $$
