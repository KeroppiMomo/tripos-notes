Using the formula for permutation characters, $$ \begin{align*} π_\text{reg}(g) = \#\sb{h\in G}{gh = h} = \begin{cases} \#G, &g = 1, \\ 0, &\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \langle π_\text{reg}, χ_i\rangle = \frac{1}{\#G} \sum_{g\in G} π_\text{reg}(g) \overline{χ_i(g)} = \frac{1}{\#G} π_\text{reg}(1) \overline{χ_i(1)} = \deg χ_i. \end{align*} $$

The result follows from orthonormality and completeness of irreducible characters.
