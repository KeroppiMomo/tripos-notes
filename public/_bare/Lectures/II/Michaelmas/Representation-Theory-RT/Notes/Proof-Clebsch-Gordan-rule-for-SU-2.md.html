WLOG letting $n\geq m$, using the characters of $V_n$ and characters of tensor products, $$ \begin{align*} χ_{V_n\otimes V_m}(z) ={}& χ_{V_n}(z) χ_{V_m}(z) \\ ={}& \frac{z^{n+1} - z^{-(n+1)}}{z - z\inv}(z^{m} + z^{m-2} + \ldots + z^{2-m} + z^{-m}) \\ ={}& \frac{(z^{n+m+1} + z^{-(n+m+1)}) + (z^{n+m-1} + z^{-(n+m-1)}) + \ldots + (z^{n-m+1} - z^{-(n-m + 1)})}{z - z^{-1}} \\ ={}& χ_{V_{n+m}}(z) + χ_{V_{n+m-2}}(z) + \ldots + χ_{V_{n-m}}(z). \end{align*} $$
