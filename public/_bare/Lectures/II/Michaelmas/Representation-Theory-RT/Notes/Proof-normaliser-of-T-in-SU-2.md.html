$$ \begin{align*} \pmat{a & b \\ -\bar b & \bar a}\pmat{z \\ &z\inv} \pmat{a & b \\ -\bar b & \bar a}\inv = \pmat{a\bar a z + b\bar b z\inv & -ab z + abz\inv \\ -\bar a\bar b z + \bar a\bar b z\inv & b\bar b z + a\bar a z\inv} \end{align*} $$ is in $T$ iff $ab(z - z\inv) = 0$. This needs to hold for all $|z| = 1$, so $a=0$ or $b=0$.
