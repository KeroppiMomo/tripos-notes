Let $e_1, \ldots, e_n$ be a basis for $V$, and let $e_1^\ast, \ldots, e_n^\ast$ be the dual basis for $V^\ast$, i.e. such that $e_i^\ast(e_j) = δ_{ij}$.

For $g\in G$, let $(a_{ij})$ be the matrix of $ρ(g\inv)$. $$ \begin{align*} ge_i^\ast = \sum_{j=1}^{n} (ge_i^\ast)(e_j) \,e_j^\ast = \sum_{j=1}^{n} e_i^\ast(g\inv e_j)\, e_j^\ast = \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij} e_j^\ast \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \tr ρ^\ast(g) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{ii} = \tr ρ(g\inv). \end{align*} $$

(Moreover $[ρ^\ast(g)] = [ρ(g\inv)]^T$.)
