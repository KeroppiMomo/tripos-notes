We'll use the definition $\op{Ind}^G_H V = \sb{f\in G\to V}{f(hg) = h\, f(g)}$.

Recall that right cosets partition $G$. Choose representatives for the right cosets $H\backslash G = \set{Hg_1, \ldots, Hg_n}$. We have $$ \begin{align*} ψ: \op{Ind}^G_H V &\to V^n \\ θ &\mapsto (θ(g_1), \ldots, θ(g_n)). \end{align*} $$ an isomorphism of vector spaces:

  • $ψ$ is $k$-linear.

  • $ψ$ is injective: if $θ(g_1) = \ldots = θ(g_n) = 0$, then $θ(hg_i) = h\, θ(g_i) = 0$ for all $h\in H$ and $i=1,\ldots,n$, so $θ = 0$.

  • $θ$ is surjective: given $(w_1, w_2, \ldots, w_n)\in V^n$, take $θ\in \op{Ind}^G_H(V)$ where $θ(hg_i) = h w_i$ for all $h\in H$ and $i=1,\ldots,n$.

Thus $\op{Ind}^G_H V$ is $(\dim V)^n$-dimensional. Write $v^{(i)} \equiv (0, \ldots, 0, \underbrace{v}_{i\text{-th entry}}, 0, \ldots, 0)\in V^n$.

Consider the corresponding $G$-action on $V^n$: $g * x = ψ(g * ψ\inv(x))$. Fix $g\in G$ and $θ\in \op{Ind}_H^G V$. For each $g_i$, let $g_i g = h_i g_{j_i}$ for some $h_i\in H$ and $j_i$. Then $$ \begin{align*} (g*θ)(g_i) = θ(g_i g) = θ(h_i g_{j_i}) = ρ(h_i) θ(g_{j_i}) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} ψ(g * θ) = (ρ(h_1)\, θ(g_{j_1}), \ldots, ρ(h_n)\, θ(g_{j_n})). \end{align*} $$ Hence taking $θ = ψ\inv(v^{(j)})$, $$ \begin{align*} g * v^{(j)} = ψ(g*ψ\inv(v^{(j)})) = (ρ(h_i) v)^{(i)}. \end{align*} $$ where $i$ is such that $j = j_i$.

Picking a basis $B$ for $V$, we obtain a basis $B' = B^{(1)} \sqcup B^{(2)} \sqcup \ldots B^{(n)}$ for $V^n$. In this basis, the action of $g$ on $V^n$ has a matrix which splits into $(\dim V) \times (\dim V)$ blocks, the $(i,j)$-th of which is $$ \begin{align*} ([g]_{B'})_{i,j} = \begin{cases} [ρ(h_i)]_B, &\text{if $j = j_i$}, \\ 0, &\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

The block $[ρ(h_i)]_B$ contributes to the trace iff $i = j_i$, which occurs iff $g_i g = h_i g_i$ for some $h_i\in H$, i.e. $h_i = g_i g g_i\inv\in H$. The trace is therefore $$ \begin{align*} \tr(g, V^{n}) = \sum_{i:\; j_i = i} \tr(ρ(h_i)) = \sum_{\substack{Hg_i \in H\backslash G:\\ g_i gg_i\inv \in H}} χ_ρ(g_ig g_i\inv). \end{align*} $$

$\displaystyle\frac{1}{\# H} \sum_{\substack{x\in G:\\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv) = \sum_{\substack{Hx\in H\backslash G: \\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv)$

The right cosets $H\backslash G$ partition $G$. For each coset $Hx\in H\backslash G$, if we choose a different coset representative $Hx = Hy$, then $y = hx$ for some $h\in H$, so $ygy\inv = hxgx\inv h\inv\in H \iff xgx\inv\in H$, and $χ_ρ(ygy\inv) = χ_ρ(xgx\inv)$ as $χ_ρ$ is a class function.

$\displaystyle\frac{1}{\# H} \sum_{\substack{x\in G:\\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv) = \# Z_G(g) \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\# Z_H(h_i)} χ_ρ(h_i)$

Counting: $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{\substack{x\in G: \\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv) ={}& \sum_{_{h\in \prescript{G}{}g \cap H}} \underbrace{\#\sb{x\in G}{xgx\inv = h}}_{\# Z_G(h)} \,χ_ρ(h) \\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{n} \#\prescript{H}{}h_i \, \#Z_G(h_i) \, χ_ρ(h_i) &&χ_ρ\in \mathcal{C}_H\\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{\#H}{\#Z_H(h_i)} \#Z_G(h_i) \,χ_ρ(h). \end{align*} $$
