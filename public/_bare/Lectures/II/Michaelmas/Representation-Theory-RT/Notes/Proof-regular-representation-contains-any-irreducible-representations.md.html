Let $ρ: G \to \GL(V)$ be irreducible.

Fix any $v\in V \setminus \set{0}$. Define the linear map by $$ \begin{align*} θ: k[G] &\to V \\ e_g &\mapsto gv. \end{align*} $$ and extending linearly.

Note $θ$ is a $G$-linear map, so $\ker θ\leq k[G]$ and $\im θ\leq V$ are $G$-invariant subspaces. But $V$ is irreducible and $θ$ is not zero (as $θ(e_1) = v\neq 0$), so $\im θ = V$. Moreover, we can obtain a $G$-invariant complement $W$ of $\ker θ$ in $k[G]$ by Maschke, i.e. $k[G] = \ker θ \oplus W$. Then the first isomorphism theorem gives a $G$-isomorphism $V \isom k[G] / \ker θ \isom W$.
