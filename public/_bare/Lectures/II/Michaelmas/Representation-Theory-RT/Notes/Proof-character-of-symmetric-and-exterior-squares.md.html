Fix $g\in G$. Let $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_d}$ be a basis for $V$ consisting of eigenvectors of $g$, say with eigenvalues $λ_1, \ldots, λ_n$.

$g$ acts on $V\otimes V$ by $$ \begin{align*} g(v_{i} \otimes v_{j}) = (gv_i) \otimes (gv_j) = (λ_iv_i) \otimes (λ_j v_j) = λ_iλ_j(v_i \otimes v_j). \end{align*} $$

$S^2 V$ has a basis $\sb{v_i \otimes v_j + v_j \otimes v_i}{i\leq j}$ and $Λ^2 V$ has a basis $\sb{v_i \otimes v_j - v_j \otimes v_i}{i<j}$, which are all eigenvectors of $g$ with eigenvalue $λ_iλ_j$.

Thus $$ \begin{align*} \tr(g, S^2V) ={}& \sum_{1\leq i\leq j\leq d} λ_{i}λ_j = \frac{1}{2}\left(\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i\Bigr)^2 + \sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i^2\right),\\ \tr(g, Λ^2V) ={}& \sum_{1\leq i< j\leq d} λ_{i}λ_j = \frac{1}{2}\left(\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i\Bigr)^2 - \sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i^2\right) \end{align*} $$ and the result follows from $χ(g) = \sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i$ and $χ(g^2) = \sum_{i=1}^{d} λ_i^2$.
