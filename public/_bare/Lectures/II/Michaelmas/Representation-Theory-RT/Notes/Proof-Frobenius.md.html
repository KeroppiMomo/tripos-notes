Let $n = |G:H|$. Each conjugate $gHg\inv$ of $H$ fixes a unique coset $gH$, and $gHg\inv$ and $g'Hg'\inv$ fixing the same coset implies they equal. Thus there are exactly $n$ conjugates of $H$, every pair of which intersects trivially. So there are exactly $n(\# H -1)$ elements of $G$ that fixes exactly one coset $G/H$, leaving $K$ with $\# G - n(\# H - 1) = n$ elements.

Let $\prescript{H}{}h_1 = \set{1}, \prescript{H}{}h_2, \ldots, \prescript{H}{}h_s$ be all the conjugacy classes of $H$. $H \cap \prescript{G}{}h = \prescript{H}{}h$ for all $h\in H$, so $\prescript{G}{}h_1, \ldots, \prescript{G}{}h_s$ are distinct conjugacy classes of $G$. Therefore all the conjugacy classes of $G$ are $$ \begin{align*} \prescript{G}{}h_1 = \set{1}, \prescript{G}{}h_2, \ldots, \prescript{G}{}h_s, \prescript{G}{}k_1, \ldots, \prescript{G}{} k_t \end{align*} $$ where $K = \set{1} \sqcup \bigsqcup_{j=1}^{t} \prescript{G}{}k_j$.

Let $χ_1, \ldots, χ_m$ be all the irreducible characters of $H$. Recalling $Z_G(h) = Z_H(h)$ for all $h\in H$, and using the formula $\op{Ind}^G_H χ(g) = \sum_{l} \frac{\# Z_G(g_l)}{\# Z_H(h_l)} χ(h_l)$ when $H \cap \prescript{G}{}g = \prescript{H}{}h_1 \sqcup \prescript{H}{}h_2 \sqcup \ldots$, consider the class functions $$ \begin{align*} θ_i = \op{Ind}^G_H χ_i - χ_i(1) \op{Ind}^G_H \mathbb{1}_H + χ_i(1) \,\mathbb{1}_G \end{align*} $$ and $$ \begin{align*} θ = \sum_{i=1}^{m} θ_i(1)\, θ_i. \end{align*} $$

Now $$ \begin{align*} \langle θ_i, θ_i\rangle ={}& \frac{1}{\#G} \sum_{g\in G} |θ_i(g)|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\# G}\left(\sum_{k\in K} |χ_i(1)|^2 + \sum_{g\in G\setminus K} |θ_i(g)|^2\right) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\# G}\left(n |χ_i(1)|^2 + n \sum_{h\in H\setminus \set{1}} |χ_i(h)|^2\right) \\ ={}& \frac{n}{\# G} \sum_{h\in H} |χ_i(h)|^2 \\ ={}& \langle χ_i, χ_i\rangle = 1. \end{align*} $$ Moreover $θ_i(1) > 0$, so $θ_i$ is an actual character of $G$.

Because $θ$ is then an actual character of $G$, $$ \begin{align*} K = \sb{g\in G}{θ(g) = θ(1)} \lhd G. \end{align*} $$
