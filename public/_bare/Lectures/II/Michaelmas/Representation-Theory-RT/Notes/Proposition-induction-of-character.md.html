Let $G$ be a finite group, $H\leq G$ be a subgroup, and $ρ: H \to \GL(V)$ be a representation.

The character of its induction $\op{Ind}^G_H ρ$ is $$ \begin{align*} \op{Ind}^G_H χ_ρ(g) ={}& \frac{1}{\# H} \sum_{\substack{x\in G:\\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv) \\ ={}& \sum_{\substack{Hx\in H\backslash G: \\ xgx\inv\in H}} χ_ρ(xgx\inv) \\ \end{align*} $$ If $\prescript{G}{}g \cap H = \prescript{H}{}h_1 \sqcup \ldots \sqcup \prescript{H}{} h_n$, then $$ \begin{align*} \op{Ind}^G_H χ_ρ(g) = \# Z_G(g) \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{\# Z_H(h_i)} χ_ρ(h_i). \end{align*} $$

Take this as a definition of the induction of a class function.
