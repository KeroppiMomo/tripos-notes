Let $f: V \to W$, $f': V'\to W'$ be linear maps between finite-dimensional vector spaces.

The tensor product of $f$ and $f'$ is the linear map $f \otimes f': V$${}\otimes{}$$V' \to W \otimes W'$ defined by $$ \begin{align*} (f\otimes f')(v \otimes w) = f(v) \otimes f'(w) \end{align*} $$ and extending linearly.

If $A$ is the matrix of $f$ in the basis $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_m}$ for $V$ and $B$ is the matrix of $f'$ in the basis $\set{w_1, \ldots, w_n}$ for $W$, then in the basis $\set{v_1 \otimes w_1, \ldots, v_1 \otimes w_n, v_2 \otimes w_1, \ldots, v_2 \otimes w_n, \ldots, v_m \otimes w_1, \ldots, v_m \otimes w_n}$ of $V \otimes W$, $f \otimes f'$ has a matrix $\pmat{a_{11} B & a_{12} B & \ldots \\$ a{21} B & a{22} B & \ldots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots}$.$> Therefore $\tr(f \otimes f') = \tr f \cdot \tr f'$.

For two representations $ρ: G\to \GL(V)$ and $ρ': G\to \GL(V')$, their tensor product $ρ \otimes ρ': g \mapsto ρ(g) \otimes ρ'(g)$ affords a character $χ_{ρ \otimes ρ'} = χ_{ρ} χ_{ρ'}$.
