Tensor product of irreducible representations is irreduicble

First we'll check if $ρ: G\to \GL(V)$ and $ρ': H \to \GL(W)$ are irreducible, then so is $$ \begin{align*} ρ \otimes ρ': G \times H &\to \GL(V \otimes W) \\ (g,h) &\mapsto ρ(g) \otimes ρ'(h) \end{align*} $$ where $ρ(g) \otimes ρ'(h)$ is a tensor product of linear maps. Clear that this is a representation. It has a character $χ_{ρ \otimes ρ'}(g, h) = \tr (ρ(g) \otimes ρ'(h)) = \tr ρ(g) \cdot \tr ρ'(h) = χ_ρ(g) χ_{ρ'}(h)$. Thus $$ \begin{align*} \langle χ_{ρ\otimes ρ'}, χ_{ρ \otimes ρ'}\rangle ={}& \langle χ_{ρ} χ_{ρ'}, χ_{ρ} χ_{ρ'}\rangle \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\#(G \times H)}\sum_{(g,h)\in G \times H} χ_{ρ}(g) χ_{ρ'}(h) \overline{χ_{ρ}(g) χ_{ρ'}(h)} \\ ={}& \left(\frac{1}{\# G} \sum_{g\in G} χ_ρ(g) \overline{χ_ρ(g)}\right) \left(\frac{1}{\# H} \sum_{h\in H} χ_{ρ'}(h) \overline{χ_{ρ'}(h)}\right) \\ ={}& \langle χ_{ρ}, χ_ρ\rangle \langle χ_{ρ'}, χ_{ρ'}\rangle \\ ={}& 1 \cdot 1 = 1. \end{align*} $$ Hence $ρ \otimes ρ'$ is irreducible.

List is exhaustive

Suppose $ρ_1, \ldots, ρ_k$ and $ρ_1', \ldots, ρ_l'$ are all the irreducible representations of $G$ and $H$ respectively.

Apply column normality: $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sum_{j=1}^{l} (\deg (ρ_i \otimes ρ_j'))^2 ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sum_{j=1}^{l} (\deg ρ_i)^2(\deg ρ_j)^2 \\ ={}& \Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^k (\deg ρ_i)^2\Bigr) \Bigl(\sum_{j=1}^{l} (\deg ρ_j')^2\Bigr) \\ ={}& \#G \cdot \#H \\ ={}& \#(G \times H) \end{align*} $$ so $ρ_i \otimes ρ_j'$ are all the irreducible representations of $G \times H$.
