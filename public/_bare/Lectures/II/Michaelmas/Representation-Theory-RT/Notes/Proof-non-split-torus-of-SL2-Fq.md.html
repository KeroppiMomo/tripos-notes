Recall $K^\times$ is cyclic for any finite field $K$. Since $\set{1, \sqrt{ε}}$ is a $\mathbb{F}_q$-basis of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$, $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: \qquad \mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times &\to \GL_2(\mathbb{F}_q)\\ a + b\sqrt{ε} &\mapsto \pmat{a & bε \\ b & a} \end{align*} $$ is a group homomorphism sending an element of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times$ to its multiplicative action on $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$ as a $\mathbb{F}_{q}$-linear map. Then ${\det} \circ ϕ$ is the norm $N_{\mathbb{F}_{q^2}/\mathbb{F}_q}$. It is a group homomorphism $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times \to \mathbb{F}_q^\times$. As $\Gal(\mathbb{F}_{q^2}/\mathbb{F}_q) = \set{\id, x\mapsto x^q}$, letting $α$ be a generator of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times$ (which has order $q^2 - 1$), $$ \begin{align*} N_{\mathbb{F}_q^2/\mathbb{F}_q}(x) = x \cdot x^q = x^{(q^2-1)/(q-1)} \end{align*} $$ has order $q-1$, so generates $\mathbb{F}_q^\times$. Thus $N_{\mathbb{F}_{q^2}/\mathbb{F}_q}$ is surjective. (Galois Theory sheet 3 Q5)

Then $\im ϕ = \sb{\pmat{a&bε \\ b&a}}{a,b\in \mathbb{F}_q, (a,b)\neq (0,0)}$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}^\times$, thus cyclic of order $q^2-1$. Therefore $T_{ns} = \det \ker|_{\im ϕ}$ is cyclic, and by the first isomorphism theorem has order $(q^2 - 1)/\# \mathbb{F}_q^\times = q+1$.
