If $g = \pmat{a & b \\ c &d}\in \op{SU}(2)$, the condition $g\bar g^T = I = \bar g^T g$ implies $$ \begin{align*} a\bar a + b\bar b = 1 &= c\bar c + d\bar d, \\ a\bar a + c\bar c = 1 &= b\bar b + d\bar d \end{align*} $$ Writing $a = e^{iα}\cosθ$, the these give $b = e^{iβ} \sin θ$, $c = e^{iγ} \sin θ$ and $d = e^{iδ}\cos θ$. $ad - bc = 1$ then requires $δ+α=0$ and $β+γ=π$.
