From row orthonormality and the fact the character table is finite, we get $$ \begin{align*} X \pmat{1/\#Z_G(g_1) \\ & \ddots \\ &&1/\# Z_G(g_l)} \bar X^T = I_k. \end{align*} $$ where $X$ is the character table.

We also showed $X$ is square, so this shows $X$ is invertible. Thus $$ \begin{align*} \bar X^T X \pmat{1/\#Z_G(g_1) \\ & \ddots \\ &&1/\# Z_G(g_l)} = I_k \end{align*} $$ and the result follows.
