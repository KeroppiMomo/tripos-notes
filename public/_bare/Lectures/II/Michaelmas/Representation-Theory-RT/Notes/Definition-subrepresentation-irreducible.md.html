Let $ρ: G\to \GL(V)$ be a representation.

We say a vector subspace $W$${}\leq{}$$V$ is a subrepresentation of $V$ or a $G$-invariant subspace if $ρ(g)w \in W$ for all $g\in G, w\in W$.

$\set{0}$ and $W$ are always $G$-invariant subspaces. Call the other ones proper.

$ρ$ is irreducible or simple if it has no proper $G$-invariant subspace.

This induces a representation $G \to \GL(V/W)$.

In matrix representation, $W$ is a $G$-invariant subspace iff in a basis $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_m, v_{m+1}, \ldots, v_n}$ of $V$ that contains a basis $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_m}$ of $W$, the matrix of $ρ(g)$ for any $g\in G$ has the form $\pmat{A & * \\ 0 & B}$, where $A$ is the matrix for $W$ and $B$ is the matrix for $V/W$.
