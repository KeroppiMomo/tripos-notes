Let $G$ be a finite group acting on finite sets $X,Y$, and let $ρ_X, ρ_Y$ be the corresponding complex permutation representation.

  • $π_X(g) :={}$$χ_{ρ_X}$$(g) = \#X^g$ where $X^g = \sb{x\in X}{gx = x}$ is the set of fixed points of $g$. (Proof)

  • $\langle π_X, \mathbb{1}\rangle = \#G\backslash X$ where $G\backslash X = \sb{Gx}{x\in X}$ is the set of orbits. (Proof)

  • $\langle π_X, π_Y\rangle = \#G\backslash (X \times Y)$ where $G$ acts on $X\times Y$ by $g(x,y) = (gx,gy)$. (Proof)

  • $π_X = \mathbb{1} + χ$ where $χ$ is irreducible iff the action $G\actson X$ is 2-transitive, i.e. $G\actson X\times X$ has exactly two orbits $Δ = \sb{(x,x)}{x\in X}$ and $(X\times X) \setminus Δ$. (Proof)
