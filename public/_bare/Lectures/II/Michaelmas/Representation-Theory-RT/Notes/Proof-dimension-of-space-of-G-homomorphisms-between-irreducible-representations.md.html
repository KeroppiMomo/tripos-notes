If $V$ and $W$ are not isomorphic, then Schur's lemma says any $G$-linear map $V\to W$ must be zero.

If $V$ and $W$ are isomorphic, then for any non-zero $G$-linear maps $ϕ, ψ: V\to W$, Schur's lemma implies $ϕ,ψ$ are isomorphisms, so invertible. $ψ\inv ϕ: V\to V$ is a $G$-linear endomorphism, so $ψ\inv ϕ = λ\,\id$ and thus $ϕ = λ ψ$.
