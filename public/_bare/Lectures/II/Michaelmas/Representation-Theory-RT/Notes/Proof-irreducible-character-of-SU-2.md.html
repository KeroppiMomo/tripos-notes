Consider the basis $\sb{x^{n-k} y^k}{k=0,1,\ldots,n}$ for $V_n$. $\pmat{z \\ &z}\in \op{SU}(2)$ acts by $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{z \\ &z\inv} * x^{n-k} y^k = (zx)^{n-k}(z\inv y)^k = z^{n-2k} x^{n-k} y^k \end{align*} $$ so the matrix is $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{z^{n} \\ & z^{n-2} \\ && \ddots \\ &&&z^{2-n} \\ &&&&z^{-n}}. \end{align*} $$ Thus $$ \begin{align*} χ_{V_n}\Bigl(\pmat{z \\ &-z}\Bigr) = z^n + z^{n-2} + \ldots + z^{2-n} + z^{-n}. \end{align*} $$
