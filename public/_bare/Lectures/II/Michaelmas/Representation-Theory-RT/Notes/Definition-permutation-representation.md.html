Suppose a finite group $G$ acts on a finite set $X$. Let $k[X] = \sb{\sum_{x\in X} λ_x e_x}{λ_x\in k}$ be the $\# X$-dimensional $k$-vector space of formal linear combinations of elements in $X$.

The corresponding permutation representation is $ρ_X: G\to \GL(k[X])$ defined by $$ \begin{align*} ρ_X(g)(e_x) = e_{gx} \end{align*} $$ and extending linearly (so $ρ_X(g)(\sum_{x\in X} λ_x e_x) = \sum_{x\in X} λ_x e_{gx}$).

The regular representation $ρ_\text{reg}$ is the permutation representation corresponding to the regular action $G\actson G$ (by left multiplication).
