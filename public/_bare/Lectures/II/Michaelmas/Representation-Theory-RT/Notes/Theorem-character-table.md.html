Let $G$ be a finite group. Listing all irreducible characters as $χ_1, \ldots, χ_k$ and all conjugacy classes as $\prescript{G}{}g_1, \ldots, \prescript{G}{}g_l$, the character table of $G$ is a $k\times l$ matrix $X = (χ_i(g_j))_{ij}$.

  1. Row orthonormality: if $ρ, ρ'$ are irreducible complex representations, then $$ \begin{align*} \langle χ_ρ, χ_{ρ'}\rangle = \begin{cases} 1, &\text{if $ρ$ and $ρ'$ are $G$-isomorphic}, \\ 0, &\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ or equivalently $$ \begin{align*} X \pmat{1/\#Z_G(g_1) \\ & \ddots \\ &&1/\# Z_G(g_l)} \bar X^T = I_k. \end{align*} $$

    (Proof)

  2. Completeness: $χ_1, \ldots, χ_k$ form an orthonormal basis for $\mathcal{C}_G$, the complex vector space of class functions on $G$.

    Therefore $k=l$, i.e. the number of irreducible representations (up to isomorphism) equals the number of conjugacy classes.

    (Proof)

  3. Column orthogonality: $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^k χ_i(g) χ_i(h\inv) = \begin{cases} 0, &\text{if $g$ and $h$ are conjugate}, \\ \# Z_G(g), &\text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ or equivalently $$ \begin{align*} \bar X^T X = \pmat{\# Z_G(g_1) \\ &\ddots \\ && \#Z_G(g_k)}. \end{align*} $$ (Proof)

    Note in particular $\# G = \sum_{i=1}^k (\deg χ_i)^2$ taking $g=h=1$. (Alternative proof)

  4. $\# \prescript{G}{}g_i$ and $\deg χ_j$ divide $\#(G/Z)$ for all $i,j$. ($\#\prescript{G}{}g_i$ is group theory; proof for $\deg χ_j$)
