If $α$ is an eigenvalue of an integer square matrix $A$, then it is a root the characteristic polynomial $χ_A(X) = \det(XI - A)$, which is a monic $\Z[X]$ polynomial.

Conversely, given a monic polynomial $X^n + a_{n-1} X^{n-1} + \ldots + a_1 X + a_0\in \Z[X]$, the companion matrix has it as its characteristic polynomial: $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{0 &&&& -a_0\\ 1 &&&& -a_1\\ & 1 &&& -a_2\\ && \ddots &&\vdots \\ &&&1 & -a_{n-1}}. \end{align*} $$
