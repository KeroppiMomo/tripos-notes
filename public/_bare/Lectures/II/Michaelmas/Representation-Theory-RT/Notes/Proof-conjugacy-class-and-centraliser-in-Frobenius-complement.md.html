Recall how conjugates of $H$ intersect.

Suppose $h' = ghg\inv\in H$ for $g\in G$. Since $H \cap gHg\inv \supseteq \set{h'}$ with $h' \neq 1$, we must have $g\in H$. Thus $H \cap \prescript{G}{}h = \prescript{H}{}h$.

Suppose $g\in Z_G(h)$, i.e. $ghg\inv = h$. Since $H \cap gHg\inv \supseteq \set{h}$ with $h\neq 1$, we must have $g\in H$, so $Z_H(h) = Z_G(h) \cap H = Z_G(h)$.
