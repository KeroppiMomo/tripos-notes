A representation $ρ: \op{SU}(2)\to \GL(V)$ restricts to a representation $S^1 \isom T \hookrightarrow \op{SU}(2) \to \GL(V)$, and by our previous results about representations of $S^1$, $$ \begin{align*} χ_ρ(z) := χ_ρ\left(\pmat{z \\ &z\inv}\right) = \sum_{n=-N}^{N} a_n z^n, \qquad a_n \in \Z_{\geq 0} \end{align*} $$ is a Laurent polynomial of non-negative integer coefficient. But $\pmat{z \\ &z\inv}$ and $\pmat{z\inv \\ &z}$ are conjugate (via $\pmat{&1 \\ -1}$), so $χ_ρ(z) = χ_ρ(z\inv)$, and hence $a_n = a_{-n}$.
