We count the set $$ \begin{align*} F = \sb{(g,x)\in G\times X}{gx = x} \end{align*} $$ in two ways:

  1. Iterating over $g\in G$, $$ \begin{align*} F = \sum_{g\in G} \#\sb{x\in X}{gx = x} = \sum_{g\in G} \#X^g. \end{align*} $$

  2. Iterating over $x\in X$, $$ \begin{align*} F ={}& \sum_{x\in X} \#\sb{g\in G}{gx = x} \\ ={}& \sum_{x\in X} \#\op{Stab}_G(x) \\ ={}& \sum_{Gx\in G\backslash X} \sum_{y\in Gx} \frac{\# G}{\# Gy}\\ ={}& \sum_{Gx\in G\backslash X} \# G \\ ={}& \#G \cdot \# G\backslash X. \end{align*} $$ where we have used orbit-stabiliser.
