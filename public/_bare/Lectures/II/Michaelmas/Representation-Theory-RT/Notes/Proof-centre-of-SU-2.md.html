If $g = \pmat{a&b \\ -\bar b & \bar a}\in Z(\op{SU}(2))$, then $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{a &b\\ - \bar b &\bar a} \pmat{& 1 \\ -1&} ={}& \pmat{&1 \\-1&}\pmat{a &b \\ -\bar b & \bar a} &\implies &a,b\in \R \\ \pmat{a & b \\ -\bar b & \bar a}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\pmat{1 &i \\ i & 1} ={}& \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\pmat{1 & i \\ i & 1} \pmat{a & b \\ -\bar b & \bar a} &\implies &b = 0 \end{align*} $$ so $g = \pm I$.
