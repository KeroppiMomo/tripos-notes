$\deg ρ\mid \# G$

Let $χ$ be the character of $ρ$. We know that $$ \begin{align*} 1 = \langle χ, χ\rangle = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{\#\mathcal{C}_i}{\#G} χ(g_i) \overline{χ(g_i)} \end{align*} $$ where $\mathcal{C}_1, \ldots, \mathcal{C}_k$ are all the conjugacy classes of $G$ with representatives $g_1, \ldots, g_k$, so $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\# G}{\deg ρ} ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{k} \underbrace{\frac{\# \mathcal{C}_i \cdot χ(g_i)}{\deg ρ}}_{ω_V(g_i)} \,\overline{χ(g_i)}. \end{align*} $$

We showed $ω_V(g_i)$ and $χ(g_i)$ are algebraic integers, and thus so is $\#G/\deg ρ$. However it is rational, so it is an integer, showing $\deg ρ \mid \# G$.

$\deg ρ \mid \#G/\#Z(G)$

For $m\in \N$, consider $ρ^{\otimes m}$${}= \underbrace{ρ \otimes \ldots \otimes ρ}_{m\text{ times}}: G^m\to \GL(V^{\otimes m})$. Since $ρ$ is an irreducible representation of $G$, $ρ^{\otimes m}$ is an irreducible representation of $G^m$.

Moreover define $$ \begin{align*} Z' := \sb{(z_1, \ldots, z_m)\in Z(G)^m}{\prod_{i=1}^{m} z_i = 1}. \end{align*} $$ Recall by Schur's lemma that any $z_i\in Z(G)$ acts on $V$ by scalar multiplication, i.e. $ρ(z_i) = λ_i\,\id$ for some $λ_i\in \C$. Therefore if $\prod_{i=1}^{m} z_i = 1$ in $G$, then $(z_1, \ldots, z_m)$ acts on $V^{\otimes m}$ by scalar multiplication by $\prod_{i=1}^{m} λ_i = 1$ i.e. trivially. Hence $Z' \leq \ker ρ^{\otimes m}$.

Thus $V^{\otimes m}$ is an irreducible representation of $G^m / Z'$, which implies by our previous result $$ \begin{align*} (\deg ρ)^m = \dim V^{\otimes m} \mid \#(G^m / Z') = \frac{(\#G)^m}{(\#Z(G))^{m-1}}. \end{align*} $$

This holds for all $m\in \N$, so $\deg ρ \mid \#G/\#Z(G)$.
