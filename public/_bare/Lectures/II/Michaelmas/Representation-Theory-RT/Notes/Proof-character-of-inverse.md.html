Let $g\in G$. There is an eigenvector basis $B = \set{v_1, \ldots, v_n}$ for $V$ such that $$ \begin{align*} [ρ(g)]_B = \pmat{λ_1 \\ &λ_2 \\&&\ddots \\ &&&λ_n} \end{align*} $$ with $|λ_i| = 1$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} [ρ(g\inv)]_B = [ρ(g)]_B\inv = \pmat{λ_1\inv \\ &λ_2\inv \\&&\ddots \\ &&&λ_n\inv}. \end{align*} $$ So $$ \begin{align*} χ_ρ(g\inv) = λ_1\inv + \ldots + λ_n\inv = \overline{λ_1} + \ldots + \overline{λ_n} = \overline{χ_ρ(g)}. \end{align*} $$
