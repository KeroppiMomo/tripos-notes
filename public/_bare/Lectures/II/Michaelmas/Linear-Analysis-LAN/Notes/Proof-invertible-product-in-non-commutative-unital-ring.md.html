If $a$ and $b$ are invertible, then $b\inv a\inv$ is the inverse of $ab$: $$ \begin{align*} (ab)(b\inv a\inv) = 1 = (b\inv a\inv) (ab). \end{align*} $$

If $ab$ is invertible and $ab = ba$, let $c$ be the inverse of $ab$. Then $$ \begin{gather*} a(bc) = 1, \\ (bc)a = bca\underbrace{(ba)c}_{1} = b\underbrace{c(ab)}_{1}ac = (ba)c = 1 \end{gather*}$$ so $bc$ is the inverse of $a$. Similarly $ac$ is the inverse of $b$.
