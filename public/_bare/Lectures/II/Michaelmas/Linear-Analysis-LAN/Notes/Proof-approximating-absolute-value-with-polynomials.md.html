Fix $ε>0$.

First we approximate $|t|$ with a holomorphic function: consider $$ \begin{align*} f(t) = \sqrt{t^2 + ε^2}. \end{align*} $$ Then by Cauchy-Schwarz $$ \begin{align*} \bigl|f(t) - |t|\bigr| \leq ε. \end{align*} $$

Next, to approximate $f$, we write $z \mapsto z^{1/2}$ for the holomorphic branch of the square root on $\C \setminus (-\infty,0]$ such that $x^{1/2} \in (0,\infty)$ for $x\in (0,\infty)$. It is holomorphic on $B_1(1) \equiv \sb{z\in \C}{|z - 1| < 1}$, so has a Taylor expansion $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n(z-1)^n$. The coefficients $a_n$ are real since $\dd[n]{z}z^{1/2}$ are real at $z=1$.

For $t\in [-1, 1]$, we have $t^2 + ε^2 \in [ε^2, 1+ε^2]$, which is a compact subset in $B_1(1)$. Since $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n(s-1)^n$ converges uniformly to $s^{1/2}$ on $s\in [ε^2, 1+ε^2]$, choose $N\in \N$ such that $|\sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n(s-1)^n - s^{1/2}| < ε$ for all $s\in [ε^2, 1+ε^2]$. Thus for all $t\in [-1,1]$, $$ \begin{align*} |q(t) - f(t)| < ε, \qquad q(t) = \sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n(t^2 + ε^2 - 1)^n. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} |q(t) - |t|| \leq |q(t) - f(t)| + |f(t) - |t|| < 2ε, \qquad t\in [-1,1]. \end{align*} $$ In particular $|q(0)| < 2ε$. Therefore taking $$ \begin{align*} p(t) = q(t) - q(0), \end{align*} $$ we get $|p(t) - |t|| < 4ε$, as required.
