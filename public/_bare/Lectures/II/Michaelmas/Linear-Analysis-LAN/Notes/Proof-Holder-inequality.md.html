Proposition (Young's inequality) open_in_new

Let $1\leq p<q\leq \infty$ be such that $1/p + 1/q = 1$. Then for all $a,b\geq 0$, $$ \begin{align*} ab \leq \frac{a^p}{p} + \frac{b^q}{q}. \end{align*} $$

Proof

If either $x$ or $y$ is zero, then we're done, so assume not.

WLOG by homogeneity, assume $\|x\|_p = 1 = \|y\|_q$. By Young's inequality, $$ \begin{align*} |x_i y_i| \leq \frac{|x_i|^p}{p} + \frac{|y_i|^q}{q}, \end{align*} $$ so summing, $$ \begin{align*} \|x\cdot y\|_1 \leq \frac{1}{p} \|x\|_p + \frac{1}{q} \|y\|_q = \frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} = 1 \end{align*} $$ as required.
