Note $σ(T)$ is closed, so $\partial σ(T) = σ(T) \setminus \op{int}(σ(T)) = σ(T) \cap \overline{ρ(T)}$.

Suppose $λ\in \partial σ(T)$. Then there exists $λ_n\in ρ(T)$ such that $λ_n \to λ\in σ(T)$ as $n\to \infty$. Then $λ_n \id - T \to λ \id - T$ where $λ_n\id - T \in \mathcal{G}(X)$ and $λ\id - T\not\in \mathcal{G}(X)$, so $\|(λ_n\id - T)\inv\| \to \infty$. By picking $y_n\in X$ such that $\|y_n\|= 1$ and $\|(λ_n \id - T)\inv y_n\| \geq \frac{1}{2}\|(λ_n \id - T)\inv\|$, we get $\|(λ_n\id - T)\inv y_n\| \to \infty$.

Define $$ \begin{align*} x_n := \frac{(λ_n\id - T)\inv y_n}{ \|(λ_n\id - T)\inv y_n\|}. \end{align*} $$ Then $\|x_n\| = 1$ and $$ \begin{align*} \|(λ\id - T)x_n\| \leq {}& \|(λ_n\id - T)x_n\| + \|(λ - λ_n)x_n\| \\ ={}& \underbrace{\frac{1}{\|(λ_n\id - T)\inv y_n\|}}_{\to 0} \underbrace{\|y_n\|}_{=1} + \underbrace{|λ-λ_n|}_{\to 0} \underbrace{\|x_n\|}_{=1} \\ \to{}& 0 \end{align*} $$ so $λ$ is an approximate eigenvalue.

Since $σ(T)$ is closed and not $\emptyset$ nor $\C$, connectedness of $\C$ implies $σ(T)$ is not open. Therefore $\op{int}(σ(T)) \neq σ(T)$ so $\partial σ(T) \neq \emptyset$. Hence $σ_\text{ap}(T)\neq \emptyset$.
