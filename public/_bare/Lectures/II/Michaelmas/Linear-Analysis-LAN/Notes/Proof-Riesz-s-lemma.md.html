Fix $ε>0$. Pick any $z\in X\setminus Y$. Since $Y$ is closed, $δ:=d(z,Y) = \inf\sb{\|z-y\|}{y\in Y}>0$.

Find $y\in Y$ such that $\|z-y\| > δ/(1-ε)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} x = \frac{z - y}{\|z-y\|} \end{align*} $$ satisfies that for any $y'\in Y$, $$ \begin{align*} \|x-y'\| ={}& \frac{\bigl\|z - y - \|z-y\|y'\bigr\|}{\|z-y\|} \\ \leq{}& \frac{δ}{\|z-y\|} &&y-\|z-y\|y'\in Y\\ ={}& 1-ε \end{align*} $$ as required.
