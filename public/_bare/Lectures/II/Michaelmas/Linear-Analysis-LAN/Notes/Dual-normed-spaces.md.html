Definition (dual normed space) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a normed space.

The dual space $X^\ast$ of $X$ is $\mathcal{B}(X, \mathbb{F})$, the space of all bounded linear functionals on $X$.

We write $\langle x,f\rangle = f(x)$ for $f\in X^\ast$, $x\in X$.

Since $\mathbb{F}$ is complete, $X^\ast$ is always complete.

Theorem (Hahn-Banach) open_in_new

(Non-examinable in II Linear Analysis)

Let $X$ be a normed space and $Y\subseteq X$ be a subspace.

For any $g\in{}$$Y^\ast$, there exists $f\in X^\ast$ such that $f|_Y = g$ and $\|f\| = \|g\|$.

Corollary (norming functional) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a normed space.

For any $x_0\in X \setminus \set{0}$, there exists $f\in{}$$X^\ast$ such that $\|f\| = 1$ and $f(x_0) = \|x_0\|$.

$f$ is called the norming functional at $x_0$.

Proof

Therefore $\sup_{f\in B_{X^\ast}} f(x_0) = \|x_0\|$ and is attained by the norming functional.
