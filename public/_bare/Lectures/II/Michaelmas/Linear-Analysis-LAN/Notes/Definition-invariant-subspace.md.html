Let $X$ be a vector space, $Y$ be a subspace and $T: X \to X$ be a linear map.

$Y$ is an invariant subspace of $T$ if $T(Y) \subseteq Y$.

  • $Y = \set{0}$ and $X$ are the trivial invariant subspaces.

  • If $λ$ is an eigenvalue of $T$, then the eigenspace $E_T(λ) = \ker(λ\id - T)$ is an invariant subspace of $T$. If $X$ is normed, then $E_T(λ)$ is closed.
