Fix $x\in X$ and $ε>0$. For each $n\in \N$, let $$ \begin{align*} P_n x = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \langle x, e_i\rangle e_i. \end{align*} $$ We need to show $\|x - P_nx\| \to 0$ as $n\to \infty$.

Since $x\in X = \overline{\op{span}}\set{e_1, e_2, \ldots}$, let $y \in \op{span}\set{e_1, e_2, \ldots}$ such that $\|y - x\| < ε$. Choose $N\in \N$ such that $y\in \op{span}\set{e_1, \ldots, e_N}$.

For $n\geq N$, since $\langle x - P_nx, e_i\rangle = \langle x,e_i\rangle - \langle x,e_i\rangle = 0$ for all $i = 1,2,\ldots,n$, we have $\langle x - P_nx, P_n x - y\rangle = 0$ as $P_nx - y \in \op{span}\set{e_1, \ldots, e_n}$. Therefore by Pythagoras, $$ \begin{align*} ε^2 > \|y - x\|^2 = \|x - P_nx\|^2 + \|P_nx + y\|^2 \geq \|x - P_nx\|^2 \end{align*} $$ so $\|x - P_nx\| \to 0$ as $n\to \infty$.
