Uniqueness

If $T = T_1 + iT_2$ with $T_1, T_2$ Hermitian, then $$ \begin{align*} T^\ast = T_1^\ast - iT_2^\ast = T_1 - iT_2. \end{align*} $$ so $T_1$ and $T_2$ are necessarily given by the specified formulae.

Existence

The specified maps work: $$ \begin{align*} T_1^\ast ={}& \frac{1}{2}(T^\ast + T) = T_1, \\ T_2^\ast ={}& \frac{1}{-2i}(T^\ast - T) = T_2, \\ T ={}& \frac{1}{2}(T + T^\ast) + \frac{1}{2}(T - T^\ast) = T_1 + iT_2. \end{align*} $$
