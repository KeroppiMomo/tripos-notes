Suppose $(x_n)_{n\in \N}$ be a sequence in $X$ such that $\|x_n\| = 1$ and $(λ\id - T)x_n\to 0$.

Since $T$ is compact and $(x_n)$ is bounded, there exists a subsequence $n_1 < n_2 < \ldots$ such that $Tx_{n_k}$ converges as $k\to \infty$, say to $z\in X$. Then as $λ\neq 0$, $$ \begin{align*} x_{n_k} = \frac{1}{λ}\underbrace{(λ\id - T)x_{n_k}}_{\to 0} + \frac{1}{λ}Tx_{n_k} \to \frac{1}{λ}z. \end{align*} $$ By continuity of $\|\cdot\|$, $\|z\| = |λ| \lim_{k\to \infty} \|x_{n_k}\| = |λ|$ so $z \neq 0$. Moreover $$ \begin{align*} Tz = \lim_{k\to \infty} T(λ x_{n_k}) = λz. \end{align*} $$ so $λ$ is an eigenvalue of $T$.
