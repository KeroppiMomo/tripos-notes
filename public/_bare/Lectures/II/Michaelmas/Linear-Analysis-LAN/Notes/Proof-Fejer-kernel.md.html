Computation: $$ \begin{align*} K_N(t) ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \sum_{n=0}^{N} \frac{e^{-int}(e^{i(2n+1)t} - 1)}{e^{it} - 1} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \frac{1}{e^{it} - 1} \sum_{n=0}^{N} (e^{i(n+1)t} - e^{-int}) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \frac{1}{e^{it} - 1} \left(\frac{e^{it}(e^{i(N+1)t} - 1)}{e^{it}-1} - \frac{e^{-i(N+1)t} - 1}{e^{-it} - 1}\right) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \frac{e^{i(N+2)t} - e^{it} - e^{it} + e^{-iNt}}{(e^{it} - 1)^2} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \frac{(e^{i\frac{N+1}{2}t} - e^{-i\frac{N+1}{2}t})^2}{(e^{i \frac{1}{2}t} - e^{-i \frac{1}{2}t})^2} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N+1} \left(\frac{\sin \frac{N+1}{2}t}{\sin \frac{1}{2}t}\right)^2. \end{align*} $$

$K_N$ is an approximate identity:

  1. $K_N \geq 0$.

  2. $\frac{1}{2π}\int_{-π}^{π} K_N(t) \d t = \frac{1}{N+1} \sum_{n=0}^{N} \frac{1}{2π} \int_{-π}^{π} D_n(t) \d t = 1$.

  3. For any $δ>0$, $K_N$ converges uniformly to $0$ on $[-π, π] \setminus [-δ, δ]$, because $$ \begin{align*} |K_N(t)| \leq \frac{1}{N+1} \frac{1}{(\sin \frac{1}{2}δ)^2} \to 0. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $f*K_N\to f$.
