Definition (small lp spaces) open_in_new

Let $\mathbb{F} = \R$ or $\C$.

For $p\in [1,\infty)$, the $lp$ space_ is the infinite-dimensional [[Definition (norm, normed space)|normed space]]$$$ \begin{align*} l_p := \sb{\text{sequence $(x_n)_{n\geq 1}$ in $\mathbb{F}$}}{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n|^p < \infty}, \qquad \|x\|_p := \left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |x_n|^p\right)^{1/p}. \end{align*} $$ $l_p^n$ is the $n$-dimensional subspace $$ \begin{align*} l_p^n := \R^n, \qquad \|x\|_p = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^p. \end{align*} $$

For $p = \infty$, $$ \begin{align*} l_\infty := \sb{\text{sequence $(x_n)_{n\geq 1}$ in $\mathbb{F}$}}{\sup_{n\geq 1} |x_n| < \infty}, \qquad \|x\|_\infty := \sup_{n\geq 1} |x_n|. \end{align*} $$ $l_\infty$ has subspaces $$ \begin{align*} c_0 &:= \sb{(x_n)\in l_\infty}{x_n\to \infty}, \\ c_{00} &:= \sb{(x_n)\in l_\infty}{\exists N: \forall n\geq N: x_n = 0}. \end{align*} $$

Triangle inequality follows from Minkowski inequality.

$l_p$ is a Banach space for $p\in [1,\infty]$. $c_0$ is closed in $l_\infty$ so is also Banach. $c_{00}$ is not closed so it is not Banach.

Theorem (Minkowski inequality) open_in_new

Let $p\in [1,\infty]$. For $x,y\in{}$$l_p$, $$\|x + y\|_p \leq \|x\|_p + \|y\|_p.$$

Theorem (Hölder inequality) open_in_new

Let $1\leq p<q\leq \infty$ such that $1/p + 1/q = 1$. $q$ is called the conjugate index of $p$.

If $x\in{}$$l_p$ and $y\in l_q$, then $$ \begin{align*} \|x\cdot y\|_1 \leq \|x\|_p \|y\|_q. \end{align*} $$

Proof

Proposition (dual of small lp spaces) open_in_new

The function $$ \begin{align*} (x_n) &\mapsto \left((y_n) \mapsto \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x_n y_n\right) \end{align*} $$ is an isometric isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} l_1 &\isom c_0^\ast, \\ l_p &\isom l_q^\ast, && p\in (1, \infty], \; p\inv + q\inv = 1. \end{align*} $$ and is an isometric embedding $$ \begin{align*} l_1 \hookrightarrow l_\infty^\ast. \end{align*} $$
