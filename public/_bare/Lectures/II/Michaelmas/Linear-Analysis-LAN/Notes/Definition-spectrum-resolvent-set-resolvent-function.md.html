Let $X\neq \set{0}$ be a complex Banach space and $T\in{}$$\mathcal{B}(X)$.

The spectrum of $T$ is $$ \begin{align*} σ(T) = \sb{λ\in \C}{λ \id - T\not\in \mathcal{G}(X)}. \end{align*} $$

The resolvent set is $ρ(T) = \C \setminus σ(T)$. The resolvent function is $$ \begin{align*} R_T: ρ(T) &\to \mathcal{B}(X) \\ λ &\mapsto (λ \id - T)\inv. \end{align*} $$

  • $σ(T)$ is a closed subset of $\sb{z\in \C}{|z|\leq \|T\|}$, hence compact. (Proof)

  • If $|λ| > \|T\|$, then we saw $$ \begin{align*} R_T(λ) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{T^n}{λ^{n+1}}. \end{align*} $$

  • $R_T$ is a holomorphic function, in particular $\lim_{μ\to λ} \frac{R_T(μ) - R_T(λ)}{μ - λ} = -R_T(λ)^2$. (Proof)
