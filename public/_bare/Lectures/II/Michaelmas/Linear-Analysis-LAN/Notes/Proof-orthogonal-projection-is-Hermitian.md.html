Suppose $Y$ is a closed subspace of $H$. Then $H = Y \oplus Y^\perp$ and we have a orthogonal projection $$ \begin{align*} P : H &\to H \\ y + z &\mapsto y, \qquad y\in Y, z\in Y^\perp. \end{align*} $$

For any $x,x'\in X$, write $x = y+z, x' = y'+z'$ where $y,y'\in Y$, $z,z'\in Y^\perp$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \langle Px, x'\rangle = \langle y, y'+z'\rangle = \langle y,y'\rangle = \langle y+z, y'\rangle = \langle x, Px'\rangle \end{align*} $$ so $P^\ast = P$.
