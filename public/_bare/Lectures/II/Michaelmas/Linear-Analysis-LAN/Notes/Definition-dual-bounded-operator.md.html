Let $X, Y$ be normed spaces and $T\in \mathcal{B}(X,Y)$.

The dual operator $T^\ast$ of $T$ is the map $$ \begin{align*} T^\ast: Y^\ast &\to X^\ast \\ g &\mapsto g \circ T. \end{align*} $$

By definition, $\langle x, T^\ast(g)\rangle = \langle T(x), g\rangle$.

See dual maps of vector spaces.

$T\in \mathcal{B}(X^\ast, Y^\ast)$ and $\|T^\ast\| = \|T\|$. (Proof)

$\widehat{Tx} = T^{\ast\ast} \hat x$. (Commutative diagram, proof)

If $X\sim Y$ via an isomorphism $f$, then $X^\ast\sim Y^\ast$ via $f^\ast$.
