$T$ invertible $\implies$ $T^\ast$ invertible

If there exists $S\in \mathcal{B}(X)$ such that $ST = TS = \id$, taking adjoint gives $T^\ast S^\ast = S^\ast T^\ast = \id$, so $S^\ast$ is the inverse of $T^\ast$.

$T^\ast$ invertible $\implies$ $T$ invertible

The above shows if $T^\ast$ is invertible, then $T^{\ast\ast}$ is invertible. But $T = T^{\ast\ast}$.
