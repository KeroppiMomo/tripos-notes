The unit ball of a normed space $X$ is $$ \begin{align*} B_X = \sb{x\in X}{\|x\|\leq 1}, \end{align*} $$ which is

  1. symmetric: $x\in B_X \iff -x\in B_X$;

  2. convex: if $x,y\in B_X$ and $λ\in [0,1]$, then $(1-t)x + ty\in B_X$; and

  3. closed.

Conversely, for a metric space $X$, if $B\subseteq X$ is a symmetric, convex, closed and bounded neighbourhood of $0$, then there is a norm on $X$ given by $$ \begin{align*} \|x\| = \inf\sb{t\geq 0}{x\in tB} \end{align*} $$ such that $B$ is the unit ball of $(X, \|\cdot\|)$.
