Let $X,Y$ be normed spaces.

If $\dim X = \dim Y < \infty$, then $X$${}\sim{}$$Y$.

Proof

  • If $X$ is a finite-dimensional normed space, then $X\sim l^{\dim X}_2$.

    Therefore $X$ is complete. Moreover, a subset of $X$ is compact iff it is closed and bounded.

  • Any finite-dimensional subspace of a normed space is complete thus closed.
