Using the Fourier series, $x = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \langle x, e_n\rangle e_n$ and $y = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \langle y,e_n\rangle e_n$, so by the continuity of $\langle \cdot ,\cdot \rangle$, $$ \begin{align*} \langle x,y\rangle ={}& \left\langle \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \langle x,e_n\rangle e_n, \sum_{m=1}^{\infty} \langle x,e_m\rangle e_m\right\rangle \\ ={}& \sum_{n,m} \langle x,e_n\rangle \overline{\langle y,e_m\rangle} \langle e_n, e_m\rangle \\ ={}& \sum_{n} \langle x,e_n\rangle \overline{\langle y,e_n\rangle}. \end{align*} $$

Take $y = x$ to see the first identity.
