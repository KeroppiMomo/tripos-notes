$\overline{TB_X}$ is a closed and bounded subspace of the finite-dimensional space $TX$, so $\overline{TB_X}$ is compact. This shows $\mathcal{F}(X,Y) \subseteq \mathcal{K}(X,Y)$.

Since $\mathcal{K}(X,Y)$ is closed in $\mathcal{B}(X,Y)$, we have $\overline{\mathcal{F}(X,Y)} \subseteq \overline{\mathcal{K}(X,Y)} = \mathcal{K}(X,Y)$.
