Let $R$ be a (non-commutative) unital ring, and let $a,b\in R$.

If both $a$ and $b$ are invertible, then so is $ab$.

If $ab$ is invertible and $ab=ba$, then both $a$ and $b$ are invertible.

By induction, for any $a_1, \ldots, a_n\in R$ that pairwise commute, $\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_i$ is invertible iff all $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ are invertible.
