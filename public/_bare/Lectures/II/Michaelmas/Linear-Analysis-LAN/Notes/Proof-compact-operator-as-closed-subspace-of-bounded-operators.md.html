$\mathcal{K}(X,Y) \subseteq \mathcal{B}(X,Y)$

For $T\in \mathcal{K}(X,Y)$, since $\overline{T(B_X)}$ is compact, $T(B_X)$ is totally bounded, so bounded.

$T,S \in \mathcal{K}(X,Y)$ $\implies$ $λT + μS \in \mathcal{K}(X,Y)$

Let $(x_n)_{n\in \N}$ be a sequence in $B_X$.

Since $T$ is compact, find a subsequence $n_1<n_2<\ldots$ such that $(Tx_{n_k})_{k\in \N}$ converges in $Y$. And since $S$ is compact, find a sub-subsequence $m_1<m_2<\ldots$ such that both $(Tx_{m_k})_{k\in \N}$ and $(Sx_{m_k})_{k\in \N}$ converge in $Y$. Then $(λTx_{m_k} + μSx_{m_k})_{k\in \N}$ converges in $Y$. Hence $λT + μS$ is compact.

This shows $\mathcal{K}(X,Y)$ is a vector subspace of $\mathcal{B}(X,Y)$.

$\mathcal{K}(X,Y)$ is sequentially closed

Suppose $(T_n)_{n\in \N}$ is a sequence in $\mathcal{K}(X,Y)$ such that $T_n \to T$ in $\mathcal{B}(X,Y)$. To show $T$ is compact, we will produce an finite $ε$-net for $TB_X$ for each $ε>0$.

Fix $ε>0$. Since $T_n\to T$, find $n\in \N$ such that $\|T_n - T\| < ε$. By compactness of $T_n$, let $x_1, \ldots, x_m\in B_X$ such that $\{T_nx_1, \ldots, T_nx_m\}$ is an $ε$-net of $T_nB_X$.

For each $x\in B_X$, find $i$ such that $\|T_nx - T_nx_i\|<ε$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \|Tx - Tx_i\| &\leq \underbrace{\|Tx - T_n x\|}_{\leq \|T - T_n\|} + \|T_n x - T_nx_i\| + \underbrace{\|T_n x_i - Tx_i\|}_{\leq \|T_n - T\|}\\ &< 3ε. \end{align*} $$ So $\set{Tx_1, \ldots, Tx_m}$ is a $3ε$-net of $TB_X$.
