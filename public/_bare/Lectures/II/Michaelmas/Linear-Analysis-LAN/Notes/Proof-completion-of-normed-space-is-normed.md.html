Define the normed space structure on $\tilde X$ as follows: for $x = \lim_{n\to \infty} j(x_n)$ and $y = \lim_{n\to \infty} j(y_n)$ where $(x_n)_{n\in \N}, (y_n)_{\in \N}$ are in $X$, $$ \begin{align*} x + y &:= \lim_{n\to \infty} j(x_n + y_n), \\ λx &:= \lim_{n\to \infty} j(λx_n), \\ \|x\| &:= \lim_{n\to \infty} \|x_n\|. \end{align*} $$

Check these are well-defined.
