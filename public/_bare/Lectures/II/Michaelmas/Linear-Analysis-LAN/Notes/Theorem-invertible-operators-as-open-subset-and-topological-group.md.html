Let $X$ be a Banach space.

  1. Let $T\in \mathcal{G}(X)$ and $S\in \mathcal{B}(X)$. If $\|S\|<1/\|T\inv\|$, then $S - T\in \mathcal{G}(X)$.

    Therefore $\mathcal{G}(X)$ is an open subset of $\mathcal{B}(X)$.

    In particular, if $\|T\|<1$, then $$ \begin{align*} (\id - T)\inv = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} T^n, \qquad \|(\id - T)\inv\| \leq \frac{1}{1 - \|T\|}. \end{align*} $$

  2. $\mathcal{G}(X)$ is a topological group, i.e. composition and inverse are continuous.

  3. If $(T_n)_{n\in \N}$ is a sequence in $\mathcal{G}(X)$ such that $T_n \to T\in \mathcal{B}(X)\setminus \mathcal{G}(X)$, then $\|T_n\inv\|\to \infty$ as $n\to \infty$.
