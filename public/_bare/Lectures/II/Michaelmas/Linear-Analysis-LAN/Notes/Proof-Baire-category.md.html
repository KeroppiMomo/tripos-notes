Intersection of countably many open dense sets is dense

Let $(U_n)_{n\in \N}$ be a countable collection of open dense sets, and fix $x_0\in X$ and $r_0>0$. We want to show $B_{r_0}[x_0] \cap \bigcap_{n\in \N} U_n \not\in \emptyset$.

We will construct a decreasing sequence of balls $(B_{r_n}(x_n))_{n\geq 0}$: given $x_n\in X$, $r_n>0$, since $U_{n+1}$ is dense, find $x_{n+1}\in B_{r_n}(x_n) \cap U_{n+1}$, and since $B_{r_n}(x_n) \cap U_{n+1}$ is open, find $r_{n+1}>0$ such that $B_{r_{n+1}}[x_{n+1}] \subseteq B_{r_n}(x_n) \cap U_{n+1}$. WLOG assume $r_{n+1}\leq \frac{1}{n+1}$.

By construction, if $n\geq N$, $$ \begin{align*} B_{r_n}(x_n) &\subseteq B_{r_{n-1}}(x_{n-1}) \cap U_n \\ &\subseteq B_{r_{n-2}}(x_{n-2}) \cap U_{n-1} \cap U_n \\ &\subseteq \ldots \\ &\subseteq B_{r_N}(x_N) \cap U_{N+1} \cap U_{N+2} \cap \ldots \cap U_n \end{align*} $$ so for $m,n\geq N$, we get $\|x_n - x_m\| \leq 2r_N \leq 2/N$. Hence $(x_n)_{n\geq 0}$ is a Cauchy sequence, thus converges to say $x$ in the complete space $X$.

Moreover if $n\geq N$, then $x_n \in B_{r_N}[x_N]$ which is closed, so $x\in B_{r_N}[x_N]$. Hence $$ \begin{align*} x\in \bigcap_{N\in \N} B_{r_N}[x_N] = B_{r_0}(x_0) \cap \bigcap_{n\in \N} U_n \end{align*} $$ as required.

Union of nowhere dense sets has empty interior

Let $(A_n)_{n\in \N}$ be a countable collection of nowhere dense sets, meaning $\op{int}(\overline{A_n}) = \emptyset$. Defining $$ \begin{align*} U_n = X \setminus \overline{A_n}, \end{align*} $$ $U_n$ is open and dense since $\overline{U_n} = X \setminus \op{int}(\overline{A_n}) = X$.

Thus by the above, $\bigcap_{n\in \N} U_n$ is dense, so $$ \begin{align*} \op{int}\left(\bigcup_{n\in \N} A_n\right) \subseteq \op{int}\left(\bigcup_{n\in \N} \overline{A_n} \right) = X \setminus \overline{\left(X \setminus \bigcup_{n\in \N} \overline{A_n}\right)} = X \setminus \overline{\bigcup_{n\in \N} U_n} = \emptyset. \end{align*} $$
