Bounded $\implies$ Lipschitz

$$ \begin{align*} \|Tx - Ty\| = \|T(x-y)\| \leq \|T\| \, \|x-y\| \end{align*} $$ so $T$ is $\|T\|$-Lipschitz.

Lipschitz $\implies$ continuous $\implies$ continuous at 0

Obvious.

Continuous at 0 $\implies$ bounded

By continuity, there exists $δ>0$ such that for all $x\in X$ where $\|x\|\leq δ$, we have $\|Tx\|\leq 1$. Then for all $x\in X\setminus \set{0}$, $$ \begin{align*} \|Tx\| = \frac{\|x\|}{δ} \, \biggl\|T\Bigl(\underbrace{\frac{δx}{\|x\|}}_{\in δB_X}\Bigr)\biggr\| \leq \frac{\|x\|}{δ}. \end{align*} $$
