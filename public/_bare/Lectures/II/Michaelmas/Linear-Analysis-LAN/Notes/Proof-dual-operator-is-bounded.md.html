$T^\ast$ is bounded since $\|T^\ast(g)\| = \|g \circ T\| \leq \|g\| \, \|T\|$ so $\|T^\ast\| \leq \|T\|$.

Conversely, $$ \begin{align*} \|T^\ast\| ={}& \sup_{g\in B_{X^\ast}} \|T^\ast(g)\| \\ ={}& \sup_{g\in B_{X^\ast}} \|g \circ T\| \\ ={}& \sup_{g\in B_{X^\ast}} \sup_{x\in B_X} \|g\circ T(x)\| \\ ={}& \sup_{x\in B_X} \sup_{g\in B_{X^\ast}} \|g(T(x))\| \\ ={}& \sup_{x\in B_X} \|T(x)\| \tag{*}\\ ={}& \|T\|. \end{align*} $$ where we have used the norming functional at $(*)$.
