Noticing $$ \begin{align*} \max\set{f,g} = \frac{f+g}{2} + \frac{|f - g|}{2}, \qquad \min\set{f,g} = \frac{f+g}{2} - \frac{|f-g|}{2}, \end{align*} $$ it suffices to show $|f|\in A$ for all $f\in A$.

Fix $f\in A$. By homogeneity, WLOG assume $\|f\|_\infty = 1$.

For any $ε>0$, find a real polynomial $p(t) = \sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n t^n$ such that $\bigl|p(t) - |t|\bigr| < ε$ on $t\in [-1,1]$.

Note $p\circ f = \sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n f^n\in A$, and since $\|f\|_\infty = 1$, $$ \begin{align*} \bigl\|p \circ f - |f|\bigr\|_\infty < ε. \end{align*} $$

Thus $|f|\in \overline{A} = A$.
