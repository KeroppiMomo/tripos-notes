Definition (completion of metric space) open_in_new

Let $(M,d)$ be a metric space.

A completion of $M$ is a complete metric space $(\tilde M, \tilde d)$ with an isometric map $j: (M, d)\to (\tilde M, \tilde d)$ such that $j(M)$ is dense in $\tilde M$.

Theorem (existence and uniqueness of completion) open_in_new

Every metric space $(M,d)$ has a completion.

Moreover if $(\tilde M_1, \tilde d_1)$ and $(\tilde M_2, \tilde d_2)$ are two completions of $(M,d)$, then there exists an isometric bijection $(\tilde M_1, \tilde d_1) \to (\tilde M_2, \tilde d_2)$.
