Since $\mathcal{G}(X) \to \mathcal{G}(X): S \mapsto S\inv$ is continuous, $R_T$ is continuous.

Now for $λ,μ\in ρ(T)$, $$ \begin{align*} R_T(μ) - R_T(λ) &= (μ \id - T)\inv - (λ \id - T)\inv \\ &= (μ \id - T)\inv \bigl((λ\id - T) - (μ\id - T)\bigr) (λ \id - T)\inv \\ &= -(μ - λ)\, R_T(μ) \, R_T(λ) \end{align*} $$ so by continuity $$ \begin{align*} \frac{R_T(μ) - R_T(λ)}{μ - λ} = -R_T(μ) R_T(λ) \to -R_T(λ)^2 \qquad \text{as }μ\to λ. \end{align*} $$
