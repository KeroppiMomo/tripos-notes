Let $H\neq \set{0}$ be a complex Hilbert space.

Suppose $T\in \mathcal{B}(H)$ is a compact Hermitian operator.

  1. If $λ\in{}$$σ(T)$ and $λ\neq 0$, then $λ$ is an eigenvalue of $T$, and the eigenspace $E_T(λ) = \ker(λ\id - T)$ is finite-dimensional.

  2. $σ(T) \setminus \set{0}$ is a sequence $λ_1, λ_2, \ldots$ which satisfies $|λ_1| \geq |λ_2| \geq \ldots$ and either is finite or converges to 0.

    In particular, $σ(T)$ is countable, and $σ(T) \cap \sb{z\in \C}{|z|\geq δ}$ is finite for all $δ>0$.

  3. There exists an orthonormal basis $e_1, e_2, \ldots$ for $(\ker T)^\perp$ and $μ_1, μ_2, \ldots\in \R$ satisfying $|μ_1| \geq |μ_2| \geq \ldots$ and either is finite or converges to 0 such that $$ \begin{align*} T\left(z +\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x_n e_n\right) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} μ_n x_n e_n, \qquad z\in \ker T. \end{align*} $$

    Equivalently, writing $P_n$ for the orthogonal projection onto $E_T(λ_n)$, $$ \begin{align*} T = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} λ_n P_n. \end{align*} $$
