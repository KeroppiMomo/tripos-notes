Write $\|\cdot\|$ to denote the norms on $X$ and $Y$.

Given $T: X\to Y$, define a new norm $\|\cdot\|'$ on $X$ by $$ \begin{align*} \|x\|' := \|x\| + \|Tx\|. \end{align*} $$

Any two norms on the finite-dimensional space $X$ are equivalent, so $\|x\|' \leq c\|x\|$ for some $c>0$. In particular $\|Tx\|\leq c\|x\|$, so $T$ is bounded.
