$T$ is compact $\implies$ $T^\ast$ is compact

Let $K = \overline{TB_X}$, which is compact.

Note for $g\in Y^\ast$, $$ \begin{align*} \|T^\ast g\| ={}& \sup_{x\in B_X} |(T^\ast g)x| \\ ={}& \sup_{x\in B_X} |g(Tx)| \\ ={}& \sup_{y\in TB_X} |g(y)| \\ ={}& \sup_{y\in K} |g(y)| &&\text{continuity of $g$} \\ ={}& \|g|_K\|_\infty. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} θ: T^\ast(B_{Y^\ast}) &\to C(K) \\ T^\ast g &\mapsto g|_K \end{align*} $$ is well-defined ($T^\ast g = T^\ast h$ $\iff$ $\|T^\ast(g - h)\| = 0$ $\iff$ $\|g|_K - h|_L\|_\infty = 0$ $\iff$ $g|_K = h|_L$) and isometric. Then $T^\ast(B_{Y^\ast})$ is totally bounded iff $\im θ = \sb{g|_K}{g\in B_{Y^\ast}}\subseteq C(K)$ is totally bounded.

Finally apply Arzelà-Ascoli to show $\im θ$ is totally bounded:

  1. Uniformly bounded: since $K\subseteq Y$ is compact, $K$ is bounded, say $K\subseteq MB_Y$. Then for $g\in B_{Y^\ast}$, $y\in K$, we have $\|g(y)\| \leq \|g\|\|y\| \leq 1\cdot M$, so $\|g|_K\|_\infty \leq M$.

  2. Equicontinuous: fixing $ε>0$, if $y,z\in K$ satisfies $\|y-z\|<ε$, then $\|g(y) - g(z)\| = \|g(y - z)\| \leq \|g\| \|y-z\| < 1\cdot ε = ε$.

$T^\ast$ is compact $\implies$ $T$ is compact

By the previous part, $T^{\ast\ast}\in \mathcal{B}(X^{\ast\ast}, Y^{\ast\ast})$ is compact. By Hahn-Banach, $\hat{\ }: X \to X^{\ast\ast}$ and $\hat{\ }: Y \to Y^{\ast\ast}$ are isometric. The identity $\widehat{Tx} = T^{\ast\ast} \hat x$ says $$ \begin{align*} \hat {\ }(TB_X ) = T^{\ast\ast}(\hat {\ }(B_X)) \subseteq T^{\ast\ast}(B_{X^{\ast\ast}}) \end{align*} $$ which is totally bounded, so $TB_X$ is also totally bounded.
