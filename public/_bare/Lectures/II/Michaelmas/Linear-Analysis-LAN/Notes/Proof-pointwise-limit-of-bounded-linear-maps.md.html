For each $x\in X$, $(T_nx)_{n\in \N}$ is convergent, hence bounded. Therefore noting $X$ is Banach, the principle of uniform boundedness says $\sup_{n\in \N} \|T_n\| < \infty$.

$T$ is linear by the linearity of limits: $$ \begin{align*} T(λx + μy) = \lim_{n\to \infty} T_n(λx + μy) = \lim_{n\to \infty} (λ T_nx + μ T_ny) = λTx + μTy. \end{align*} $$

$T$ is bounded because for all $x\in B_X$, $$ \begin{align*} \|Tx\| = \lim_{n\in \N} \|T_n x\| \leq \liminf_{n\to \infty} \|T_n\| \leq \sup_{n\in \N} \|T_n\| <\infty. \end{align*} $$
