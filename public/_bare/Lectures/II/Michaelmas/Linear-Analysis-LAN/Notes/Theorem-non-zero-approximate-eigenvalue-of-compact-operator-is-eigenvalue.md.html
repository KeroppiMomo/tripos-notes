Let $X\neq \set{0}$ be a complex Banach space and $T\in{}$$\mathcal{B}(X)$.

Suppose $T$ is compact. If $λ$ is a non-zero approximate eigenvalue of $T$, then $λ$ is an eigenvalue of $T$, i.e. $σ_\text{ap}(λ) \subseteq σ_\text{p}(λ) \cup \set{0}$.

Proof

  • In fact $σ_\text{ap}(λ) \subseteq σ_\text{p}(λ) \cup \set{0}$.
