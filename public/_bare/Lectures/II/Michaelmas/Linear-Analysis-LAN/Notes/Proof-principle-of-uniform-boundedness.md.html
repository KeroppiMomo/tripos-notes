Define for $n\in \N$ $$ \begin{align*} A_n = \sb{x\in X}{\|Tx\|\leq n \; \text{ for all $T\in \mathcal{T}$}} = \bigcap_{T\in \mathcal{T}} \sb{x\in X}{\|Tx\| \leq n}. \end{align*} $$ $A_n$ is the intersection of closed preimages under the continuous functions $x\mapsto \|Tx\|$, so $A_n$ is closed.

Since $\mathcal{T}$ is pointwise bounded, $\bigcup_{n\in \N} A_n = X$. By the Baire category theorem, noting $X$ is complete and non-empty, there exists $n\in \N$ such that $\op{int} A_n = \op{int}\overline{A_n} \neq \emptyset$. Let $r>0$ and $x\in A_n$ such that $B_r[x] \subseteq A_n$.

For each $z\in B_X$, $x \pm rz\in B_r[x] \subseteq A_n$, so $$ \begin{align*} \|Tz\| = \left\|\frac{1}{2r}((x + rz) - (x - rz))\right\| \leq \frac{1}{2r}\left(\|x + rz\| + \|x - rz\|\right) \leq \frac{n}{r} \end{align*} $$ and hence $\|T\| \leq n/r$ for all $T\in \mathcal{T}$.
