Let $X,Y$ be a normed space, and $(T_n)_{n\in \N}$ is a sequence in $\mathcal{B}(X,Y)$.

If $X$ is a Banach space and there is a map $T: X\to Y$ such that $T_n x \to Tx$ for all $x\in X$, then $\sup_{n\in \N} \|T_n\| < \infty$, $T\in \mathcal{B}(X,Y)$ and $\|T\| \leq \liminf_{n\to \infty} \|T_n\|$.
