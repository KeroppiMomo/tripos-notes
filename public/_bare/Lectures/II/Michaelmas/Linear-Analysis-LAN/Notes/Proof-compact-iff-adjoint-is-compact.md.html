Let $H$ be a Hilbert space and $T\in \mathcal{B}(H)$ be compact. Fix a bounded sequence $(x_n)_{n\in \N}$ in $H$. We want to show $(T^\ast x_n)_{n\in \N}$ has a convergent subsequence.

Let $M>0$ such that $\|x_n\|<M$ for all $n$. $(T^\ast x_n)$ is bounded: $$ \begin{align*} \|T^\ast x_n\| \leq \|T^\ast\| \|x_n\|\leq M\|T\| \end{align*} $$ so $(TT^\ast x_n)_n$ has a convergent subsequence $(TT^\ast x_{n_k})_k$.

Notice $(T^\ast x_{n_k})_{k}$ is Cauchy: $$ \begin{align*} \|T^\ast x_{n_k} - T^\ast x_{n_l}\|^2 ={}& \langle T^\ast (x_{n_k} - x_{n_l}), T^\ast (x_{n_k}, x_{n_l})\rangle \\ ={}& \langle x_{n_k} - x_{n_l}, TT^\ast(x_{n_k} - x_{n_l})\rangle \\ \leq{}& \|x_{n_k} - x_{n_l}\| \|TT^\ast(x_{n_k} - x_{n_l})\| \\ \leq{}& 2M\|TT^\ast x_{n_k} - TT^\ast x_{n_l}\| \\ \to{}& 0 \end{align*} $$ as $m,n\to \infty$. Therefore $T^\ast x_{n_k}$ converges.
