Let $X$ be a vector space.

Two norms $\|\cdot \|$, $\|\cdot \|'$ on $X$ are said to be equivalent if $\id: (X, \|\cdot \|)\to (X, \|\cdot \|')$ is an isomorphism of normed spaces.

Equivalently, there exists $a,b>0$ such that $$ \begin{align*} a\|x\| \leq \|x\|' \leq b \|x\| \qquad \text{for all }x\in X. \end{align*} $$
