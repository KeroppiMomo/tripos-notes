The function $$ \begin{align*} f: \C &\to \mathcal{B}(X) \\ λ &\mapsto λ\id - T \end{align*} $$ is continuous. Since $σ(T) = f\inv(\mathcal{B}(X) \setminus \mathcal{G}(X))$ and $\mathcal{G}(X)$ is open, $σ(T)$ is closed.

Moreover if $λ > \|T\|$, then $\|T\| < 1/\|(λ\id)\inv\|$, so $λ\id - T$ is invertible.
