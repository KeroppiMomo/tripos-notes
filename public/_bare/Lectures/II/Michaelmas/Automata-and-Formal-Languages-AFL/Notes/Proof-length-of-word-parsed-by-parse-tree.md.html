$|w|$ is the number of leaves in $\mathbf{T}$. Parse trees for grammars in Chomsky normal form are at most binary branching. A full binary tree of height $h+1$ has $2^{h+1}$ many leaves. However the parse tree for $w\in \mathbb{W}$ has $|w|$ many unary rule applications, each reducing the number of leaves by at least 1. Thus $|w| \leq 2^{h+1} - |w|$ and so $|w| \leq 2^h$.
