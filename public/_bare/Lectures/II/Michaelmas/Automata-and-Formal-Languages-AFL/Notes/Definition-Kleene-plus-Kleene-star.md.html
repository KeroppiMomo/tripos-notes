Let $L\subseteq Σ^\ast$ be a language.

The Kleene plus operation is $$ \begin{align*} L^+ = \sb{w\in Σ^\ast}{\exists w_0, \ldots, w_n\in L \text{ such that } w = w_0 \ldots w_n}, \end{align*} $$ i.e. finite concatenation of one or more elements of $L$.

The Kleene star operation is $$ \begin{align*} L^\ast = L^+ \cup \set{ε}. \end{align*} $$
