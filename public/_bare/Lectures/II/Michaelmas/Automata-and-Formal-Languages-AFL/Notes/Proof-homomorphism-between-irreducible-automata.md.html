Let $D = (Σ, Q, δ, q_0, F)$ and $D' = (Σ, Q', δ', q_0', F')$.

Injectivity

If $f(p) = f(q)$, then $δ(p,w)\in F \iff δ'(f(p), w) \in F' \iff δ'(f(q), w) \in F' \iff δ(q, w) \in F'$ so $p$ and $q$ are indistinguishable. Hence $D$ having no distinct indistinguishable states implies $p=q$.

Surjectivity

Any state $q'$ in $D'$ is accessible, so $q' = δ'(q_0', w) = f(δ(q_0, w))$ for some word $w$. Thus $q'\in \mathrm{range}(f)$.
