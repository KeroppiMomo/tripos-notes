Let $G = (Σ, V, P, S)$ be a $\mathsf{Q}$ grammar. Take a new variable $T\not\in V$ and define $$ \begin{align*} G' = (Σ, V \cup \set{T}, P \cup \set{T\to S}, T). \end{align*} $$ All rules are $T$-safe so $G'$ is $ε$-adequate. Clearly $\mathcal{L}(G) = \mathcal{L}(G')$. Also $G'$ preserves the property $\mathsf{Q}$.
