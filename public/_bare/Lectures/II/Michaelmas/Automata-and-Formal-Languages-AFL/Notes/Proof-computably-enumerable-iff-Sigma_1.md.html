Computably enumerable $\implies Σ_1$

Let $f$ be a partial computable function such that $X = \mathrm{dom}(f)$, computed by register machine $M$.

The truncated computation function $t_{M,k}$ is a characteristic function of a set $Y\subseteq \mathbb{W}^{k+1}$ where $(\v w, v)\in Y$ iff the computation of $M$ with input $\v w$ has halted after at most $\#(v)$ steps. Thus $$ \begin{align*} \v w \in \mathrm{dom}(f) = X \iff \exists v (t_{M,k}(\v w, v) = a) \iff \exists v(\v w,v)\in Y. \end{align*} $$

$Σ_1 \implies$ computably enumerable

Suppose $X\subseteq \mathbb{W}^{k}$ is $Σ_1$, i.e. there exists some computable $Y\subseteq \mathbb{W}^{k+1}$ such that $\v w\in X$ iff there exists $v$ such that $(\v w, v)\in Y$.

$\mathbb{W}^{k+1}\setminus Y$ is computable, so let $χ_{\mathbb{W}^{k+1}\setminus Y}: \mathbb{W}^{k+1} \to \set{a,ε}$ be its computable characteristic function. Its minimisation $h: \mathbb{W}^k \dashrightarrow \mathbb{W}$ has the property $$ \begin{align*} h(\v w) \downarrow \iff \exists v(χ_{\mathbb{W}^{k+1}\setminus Y}(\v w, v) = ε) \iff \exists v (\v w,v)\in Y \iff \v w\in X. \end{align*} $$ Thus $X = \mathrm{dom}(h)$ is computably enumerable.
