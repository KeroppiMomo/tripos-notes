Computability and $Δ_1$ are the same. So we only need to show for any computable language $X$, we have $X \leq_m L$.

By hypothesis, fix some $v_1\in L$ and $v_0\in \mathbb{W}\setminus L$. Define $$ \begin{align*} f(w) = \begin{cases} v_1 &\text{if }w\in X, \\ v_0 &\text{if }w\not\in X. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Since $X$ is computable, $f$ is a (computable) reduction of $X$ to $L$.
