Let $L\subseteq \mathbb{W}$ be a language. We say $L$ _satisfies the (regular) pumping lemma with pumping number $n$_ if for every word $w\in L$ with length $|w|\geq n$, there exists words $x,y,z\in \mathbb{W}$ such that

  • $w = xyz$;

  • $|y|>0$;

  • $|xy|\leq n$;

  • $xy^k z\in L$ for all $k=0,1,2,\ldots$.

For every regular language $L$, there exists some $n$ such that $L$ satisfies the regular pumping lemma with pumping number $n$.
