Let $L$ be a regular language, and take a deterministic automaton $D = (Σ, Q, δ, q_0, F)$ that accepts $L$.

We can assume WLOG that $q_0$ is not in the range of $δ$: let a new state $q_0^\ast$ and let $$ \begin{align*} δ'(q,a) = \begin{cases} q_0^\ast &\text{if } δ(q,a) = q_0, \\ δ(q,a) &\text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Then $(Σ, Q\cup \set{q_0^\ast}, δ', q_0, F)$ accepts the same language as $D$ and does not have $q_0$ in the range of $δ'$.

Define $$ \begin{align*} \overline{D} = (Σ, Q, δ, q_0, Q\setminus (F \cup \set{q_0})). \end{align*} $$ We claim that $\mathcal{L}(\overline{D}) = \mathbb{W}^+ \setminus \mathcal{L}(D)$.

If $w\in \mathcal{L}(\overline{D})$, then $\hat δ(q_0, w) \in Q\setminus (F \cup \set{q_0})$. Thus $w \neq ε$ (since $\hat δ(q_0, ε) = q_0$) and $\hat δ(q_0, w) \not\in F$.

If $w\neq ε$ and $\hat δ(q_0, w)\not\in F$, then as $q_0$ is not in the range of $δ$, we know $\hat δ(q_0, w) \neq q_0$ by $w\neq ε$. Thus $\hat δ(q_0, w) \in Q\setminus (F\cup \set{q_0})$.
