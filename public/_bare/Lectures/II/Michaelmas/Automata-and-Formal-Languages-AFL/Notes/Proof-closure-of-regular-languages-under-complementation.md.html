Let $L$ be a regular language, and take a deterministic automaton $D = (Q, Σ, δ, q_0, F)$ that accepts $L$.

Define $$ \begin{align*} \overline{D} = (Σ, Q, δ, q_0, Q\setminus F). \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} w\in \mathcal{L}(D) \iff \hat δ(q_0, w) \in F \iff w\not\in \mathcal{L}(\bar D) \end{align*} $$ as required.
