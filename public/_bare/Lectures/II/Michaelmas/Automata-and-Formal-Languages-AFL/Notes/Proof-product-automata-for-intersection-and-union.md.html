By induction, $\widehat{δ\times δ'}(w, (q,q')) = (\hat δ(w,q), \hat δ'(w,q'))$. Thus $$ \begin{align*} w\in \mathcal{L}(D\wedge D') &\iff \widehat{δ\times δ'}(w, (q_0, q_0')) \in F\wedge F' \\ &\iff (\hat δ(w,q_0), \hat δ'(w, q_0')) \in F \wedge F' \\ &\iff \hat δ(w,q_0) \in F \text{ and }\hat δ'(w, q_0') \in F' \\ &\iff w\in \mathcal{L}(D) \text{ and } w\in \mathcal{L}(D') \end{align*} $$ and similarly for $D\vee D'$.
