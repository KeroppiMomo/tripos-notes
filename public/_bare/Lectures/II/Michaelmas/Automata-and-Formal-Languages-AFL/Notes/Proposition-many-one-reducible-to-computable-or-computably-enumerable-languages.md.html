Let $L,L'\subseteq \mathbb{W}$. Suppose $L$${}\leq_m{}$$L'$.

  1. If $L'$ is computable, then so is $L$.

  2. If $L'$ is computably enumerable, then so is $L$.

Proof

  • Interpret $L\leq_m L'$ as "$L$ is at most as complicated as $L'$".

  • $\mathbf{K}$ and $\mathbb{W}\setminus \mathbf{K}$ are incomparable in $\leq_m$: otherwise $\mathbb{W}\setminus \mathbf{K}\equiv_m \mathbf{K}$ and in particular $\mathbb{W}\setminus \mathbf{K}\leq_m \mathbf{K}$, contradicting with $\mathbf{K}$ being $Σ_1$ but not $Π_1$.
