We need to check $[δ]$ is well-defined. For any $q,q'\in Q$ and $a\in Σ^\ast$, if a word $w\in \mathbb{W}$ distinguishes $δ(q,a)$ and $δ(q',a)$, then $aw$ distinguishes $q$ and $q'$. Thus $[q] = [q']$ implies $[δ(q,a)] = [δ(q',a)]$.

Also we should check $[q_0] \not\in [F]$. For any $q\in F$, the empty word $ε$ distinguishes $q_0$ and $q$, so $[q] \neq [q_0]$.
