Every deterministic automaton $D$ has a minimal automaton $I$, unique up to isomorphism. That is, there exists an irreducible automaton $I$ with $\mathcal{L}(D) = \mathcal{L}(I)$ and at most as many states as $D$. Moreover, if $I$ and $I'$ are irreducible automata, then $\mathcal{L}(I) = \mathcal{L}(I')$ iff they are isomorphic.
