For some $v\in \mathbb{W}$, pick some longer $w\in \mathbb{W}$ ($|v|<|w|$ so $v<w$). The initial segment $\op{pred}_<(w):= \sb{u\in \mathbb{W}}{u<w}$ contains $v$ and only finitely many words. Therefore we can assign a natural number $n$ to each $u$ so that $u$ is the $n$-th word in the shortlex ordering of $\op{pred}_<(w)$. But since $\op{pred}_<(w)$ is an initial segment of $(\mathbb{W}, <)$, $u$ is also the $n$-th word in $(\mathbb{W},<)$. Thus this assigns a natural number to every $v$.
