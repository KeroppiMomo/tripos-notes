Obtain a grammar $G'$ where all its rewrite rules are either $A \to a$ or $A \to B_1 B_2 \ldots B_k$ (where $A, B_1, \ldots, B_k\in N$, $a\in Σ$ and $k\geq 2$), by this and this.

If a rule of the form $A \to B_1 B_2 B_3 \ldots B_k$ with $k\geq 3$ exists, replace it with $A\to B_1 C$ and $C \to B_2 B_3 \ldots B_k$. This does not change the language, since $C$ only appears on the LHS of one rule. Repeat until the RHS has length 2.
