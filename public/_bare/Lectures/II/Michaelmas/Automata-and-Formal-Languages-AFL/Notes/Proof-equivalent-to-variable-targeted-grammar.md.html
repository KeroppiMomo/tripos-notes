Let $G = (Σ, V, P, S)$ be context-free. Use ideas from the proof for variable-based grammars: for each $a\in Σ$, introduce a new variable $X_a$. For $α\in Ω^\ast$, let $X(α)$ be the string $α$ which each occurrence of a letter is replaced by the corresponding new variable. Define $G' = (Σ, V', P', S)$ where $V' = V \cup \sb{X_a}{a\in Σ}$ and $$ \begin{align*} P' ={}& \sb{A \to a}{A \to a \in P} \\ &\cup \sb{A \to X(α)}{A \to α\in P, |α|\geq 2} \\ &\cup \sb{X_a\to a}{a\in Σ}. \end{align*} $$

$G'$ produces the same language as $G$ and is variable-targeted.
