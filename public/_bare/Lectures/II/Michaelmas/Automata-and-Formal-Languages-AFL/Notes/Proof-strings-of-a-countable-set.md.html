$X^\ast$ is infinite

Since $X$ is non-empty, let some $x\in X$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \N &\to X^\ast \\ n &\mapsto x^n \end{align*} $$ is an injection so $X$ is infinite.

$X^\ast$ is countable

Since $X$ is countable, let $π: \N \to X$ be a surjection. Let $p_0 = 2, p_1 = 3, p_2 = 5, \ldots$ be the prime numbers. For $k\in \N$ arbitrary, let $k = \prod_{i \in \N} p_i^{n_i}$ be its prime factorisation. The function $$ \begin{align*} p: \N &\to X^\ast \\ k &\mapsto (π(n_1), π(n_2), \ldots, π(n_{n_0})) \end{align*} $$ is a surjection: $n_0$ encodes the length of the string, and $n_1, \ldots, n_{n_0}$ are the elements of the string.
