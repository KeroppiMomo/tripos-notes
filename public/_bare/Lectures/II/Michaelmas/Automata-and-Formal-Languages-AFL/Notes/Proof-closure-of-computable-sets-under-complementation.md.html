Consider $$ \begin{align*} f: \mathbb{W} &\to \mathbb{W} \\ w &\mapsto \begin{cases} ε, &w\neq ε, \\ a, &w = ε, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ which is computable using case distinction:

  • if register 0 is empty, then

    • empty register 0, and

    • add $a$ to register 0.

  • Otherwise

    • empty register 0.

Note that $$ \begin{align*} χ_{\mathbb{W}^k \setminus X} = f \circ χ_X \end{align*} $$ is a concatenation of computable functions and thus computable.
