Let $G = (Σ, V, P, S)$ and $G'=(Σ, V', P', S')$ be a context-sensitive grammars with disjoint sets of variables.

The regular union grammar of $G$ and $G'$ $$ \begin{align*} H = (Σ, \quad V\cup V' \cup \set{T}, \quad P \cup P' \cup \sb{T \to α}{S\to α\in P} \cup \sb{T\toα}{S' \to α\in P'}, \quad T) \end{align*} $$ produces $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(H) = \mathcal{L}(G) \cup \mathcal{L}(G'). \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • This shows that the class of regular languages is closed under union.
