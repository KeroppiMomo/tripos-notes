Computably enumerable

By definition, $f_{w,1}(v) = f_{U,2}(w,v)$ is computable, so $\mathbf{K}_0 = \op{dom}(f_{U,2})$ is computably enumerable. Moreover $w\mapsto (w,w)$ can be performed by a register machine, so $f: w\mapsto f_{U,2}(w,w) = f_{w,1}(w)$ is computable and thus $\mathbf{K}=\op{dom}(f)$ is computably enumerable.

Not computable

Suppose either of them is, i.e. $χ_{\mathbf{K}}$ or $χ_{\mathbf{K}_0}$ is a computable function. Define $$ \begin{align*} f(w) = \begin{cases} \uparrow, &χ_{\mathbf{K}_0}(w,w) = χ_{\mathbf{K}}(w) = a, \\ a, &χ_{\mathbf{K}_0}(w,w) = χ_{\mathbf{K}}(w) = ε. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Since this is computable, let $d\in \mathbb{W}$ be such that $f = f_{d,1}$.

However $$ \begin{align*} f(d) \downarrow \iff χ_{\mathbf{K}}(d) = a \iff χ_{\mathbf{K}_0}(d,d) = a \iff f(d) \downarrow. \end{align*} $$ Contradiction!
