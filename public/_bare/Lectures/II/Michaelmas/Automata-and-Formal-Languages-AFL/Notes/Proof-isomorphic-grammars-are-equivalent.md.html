Let $f: Ω\to Ω'$ be an isomorphism between $G$ and $G'$.

Suppose $w\in \mathcal{L}(G)$, with a derivation $$ \begin{align*} S = σ_0 \xrightarrow{G}_1 σ_1 \xrightarrow{G}_1 σ_2 \xrightarrow{G}_1 \ldots\xrightarrow{G}_1 σ_n = w \in \mathbb{W}. \end{align*} $$ Apply $f$: $$ \begin{align*} S' = f(σ_0) \xrightarrow{G'}_1 f(σ_1) \xrightarrow{G'}_1 f(σ_2) \xrightarrow{G'}_1 \ldots\xrightarrow{G'}_1 f(σ_n) = w. \end{align*} $$ Property 4 implies this is a $G'$-derivation; property 2 implies it starts $f(S) = S'$; property 1 implies it derives $f(w) = w$. Thus $w\in \mathcal{L}(G')$.

Property 3 ensures $f\inv$ exists and is an isomorphism between $G'$ and $G$, so reverse inclusion holds.
