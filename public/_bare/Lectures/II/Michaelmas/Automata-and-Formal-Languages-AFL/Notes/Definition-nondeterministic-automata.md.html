A nondeterministic automaton is a tuple $N = (Σ, Q, δ, q_0, F)$ where

  • $Σ$ is a non-empty finite set (the alphabet);

  • $Q$ is a non-empty finite set (of states);

  • $δ: Q\times Σ\to \mathcal{P}(Q)$ (the transition function);

  • $q_0 \in Q$ (the start state); and

  • $F\subseteq Q\setminus \set{q_0}$ a subset (of accepting states).

Extend $δ$ to $\hat δ: Q\times \mathbb{W}\to \mathcal{P}(Q)$ by $$ \begin{align*} \hat δ(q,ε) = \set{q}, \qquad \hat δ(q, wa) = \bigcup \sb{δ(p,a)}{p\in \hat δ(q,w)}. \end{align*} $$

For every word $w=a_0 a_1 \ldots a_n\in \mathbb{W}$, the state set sequence is $$ \begin{align*} X_0 &= \set{q_0}, \qquad X_{i+1} = \bigcup \sb{δ(p,a_i)}{p\in X_i}. \end{align*} $$

The language accepted by $N$ is $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(N) = \sb{w\in \mathbb{W}}{\hat δ(q_0, w) \cap F \neq \emptyset}. \end{align*} $$
