Let $K$ be a simplicial complex and $K'$ be its barycentric subdivision.

Any function $V_{K'} \to V_K$ that sends $\hat σ\in V_{K'}$ to a vertex of $σ$ defines a simplicial approximation of the identity map $|K'| = |K| \to |K|$. Moreover, every simplicial approximation of the identity map is of this form.
