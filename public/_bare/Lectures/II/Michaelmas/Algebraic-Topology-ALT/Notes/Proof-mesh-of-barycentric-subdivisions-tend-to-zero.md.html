Let $\langle \hat τ, \hat σ\rangle \in K'$, so $τ<σ$ as simplices in $K$.

Let $σ = \langle a_0, a_1, \ldots, a_p\rangle$. Since $\hat τ \in σ$, estimate $$ \begin{align*} \|\hat τ - \hat σ\| \leq \max\{\|a_0 - \hat σ\|, \ldots, \|a_p - \hat σ\|\} \end{align*} $$ by convexity of $σ$.

Suppose $\|a_0 - \hat σ\|$ is maximal. Then $$ \begin{align*} \|\hat τ - \hat σ\| \leq{}& \|a_0 - \hat σ\| \\ ={}& \left\|a_0 - \frac{1}{p+1}\sum_{i=0}^{p} a_i\right\| \\ ={}& \left\|\frac{1}{p+1}\sum_{i=0}^{p} (a_0 - a_i)\right\| \\ \leq{}& \frac{1}{p+1} \sum_{i=1}^{p} \|a_0 - a_i\| \\ \leq{} & \frac{p}{p+1} μ(K) \\ \leq{}& \frac{n}{n+1} μ(K). \end{align*} $$
