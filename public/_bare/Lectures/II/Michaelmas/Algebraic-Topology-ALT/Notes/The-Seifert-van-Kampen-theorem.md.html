Theorem (Seifert-van Kampen) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a space, $A, B\subseteq X$ be subsets and $x_0\in A\cap B$. Inclusion maps induce homomorphisms between fundamental groups, and the universal property of amalgamated free product gives a group homomorphism $$ \begin{align*} Φ: π_1(A, x_0) *_{π_1(A\cap B, x_0)} π_1(B, x_0) \to π_1(X, x_0). \end{align*} $$

If

  1. $A,B$ are open subsets;

  2. $A\cup B = X$; and

  3. $A\cap B$ is path-connected,

then $Φ$ is an isomorphism.

Proof

Definition (cell complex) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a topological space and $f: S^{n-1}\to X$ be a map.

The space obtained by attaching an $n$-cell to $X$ along $f$ is the quotient space $$ \begin{align*} X \cup_f D^n := (X\sqcup D^n)/{\sim} \end{align*} $$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation identifying $x \sim f(x)$ for all $x\in S^{n-1} = \partial D^n$, and an subset of $X \sqcup D^n$ is open iff its intersections with $X$ and with $D^n$ are open.

A finite cell complex is a space obtained with finitely many steps as follows:

  • Step 0: start with a finite set $X^0$ of discrete points.

  • Step $i\geq 1$: attach finitely many $i$-cells to $X^{i-1}$ to form $X^i$.

Taking $X = X^n$, we say $n$ is the dimension of $X$. $X^i$ is called the $i$-skeleton of $X$.

Lemma (fundamental group when attaching cell) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a path-connected space and $f: (S^{n-1}, *)\to (X, x_0)$ be a based map. Attach an $n$-cell to $X$ along $f$ to form $Y$. Let $i_\ast: π_1(X, x_0)\to π_1(Y, x_0)$ be the homomorphism induced by inclusion $i: X \hookrightarrow Y$.

  1. If $n\geq 3$, then $i_\ast$ is an isomorphism.

  2. If $n=2$, then $i_\ast$ is a surjection with $\ker i_\ast = \langle \langle [f]\rangle\rangle$, the smallest normal subgroup containing $[f]\in π_1(X,x_0)$. In particular, $π_1(Y, x_0) = π_1(X, x_0)/\langle \langle [f]\rangle\rangle$.

Proof

Therefore any finitely presented group arises as a fundamental group of some two-dimensional finite cell complex. (Proof)

Definition (neighbourhood deformation retract) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a space.

A subset $A\subseteq X$ is called a neighbourhood deformation retract if there is an open neighbourhood $A\subseteq U\subseteq X$ and a deformation retraction of $U$ onto $A$.

Theorem (Seifert-van Kampen for closed cover) open_in_new

Let $X$ be a space.

If $A,B\subseteq X$ are closed subsets such that $A\cup B = X$ and $A\cap B$ is path-connected and a neighbourhood deformation retract in both $A$ and $B$, then for $x_0\in A\cap B$, the homomorphism $$ \begin{align*} Φ: π_1(A, x_0) *_{π_1(A\cap B, x_0)} π_1(B, x_0) \to π_1(X, x_0) \end{align*} $$ given by inclusion maps is a isomorphism.

Proof

Theorem (classification of compact connected surfaces) open_in_new

Any compact connected surface is homeomorphic to one of the following:

  1. the sphere $S^2$;

  2. $Σ_g$, the connect sum of $g$ tori $S^1 \times S^1$, for $g\geq 1$;

  3. $S_k$, the connect sum of $k$ real projective planes $\mathbb{RP}^2 = S^2/\{\pm 1\}$, for $k\geq 1$.

Theorem (fundamental group of compact connected surfaces) open_in_new

The fundamental group of the compact connected surfaces are

  1. $π_1(S^2, *) \isom 1$;

  2. $π_1(Σ_g, *)\isom{}$$\langle a_1, b_1, \ldots, a_g, b_g\mid \prod_{i=1}^{g} a_i b_i a_i\inv b_i\inv\rangle$;

  3. $π_1(S_k, *)\isom \langle a_1, \ldots, a_k\mid \prod_{i=1}^{k} a_i^2\rangle$.
