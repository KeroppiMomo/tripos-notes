Let $S$ be a set (the alphabet) and suppose $S\inv = \sb{s\inv}{s\in S}$ is disjoint from $S$.

A word in $S$ is a (possibly empty) finite sequence $(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ of elements in $S\cup S\inv$.

An elementary reduction of a word removes a subword $(s, s\inv)$ or $(s\inv, s)$. A word is reduced if it does not admit any elementary reductions.

The free group $F(S)$ on the alphabet $S$ is the set of reduced words in $S$, with the operation being concatenation and then applying elementary reduction until the word is reduced.

Define a function $ι: S \to F(S)$ sending $s \mapsto (s)$.
