Let $a: V_{K'} \to V_K$ be such that $a(\hat σ)$ is a vertex of $σ\in K$. To show that $\op{St}_{K'}(\hat σ) \subseteq \op{St}_{K}(a(\hat σ))$, suppose $\hat σ$ is a vertex of a simplex $τ = \langle \hat σ_1, \ldots, \hat σ_p\rangle\in K'$ where $σ_1 < \ldots < σ_p$, say $\hat σ = \hat σ_i$. Note that $τ \subseteq σ_p$ and $a(\hat σ)$ is a vertex of $σ_i$ and thus of $σ_p$. Hence $\mathring τ \subseteq \mathring σ_p \subseteq \op{St}_K(a(\hat σ))$.

Conversely suppose $g: V_{K'}\to V_K$ is a simplicial approximation to the identity map. For any $\hat σ\in V_{K'}$, we have $\hat σ \in \op{St}_{K'}(\hat σ) \subseteq \op{St}_K(g(\hat σ))$, meaning that there is a simplex with vertex $g(\hat σ)$ whose interior contains $\hat σ$. But $σ$ is the unique simplex whose interior contains $\hat σ$, so $g(\hat σ)$ is a vertex of $σ$.
