We need to show $$ \begin{align*} \tilde d_n: O_n(K) &\to O_{n-1}(K) \\ [v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_n] &\mapsto \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_n] \end{align*} $$ sends $T_n(K)$ to $T_{n-1}(K)$.

Since permutations are generated by adjacent transpositions, $T_n(K)$ is generated by elements of the form $[v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_j, v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] + [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, {\color{red}v_{j+1}, v_j}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n]$. We compute $$ \begin{align*} \tilde d_n([v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, {\color{red}v_{j+1}, v_{j}}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n]) ={}& \sum_{i=0}^{j-1} (-1)^i \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_{j-1}, {\color{red}v_{j+1}, v_j}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &+ (-1)^{j} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_j, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &+ (-1)^{j+1} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &+ \sum_{i=j+2}^{n} (-1)^{i}\cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, {\color{red}v_{j+1}, v_j}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_n] \\ \equiv{}& -\sum_{i=0}^{j-1} (-1)^{i} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_{j}, v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &- (-1)^{j+1} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_j, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &- (-1)^{j} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &- \sum_{i=j+2}^{n} (-1)^{i} \cdot [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_{j}, v_{j+1}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_n] \\ ={}& -\tilde d_n([v_0, \ldots, v_n]) \end{align*} $$ mod $T_{n-1}(K)$.
