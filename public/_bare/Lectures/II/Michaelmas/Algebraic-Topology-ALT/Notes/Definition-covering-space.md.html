Let $X$ be a topological space.

A covering space of $X$ is a pair $(\tilde X, p)$ of a space $\tilde X$ and a map $p: \tilde X \to X$ satisfying that for each $x\in X$, there exists an open neighbourhood $U\subseteq X$ of $x$ which is evenly covered by $p$; i.e. $p\inv(U) = \bigsqcup_{α\in I} V_α$ is a disjoint union of open sets $\set{V_α}_{α\in I}$ in $Y$ such that each $p|_{V_{α}}: V_α \to U$ is a homeomorphism.

We call $p$ a covering map.

  • A homeomorphism is a covering map.
