Proof

Any group $G$ has a canonical presentation: the identity $\id_G: G\to G$ induces a surjective homomorphism $\varphi : F(G) \to G$. This further induces $ψ: \langle G\mid \ker \varphi\rangle \to G$, which is an isomorphism by the first isomorphism theorem.
