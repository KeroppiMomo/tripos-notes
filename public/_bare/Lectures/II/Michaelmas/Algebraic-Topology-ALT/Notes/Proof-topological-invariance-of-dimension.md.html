If $f: \R^n\to \R^m$ is a homeomorphism, then it restricts to a homeomorphism $\R^n\setminus \set{0} \isom \R^n\setminus \set{x_0} \to \R^m \setminus \set{f(x_0)} \isom \R^m\setminus \set{0}$.

Now $\R^n \setminus \set{0}$ deformation retracts to $S^{n-1}$ via $$ \begin{align*} (x,t) \mapsto tx + (1-t) \frac{x}{\|x\|} \end{align*} $$ hence is homotopic equivalent to it. Same for $\R^m\setminus \set{0}$. Thus $S^{n-1}$ is homotopic equivalent to $S^{m-1}$.

This implies the homology of $S^{n-1}$ and $S^{m-1}$ are isomorphic, which occurs only if $n=m$.
