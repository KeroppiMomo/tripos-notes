We compute $$ \begin{align*} d_{n-1} \circ d_n([[v_0, \ldots, v_n]]) ={}& d_{n-1}\biggl(\sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i [v_0, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_n]\biggr) \\ ={}& \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i \biggl(\sum_{j=0}^{i-1} (-1)^j [v_0, \ldots, v_{j-1}, v_{j+1}, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_n] \\ &\qquad \qquad \quad + \sum_{j=i}^{n-1} (-1)^j[v_0, \ldots, v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, \ldots, v_{j}, v_{j+2}, \ldots, v_n]\biggr). \end{align*} $$

The term $[v_0, \ldots, v_{a-1}, v_{a+1}, \ldots, v_{b-1}, v_{b+1}, \ldots, v_n]$ appears twice: once with coefficient $(-1)^{a}(-1)^b$ when $i=b$ and $j=a$, and another time with coefficient $(-1)^a (-1)^{b-1}$ when $i=a$ and $j=b-1$. These cancel out, so $d_{n-1}\circ d_n = 0$.
