A chain map $f_\bullet: C_\bullet \to D_\bullet$ induces a homomorphism $$ \begin{align*} f_\ast: H_n(C_\bullet) &\to H_n(D_\bullet) \\ [x] &\mapsto [f_n(x)]. \end{align*} $$

Moreover, if $f_\bullet$ and $g_\bullet$ are chain homotopic, then $f_\ast = g_\ast$.

If $f_\bullet$ is a chain homotopy equivalence, then $f_\ast$ is an isomorphism.
