Suppose a space $X$ is path-connected and locally path-connected. Then $X$ is semi-locally simply connected iff there exists a universal cover $p: \tilde X \to X$.

One such universal cover is given by, fixing some $x_0\in X$, $$ \begin{align*} \tilde X ={}& \set{\text{homotopy classes of paths in $X$ starting at $x_0$}}, \\ p([γ]) ={}& γ(1). \end{align*} $$

Proof

Note $π_1(X,x_0)$ acts on $\tilde X$ by homeomorphism $γ\bullet [α] = [γ \bullet α]$, and $p$ induces a homeomorphism $π_1(X,x_0) \backslash \tilde X\to X$. This action is free and properly discontinuous (Proof).
