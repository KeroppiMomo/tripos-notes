For any based map $f: (X, x_0) \to (Y, y_0)$, there is a well-defined group homomorphism on fundamental groups $$ \begin{align*} f_\ast \equiv π_1(f) : π_1(X, x_0) &\to π_1(Y, y_0) \\ [γ] &\mapsto [f\circ γ]. \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • If $f$ is based homotopic to $f'$, then $π_1(f) = π_1(f')$.

  • $π_1$ preserves composition: $π_1(g\circ f) = π_1(g)\circ π_1(f)$ for based maps $f: (X,x_0)\to (Y,y_0), g: (Y,y_0)\to (Z,z_0)$.

  • $π_1(\id_X) = \id_{π_1(X,x_0)}$.
