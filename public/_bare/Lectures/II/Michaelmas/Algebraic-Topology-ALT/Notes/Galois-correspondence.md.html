Definition (locally path-connected) open_in_new

A topological space $X$ is called locally path-connected if for every point $x\in X$ and neighbourhood $U$ of $x$, there exists a path-connected neighbourhood $V\subseteq U$ of $x$.

Definition (semi-locally simply connected) open_in_new

A topological space $X$ is called semi-locally simply connected if for all points $x_0\in X$, there exists a neighbourhood $U\subseteq X$ of $x_0$ such that any loop in $U$ based at $x_0$ is contractible (i.e. homotopic to $c_{x_0}$ as paths) in $X$. In other words, the homeomorphism $i_\ast$${}: π_1(U, x_0)\to π_1(X, x_0)$ induced by inclusion $i: U \to X$ is trivial.
