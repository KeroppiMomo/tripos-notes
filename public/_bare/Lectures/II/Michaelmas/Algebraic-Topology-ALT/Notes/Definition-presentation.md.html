Let $S$ be an alphabet and $R \subseteq{}$$F(S)$ be a subset.

The group with generators $S$ and relations $R$ is defined as the quotient $$ \begin{align*} \langle S \mid R\rangle = F(S)\; /\; \langle \langle R\rangle\rangle \end{align*} $$ where $\langle \langle R\rangle\rangle$ is the largest normal subgroup containing $R$: $$ \begin{align*} \langle \langle R\rangle\rangle = \sb{\prod_{i=1}^{n} g_i\, r_i^{ε_i}\, g_i\inv}{g_i\in F(S), \; r_i\in R, \; ε_i \in \set{\pm 1}}. \end{align*} $$ The data $(S,R)$ is called a presentation of the group.

If $S$ and $R$ are finite, we call it a finite presentation.
