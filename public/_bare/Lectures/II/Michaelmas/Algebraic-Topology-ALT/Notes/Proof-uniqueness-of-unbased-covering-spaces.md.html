If $h$ exists, pick any $\tilde x_1\in \tilde X_1$ and let $\tilde x_2 = h(\tilde x_1)\in \tilde X_2$ and $x_0 = p_1(\tilde x_1)$. Everything is then based and so $(p_1)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_1, \tilde x_1)) = (p_2)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_2, \tilde x_2))$.

Conversely suppose $$ \begin{align*} [γ]\inv \cdot (p_1)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_1, \tilde x_1)) \cdot [γ] = (p_2)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_2, \tilde x_2)). \end{align*} $$ for some $[γ]\in π_1(X, x_0)$. The lift $\tilde γ$ of $γ$ to $\tilde X_1$ starting at $\tilde x_1$ ends at some $\tilde x_1'$, and we get $$ \begin{align*} (p_2)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_2, \tilde x_2)) ={}& [γ]\inv \cdot (p_1)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_1, \tilde x_1)) \cdot [γ] \\ ={}& (p_1)_\ast([\tilde γ]\inv\cdot π_1(\tilde X_1, \tilde x_1)\cdot [\tilde γ]) \\ ={}& (p_1)_\ast(π_1(\tilde X_1, \tilde x_1')). \end{align*} $$

Then the previous proposition gives a homeomorphism.
