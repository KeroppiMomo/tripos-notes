$p$ is a covering map

Fix $y\in Y$. Let $U\subseteq Y$ be an open neighbourhood of $y$ which is evenly covered, so $p\inv(U) = \bigsqcup_{α\in I} V_α$.

If $y\in S$, then $\tilde f_0(y) = \tilde f_1(y)$ lies in one of the open sets, say $V_β$. Then $N = \tilde f_0\inv(V_β) \cap \tilde f_1\inv(V_β)$ is a neighbourhood of $y$, open by continuity. Since $p|_{V_β} \circ \tilde f_{0}|_N = f|_N = p|_{V_β} \circ \tilde f_1|_N$ and $p|_{V_β}$ is a homeomorphism, we have $\tilde f_0|_N = \tilde f_1|_N$. Thus $y\in N\subseteq S$ and so $S$ is open.

If $y\in Y\setminus S$, then $\tilde f_0(y) \neq \tilde f_1(y)$ lie in say $V_β$ and $V_γ$ respectively. If $β = γ$, then $p|_{V_β}$ being a homeomorphism would imply $f(y) = p|_{V_β}\circ \tilde f_0(y) \neq p|_{V_β}\circ \tilde f_1(y) = f(y)$, which is a contradiction. Hence $β\neq γ$ and $V_β \cap V_γ = \emptyset$. Thus $\tilde f_0$ and $\tilde f_1$ differ everywhere on the open neighbourhood $N = \tilde f_0\inv(V_β) \cap \tilde f_1\inv(V_γ)$ of $y$. This gives $y\in N\subseteq Y\setminus S$.

$\tilde X$ is Hausdorff and $p$ is a local homeomorphism

To show $S$ is open, let $y\in S$, so $\tilde f_0(y) = \tilde f_1(y) =: \tilde x$. There is an open neighbourhood $V\subseteq \tilde X$ of $\tilde x$ such that $p|_{V}$ is a homeomorphism. Then $N = \tilde f_0\inv(V) \cap \tilde f_1\inv(V)$ is a neighbourhood of $y$, open by continuity. Since $p|_{V} \circ \tilde f_0|_N = f|_N = p|_{V_β} \circ \tilde f_1|_N$ and $p|_{V}$ is a homeomorphism, we have $\tilde f_0|_N = \tilde f_1|_N$. Thus $y\in N \subseteq S$.

To show $S$ is closed, note $F := (\tilde f_0, \tilde f_1): Y\to \tilde X\times \tilde X$ is continuous, and $S = F\inv(Δ_{\tilde X})$ where $Δ_{\tilde X}\subseteq \tilde X\times \tilde X$ is the diagonal. Since $\tilde X$ is Hausdorff, $Δ_\tilde X$ is closed, so $S$ is closed by continuity.
