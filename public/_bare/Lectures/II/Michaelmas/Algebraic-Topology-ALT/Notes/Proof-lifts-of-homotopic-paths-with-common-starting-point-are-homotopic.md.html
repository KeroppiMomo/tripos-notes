  • Note $H(0,\cdot)$ and $H(1,\cdot )$ are constant paths, so by uniqueness so are their lifts $\tilde H(0,\cdot)$ and $\tilde H(1,\cdot)$.

  • Also $\tilde H(\cdot,1)$ is a lift of $\tilde γ$ starting at $\tilde H(0,1) = \tilde H(0,0) = \tilde x_0$, so it equals $\tilde γ$.
