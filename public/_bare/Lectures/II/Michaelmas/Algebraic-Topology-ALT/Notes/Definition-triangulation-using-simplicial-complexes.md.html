Let $X$ be a topological space.

A triangulation of $X$ is a homeomorphism $h: |K|\to X$ for some simplicial complex $K$. If such a triangulation exists, we say $X$ is triangulable.

The $n$-th homology group of $X$ is $H_n(X) := H_n(K)$, which is defined up to isomorphism.

A map $X\to Y$ between triangulable spaces induces a homomorphism $f_\ast: H_n(X)\to H_n(Y)$.

If $f: X\to Y$ is a homotopy equivalence, then $f_\ast: H_n(X)\to H_n(Y)$ is an isomorphism.
