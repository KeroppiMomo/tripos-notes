Let $p(z) = z^n + a_1 z^{n-1} + \ldots + a_n$ be a monic polynomial having no roots.

Define, for $t\in [0,1]$ $$ \begin{align*} p_t(z) = z^n + t\cdot (a_1 z^{n-1} + \ldots + a_n). \end{align*} $$

Fix some $r>\max\set{1, |a_1| + \ldots + |a_n|}$. Then on the circle $|z| = r$, the triangle inequality gives $$ \begin{align*} |p_t(z)| &\geq |z|^n - t \cdot (|a_1||z|^{n-1} + \ldots + |a_n|) \\ &\geq r^n - (|a_1| + \ldots + |a_n|) r^{n-1} \\ &> 0 \end{align*} $$ so $p_t$ has no roots on $|z| = r$.

Consider the homotopy of loops in $S^1$ $$ \begin{align*} F_r: [0,1] \times [0,1] &\to S^1 \\ (s,t) &\mapsto \frac{p_t(re^{2πis}) / p_t(r)}{|p_t(re^{2πis}) / p_t(r)|}, \end{align*} $$ which is well-defined and continuous as $p_t(re^{2πis})$ is never zero. At $t=0$, the loop is $s \mapsto e^{2πins}$, which represents $n \in π_1(S^1, 1)$. At $t=1$, the loop is $$ \begin{align*} f_r(s) = F_r(s, 1) = \frac{p(re^{2πis}) / p(r)}{|p(re^{2πis}) / p(r)|}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $[f_r] = n\in π_1(S^1, 1)$.

However, consider another homotopy of loops in $S^1$ $$ \begin{align*} H: [0, 1] \times [0,1] &\to S^1 \\ (s,t) &\mapsto f_t(s) \end{align*} $$ which is well-defined and continuous as $p$ has no roots. This shows that $[f_r] = [f_0] = [c_1] = 0 \in π_1(S^1,1)$.

Thus $n = 0$ and $p$ is constant.
