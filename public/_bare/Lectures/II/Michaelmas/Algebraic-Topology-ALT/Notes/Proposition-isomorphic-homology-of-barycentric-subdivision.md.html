Let $K$ be a simplicial complex and $K'$ be its barycentric subdivision.

Any simplicial approximation $K' \to K$ to the identity induces an isomorphism $ν_K:{}$$H_n$$(K') \to H_n(K)$ for all $n$. Moreover this isomorphism does not depend on the simplicial approximation.

Furthermore, the maps $$ \begin{align*} s_0([[v_0]]) ={}& [[\widehat{\langle v_0\rangle}]], \\ s_{n}(σ) ={}& [[\hat σ, s_{n-1} \circ d_n(σ)]], \\ \end{align*} $$ (where $[[\hat σ, -]]$ means expand out $-$ to obtain a linear combination of ordered simplices) define a chain map $s_\bullet: C_\bullet(K) \to C_\bullet(K')$ and induces $s_\ast = ν_K\inv$.

Proof

Write $ν_{K,r}: H_n(K^{(r)}) \to H_n(K)$ for the isomorphism $ν_K \circ ν_{K'} \circ \ldots \circ ν_{K^{(r-1)}}$.
