Let $p: \tilde X \to X$ be a covering space.

If there exists a path $γ: x_0 \rightsquigarrow x_1$ on $X$, then it induces a bijection $$ \begin{align*} γ_\ast : p\inv(x_0) &\to p\inv(x_1) \\ y_0 &\mapsto \tilde γ_{y_0}(1) \end{align*} $$ where $\tilde γ_{y_0}$ is the unique lift of $γ$ starting at $y_0$.

Proof

If $γ \simeq γ'$ as paths, then $\tilde γ_{y_0} = \widetilde{(γ')}_{y_0}$ as paths so $γ_\ast = (γ')_\ast$.
