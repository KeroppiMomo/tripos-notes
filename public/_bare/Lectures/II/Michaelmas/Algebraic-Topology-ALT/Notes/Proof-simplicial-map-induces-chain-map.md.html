On the level of $O_n$, the homomorphism $$ \begin{align*} O_n(K) &\to O_n(L) \\ [a_0, \ldots, a_n] &\mapsto [f(a_0), \ldots, f(a_n)] \end{align*} $$ sends $T_n(K)$ to $T_n(L)$, so $f_n: O_n(K)/T_n(K)\to O_n(L)/T_n(L)$ is well-defined.

Now we check $$ \begin{align*} f_{n-1}(d_n([[a_0, \ldots, a_n]])) ={}& f_{n-1}\biggl(\sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i [[a_0, \ldots, a_{i-1}, a_{i+1}, \ldots, a_n]]\biggr) \\ ={}& \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i [[f(a_0), \ldots, f(a_{i-1}), f(a_{i+1}), \ldots, f(a_n)]] \\ ={}& d_n(f_n([[a_0, \ldots, a_n]])). \end{align*} $$
