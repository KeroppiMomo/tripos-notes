By the homotopy lifting lemma, the existence of $\tilde γ_{y_0}$ is guaranteed, so $γ_\ast$ is well-defined.

The inverse path $γ\inv$ defines a function $(γ\inv)_\ast: p\inv(x_1) \to p\inv(x_0)$, which we will show is the inverse of $γ_\ast$. Suppose $γ_\ast(y_0) = y_1$, which means a lift $\tilde γ_{y_0}$ of $γ$ that starts at $y_0$ and ends at $y_1$. Its inverse $\tilde γ_{y_0}\inv$ is then a lift of $γ\inv$ starting at $y_1$ and ending at $y_0$, so equals $\widetilde{(γ\inv)}_{y_1}$. Hence $(γ\inv)_\ast(y_1) = y_0$.
