Let $K$ and $L$ be simplicial complexes.

For any continuous map $f: |K| \to |L|$, there exists a simplicial approximation $g:{}$$K^{(r)}$${}\to L$ for some $r$.

Moreover, if $f|_{|N|}: |N|\to |L|$ is already a simplicial map on a subcomplex $N \subseteq K$, then $g$ can be taken to agree with $f$ on $V_N$.
