Let $L$ be the simplicial complex given by $Δ^n$ and all its faces, and $K$ be the simplicial $(n-1)$-sphere.

For $i\geq n+1$, both $C_i(K)$ and $C_i(L)$ are trivial as $K$ and $L$ have dimensions at most $n$.

For $i\leq n-1$, $K$ is the $(n-1)$-skeleton of $L$ so $C_i(K) = C_i(L)$.

For $i = n$, we see that $C_n(L) = \Z\set{[[e_1, \ldots, e_{n+1}]]}$ and $C_n(K) = 0$.

It is clear that $H_i(K)=0$ for $i\geq n$. Moreover since the two chain complexes agree for $i\leq n-1$, we have $H_i(K) = H_i(L)$ for $i\leq n-2$.

Since $0 = H_n(L) = \ker d_n^L$, $d_n^L$ is injective, so $\im d_n^L \isom C_n(L) \isom \Z$. Furthermore $0 = H_{n-1}(L) = \ker d_{n-1}^L / \im d_n^L$ so $\ker d_{n-1}^L = \im d_n^L$. This implies $H_{n-1}(K) = \ker d_{n-1}^K = \ker d_{n-1}^L \isom \Z$.
