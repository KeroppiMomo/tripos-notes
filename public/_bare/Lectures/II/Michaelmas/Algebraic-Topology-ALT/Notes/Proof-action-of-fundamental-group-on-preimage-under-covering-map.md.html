Orbit

$y_1 = γ_\ast(y_0)$ $\iff$ exists a path $\tilde γ_{y_0}$ on $\tilde X$ from $y_0$ to $y_1$ $\iff$ $y_0$ and $y_1$ are in the same path component.

Transitive action

If $\tilde X$ is path-connected, then any two $y_0, y_1\in p\inv(x_0)$ have a path $γ: [0,1]\to \tilde X$ between them. Then $γ$ is the lift of $p\circ γ\in π_1(X, x_0)$ starting at $y_0$, so $y_0 \bullet [p\circ γ] = y_1$.

Conversely suppose $π_1(X,x_0)$ acts transitively on $p\inv(x_0)$, and fix some $y_0,z_0\in \tilde X$. By path-connectedness of $X$, let $γ_y$ be a path on $X$ from $p(y_0)$ to $x_0$, and $γ_z$ be a path on $X$ from $p(z_0)$ to $x_0$. Find their lifts $\tilde γ_y$ and $\tilde γ_z$ starting from $y_0$ and $z_0$, and they end at some $y_1,z_1\in π\inv(x_0)$ respectively. As the group action is transitive, there is some $[γ]\in π_1(X,x_0)$ such that $y_1\bullet [γ] = z_1$, which means a lift $\tilde γ$ of $γ$ starts at $y_1$ and ends at $z_1$. Therefore $\tilde γ_y \cdot \tilde γ \cdot \tilde γ_z\inv$ is a path on $\tilde X$ from $y_0$ to $z_0$.

Stabiliser

Suppose $y_0 \bullet [γ] = y_0$, meaning that the lift $\tilde γ$ of $γ$ starting at $y_0$ ends at $y_0$. Hence $[\tilde γ] \in π_1(\tilde X, y_0)$ and $p_\ast([\tilde γ]) = [p\circ \tilde γ] = [γ]$.

Conversely for any $[\tilde γ]\in π_1(\tilde X, y_0)$, the lift of $γ = p\circ \tilde γ$ starting at $y_0$ is $\tilde γ$, which ends at $y_0$. So $y_0 \bullet [γ] = y_0$.
