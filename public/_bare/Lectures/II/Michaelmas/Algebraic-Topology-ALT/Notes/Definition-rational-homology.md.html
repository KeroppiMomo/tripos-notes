Let $K$ be a simplicial complex.

Let $O_n(K; \Q)$ be the $\Q$-vector space (i.e. freely generated $\Q$-module) with a basis given by symbols $\sb{[v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_n]}{\{v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_n\}\text{ spans a simplex in }K}$. Let $T_n(K; \Q) \leq O_n(K; \Q)$ be the subspace generated by $[v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_n] - \sign(σ) \, [v_{σ(0)}, v_{σ(1)}, \ldots, v_{σ(n)}]$ for permutations $σ$.

Define the group of rational $n$-chains $C_n(K; \Q) = O_n(K; \Q) / T_n(K; \Q)$.

Define the boundary map $d_n: C_n(K; \Q) \to C_{n-1}(K; \Q)$ as before, and the $n$-th rational homology as $$ \begin{align*} H_n(K; \Q) := \frac{\ker d_n}{\im d_{n+1}}. \end{align*} $$

Previous results on ordinary homology still hold for rational homology.
