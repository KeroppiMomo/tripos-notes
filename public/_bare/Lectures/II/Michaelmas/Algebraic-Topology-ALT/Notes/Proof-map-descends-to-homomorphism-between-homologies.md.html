$f_\ast$ does not depend on $s$ or $r$

Suppose $s: K^{(r)} \to L$ and $t: K^{(q)} \to L$ are simplicial approximation to $f$, WLOG assuming $r\geq q$. Choose a simplicial approximation $a: K^{(q)} \to K^{(r)}$ to the identity map. Then $t$ and $s \circ a: K^{(q)}\to L$ are both simplicial approximation to $f$, and so are contiguous and induces the same homomorphism $t_\ast = (s \circ a)_\ast : H_n(K^{(q)}) \to H_n(L)$.

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} t_\ast \circ ν_{K,q}\inv = s_\ast \circ a_\ast \circ ν_{K,q}\inv = s_\ast \circ ν_{K,r}\inv. \end{align*} $$

Composition

Let $t: L^{(q)} \to M$ be a simplicial approximation to $g$, and $s: K^{(r)} \to L^{(q)}$ be a simplicial approximation to $f$. Then $t \circ s$ is a simplicial approximation to $g \circ f$, so $$ \begin{align*} (g \circ f)_\ast ={}& (t \circ s)_\ast \circ ν_{K,r}\inv \\ ={}& t_\ast \circ s_\ast \circ ν_{K,r}\inv \\ ={}& (t_\ast \circ ν_{L,q}\inv) \circ (ν_{L,q} \circ s_\ast \circ ν_{K,r}\inv). \end{align*} $$

Homeomorphism

Follows from $(f\inv)_\ast = (f_\ast)\inv$ being continuous.

Homotopic maps

Let $f,g: |K|\to |L|$ be continuous maps.

Existence of $ε>0$ such that if $|f(x) - g(x)| < ε$ for all $x\in |K|$, then $f_\ast = g_\ast$

$\sb{\op{St}_L(w)}{w\in V_L}$ is an open cover of $|L|$, which is compact. Take $2ε$ to be the Lesbegue number.

Furthermore $\sb{f\inv(B_ε(y))}{y\in |L|}$ is an open cover of the compact set $|K|$, so there exists some $δ>0$ such that for all $x\in |K|$, there is some $y\in |L|$ such that $f(B_δ(x)) \subseteq B_ε(y)$. This implies $g(B_{δ}(x)) \subseteq B_{2ε}(y)$, so there is some $w\in V_L$ such that $f(B_{δ}(x))$ and $g(B_{δ}(x))$ both lie in $\op{St}_L(w)$.

Now we'll construct one simplicial approximation $s: K^{(r)} \to L$ to both $f$ and $g$: take $r$ sufficiently large such that $μ(K^{(r)}) < δ/2$. For each $v\in V_{K^{(r)}}$, $\op{St}_{K^{(r)}}(v)$ lies within a ball of radius $δ$, so $f(\op{St}_{K^{(r)}}(v))$ and $g(\op{St}_{K^{(r)}}(v))$ lies within some $\op{St}_L(w)$. Setting $s(v) = w$ works. Thus $f_\ast = s_\ast \circ ν_{K,r}\inv = g_\ast$.

Homotopy

Let $H: |K|\times [0,1] \to |L|$ be a homotopy from $f$ to $g$. Since $|K| \times [0,1]$ is compact, $H$ is uniformly continuous, so there exists some $δ>0$ such that if $|s-t|<δ$, then $|H(x,s) - H(x,t)| < ε$.

Thus choosing $0 = t_0 < t_1 < \ldots < t_k = 1$ where $t_{i+1} - t_i < δ$ for all $i$ and letting $f_i(x) = H(x, t_i)$, we have that $|f_{i+1} - f_i| < ε$, so by the definition of $ε$, $f_\ast = (f_0)_\ast = (f_1)_\ast = \ldots = (f_k)_\ast = g_\ast$.
