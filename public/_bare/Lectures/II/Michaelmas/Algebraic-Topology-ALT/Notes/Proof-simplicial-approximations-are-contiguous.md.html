Suppose $f,g: K\to L$ are simplicial approximations to a continuous map $F: |K|\to |L|$, and let $σ = \langle a_0, \ldots, a_n\rangle\in K$.

Following the proof here, take any $x\in \mathring σ$, and $τ\in L$ be the unique simplex such that $F(x) \in \mathring τ$. Then $τ$ has vertices $f(a_0), \ldots, f(a_n)$ and $g(a_0), \ldots, g(a_n)$, so $f(σ)$ and $g(σ)$ are faces of $τ$.
