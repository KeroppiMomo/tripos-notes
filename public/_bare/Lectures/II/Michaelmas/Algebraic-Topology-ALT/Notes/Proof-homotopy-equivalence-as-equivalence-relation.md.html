$X\simeq X$

Take $f=g=\id_X$ and all homotopies as constant.

$X\simeq Y \implies Y\simeq X$

If $f:X\to Y$ is a homotopy equivalence with homotopy inverse $g: Y\to X$, then $g$ is also a homotopy equivalence with homotopy inverse $f$.

$X\simeq Y\simeq Z\implies X\simeq Z$

Let $f: X\to Y, f': Y\to Z$ be homotopy equivalences with homotopy inverses $g: Y\to X, g': Z\to Y$ respectively. This means $$ \begin{align*} f\circ g\simeq \id_Y, \quad f'\circ g'\simeq \id_Z, \quad g\circ f \simeq \id_X, \quad g'\circ f' \simeq \id_Y. \end{align*} $$ Using how composition preserves homotopy, we obtain $$ \begin{gather*} f'\circ f\circ g\circ g' \simeq f'\circ \id_Y \circ g' = f'\circ g' \simeq \id_Z, \\ g \circ g' \circ f' \circ f \simeq g\circ \id_Y \circ f \simeq g\circ f \simeq \id_X. \end{gather*} $$ Hence $f'\circ f$ is a homotopy equivalence with homotopy inverse $g\circ g'$.
