Follows the usual Cauchy-Schwarz.

WLOG replace $X$ with $X - \expect[X]$ and $Y$ with $Y - \expect[Y]$ to assume $\expect[X] = 0$ and $\expect[Y] = 0$.

For any $n\times m$ matrix $A$, $(Y - AX)(Y - AX)^T$ is positive semidefinite, so $$ \begin{align*} 0 \preceq \expect[(Y - AX)(Y - AX)^T] ={}& \expect[YY^T] - A\expect[XY^T] - \expect[YX^T] A^T + A\expect[XX^T] A^T \\ ={}& \var(Y) - A \cov(X, Y) + (-\cov(Y,X) + A\var(X))A^T. \end{align*} $$ Set $A = \cov(Y,X) \var(X)\inv$ to have $$ \begin{align*} \var(Y) - \cov(Y,X)\var(X)\inv \cov(X,Y) \succeq 0. \end{align*} $$
