Let $δ$ be another decision rule. Then $$ \begin{align*} R_m(δ) &\geq R_λ(δ) &&\text{maximality}\\ &\geq R_{λ}(δ_λ) &&\text{$λ$-Bayes rule} \tag{\dagger}\\ &= R_m(δ_λ) &&\text{hypothesis}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $δ_λ$ is minimax.

If $δ_λ$ is the unique $λ$-Bayes rule, then for $δ\neq δ_λ$, $(\dagger)$ is strict, so $δ$ is not a minimax rule.

For another prior $π$, we have $$ \begin{align*} R_π(δ_π) &\leq R_m(δ_π) &&\text{maximality} \\ &\leq R_m(δ_λ) &&\text{minimax} \\ &= R_λ(δ_λ) &&\text{hypothesis} \end{align*} $$ so $λ$ is least favourable.
