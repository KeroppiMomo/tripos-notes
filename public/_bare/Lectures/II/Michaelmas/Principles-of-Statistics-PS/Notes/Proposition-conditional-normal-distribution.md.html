Let $Y\in \R^m, W\in \R^n$ be jointly Gaussian $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{Y \\ W} \sim N\left(\pmat{μ_1 \\ μ_2}, \pmat{Σ_{11} & Σ_{12} \\ Σ_{21} & Σ_{22}}\right) \end{align*} $$ where $Σ = \pmat{Σ_{11} & Σ_{12} \\ Σ_{21} & Σ_{22}}$ is positive definite. Then $$ \begin{align*} Y \mid W = w \sim N(μ_1 + Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv(w - μ_2),\; Σ_{11} - Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv Σ_{21}). \end{align*} $$

Proof

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} Y = μ_1 + Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv(W - μ_2) + ε \end{align*} $$ where $ε\sim N(0, Σ_{11} - Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv Σ_{21})$ and $W$ are independent.

Note $\var(Y \mid Z) = Σ_{11} - Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv Σ_{21}$ does not depend on $Z$.
