Let $Z_0 \sim N(0, I(θ_0)\inv)$ and let $Φ_0$ be the cumulative distribution function of $|Z_0|$.

We claim $R_n \xrightarrow{a.s.} Φ_0\inv(1-α)$. Since $$ \begin{align*} Φ_0(R_n) ={}& \prob[|Z_0| \leq R_n] = \hat ϕ_n(C_n), \end{align*} $$ $\hat ϕ_n(C_n) - π_n(C_n) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$ and $π_n(C_n) = 1-α$, we get $Φ_0(R_n) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 1-α$. Continuous mapping theorem then proves our claim.

Slutsky's lemma then implies $$ \begin{align*} \underbrace{\frac{Φ_0\inv(1-α)}{R_n}}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} 1} \underbrace{\sqrt{n}(\hat θ_n - θ_0)}_{\xrightarrow{d} N(0, I(θ_0)\inv)} \xrightarrow{d}N(0, I(θ_0)\inv) \end{align*} $$ and so $$ \begin{align*} \prob_{θ_0}[θ_0\in C_n] ={}& \prob_{θ_0}\left[\frac{Φ_0\inv(1-α)}{R_n}\sqrt{n}|\hat θ_n - θ_0| < Φ_0\inv(1 - α)\right] \\ \to{}& \prob_{θ_0}[|Z_0| < Φ_0\inv(1-α)] \\ ={}& 1-α. \end{align*} $$
