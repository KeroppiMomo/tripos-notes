By the strong law of large numbers, $\hat F_n(t) \xrightarrow{a.s.} F(t)$ for all $t\in \R$.

Fix $m\in \N$. Since $F$ is continuous and increasing, let $$ \begin{align*} -\infty = t_0 < t_1 < \ldots < t_{m-1} < t_m = \infty \end{align*} $$ such that $F(t_i) - F(t_{i-1}) = 1/m$.

Then for any $t\in \R$, there exists $j$ such that $t\in [t_{j-1}, t_j]$, so $$ \begin{align*} \hat F_n(t) - F(t) &\leq \hat F_n(t_j) - F(t_{j-1}) = \hat F_n(t_j) - F(t_j) + \frac{1}{m}, \\ \hat F_n(t) - F(t) &\geq \hat F_n(t_{j-1}) - F(t_j) = \hat F_n(t_{j-1}) - F(t_{j-1}) - \frac{1}{m}. \end{align*} $$ Thus $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t\in \R} |\hat F_n(t) - F(t)| \leq \underbrace{\sup_{j=0,\ldots,m} |\hat F_n(t_j) - F(t_j)|}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} 0} + \frac{1}{m}. \end{align*} $$

Therefore almost surely $$ \begin{align*} \limsup_{n\to \infty} \sup_{t\in \R}|\hat F_n(t) - F(t)| \leq \frac{1}{m}. \end{align*} $$ Let $E_m$ be the event where this holds. Then in $\bigcap_{m\in \N} E_m$, which has probability 1, $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t\in \R} |\hat F_n(t) - F(t)| \to 0. \end{align*} $$
