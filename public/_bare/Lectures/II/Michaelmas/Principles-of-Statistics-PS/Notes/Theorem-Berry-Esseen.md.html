(Non-examinable)

Suppose $Z_1, Z_2, \ldots$ are iid with mean $μ$ and variance $σ^2$. Then for all $δ\in (0,1]$, $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t\in \R} |\prob(\sqrt{n}(\overline{Z_n} - μ) \leq t) - Φ(t/σ)| \leq \frac{8 \expect[|Z_1 - μ|^{2+δ}]}{σ^{2+δ} n^{δ/2}} \end{align*} $$ where $Φ$ is the distribution function of $N(0,1)$.
