In addition to the assumptions here, we further assume:

  1. the true parameter $θ_0$ is in $\op{int}(Θ)$;

  2. there is an open neighbourhood $U \subseteq Θ$ of $θ_0$ such that $θ \mapsto f(x, θ)$ is $C^2$ for each $x\in \mathcal{X}$;

  3. the Fisher information matrix $I(θ_0)$ is non-singular and $\expect_{θ_0}[\left\|\pdfd{\log f(X,θ)}{θ}(θ_0)\right\|_2] < \infty$;

  4. there is a compact ball $K \subseteq U$ of non-empty interior, centred at $θ_0$, such that

    • $\expect_{θ_0}[\sup_{θ\in K} \|\pdd{θ}\pdd{θ^T} \log f(X,θ)\|_2] < \infty$;

    • $\int_{\mathcal{X}} \sup_{θ\in K} \|\pdd{θ} \log f(X,θ)\|_2 \d x < \infty$;

    • $\int_{\mathcal{X}} \sup_{θ\in K} \|\pdd{θ}\pdd{θ^T} \log f(X,θ)\|_2 \d x < \infty$.
