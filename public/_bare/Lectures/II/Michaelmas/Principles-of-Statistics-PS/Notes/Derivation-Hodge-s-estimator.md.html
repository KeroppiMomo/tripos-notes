Under $P_{θ_0}$ with $θ_0\neq 0$, $$ \begin{align*} P_{θ_0}(\tilde θ\neq \hat θ) ={}& P_{θ_0}(\underbrace{|\hat θ|}_{\geq |θ_0| - |\hat θ-θ_0|} < n^{-1/4}) \\ \leq{}& P_{θ_0}(|θ_0| - |\hat θ - θ_0| < n^{-1/4}) \\ ={}& P_{θ_0}(|θ_0| - n^{-1/4} < |\hat θ - θ_0|) \to 0 \end{align*} $$ because $\hat θ \xrightarrow{p} θ_0$ (by consistency of MLE).

Therefore under $P_{θ_0}$, $\sqrt{n}(\tilde θ - θ_0) \xrightarrow{d} N(0, I\inv(θ))$ by the asymptotic normality of $\hat θ$.

However if $θ_0 = 0$, $$ \begin{align*} P_{θ_0}(\tilde θ = \hat θ) = P_{θ_0}(|\hat θ| \geq n^{-1/4}) = P_{θ_0}(|\hat θ - θ_0| \sqrt{n} \geq n^{1/4}). \end{align*} $$ Recall that $\sqrt{n}(\hat θ - θ_0)$ are bounded in probability ($O_{P_{θ_0}}(1)$, by Proposition 1(4) in Lecture 4). Hence $P_{θ_0}(\tilde θ = \hat θ) \xrightarrow{n\to \infty}0$. This implies that $\sqrt{n}(\hat θ - θ_0) \xrightarrow{d} δ_{\set{0}}$ and this has variance $< I(θ_0)\inv$ in general.
