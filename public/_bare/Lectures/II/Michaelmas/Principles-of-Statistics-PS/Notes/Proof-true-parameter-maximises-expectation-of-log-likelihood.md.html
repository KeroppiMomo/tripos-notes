Application of Jensen's inequality: $$ \begin{align*} l(θ) - l(θ_0) ={}& \expect_{θ_0} \log \frac{f(X, θ)}{f(X, θ_0)} \\ \leq{} & \log \expect_{θ_0} \frac{f(X, θ)}{f(X, θ_0)} \\ ={}& \log \int_{\mathcal{X}} \frac{f(x,θ)}{f(x, θ_0)} \cdot f(x, θ_0) \d x \\ ={}& \log 1 = 0 \end{align*} $$ with equality iff $f(X, θ)/f(X,θ_0)$ is almost surely constant.

Since $\expect_{θ_0}|\log f(X, θ)|<\infty$, we have $f(X,θ)>0$ almost surely, so if we assume $θ$ is identifiable, $f(X,θ)/f(X,θ_0)$ is almost surely constant iff $θ = θ_0$.
