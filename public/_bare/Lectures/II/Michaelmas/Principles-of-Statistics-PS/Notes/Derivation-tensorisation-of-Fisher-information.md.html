Expand $$ \begin{align*} I_n(θ) :={}& \expect_θ\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \pdd{θ} \log f(X_i, θ) \pdd{θ^T} \log f(X_j, θ)\right] \\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{n} \expect_θ\left[\pdd{θ} \log f(X_i, θ) \pdd{θ^T} \log f(X_i, θ)\right] \\ &+ \sum_{i\neq j} \expect_θ\left[\pdd{θ}\log f(X_i, θ)\right] \expect_θ\left[\pdd{θ^T} \log(X_j, θ)\right] \end{align*} $$ by independence.

But by regularity, $\expect_θ[\pdd{θ} \log f(X_i, θ)] = 0$, so $I_n(θ) = n I(θ)$ as $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ are identically distributed.
