Let $T_1(X_1), T_2(X_1, X_2), \ldots$ be a sequence of estimators of $θ$ based on iid sample $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ of $X$.

If the bias of $T_n$ follows $$ \begin{align*} \expect_θ[T_n] - θ = \frac{a}{n} + \frac{b}{n^2} + O\left(\frac{1}{n^3}\right), \end{align*} $$ then the bias of the jackknife estimator $\tilde T_\text{JACK}$ follows $$ \begin{align*} \expect_θ[\tilde T_{\text{JACK}}] - θ = O\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right). \end{align*} $$

Proof

If $θ = g(\expect[X])$ for some $g: \R^p \to \R$ is a smooth, then $T_n(X_1, \ldots, X_n) = g(\overline{X_n})$ satisfies the hypothesis. (Informal derivation)
