Let $M = Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv$. Note $$ \begin{align*} \cov(W, Y - MW) = \cov(W,Y) - \cov(W, W)M^T = Σ_{21} - Σ_{22} M^T = 0 \end{align*} $$ and since $(W, Y - MW)$ is jointly Gaussian, $W$ and $Y - MW$ are independent. $Y - MW$ is Gaussian $N(\tilde μ, \tilde Σ)$ with $$ \begin{align*} \tilde μ ={}& \expect[Y - MW] = μ_1 - Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv μ_2, \\ \tilde Σ ={}& \var[Y - MW] = Σ_{11} - Σ_{12} M^T - MΣ_{21} + MΣ_{22}M^T = Σ_{11} - Σ_{12} Σ_{22}\inv Σ_{21}. \end{align*} $$

Therefore as $Y = MW + (Y - MW)$, $$ \begin{align*} Y\mid W = w \sim N(Mw + \tilde μ, \tilde Σ) \end{align*} $$ as required.
