Theorem (multivariate Cauchy-Schwarz) open_in_new

Let $X\in \R^m$ and $Y\in \R^n$ be square-integrable random variables.

If $\var(X)$${}\succ{}$$0$, then $$ \begin{align*} \var(Y) \succeq \cov(Y,X) \var(X)\inv \cov(X,Y). \end{align*} $$

Proof

Set $Y$ to be $\tilde Φ$ and $X$ to be $\pdd{θ}\log f(X,θ)$. Then since $\expect_θ[\pdd{θ} \log f(X, θ)] = 0$, it suffices to show $\cov_θ(\tilde θ, \pdd{θ}) = \pdd{θ}\expect_θ[\tilde Φ]$.

$$ \begin{align*} \cov_θ(X,Y) ={}& \expect_θ\left[(\tilde Φ - \expect_θ[\tilde Φ]) \pdd{θ}\log f(X, θ)\right] \\ ={}& \expect_θ\left[\tilde Φ \pdd{θ} \log f(X,θ)\right] - \expect_θ[\tilde Φ] \underbrace{\expect_θ\left[\pdd{θ}\log f(X,θ)\right]}_{0} \\ ={}& \int_{\mathcal{X}} \tilde Φ(x) \frac{\pdd{θ}f(x,θ)}{f(x,θ)}\cdot f(x,θ) \d x \\ ={}& \pdd{θ}\int_{\mathcal{X}} \tilde Φ(x) f(x,θ) \d x &&\text{regularity} \\ ={}& \pdd{θ} \expect_θ[\tilde Φ]. \end{align*} $$
