Note we showed that $Ω :=\var(W\mid Z)$ does not depend on $Z$.

Let $M = \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^T$ be the projection onto $\mathbf{1}$. Recall $P$ is the projection onto the column space of $\pmat{\mathbf{1} & Z}$, which includes $\mathbf{1}$.

We noted $$ \begin{align*} \hat Σ := \frac{1}{n} \pmat{\mathbf{W}^T \\ \mathbf{Z}^T} (I-M) \pmat{\mathbf{W} & \mathbf{Z}} = \pmat{\frac{1}{n}\mathbf{W}^T(I-M) \mathbf{W} & \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{W}^T(I-M)\mathbf{Z} \\ \frac{1}{n} \mathbf{Z}^T (I-M) \mathbf{W} & \frac{1}{n} \mathbf{Z}^T (I-M) \mathbf{Z}} =: \pmat{\hat Σ_{WW} & \hat Σ_{WZ} \\ \hat Σ_{ZW} & \hat Σ_{ZZ}} \end{align*} $$ is a maximum likelihood estimator for $Σ := \var\pmat{W \\ Z} =: \pmat{Σ_{WW} & Σ_{WZ} \\ Σ_{ZW} & Σ_{ZZ}}$.

We showed $$ \begin{align*} Ω = Σ_{WW} - Σ_{WZ} Σ_{ZZ}\inv Σ_{ZW} \end{align*} $$ so a maximum likelihood estimator of $Ω$ is $$ \begin{align*} \hat Ω :={}& \hat Σ_{WW} - \hat Σ_{WZ} \hat Σ_{ZZ}\inv \hat Σ_{ZW} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{n} \mathbf{W}^T(I-M)\mathbf{W} - \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{W}^T (I-M) \mathbf{Z} \left(\mathbf{Z}^T (I-M) \mathbf{Z}\right)\inv \mathbf{Z}^T (I-M) \mathbf{W} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{W}^T(I-M - \tilde{\mathbf{Z}}(\tilde{\mathbf{Z}}^T \tilde{\mathbf{Z}})\inv \tilde{\mathbf{Z}}^T) \mathbf{W} \end{align*} $$ where $\tilde {\mathbf{Z}} := (I-M)\mathbf{Z}$. Since the column space of $\pmat{\mathbf{1} & \mathbf{Z}}$ is the orthogonal direct sum of $\v 1$ and the column space of $\tilde{\mathbf{Z}}$, we have $$ \begin{align*} P = \underbrace{M}_{\text{projection onto $\mathbf{1}$}} + \underbrace{\tilde{\mathbf{Z}}(\tilde{\mathbf{Z}}^T \tilde{\mathbf{Z}})\inv \tilde{\mathbf{Z}}^T}_{\text{projection onto $\op{Col}(\tilde{\mathbf{Z}})$}}. \end{align*} $$

Hence $$ \begin{align*} \hat Ω = \frac{1}{n} \mathbf{W}^T (I- P) \mathbf{W}. \end{align*} $$

Since $ρ_{W^{(j)}, W^{(k)}\mid Z} = \frac{Ω_{jk}}{\sqrt{Ω_{jj} Ω_{kk}}}$, plugging the MLE $\hat Ω$ in gives an MLE of $ρ_{W^{(j)}, W^{(k)}|Z}$.
