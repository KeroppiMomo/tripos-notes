Suppose $ϕ = ϕ(θ)$ is a change of coordinates. The Fisher information changes as $$ \begin{align*} I_ϕ(ϕ) ={}& \expect_ϕ\left[\pdd{ϕ}\log f(X, θ) \pdd{ϕ^T} \log f(X, θ)\right] \\ ={}& \expect_θ \left[\pdfd{θ^T}{ϕ}\pdd{θ}\log f(X,θ) \pdd{θ^T}\log f(X,θ)\pdfd{θ}{ϕ^T}\right] \\ ={}& \pdfd{θ^T}{ϕ} I_θ(θ) \pdfd{θ}{ϕ^T} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \det I_{ϕ}(ϕ) = \left(\pdfd{ϕ}{θ^T}\right)^{-2}\det I_θ(θ). \end{align*} $$ Thus the Jeffreys prior is unchanged.
