Suppose $\hat θ_n$ is a sequence of estimators in $Θ\subseteq \R^d$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} r_n(\hat θ_n - θ_0) \xrightarrow{d} Z \end{align*} $$ for some deterministic $r_n \to \infty$.

For a continuously differentiable function $Φ: Θ \to \R^k$, $$ \begin{align*} r_n(Φ(\hat θ_n) - Φ(θ_0)) \xrightarrow{d} \pdfd{Φ}{θ^T}(θ_0) \cdot Z. \end{align*} $$

Proof

If $\hat θ_n$ is the maximum likelihood estimator in a regular model, then $$ \begin{align*} \sqrt{n}(Φ(\hat θ_n) - Φ(θ_n)) \xrightarrow{d}N\Bigl(0, \pdfd{Φ}{θ^T}(θ_0)\; I(θ_0)\inv \;\pdfd{Φ}{θ}(θ_0)\Bigr) \end{align*} $$ and hence the plug-in MLE $Φ(\hat θ_n)$ achieves the Cramér-Rao lower bound asymptotically.
