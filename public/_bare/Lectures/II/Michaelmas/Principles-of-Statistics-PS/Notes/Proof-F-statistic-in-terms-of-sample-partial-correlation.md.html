As $P$ projects onto a larger space than $P_0$, we have $PP_0 = P_0 P = P_0$. Note $I-P_0 = (I-P) + (P-P_0)$ and $(I-P)(P - P_0) = 0$, so by we have Pythagoras $$ \begin{align*} \|(I - P_0)\mathbf{Y}\|^2 = \|(I - P)\mathbf{Y}\|^2 + \|(P - P_0)\mathbf{Y}\|^2. \end{align*} $$

Therefore it suffices to show $$ \begin{align*} \hat ρ_{X,Y\mid Z} \equiv \frac{(\mathbf{X}^T (I-P_0) \mathbf{Y})^2}{\|(I - P_0) \mathbf{X}\|^2\|(I - P_0) \mathbf{Y}\|^2} = \frac{\|(P - P_0)\mathbf{Y}\|^2}{\|(I - P_0)\mathbf{Y}\|^2}. \end{align*} $$

$(P - P_0)$ has rank 1, and $(I-P_0) \mathbf{X} = (P-P_0)\mathbf{X}$ is an eigenvector of $(P-P_0)$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} P - P_0 = \frac{((I-P_0)\mathbf{X})((I-P_0)\mathbf{X})^T}{\|(I-P_0)\mathbf{X}\|^2} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \|(P - P_0) \mathbf{Y}\|^2 ={}& \mathbf{Y}^T (P - P_0) \mathbf{Y} = \frac{(\mathbf{X}^T (I - P_0) \mathbf{Y})^2}{\|(I - P_0)\mathbf{X}\|^2} \end{align*} $$ as required.
