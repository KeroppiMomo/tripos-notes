Compute $$ \begin{align*} &\expect_θ\left[\pdd{θ}\pdd{θ^T} \log f(X, θ)\right]\\ ={}& \expect_θ \left[\pdd{θ} \frac{1}{f(X,θ)} \pdd{θ^T} f(X, θ)\right] \\ ={}& -\expect_θ \left[\frac{1}{f(X,θ)^2} \pdd{θ} f(X, θ)\pdd{θ^T} f(X, θ)\right] + \expect_θ\left[\frac{1}{f(X,θ)} \pdd{θ} \pdd{θ^T} f(X,θ)\right] \\ ={}& -\expect_θ \left[\pdd{θ} \log f(X, θ)\pdd{θ^T} \log f(X, θ)\right] + \expect_θ\left[\frac{1}{f(X,θ)} \pdd{θ} \pdd{θ^T} f(X,θ)\right]. \end{align*} $$

The second term is $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\mathcal{X}} \frac{1}{f(x,θ)} \pdd{θ} \pdd{θ^T} f(x,θ)\cdot f(x,θ)\d x = \pdd{θ} \pdd{θ^T} \underbrace{\int f(x,θ) \d x}_{1} = 0 \end{align*} $$ by regularity.
