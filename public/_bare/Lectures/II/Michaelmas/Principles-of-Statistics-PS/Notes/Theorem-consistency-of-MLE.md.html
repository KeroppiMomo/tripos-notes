Let $\sb{f(\cdot ,θ)}{θ\in Θ}$ be a statistical model, with true parameter $θ_0$ for $X$. Let $\hat θ_n$ be the maximum likelihood estimator based on $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ iid copies of $X$.

Suppose

  1. $Θ$ is compact;

  2. $θ\mapsto f(x, θ)$ is continuous as $x\in \mathcal{X}$;

  3. $f(x,θ) > 0$ for all $x\in \mathcal{X}, θ\in Θ$;

  4. $\expect \sup_{θ\in Θ}|\log f(X, θ)| < \infty$; and

  5. identifiability: $f(\cdot, θ) = f(\cdot, θ')\iff θ = θ'$;

Then the maximum likelihood estimator $\hat θ_n$ exists and is consistent, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \hat θ_n \xrightarrow{p} θ_0 \qquad \text{as $n\to \infty$}. \end{align*} $$
