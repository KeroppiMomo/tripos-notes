We will only show the case $X_i \sim N(θ, 1)$ iid, and the prior is $θ\sim N(0,1)$.

As $π_n$ and $\hat ϕ_n$ are probability densities, $$ \begin{align*} \int_{Θ} π_n(θ) - \hat ϕ_n(θ) \d θ = 1 - 1 = 0. \end{align*} $$ Letting $(\cdot )_+ = \max(\cdot , 0)$ and $(\cdot)_- = \min(\cdot ,0)$, we can write it as $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \int_{Θ} (π_n(θ) - \hat ϕ_n(θ))_+ \d θ + \int_{Θ} (π_n(θ) - \hat ϕ(θ))_- \d θ. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\R^p} |\hat ϕ_n(θ) - π_n(θ)| \d θ ={}& \int_{Θ} (\hat ϕ_n(θ) - π_n(θ))_+ \d θ - \int_{Θ} (\hat ϕ_n(θ) - π_n(θ))_- \d \\ ={}& 2 \int_{Θ} (\hat ϕ_n(θ) - π_n(θ))_+ \d θ \\ ={}& 2 \int_{Θ} \left(1 - \frac{π_n(θ)}{\hat ϕ_n(θ)}\right)_+ \hat ϕ_n(θ) \d θ. \end{align*} $$

Using our assumed settings, we have $$ \begin{align*} \frac{π_n(θ)}{\hat ϕ_n(θ)} = \frac{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2π/(n+1)}}\exp(-\frac{n+1}{2} (θ - \overline{θ_n})^2)}{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2π/n}} \exp(-\frac{n}{2}(θ - \hat θ_n)^2)}. \end{align*} $$

Substituting $v = \sqrt{n}(θ - \hat θ_n)$ and $\d v = \sqrt{n} \d θ$, $$ \begin{align*} 2 \|π_n - \hat ϕ_n\|_{TV} = 2 \int \left(\underbrace{1 - \sqrt{\frac{n+1}{n}} \exp\left(-\frac{n+1}{2n}(v + \sqrt{n}(\hat θ_n - \overline{θ_n}))^2 + \frac{v^2}{2}\right)}_{F_n(v)}\right)_+ \times \frac{1}{2π}\exp(-v^2/2) \d v \end{align*} $$

Claim: for all $v\in \R$, $F_n(v) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$.

We saw previously that $$ \begin{align*} \sqrt{n}(\overline{θ_n} - \hat θ_n) = -\frac{\sqrt{n}}{n+1} \overline{X_n} \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0 \end{align*} $$ as $\overline{X_n} \xrightarrow{a.s.} θ_0$ by the strong law of large numbers, and $-\sqrt{n}/(n+1)\to 0$.

This implies by the continuous mapping theorem that $F_n(v) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$.

Note $|(F_n(v))_+| \leq 1$, which is integrable under $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2π}}e^{-v^2/2}\d v$ ($\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} 1 \frac{e^{-v^2/2}}{\sqrt{2π}} \d v =1$). Hence by the dominated convergence theorem, $$ \begin{align*} \lim_{n\to \infty} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (F_n(v))_+ \frac{e^{-v^2/2}}{\sqrt{2π}} \d v = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \lim_{n\to \infty} (F_n(v))_+ \frac{e^{-v^2/2}}{\sqrt{2π}} \d v = 0 \end{align*} $$ almost surely. Hence $\|π_n - \hat ϕ_n\|_{TV} \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$ as $n\to \infty$.
