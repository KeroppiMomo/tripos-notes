Expand $$ \begin{align*} \expect_θ[\tilde T_\text{JACK}] ={}& n\expect_θ[T_n] - \frac{n-1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n} \expect_θ[T_{(-j)}] \\ ={}&n\expect_θ[T_n] - (n-1) \expect_θ[T_{n-1}]. \end{align*} $$ Then substitute the series to get $$ \begin{align*} \expect_θ[\tilde J_\text{JACK}] ={}& n\left(θ + \frac{a}{n} + \frac{b}{n^2} + O(\frac{1}{n^3})\right) - (n-1)\left(θ + \frac{a}{n-1} + \frac{b}{(n-1)^2} + O(\frac{1}{n^3})\right) \\ ={}& θ + b\left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n-1}\right) + O(\frac{1}{n^2}) \\ ={}& θ + O(1/n^2). \end{align*} $$
