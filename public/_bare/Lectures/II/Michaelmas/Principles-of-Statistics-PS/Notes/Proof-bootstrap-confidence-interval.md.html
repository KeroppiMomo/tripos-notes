Let $Y_n = \sqrt{n}(\hat θ_n - θ)$ and $Y\sim F$, so $Y_n \xrightarrow{d} Y$ by condition 1.

We claim $\hat F_n\inv(p) \xrightarrow{p} F\inv(p)=: x$ for any $p\in (0,1)$. Fix $ε>0$. As $F$ is strictly increasing, $δ^+ := F(x+ε) - F(x) > 0$ and $δ^- := F(x) - F(x-ε)> 0$. Using condition 2, the event $E^{\pm}_n := \{|\hat F_n(x\pm ε) - F(x\pm ε)| < δ_\pm\}$ has probability $\prob(E^{\pm}_n) \to 1$. Note in $E^+_n \cap E^-_n$, $$ \begin{align*} \hat F_n(x-ε) < F(x-ε) + δ^- = F(x) = F(x+ε) + δ^+ < \hat F_n(x+ε). \end{align*} $$ Since $F$ is continuous, $F\inv(x) = F\inv(F(p)) = p$, so $x-ε \leq \hat F_n\inv(p) \leq x+ ε$. Therefore $\prob[|\hat F_n\inv(p) - F\inv(p)| < ε] \geq \prob(E_n^+ \cap E_n^-)\to 1$, as claimed.

This shows $\hat l_n \xrightarrow{p} l := F\inv(α/2)$ and $\hat u_n \xrightarrow{p} u := F\inv(1-α/2)$. Then by Slutsky, $$ \begin{align*} Y_n - \hat l_n \xrightarrow{p} Y - l, \qquad Y_n - \hat u_n \xrightarrow{p} Y - u \end{align*} $$ so since $F$ is continuous, $$ \begin{align*} \prob[Y_n \geq \hat l_n] \to \prob[Y\geq l], \qquad \prob[Y_n \leq \hat u_n] \to \prob[Y \leq u]. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \prob[\hat l_n \leq Y_n\leq \hat u_n] ={}& \prob[Y_n \geq \hat l_n] + \prob[Y_n \leq \hat u_n] - 1 \\ \to{}& \prob[Y \geq l] + \prob[Y \leq u] - 1 \\ ={}& \prob[l\leq Y\leq u] = 1-α. \end{align*} $$
