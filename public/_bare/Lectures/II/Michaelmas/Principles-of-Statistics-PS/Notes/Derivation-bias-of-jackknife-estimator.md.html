Suppose $\expect[X_1] = μ\in \R^p$, and we wish to estimate $θ = g(μ)$ for some $g: \R^p \to \R$ smooth.

Consider the estimator $$ \begin{align*} \hat θ = T(X_1, \ldots, X_n) = g(\bar X_n). \end{align*} $$ Then, by a Taylor expansion, $$ \begin{align*} \hat θ_n - θ = g(\bar X_n) - g(μ) \approx ∇g(μ)^T (\bar X_n - μ) + \frac{1}{2}(\bar X_n - μ)^T ∇^2 g(μ) (\tilde X_n - μ). \end{align*} $$ Taking expectation, $$ \begin{align*} \expect\hat θ_n - θ \approx{}& \frac{1}{2} \expect[\tr((\bar X_n - μ)(\tilde X_n - μ)^T) ∇^2 g(μ)] \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2} \tr(\expect[(\tilde X_n - μ)(\tilde X_n-μ)^T] ∇^2 g(μ)) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2} \tr\left(\frac{\var X_1}{n} ∇^2 g(μ)\right) \\ ={}& O(1/n). \end{align*} $$
