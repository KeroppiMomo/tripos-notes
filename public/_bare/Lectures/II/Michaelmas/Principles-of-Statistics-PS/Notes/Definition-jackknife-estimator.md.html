Let $X_1, X_2, \ldots$ be iid copies of $X$. Let $T_n := T(X_1, \ldots, X_n)$ be an estimator of $θ$, and write $T_{(-j)} := T(X_1, \ldots, X_{j-1}, X_{j+1}, \ldots, X_n)$.

The jackknife bias estimate is $$ \begin{align*} \hat B_n := (n-1)\left(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} T_{(-j)} - T_n\right) \end{align*} $$ and the jackknife estimator is $$ \begin{align*} \tilde T_{\text{JACK}} := T_n - \hat B_n = nT_n - \frac{n-1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} T_{(-j)}. \end{align*} $$
