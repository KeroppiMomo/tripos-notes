Apply Berry-Esseen to $X_1^\ast, \ldots, X_n^\ast \mid X_1, \ldots, X_n\sim \mathbb{P}_n$. We have $$ \begin{align*} \expect[X_1^\ast] ={}& \overline{X_n}, \\ \var[X_1^\ast] ={}& \hat σ_n^2 \end{align*} $$ where $$ \begin{align*} \hat σ_n^2 := \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - \overline{X_n})^2. \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t\in \R} |\underbrace{\prob[\sqrt{n}(\overline{X_n^\ast} - \overline{X_n}) \leq t \mid X_1, \ldots, X_n]}_{\hat F_n(t)} - Φ(t/\hat σ_n)| &\leq \frac{8\expect[|X_1^\ast - \overline{X_n}|^{2+δ}\mid X_1, \ldots, X_n]}{\hat σ_{n}^{2+δ} n^{δ/2}} \\ &= \frac{\frac{8}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} |X_i - \overline{X_n}|^{2+δ}}{\hat σ_n^{2+δ } n^{δ/2}}. \end{align*} $$

We want to show RHS $\xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$. Note $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} |X_i - \overline{X_n}|^{2+δ} &\leq \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (2\max\{|X_i - θ|, |\overline{X_n}-θ|\})^{2+δ} \\ &\leq 2^{2+δ} \cdot \underbrace{\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} |X_i - θ|^{2+δ}}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} \expect[|X - θ|^{2+δ}]} + 2^{2+δ} \underbrace{|\overline{X_n} - θ|}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} 0} \end{align*} $$ by the strong law of large numbers. Moreover $$ \begin{align*} \hat σ_n^2 = \underbrace{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - θ)^2}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} σ^2} + \underbrace{(\overline{X_n} - θ)^2}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.}0} \xrightarrow{a.s.} σ^2. \end{align*} $$ Therefore RHS $\xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$ since $n^{δ/2}\to \infty$.

Finally since $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t\in \R} |\hat F_n(t) - Φ(t/σ)| \leq \sup_{t\in \R} |\hat F_n(t) - Φ(t/\hat σ_n)| + \underbrace{\sup_{t\in \R}\left|Φ(t/σ) - Φ(t/\hat σ_n)\right|}_{(*)} \end{align*} $$ it suffices to show $(*) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0$. By the mean value theorem, there exists $\tilde σ$ such that $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{t} |Φ(t/σ) - Φ(t/\hat σ_n)| ={}& \left|\frac{1}{σ} - \frac{1}{\hat σ_n}\right| \sup_{t} |t| |Φ'(t/\tilde σ)| \\ \leq{}& \underbrace{\left|\frac{1}{σ} - \frac{1}{\hat σ_n}\right|}_{\xrightarrow{a.s.} 0} \underbrace{\sup_{\tilde σ>0, t} \frac{|t|^2/|\tilde σ|}{\sqrt{2π}} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2} \frac{t^2}{\tilde σ^2}\right)}_{<\infty} \end{align*} $$ so we are done.
