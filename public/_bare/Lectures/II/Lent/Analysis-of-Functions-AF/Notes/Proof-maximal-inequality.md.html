Lemma (Vitali covering) open_in_new

Let $\mathcal{U}$ be a collection of balls in $\R^d$.

If $\mathcal{U}$ has bounded diameter, then there exists a countable (or finite) subcollection $\set{B_{r_1}(x_1), B_{r_2}(x_2), \ldots} \subseteq \mathcal{U}$ which is disjoint and either $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |B_{r_i}(x_i)| = \infty$ or $\bigcup \mathcal{U} \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty} B_{5r_i}(x_i)$.

Proof

For each $x\in \R^d$ such that $Mf(x) > t$, by definition there exists some $r(x)$ such that $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{t} \int_{B_{r(x)}(x)} |f(y)| \d y > |B_{r(x)}(x)|. \end{align*} $$

Taking $\mathcal{U} = \sb{B_{r(x)}(x)}{x\in \R^d, Mf(x)>t}$, construct a Vitali covering $\mathcal{V} = \set{B_{r(x_1)}(x_1), B_{r(x_2)}(x_2), \ldots}\subseteq \mathcal{U}$. By disjointness $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |B_{r(x_i)}(x_i)| < \frac{1}{t} \int_{\mathcal{\bigsqcup V}} |f(y)| \d y \leq \frac{1}{t} \|f\|_1 \end{align*} $$ However $f\in L^1(\R^d)$, so $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} |B_{r(x_i)}(x_i)| < \infty$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \sb{x\in \R^d}{Mf(x) > t} \subseteq \bigcup U \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty} B_{5r_i}(x_i) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} |\sb{x\in \R^d}{Mf(x) > t}| \leq \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} 5^d |B_{r_i}(x_i)| < \frac{5^d}{t}\|f\|_1. \end{align*} $$
