Suppose $μ_1$ and $μ_2$ are finite measures such that $\int f \d μ_1 = \int f \d μ_2$ for all $f\in C(E)$. We need to show $μ_1(A) = μ_2(A)$ for all $A\in \mathcal{B}$.

To approximate $\mathbb{1}_A$, fix $ε>0$. Then $U := U_1 \cap U_2$ is still open, $K := K_1 \cup K_2$ is still compact, still $K \subseteq A \subseteq U$ and $μ_i(U \setminus K) < ε$ for both $i=1,2$.
