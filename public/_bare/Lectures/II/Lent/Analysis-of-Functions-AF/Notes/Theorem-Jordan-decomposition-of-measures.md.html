Let $(E, \mathcal{B}, μ)$ be a signed measure space.

There exists unique positive measures $μ^+$ and $μ^-$ such that $μ = μ^+ - μ^-$ and $μ^+$${}\perp{}$$μ^-$, given by $$ \begin{align*} μ^+(A) = μ(A \cap P), \quad μ^-(A) = -μ(A \cap N) \end{align*} $$ where $E = P \sqcup N$ is any Hahn decomposition.

Proof

We can define integrals as $$ \begin{align*} \int f \d μ = \int f \d μ^+ - \int f \d μ^-. \end{align*} $$

The total variation measure is $|μ| = μ^+ - μ^-$. The total variation norm is $\|μ\| = |μ|(E)$. The space of all signed measures on $(E, \mathcal{B})$ then forms a Banach space.
