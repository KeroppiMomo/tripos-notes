$p=\infty$

Since $\int \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_\infty \d ν(y)<\infty$, we have $\|G(\cdot, y)\|_\infty < \infty$ for $ν$-almost every $y$. By the definition of $\|\cdot\|_\infty$, $|G(x,y)| \leq \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_\infty < \infty$ for $μ\otimes ν$-almost every $x,y$. Thus $$ \begin{align*} |g(x)| \leq \int |G(x,y)| \d ν(y) \leq \int \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_\infty \d ν(y) \end{align*} $$ for $μ$-almost every $x$.

$1\leq p<\infty$

First suppose $G\geq 0$. We want to show $$ \begin{align*} \|g\|_p \leq \int \|G(\cdot,y)\|_p \d ν(y). \end{align*} $$ If $p=1$, this is [[FILE NOT FOUND: Fubini]]. If $p>1$, we apply [[FILE NOT FOUND: Fubini]] and [[FILE NOT FOUND: Hölder]]: $$ \begin{align*} \|g\|_p^p ={}& \int \left(\int G(x,y) \d ν(y)\right) \cdot g(x)^{p-1} \d μ(x) \\ \leq{}& \iint G(x,y) \cdot g(x)^{p-1} \d μ(x) \d ν(y) &&\text{Fubini}\\ \leq{}& \int \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_p \cdot \|g^{p-1}\|_{p/(p-1)} \d ν(y) &&\text{Hölder} \\ \leq{}& \int \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_p \d ν(y) \cdot \|g\|_p^{p-1} \end{align*} $$ and rearrange terms.

Since $\int g(x)^p \d μ(x) = \|g\|_p \leq \int \|G(\cdot ,y)\|_p \d ν(y) < \infty$, we have $g(x) < \infty$ for $μ$-almost every $x$.

Finally for a general $G$, the above shows $\int |G(x,y)| \d ν(y) < \infty$ for $μ$-almost every $x$, so $g(x) = \int G(x,y) \d ν(y)$ exists.
