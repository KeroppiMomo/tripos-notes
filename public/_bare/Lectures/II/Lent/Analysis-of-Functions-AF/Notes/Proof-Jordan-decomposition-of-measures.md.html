Existence

Obvious from Hahn decomposition: $A = (A \cap P) \sqcup (A \cap N)$, so $$ \begin{align*} μ(A) = μ(A \cap P) + μ(A \cap N) = μ^+(A) - μ^-(A). \end{align*} $$

Uniqueness

Suppose $μ = μ^+ - μ^-$ for some positive measures $μ^+, μ^-$ which satisfies $μ^+ \perp μ^-$, and take any $A\in \mathcal{B}$.

If $μ(A) \geq 0$, then we cannot have $μ^-(A) > 0$, since then $μ^+(A) = 0$ by singularity so $μ(A) = -μ^-(A) < 0$. Therefore $μ^-(A) = 0$ and $μ^+(A) = μ(A)$.

Similarly if $μ(A) \leq 0$, then $μ^+(A) = 0$ and $μ^-(A) = μ(A)$.
