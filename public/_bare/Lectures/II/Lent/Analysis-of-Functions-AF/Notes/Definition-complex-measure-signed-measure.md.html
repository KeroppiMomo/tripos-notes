Let $(E, \mathcal{B})$ be a measurable space. A function $μ: E \to X$ is said to be

  • a complex measure if $X=\C$...

  • a signed measure if $X=\R$...

and $μ(\emptyset) = 0$ and $μ$ is $σ$-additive.

Signed measure cannot take value in $\infty$, so a (positive) measure need not be a signed measure.
