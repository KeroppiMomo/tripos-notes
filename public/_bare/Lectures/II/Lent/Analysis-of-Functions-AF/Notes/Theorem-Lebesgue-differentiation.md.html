Let $f\in L^1(\R^d)$.

Almost every $x\in \R^d$ is a Lebesgue point of $f$, i.e. satisfies $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{|B_r(x)|} \int_{B_r(x)} |f(y) - f(x)| \d y \to 0 \qquad \text{as $r\to 0$}. \end{align*} $$

This implies $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{|B_r(x)|} \int_{B_r(x)} f(y) \d y \to f(x) \qquad \text{as $r\to 0$}. \end{align*} $$
