Let $(E, \mathcal{B}, μ)$ be a finite measure space and $f, f_1, f_2, \ldots: E\to \C$ be measurable functions.

If $f_n(x)\to f(x)$ at almost every $x$, then for any $ε>0$, there exists a measurable set $A\in \mathcal{B}$ such that $f_n|_{A}$ converges uniformly to $f|_A$ and $μ(E \setminus A) < ε$.
