Let $(E, \mathcal{A}, μ)$ be a measure space and $f: E\to \C$ be a measurable function.

For $1\leq p<\infty$, the $L^p$ norm of $f$ is $$ \begin{align*} \|f\|_p := \int |f(x)|^p \d μ(x). \end{align*} $$ For $p=\infty$, the $L^\infty$ norm of $f$ is $$ \begin{align*} \|f\|_\infty := \inf \sb{t>0}{|f(x)|\leq t \text{ for $μ$-almost every $x$}} \end{align*} $$ with the convention $\inf \emptyset = \infty$.

Write $L^p(E) = L^p(E, \mathcal{A}, μ)$ for the space of measurable functions $f: E\to \C$ with $\|f\|_p<\infty$, and identifying functions which agree $μ$-almost everywhere.
