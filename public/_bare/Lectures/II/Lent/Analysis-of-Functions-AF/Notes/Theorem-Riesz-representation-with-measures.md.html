Let $E$ be a compact metric space,

For any linear functional $L:C(E)\to \C$ (not necessarily bounded), there exists a unique complex Borel measure $μ$ such that $$ \begin{align*} L(f) = \int f \d μ \end{align*} $$ and $\|L\| ={}$$\|μ\|$.

If $L$ is positive, i.e. $L(f) \geq 0$ if $f\in C(E)$ is such that $f: E\to [0,\infty)$, then $μ$ is a finite measure and $\|L\| = μ(E)$.
