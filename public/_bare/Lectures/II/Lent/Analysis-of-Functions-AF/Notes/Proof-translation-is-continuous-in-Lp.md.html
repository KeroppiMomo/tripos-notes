Fix $ε>0$. Approximate $f$ with some $g\in C_c(\R^d)$ such that $\|f-g\|_p < ε$. Let $R>0$ be such that $g(x) = 0$ on $x\in \R^d \setminus B_R(0)$.

As $g$ is uniformly continuous, for any $ε_1>0$, there exists $δ>0$ such that $|x-y|<δ$ implies $|g(x) - g(y)| < ε_1$. Then for any $a,b\in \R^d$, if $|a-b|<δ$, then $|τ(a)(x) - τ(b)(x)| < ε_1$ for all $x\in \R^d$. Moreover $τ(a)(x) = 0 = τ(b)(x)$ outside $B_R(a) \cup B_R(b)$, which has measure at most $2|B_R(0)|$, depending only on $g$.

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \|τ_g(a) - τ_g(b)\|_p^p ={}& \int_{B_R(a) \cup B_R(b)} |τ(a)(x) - τ(b)(x)|^p \d x \\ \leq{}& ε_1^p \cdot 2|B_R(0)| < ε^p \end{align*} $$ for sufficiently small $ε_1$. Finally $$ \begin{align*} \|τ_f(a) - τ_f(b)\|_p \leq{}& \|τ_f(a) - τ_g(a)\|_p + \|τ_g(a) - τ_g(b)\|_p + \|τ_g(b) - τ_f(b)\|_p \\ \leq{}& \|f -g\|_p + \|τ_g(a) - τ_g(b)\|_p + \|f - g\|_p < 3ε \end{align*} $$ by the translation invariance of the Lebesgue measure.
