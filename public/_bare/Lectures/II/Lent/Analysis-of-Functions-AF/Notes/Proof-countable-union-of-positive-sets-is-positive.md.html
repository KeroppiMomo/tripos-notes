Let $A_1, A_2,\ldots$ be positive sets. For $B \subseteq \bigcup_{i\in I} A_i =: A$, since $$ \begin{align*} A = A_1 \sqcup (A_2 \setminus A_1) \sqcup (A_3 \setminus (A_1 \cup A_2)) \sqcup \ldots, \end{align*} $$ intersecting with $B$ gives $$ \begin{align*} B = (B \cap A_1) \sqcup (B \cap A_2 \setminus A_1) \sqcup (B \cap A_3 \setminus (A_1 \cup A_2)) \sqcup \ldots \end{align*} $$ so by $σ$-additivity $$ \begin{align*} μ(B) ={}& μ(\underbrace{B \cap A_1}_{\subseteq A_1}) + μ(\underbrace{B \cap A_2 \setminus A_1}_{\subseteq A_2}) + μ(\underbrace{B \cap A_3 \setminus (A_1 \cup A_2)}_{\subseteq A_3}) + \ldots \geq 0. \end{align*} $$
