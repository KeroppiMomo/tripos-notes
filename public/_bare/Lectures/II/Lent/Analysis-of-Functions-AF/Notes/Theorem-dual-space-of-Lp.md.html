Let $(E,\mathcal{B},μ)$ be a $σ$-finite measure space and $1\leq p,q\leq \infty$ with $1/p + 1/q = 1$.

There is an isometric embedding from $L_q(E)$ to $L_p(E)^\ast$ $$ \begin{align*} L_q(E) &\mapsto L_p(E)^\ast \\ g &\mapsto (L_g: f \mapsto \int f\cdot g \d μ). \end{align*} $$ If $p<\infty$, then this is an isometric isomorphism.
