Let $(E, \mathcal{B}, μ)$ be a measure space.

A measurable function $f: E \to \R$ or $\C$ is said to be in weak $L^p(E, \mathcal{B}, μ)$ if there exists some constant $C>0$ such that $$ \begin{align*} μ(\sb{x\in E}{|f(x)| > t}) \leq \frac{C}{t^p}. \end{align*} $$

If $f$ is in $L^p$, then $f$ is in weak $L^p$ by the Markov inequality.
