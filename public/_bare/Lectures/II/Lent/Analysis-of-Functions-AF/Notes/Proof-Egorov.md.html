Define the measurable sets $$ \begin{align*} A_{k,N} := \sb{x\in E}{|f_n(x) - f(x)| < 1/k \quad \forall n\geq N}. \end{align*} $$ Note for all $k$, as $A_{k,N}$ is increasing and $μ$ is finite, $$ \begin{align*} \lim_{N\to \infty} μ(E \setminus A_{k,N}) = μ(E \setminus \bigcap_{N} A_{k,N}) = μ(\sb{x\in E}{f_n(x)\not\to f(x)}) = 0. \end{align*} $$

Fix $ε>0$. For each $k$, let $N(k)$ be such that $μ(E \setminus A_{k,N(k)}) < ε/2^k$, and define $$ \begin{align*} A := \bigcap_{k} A_{k,N(k)}. \end{align*} $$

Then $f_n|_A$ converges uniformly to $f|_A$ as for all $k$ and $x\in A$, we have $|f_n(x) - f(x)|<1/k$ for all $n\geq N(k)$. Moreover $$ \begin{align*} μ(E \setminus A) \leq \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} μ(E \setminus A_{k,N(k)}) < ε. \end{align*} $$
