Let $f: D\to \C$ be a holomorphic function on a domain $D\subseteq \C$.

If $f'(z_0) \neq 0$, then there exist open neighbourhoods $U\subseteq D$ of $z_0$ and $V$ of $f(z_0)$ such that $f|_U: U\to V$ is biholomorphic, i.e. $f|_U$ has an holomorphic inverse.

Proof

If $f$ is a holomorphic bijection, then $f\inv$ is holomorphic as well by applying this result to a small neighbourhood.
