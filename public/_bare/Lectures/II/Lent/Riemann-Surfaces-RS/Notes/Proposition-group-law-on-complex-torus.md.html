Let $Λ = \Zω_1 + \Z ω_2\subseteq \C$ be a lattice ($ω_1, ω_2\neq 0$, $ω_1/ω_2\not\in \R$), and $a,b,c\in \C\setminus Λ$ be distinct mod $Λ$.

The conformal equivalence $h: \C/Λ\to \hat X$ gives $\hat X$ a group structure, given by $$ \begin{align*} a+b+c \in Λ &\iff h(a), h(b), h(c)\in \C\times \C \text{ are colinear} \\ &\iff \begin{vmatrix}1 & \wp(a) & \wp'(a) \\ 1 & \wp(b) & \wp'(b) \\ 1 & \wp(c) & \wp'(c) \\ \end{vmatrix} = 0 \end{align*} $$

From this we can derive the formula $$ \begin{align*} \wp(a+b) = \frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{\wp'(a) - \wp'(b)}{\wp(a) - \wp(b)}\right)^2 -\wp(a) - \wp(b). \end{align*} $$
