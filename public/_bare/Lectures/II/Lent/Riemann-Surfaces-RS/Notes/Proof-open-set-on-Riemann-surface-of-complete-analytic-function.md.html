Check the quotient topology. $$ \begin{align*} q\inv([f,D]) ={}& \sb{(z,g)\in S}{(z,g)\sim (w,f) \quad \text{for some $w\in D$}} \\ ={}& \sb{(z,g)\in S}{z\in D, \; f = g \text{ on a neighbourhood of $z$}} \\ ={}& \bigsqcup_{(g,E)\in \mathcal{F}} W_{f,g} \times \set{g} \end{align*} $$ where $$ \begin{align*} W_{f,g} := \sb{z\in D\cap E}{f=g\text{ on a neighbourhood of $z$}} \end{align*} $$ which is by definition open. Therefore $q\inv([f,D])$ is open.
