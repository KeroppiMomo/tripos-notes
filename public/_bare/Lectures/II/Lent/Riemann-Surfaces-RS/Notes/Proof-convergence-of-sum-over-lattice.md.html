Define $\|z\|_Λ$ for $z\in \C$ by $$ \begin{align*} \|λ_1 ω_1 + λ_2 ω_2\|_Λ := \max\{|λ_1|, |λ_2|\}. \end{align*} $$ This is a norm on the $\R$-vector space $\C$. Therefore as $\C$ is finite-dimensional, all norms are equivalent: we can find constants $A_1, A_2>0$ such that $$ \begin{align*} A_1|z| \leq \|z\|_Λ \leq A_2|z|. \end{align*} $$

Hence $\sum_{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}} 1/|ω|^α$ converges iff $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}} \frac{1}{\|Aω\|^α} = \sum_{m\geq 1} \sum_{\substack{n_1, n_2\in \Z: \\\max\{|n_1|, |n_2|\} = m}} \frac{1}{(Am)^α} = \sum_{m\geq 1} \frac{8m}{(Am)^α} = \frac{8}{A} \sum_{m\geq 1} \frac{1}{(Am)^{α-1}} \end{align*} $$ for $A>0$, which occurs iff $α>2$. We have used the fact that there are exactly $8m$ lattices points on the square $(\pm m, \pm m)$.
