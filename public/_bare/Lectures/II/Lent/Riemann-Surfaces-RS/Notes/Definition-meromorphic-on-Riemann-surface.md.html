Let $X$ be a Riemann surface.

A meromorphic function on $X$ is an analytic map $X\to \C_\infty$ which is not identically $\infty$.

Here $\C_\infty$ is the Riemann sphere $\C \cup \set{\infty}$, homeomorphic to $S^2$ via the stereographic projection and with the conformal structure $$ \begin{align*} \set{(\C, z \mapsto z), (\C^\times \cup \set{\infty}, (z \mapsto 1/z, \infty \mapsto 0))}. \end{align*} $$

This agrees with our previous definition of meromorphic functions on $U \subseteq \C$.
