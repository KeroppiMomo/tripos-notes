Let $f: \C/\Z\to \C_\infty$ be a meromorphic function (or equivalently, let $f: \C\to C_\infty$ be a $\Z$-periodic meromorphic function).

If $f$ is analytic on $\set{A < \Im z < B}$, then there $f$ has a Fourier series representation $$ \begin{align*} f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n e^{2πiz}, \end{align*} $$ uniformly convergent on compact sets.

In particular, all analytic $\C/\Z\to \C_\infty$ meromorphic functions are given by a Fourier series $f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n e^{2πi z}$.
