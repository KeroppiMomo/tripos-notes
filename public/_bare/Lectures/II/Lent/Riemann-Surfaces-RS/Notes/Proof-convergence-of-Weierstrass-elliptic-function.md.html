Lemma (convergence of sum over lattice) open_in_new

Let $Λ = \Zω_1 + \Z ω_2\subseteq \C$ be a lattice ($ω_1, ω_2\neq 0$, $ω_1/ω_2\not\in \R$). Then for $α\in \R$, the series $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}} \frac{1}{|ω|^α} \end{align*} $$ converges iff $α > 2$.

Proof

Fix $R>0$ and consider $|z|<R$. For $|ω|>2R$, we get $$ \begin{align*} \left|\frac{1}{(z-ω)^2} - \frac{1}{ω^2}\right| ={}& \frac{|z||2ω - z|}{|ω|^2 |z - ω|^2} \\ \leq{}& \frac{|z|\cdot \frac{5}{2}|ω|}{|ω|^2 \cdot \frac{1}{4}|ω|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{10 |z|}{|ω|^3} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{\substack{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}: \\ |ω|>2R}} \left|\frac{1}{(z-ω)^2} - \frac{1}{ω^2}\right| \leq 10|z| \sum_{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}} \frac{1}{|ω|^3} < \infty. \end{align*} $$ Hence $\sum_{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}: |ω|>2R} \left(\frac{1}{(z-ω)^2} - \frac{1}{ω^2}\right)$ converges absolutely uniformly on $|z|<R$, so is analytic on $|z| < R$.

The remaining term $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{z^2} + \sum_{\substack{ω\in Λ\setminus \set{0}: \\|ω|\leq 2R}} \left(\frac{1}{(z-ω)^2} - \frac{1}{ω^2}\right) \end{align*} $$ is a finite sum of meromorphic functions, so meromorphic. We observe it has double roots at every $ω\in Λ$ with zero residue.
