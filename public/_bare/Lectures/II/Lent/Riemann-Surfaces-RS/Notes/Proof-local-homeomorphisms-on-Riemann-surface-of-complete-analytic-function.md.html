The homeomorphism $$ \begin{align*} π_S: D \times \set{f} &\to D \\ (z,f) &\mapsto z \end{align*} $$ factors through $$ \begin{alignat}{2} π_S : D \times \set{f} &\xrightarrow{q} [f,D] &&\xrightarrow{π} D \\ (z,f)&\mapsto [(z,f)] &&\mapsto z. \end{alignat} $$

$π$ is a continuous bijection, and $π\inv = q \circ π_S\inv$ is continuous. Hence $π$ is a homeomorphism, so $q = π\inv \circ π_S$ is a homeomorphism also.
