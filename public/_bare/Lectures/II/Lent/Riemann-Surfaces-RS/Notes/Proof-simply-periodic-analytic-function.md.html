Use the conformal equivalence $$ \begin{align*} \C/\Z &\xrightarrow{\sim} \C^\times \\ z &\mapsto e^{2πiz}. \end{align*} $$ Let $\tilde f: \C^\times\to \C_\infty$ be the meromorphic function given by $\tilde f(e^{2πi z}) = f(z)$. Then by hypothesis, $\tilde f$ is analytic on the annulus $\set{e^{-2πB} < |w| < e^{-2πA}}$, so it has a Laurent series $$ \begin{align*} \tilde f(w) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n w^n. \end{align*} $$ Translating back to $f$ gives the Fourier series representation.
