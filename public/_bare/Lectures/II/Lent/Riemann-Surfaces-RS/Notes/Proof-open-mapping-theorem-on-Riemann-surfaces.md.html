Let $V\subseteq X$ be open, and fix $x\in U$. Take charts $(U, ϕ)$ near $x$ and $(\tilde U, \tilde ϕ)$ near $f(x)$ such that $ϕ(x) = 0$, $\tilde ϕ(f(x)) = 0$ and $\tilde ϕ \circ f \circ ϕ\inv(z) = z^d$. As $ϕ(V \cap U)$ is open, find $r>0$ such that $D(0,r)\subseteq ϕ(V \cap U)$. Then $\tilde ϕ\circ f\circ ϕ\inv(D(0,r)) = D(0,r^d)$, which is open, so $f(V) \supseteq f(V \cap U) \supseteq \tilde ϕ\inv \circ \tilde ϕ \circ f \circ ϕ\inv(D(0,r)) = \tilde ϕ\inv(D(0,r^d))$ is an open neighbourhood of $f(x)$.
