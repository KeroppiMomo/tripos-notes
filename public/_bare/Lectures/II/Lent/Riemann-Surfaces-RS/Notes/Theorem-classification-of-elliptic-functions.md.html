Let $Λ = \Zω_1 + \Z ω_2\subseteq \C$ be a lattice ($ω_1, ω_2\neq 0$, $ω_1/ω_2\not\in \R$).

Let $f$ be an elliptic function, i.e. a meromorphic function $f: \C/Λ \to \C_\infty$, or equivalently, a meromorphic function $f: \C\to \C_\infty$ with periodic $Λ$.

There exists rational functions $Q_1, Q_2$ such that $$ \begin{align*} f = Q_1 \circ \wp + \wp' \cdot (Q_2 \circ \wp) \end{align*} $$ where $\wp$ is the Weierstrass elliptic function. In more sophisticated language, the field of elliptic functions is $\C(\wp, \wp')$.

In particular, there is an algebraic relation $$ \begin{align*} (\wp(z)')^2 = 4(\wp(z) - e_1)(\wp(z) - e_2)(\wp(z) - e_3) = 4\wp^3 + B\wp + C. \end{align*} $$
