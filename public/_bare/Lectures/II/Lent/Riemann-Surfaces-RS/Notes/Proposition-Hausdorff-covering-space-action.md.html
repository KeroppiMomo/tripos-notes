Let $X$ be a topological space and $G$ is a group acting on $X$ by homeomorphisms.

Suppose

  1. $X$ is Hausdorff;

  2. $X$ is locally compact, i.e. any $x\in X$ has a compact neighbourhood;

  3. the action is free, i.e. $\op{Stab}$$_G(x) = \set{e}$ for all $x\in X$; and

  4. the action is properly discontinuous, i.e. for all compact $K\subseteq X$, the set $\sb{g\in G}{gK\cap K \neq \emptyset}$ is finite.

Then the quotient space $X\backslash G$ is Hausdorff and $q: X\to X\backslash G$ is a covering space.

Proof

If $X$ is a Riemann surface and $G$ acts by conformal equivalences, then 1 and 2 are automatically satisfied. Then $X\backslash G$ has a unique conformal structure such that $q$ is analytic.
