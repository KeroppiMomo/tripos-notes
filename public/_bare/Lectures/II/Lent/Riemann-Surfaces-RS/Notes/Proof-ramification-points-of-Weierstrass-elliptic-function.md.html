The only pole of $\wp$ in $\C/Λ$ is the double pole at $0$. Therefore the degree of $\wp$ is 2.

The ramification degree of a point is therefore 1 or 2. By Riemann-Hurwitz, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = 2\cdot 2 - \sum_{x\in \C/Λ} (d_x-1) \end{align*} $$ so there are exactly 4 ramification points. One of them is 0.

The other ramification points satisfy $\wp'(z) = 0$. As $\wp$ is even, $\wp'$ is odd (or observe that from the series), so $\wp'(ω_i/2) = -\wp'(-ω_i/2) - \wp'(-ω_i/2 + ω_i)$ and hence $\wp'(ω_i) = 0$ for all $i=1,2,3$ (defining $ω_3 = ω_1+ω_2$).
