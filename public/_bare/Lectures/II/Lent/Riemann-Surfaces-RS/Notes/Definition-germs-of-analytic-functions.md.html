Define the space of germs $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{G} ={}& \biggl(\bigsqcup_{(f,D)} D \times \set{f}\biggr)/{\sim} \end{align*} $$ where the union ranges over all function elements $(f,D)$ and $\sim$ is defined as before, and the local homeomorphism $$ \begin{alignat*}{2} π_\mathcal{G} : &&\mathcal{G}&\to \C \\ &&[(z,f)] &\mapsto z. \end{alignat*} $$

A germ of analytic function is a set of equivalence classes of the form $π_\mathcal{G}\inv(z_0)$.

Observe $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{G} = \bigsqcup_{\mathcal{F}} R_\mathcal{F} \end{align*} $$ the disjoint union of the Riemann surfaces of all complete analytic functions., so $\mathcal{G}$ is Hausdorff and disconnected.

For $z_0\in \C$, $(f,D)$ and $(g,E)$ determine same germ iff $f$ and $g$ agree on a neighbourhood of $z_0$, so $$ \begin{align*} π_\mathcal{G}\inv(z_0) \simeq \set{\text{power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n(z-z_0)^n$ with a non-zero radius of convergence}}. \end{align*} $$

As before there is a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{analytic function $D\to \C$}} &\xrightarrow{\sim} \set{\text{continuous section of $π_\mathcal{G}$ over a domain $D\subseteq \C$}} \\ f &\mapsto π_{\mathcal{G}}|_{[f,D]}\inv. \end{align*} $$
