Fix $a,b\not\in Λ$, and consider the elliptic function $$ \begin{align*} g(u) :={}& \begin{vmatrix} 1 & \wp(a) & \wp'(a) \\ 1 & \wp(b) & \wp'(b) \\ 1 & \wp(u) & \wp'(u) \\ \end{vmatrix} \\ ={}& \wp'(u) (\wp(b) - \wp(a)) + \wp(u)(\wp'(a) - \wp'(b)) + \wp(a) \wp'(b) - \wp'(a) \wp(b). \end{align*} $$ The determinant is $g(c)$.

First suppose $a\not\equiv -b\pmod Λ$, so $\wp(b) - \wp(a) \neq 0$ (as $a\not\equiv b \pmod Λ$). Then $g$ has a triple pole at $0$, so $\deg(g) = 3$. Two zeros of $g$ are clearly $a$ and $b$. Sheet 2 Q10 tells us the remaining zero is $-a-b$. Therefore for $c$ distinct from $a,b$, we have $a+b+c \in Λ$ iff $g(c) = 0$.

Now suppose $a\equiv -b\pmod Λ$, so $\wp(b) - \wp(a)= 0$. Then $g$ has a double pole at $0$, so $\deg(g) = 2$. Then the two zeros of $g$ are $a$ and $b$, so $g(u)\neq 0$ for all $u\not\in \set{a,b}$.
