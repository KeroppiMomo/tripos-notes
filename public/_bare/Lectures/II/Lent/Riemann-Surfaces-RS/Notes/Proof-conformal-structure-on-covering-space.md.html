Existence

Choose an atlas $\mathcal{A}$ for $X$ and a covering $\tilde X = \bigcup_{i\in I} \tilde U_i$ of $X$ by open sets such that $p|_{\tilde U_i}: \tilde U_i \to p(\tilde U_i) =: U_i$ is a homeomorphism and such that there exists a chart $(U_i, ϕ_i)\in \mathcal{A}$ for all $i\in I$.

Define $$ \begin{align*} \tilde{\mathcal{A}} = \sb{(\tilde U_i, \tilde ϕ_i := ϕ_i \circ p|_{\tilde U_i})}{i\in I}. \end{align*} $$ This is an atlas for $\tilde X$: if $\tilde U_i \cap \tilde U_j \neq \emptyset$, then $U_i \cap U_j = p(\tilde U_i \cap \tilde U_j) \neq \emptyset$, so $$ \begin{align*} \tilde ϕ_j \circ \tilde ϕ_i\inv = ϕ_j \circ p|_{\tilde U_j} \circ p|_{\tilde U_i}\inv \circ ϕ_i\inv = ϕ_j \circ ϕ_i\inv \end{align*} $$ is analytic.

Then $p$ is analytic, because $$ \begin{align*} ϕ_j \circ p \circ \tilde ϕ_i\inv = ϕ_j \circ p \circ p|_{\tilde U_i}\inv \circ ϕ_i\inv = ϕ_j \circ ϕ_i\inv \end{align*} $$ is analytic.

Uniqueness

Suppose $(V, ψ)$ is a chart on $\tilde X$ such that $ϕ_i \circ p \circ ψ\inv$ is analytic for all $i$. But this means $\tilde ϕ_i \circ ψ\inv$ is analytic. As this is a bijection, its inverse $ψ \circ \tilde ϕ_i\inv$ is also analytic. Thus $\mathcal{A}\cup \set{(V, ψ)}$ is also an atlas.
