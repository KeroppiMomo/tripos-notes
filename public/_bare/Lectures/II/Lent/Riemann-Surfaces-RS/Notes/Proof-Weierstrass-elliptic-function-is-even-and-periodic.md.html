$\wp$ is even because $Λ = -Λ$.

For periodicity, take derivative. As the series converges locally uniformly, we can do so term-by-term: $$ \begin{align*} \wp'(z) = -2 \sum_{ω\in Λ} \frac{1}{(z-ω)^3}, \end{align*} $$ which is clearly $Λ$-periodic. Hence there are constants $c_i\in \C$ (given by appropriate line integrals of $\wp'$) such that $\wp(z + ω_i) = \wp(z) + c_i$ for all $z\in \C$, $i = 1,2$. Letting $z = -ω_i/2$ and using $\wp(z) = \wp(-z)$, we get $c_i = 0$. Hence $\wp$ is $Λ$-periodic.
