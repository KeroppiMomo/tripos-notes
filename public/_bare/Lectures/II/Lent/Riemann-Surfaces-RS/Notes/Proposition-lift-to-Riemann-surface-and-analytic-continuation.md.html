Let $(f,D)$ be a function element, $\mathcal{F}$ be the complete analytic function of $(f,D)$, and $π: R_\mathcal{F}\to \C$ be the associated Riemann surface.

If $γ: [0,1]\to \C$ is a path such that $γ(0) \in D$, the following are equivalent:

  1. there is a lift $\tilde γ$ of $γ$ with $\tilde γ(0) = (γ(0), f) \in [f,D]$;

  2. there is an analytic continuation $(g,E)\approx (f,D)$ with $γ(1) \in E$.
