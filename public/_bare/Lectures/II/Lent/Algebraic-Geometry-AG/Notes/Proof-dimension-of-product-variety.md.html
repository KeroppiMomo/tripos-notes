If $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ is a transcendence basis for $k(X)$ and $y_1, \ldots, y_m$ is a transcendence basis for $k(Y)$, then $x_1, \ldots, x_n, y_1, \ldots, y_m$ is a transcendence basis for $k(X \times Y)\simeq k(X) \otimes_k k(Y)$. (Any function on $X \times Y$ is determined by its projections onto $X$ and $Y$.)
