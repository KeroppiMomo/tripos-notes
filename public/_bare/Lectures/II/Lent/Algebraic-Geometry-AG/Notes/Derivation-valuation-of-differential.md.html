$\d π_P$ is a basis for $Ω_V$, so any $ω\in Ω_V$ can be written as $h \d π_P$.

If $π_P, π_P'$ are two local parameters, then $π_P' = u \cdot π_P$ for some unit $u\in O_{V,P}$. Because $\d u\in{}$$Ω_{V,P} = O_{V,P} \d π_P$, there exists $f\in O_{V,P}$ such that $\d u = f \d π_P$, so $$ \begin{align*} \d π_P' = (fπ_P + u) \d π_P. \end{align*} $$ Because $ν_P(fπ_P) \geq 1$ and $ν_P(u) = 0$, the ultrametric inequality gives $ν_P(fπ_P + u) = 0$.
