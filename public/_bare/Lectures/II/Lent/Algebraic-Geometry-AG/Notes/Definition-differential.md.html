Let $K/k$ be a field extension.

The space of differentials $Ω_{K/k}$ is the quotient $K$-vector space $M/N$, where $$ \begin{align*} M = \langle δx\mid x\in K\rangle \end{align*} $$ is the $K$-vector space spanned by formal symbols $δx$, and $N$ is the subspace of rules of ordinary calculus: $$ \begin{align*} N = \langle δ(x+y) - δx - δy, δ(xy) - xδy - yδx, δa \mid x,y\in K, a\in k\rangle. \end{align*} $$ We define $\d x$ to be the coset $δx + N$, The exterior derivative is the $k$-linear map $\d : K \to Ω_{K/k}$.

Quotient rule and power rule follow formally.

For $f\in k(X_1, \ldots, X_n)$ a rational function and $y = f(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ for some $x_1, \ldots, x_n\in K$, we have $$ \begin{align*} \d y = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdfd{f}{X_i}(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \d x_i. \end{align*} $$
