$k(X)/k$ is finitely generated (because the function field of any affine patch is generated by the coordinates), so $k(X) = k(x_1, \ldots, x_n, y)$ where $\set{x_1, \ldots, x_n}$ is a transcendence basis and $y$ is algebraic over $k(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$. Let $f\in k[X_1, \ldots, X_n, T]$ be a minimal polynomial of $y$.

Note $$ \begin{align*} k[x_1, \ldots, x_n] &\isom k[X_1, \ldots, X_n], \\ k[x_1, \ldots, x_n, y] &\isom k[X_1, \ldots, X_n, T] / (f). \end{align*} $$ Taking the field of fractions on both sides, we get $$ \begin{align*} k(X) = k(x_1, \ldots, x_n, y) \isom k(Z(f)) \end{align*} $$ where $Z(f) \subseteq \mathbb{A}^{n+1}$.

Hence by the geometric-algebraic correspondence, $X$ is birational to $Z(f)$.
