WLOG assume $Y\subseteq Z\subseteq \mathbb{A}^r$ are affine. Let $z_1, \ldots, z_n$ be a transcendence basis for $k(Z)$. WLOG, by cancelling denominators, assume these are in $A(Z)$. There exists some polynomial $g\in I(Y) \setminus I(Z)$. Then $z_1, \ldots, z_n, g$ is not algebraically independent (by transcendence degree) in $k(Z)$, so there exists $f$ a polynomial with $f(z_1, \ldots, z_n, g) = 0$ in $A(Z)$ Since $f$ is irreducible, $g\not\mid f$, so as a single-variable polynomial in $(n+1)$-st coordinates, $f$ has non-zero constant term. So in $A(Y)$, since $g = 0$, we have algebraic relation on $z_1, \ldots, z_n$. So $\op{trdeg}_k k(Y) < n$ as needed.
