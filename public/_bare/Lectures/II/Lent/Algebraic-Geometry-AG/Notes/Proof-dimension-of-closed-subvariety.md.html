Recall dimension equals transcendence degree of the function field.

By restricting to an affine patch (which does not change the function field so dimension), WLOG assume $Y\subseteq Z\subseteq \mathbb{A}^r$ are affine.

Let $y_1, \ldots, y_n\in k(Y)$ be a transcendence basis of $k(Y)/k$, and by clearing denominators, WLOG assume $y_1, \ldots, y_n\in A(Y)$. Viewing in $A(Z)$, $y_1, \ldots, y_n\in A(Z)$ is still algebraically independent. Suppose $\op{trdeg}_k Y = \op{trdeg}_k Z$, so $y_1, \ldots, y_n$ is a transcendence basis of $k(X)/k$.

As $Y\subsetneq Z$, there exists some $g\in I(Y)\setminus I(Z)$. Viewing in $k(Z)$, $g\neq 0$ , so there exists a minimal polynomial $f\in k[Y_1, \ldots, Y_n, T]$ such that $$f(y_1, \ldots, y_n, g) = 0 \qquad \text{in }k(Z).$$ Now viewing this in $k(Y)$, we get $$f(y_1, \ldots, y_n, 0) = 0 \qquad \text{in }k(Y),$$ which is a non-zero algebraic relation as $T \not\mid f$ by irreducibility. This contradicts that $y_1, \ldots, y_n$ are algebraically independent.
