Let $ϕ: X\to Y$ be a non-constant morphism of (possibly singular) curves.

$ϕ$ is surjective. For all but finitely many $P\in X$, $ϕ$ is unramified at $P$.

Furthermore, for all $Q\in Y$, $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{P\in ϕ\inv(Q)} e_P = \deg ϕ \end{align*} $$ where $e_P$ is the ramification degree at $P$ and $\deg ϕ$ is the degree of $ϕ$.
