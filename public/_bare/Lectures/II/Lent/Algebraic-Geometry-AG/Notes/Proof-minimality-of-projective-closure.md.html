For any $F\in I(Y)$, its dehomogenisation $F_\ast = F(1, x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ vanishes on $U_0 \cap Y$ so $F_\ast\in I(U_0 \cap Y)$. As $U_0 \cap Y \supseteq X$, we have $F_\ast\in I(X)$, so $F = x_0^r (F_\ast)^\ast \in I(X)^\ast$ (where $r$ is the maximal such that $x_0^r \mid F$). Hence $I(Y) \subseteq I(X)^\ast$. Applying $Z$ on both sides yields $Y \supseteq Z(I(X)^\ast)\equiv X^\ast$.
