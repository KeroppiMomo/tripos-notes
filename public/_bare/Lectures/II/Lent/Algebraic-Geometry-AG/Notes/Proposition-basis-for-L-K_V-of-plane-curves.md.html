Let $V = Z(f)\subseteq \mathbb{P}^2$ be a plane curve of degree $d\geq 3$, not contained in $\set{x_0 = 0}$. Letting $x = x_1/x_0$, $y = x_2/x_0$, $$ \begin{align*} \sb{x^r y^s}{r,s\geq 0, \; 0\leq r+s\leq d-3} \end{align*} $$ is a basis for $L$$($$K_V$$)$.
