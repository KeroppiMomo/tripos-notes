Let $ϕ: V\to W$ be a non-constant morphism between curves, so dominant and induces a field embedding $k(W)\hookrightarrow k(V)$. Fix any $t\in k(W) \setminus k$. (Since $t$ and $ϕ^\ast t\not\in k$, $\d t$ spans $Ω_W$ and $\d (ϕ^\ast t)$ spans $Ω_V$.)

Define the pullback of $ϕ$ as the injective $k$-linear map $$ \begin{align*} ϕ^\ast: Ω_V &\to Ω_W \\ f \d t &\mapsto (ϕ^\ast f) \d(ϕ^\ast t). \end{align*} $$

This does not depend on the choice of $t$. (Proof)

If $ω\in Ω_W$, then for $P\in V$ and $Q = ϕ(P)\in W$, $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(ϕ^\ast(ω)) = e_P ν_{Q}(ω) + e_P - 1. \end{align*} $$ where $e_P$ is the ramification degree. In particular $ν_P(ϕ^\ast (\d π_Q)) = e_P - 1$ for a local parameter $π_Q$ of $W$ at $Q$. (Proof)
