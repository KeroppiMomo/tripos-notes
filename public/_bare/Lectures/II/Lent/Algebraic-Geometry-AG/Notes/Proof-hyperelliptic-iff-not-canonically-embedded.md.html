$K_V$ is not canonically embedded iff there exists $P,Q\in V$ such that $$l(K_V-P-Q) \geq l(K_V)-1 = g-1.$$ Let $D = P+Q$. By Riemann-Roch, this is equivalent to $$ \begin{align*} l(D) = 1-g+\deg D + l(K_V - D) \geq 2. \end{align*} $$ This occurs iff $l(D) = 2$: since there is no non-constant degree 1 morphism (i.e. isomorphism) $V\to \mathbb{P}^1$, $l(P) = 1$, so $l(D) \leq l(P)+1 = 2$. We showed this is equivalent to $V$ being hyperelliptic.
