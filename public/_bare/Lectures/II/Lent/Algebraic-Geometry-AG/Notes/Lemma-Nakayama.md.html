Let $R$ be a ring, $J$ be an ideal of $R$, $M$ be an $R$-module, and $N$ be an $R$-submodule of $M$.

Suppose $M$ is finitely generated.

  1. If $JM = M$, then there exists $r\in J$ such that $(1+r)M = 0$.

  2. If $JM + N = M$, then there exists $r\in J$ such that $(1+r)M \subseteq N$.

Proof

Note if $J$ is the unique maximal ideal of $R$ (meaning $R$ is a local ring), then $1+r$ is a unit for any $r\in J$, so we obtain $M \subseteq N$.
