Let $g = u \cdot π_P^n$ for $u\in O_{V,P}$ a unit and $n = ν_P(g) \geq 1$. Same technique as before: since $\d u\in Ω_{V,P}$, there exists $h\in O_{V,P}$ such that $\d u = h \cdot \d π_P$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \d g = π_P^n \d u + nπ_P^{n-1} u \d π_P = (hπ_P + nu) π_P^{n-1} \d π_P. \end{align*} $$ Because $n\neq 0$, we have $ν_P(hπ_P) \geq 1 > 0 = π_P(u) = π_P(nu)$, so the ultrametric inequality gives $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(\d g) = ν_P(hπ_P + nu) + ν_P(π_P^{n-1}) = 0 + n-1 = n-1. \end{align*} $$
