Pick any $ω\in Ω_W \setminus \set{0}$. Then $ϕ^\ast ω$${}\in Ω_V \setminus \set{0}$, so $K_W = \op{div}(ω)$ and $K_V = \op{div}(ϕ^\ast ω)$ are canonical divisors.

Using $\deg(K_V) = 2g(V) - 2$ and likewise for $W$, $$ \begin{align*} \deg(K_V) ={}& \sum_{P\in V} ν_P(ϕ^\ast ω) \\ ={}& \sum_{Q\in W} \sum_{P\in ϕ\inv(Q)} ν_P(ϕ^\ast ω) \\ ={}& \sum_{Q\in W} \sum_{P\in ϕ\inv(Q)} (e_P ν_Q(ω) + e_P - 1) \tag{1}\\ ={}& \sum_{Q\in W} ν_Q(ω) \sum_{P\in ϕ\inv(Q)} e_P + \sum_{P\in V} (e_P -1) \\ ={}& \deg(ϕ) \sum_{Q\in W} ν_Q(ω) + \sum_{P\in V} (e_P - 1) \tag{2} \\ ={}& \deg(ϕ) \deg(K_W) + \sum_{P\in V} (e_P - 1). \end{align*} $$ ($(1)$, $(2)$)
