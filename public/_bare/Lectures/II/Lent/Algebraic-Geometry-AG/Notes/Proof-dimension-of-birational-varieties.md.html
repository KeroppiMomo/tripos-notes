A birational map $ϕ: X \dashrightarrow Y$ gives an isomorphism $ϕ: U \to V$ between dense open sets $U\subseteq X$ and $V\subseteq Y$.

For any $P\in U$, since $P$ is regular for $ϕ$ and $ϕ(P)\in V$ is regular for $ϕ\inv(P)$, we noted that $\d ϕ_P: T_{U,P} \to T_{V, ϕ(P)}$ is a vector space isomorphism, so $\dim T_{U,P} = \dim T_{V,ϕ(P)}$.

Since the set $X^\text{sm}$ of smooth points is dense and $U$ is open, there exists $P\in X^\text{sm} \cap U$, so $$ \begin{align*} \dim X = \dim T_{U, P} = \dim T_{V, ϕ(P)} \geq \dim Y. \end{align*} $$ By symmetry, $\dim X \leq \dim Y$ as well.
