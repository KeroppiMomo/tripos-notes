For any irreducible affine or projective variety $X$, $\dim X$${}={}$$\op{trdeg}_k{}$$k(X)$.

Proof TODO

If there exists $f: X\dashrightarrow Y$ a dominant rational map, then it induces a field embedding $f^\ast: k(Y) \hookrightarrow k(X)$. The transcendence basis of $k(Y)$ remains algebraically independent under $f^\ast$, so $\dim(X) \geq k(Y)$.
