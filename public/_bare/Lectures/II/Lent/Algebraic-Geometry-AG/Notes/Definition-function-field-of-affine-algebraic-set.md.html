Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ be an irreducible affine algebraic set. (So $A(X)$ is an integral domain.)

The function field of $X$, denoted $k(X)$, is the field of fractions of $A(X)$. The elements are called rational functions or meromorphic functions.

Let $ϕ\in k(X)$ and $p\in X$. If $ϕ$ has a representative $f/g$ where $f,g\in A(X)$ and $g(p)\neq 0$, then we say $ϕ$ is regular at $p$.
