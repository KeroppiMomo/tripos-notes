Suppose $u,t\in k(W)\setminus k$. Because $\op{trdeg}_k k(W) = 1$, there is a non-zero polynomial $p\in k[U,T]^\times$ such that $p(u,t) = 0$. Taking the differential, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \pdfd{p}{U}(u,t) \d u + \pdfd{p}{T}(u,t)\d t \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \d u = \underbrace{-\frac{\partial p/\partial T}{\partial p/\partial U}(u,t)}_{h(u,t)\in k(W)} \d t. \end{align*} $$

Furthermore, $ϕ^\ast(p(u,t)) = 0$ implies $p(ϕ^\ast u, ϕ^\ast t) = 0$, and taking the differential, $$ \begin{align*} \d ϕ^\ast u = h(ϕ^\ast u, ϕ^\ast t) \dϕ^\ast t. \end{align*} $$

So if $f \d t = g \d u$, then $f = g\cdot h(u,t)$, and so $$ \begin{align*} ϕ^\ast f \d ϕ^\ast t = ϕ^\ast g \cdot h(ϕ^\ast u, ϕ^\ast t) \d ϕ^\ast t = ϕ^\ast g \d ϕ^\ast u. \end{align*} $$
