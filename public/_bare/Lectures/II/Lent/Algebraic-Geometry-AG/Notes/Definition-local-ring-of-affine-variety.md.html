Let $X$ be an irreducible affine variety, and let $x\in X$.

The local ring of $X$ at $x$ is the localisation of $A(X)$ at $\mathfrak{m}_x$ $$ \begin{align*} O_{X,x} := \sb{f/g\in k(X)}{g\not\in \mathfrak{m}_x} = \sb{f/g\in k(X)}{g(x) \neq 0}. \end{align*} $$ If $ϕ\in k(X)$ is in $O_{X,x}$, we say $ϕ$ is regular at $x$.

For $U$ a Zariski open set of $X$, define $$ \begin{align*} O_X(U) := \bigcap_{x\in U} O_{X,x}. \end{align*} $$
