WLOG let $X,Y$ be affine (if they are projective, consider their affine patches, which are dense open subsets, and rational maps are defined on dense open subsets), $X \subseteq \mathbb{A}^m$, $Y \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$, so $k(X)$ and $k(Y)$ are the fraction field of $A(X)$ and $A(Y)$ respectively.

If $i: k(Y) \hookrightarrow k(X)$, let $\set{y_j}$ be the generators of $k(Y)$ as a $k$-algebra, and write $i(y_j) = a_j/b_j$ where $a_j,b_j\in A(X)$. These are all regular on $X' = X \setminus \bigcup_{j} Z(b_j)$, so we have a map $A(Y) \to A(X')$. By affine algebra/geometry correspondence, we obtain a morphism $X' \to Y$. As $X'$ is dense open, this is a rational map $X \dashrightarrow Y$.
