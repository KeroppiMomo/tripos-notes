Let $ϕ: X\dashrightarrow Y$ be a rational map between quasiprojective varieties $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ and $Y \subseteq \mathbb{P}^m$, and let $P\in X$ be in the domain of $ϕ$. Consider affine patches $X_0 = X \cap \set{x_0 \neq 0}$ containing $P$ and $Y \cap \set{y_0 \neq 0}$ containing $ϕ(P)$. Choosing any representative $(F_0 : \ldots : F_m)$ of $ϕ$ defined at $P$.

The derivative or linearisation of $ϕ$ at $P$ is the linear map $$ \begin{align*} \d ϕ_P: T_{X,P} &\to T_{Y,ϕ(P)}, \\ (v_1, \ldots, v_n) &\mapsto \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdfd{f_1}{x_i}(P) \cdot v_1, \ldots, \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdfd{f_m}{x_i}(P)\cdot v_n\right). \end{align*} $$
