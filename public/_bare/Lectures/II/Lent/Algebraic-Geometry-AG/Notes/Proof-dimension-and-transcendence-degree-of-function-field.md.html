WLOG (by the previous corollary and proposition that birational varieties have isomorphic tangent spaces), assume $X$ is a hypersurface: $X = Z(f) \subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$. Then $\op{trdeg}_k k(Z(f)) = n-1$ for $f$ non-constant irreducible.

Since we only have one polynomial here, the Jacobian is just a vector. $Z(f)$ has dimension $n-1$ unless the $1\times n$ Jacobian is trivial at all points, i.e. all partials of $f$ vanish at all points of $Z(f)\subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$. All partials are then in $I(X) = (f)$, so by degree consideration they are all zero. Hence (as characteristic is zero) $f$ is constant.
