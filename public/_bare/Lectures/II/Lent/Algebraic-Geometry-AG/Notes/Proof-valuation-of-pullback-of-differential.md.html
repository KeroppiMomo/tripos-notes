We showed in general that $ν_P(\d g) = ν_P(g) - 1$ for $g\in k(V)$, so $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(ϕ^\ast(\d π_Q)) = ν_P(\d (ϕ^\ast π_Q)) = ν_P(ϕ^\ast (π_Q)) - 1 = e_P - 1. \end{align*} $$

For $ω\in Ω_W$, write $ω = u π_Q^{ν_Q(ω)} \d π_Q$ for some unit $u\in O_{W,Q}$. Then $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(ϕ^\ast(ω)) = ν_P(ϕ^\ast(u π_Q^{ν_Q(ω)} \d π_Q)) ={}& \underbrace{ν_P(ϕ^\ast u)}_{0} + ν_Q(ω) ν_P(ϕ^\ast π_Q) + ν_P(\d ϕ^\ast π_Q) \\ ={}& e_P ν_Q(ω) + e_P - 1. \end{align*} $$
