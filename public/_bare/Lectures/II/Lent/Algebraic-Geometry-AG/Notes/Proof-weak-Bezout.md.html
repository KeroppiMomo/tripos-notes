Let $W = Z(g)$ where $g$ is a homogeneous polynomial of degree $n$. Consider the divisor $\op{div}(g)$ of $g$ on $V$, i.e. given by $ν_P(\op{div}(g)) = ν_P(g/x_i^d)$ for any homogeneous coordinate $x_i$ with $x_i(P)\neq 0$. It suffices to show $\deg(\op{div}(g)) = mn$.

Let $L$ be any linear form not identically vanishing on $V$, so $\op{div}(L)$ is a hyperplane section and hence $\op{deg}(\op{div}(L)) = m$. Because $g/L^n$ is a rational function, $\op{div}(g)$ is linearly equivalent to $\op{div}(L^n)$, so $$ \begin{align*} \op{deg}(\op{div}(g)) = \op{deg}(\op{div}(L^n)) = \op{deg}(n\op{div}(L)) = mn. \end{align*} $$
