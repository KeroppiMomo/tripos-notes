Theorem (local parameter) open_in_new

Let $C$ be a (possibly singular) curve.

If $P\in C$ is a smooth point, then $\mathfrak{m}_P$ is a principal ideal of the local ring $O_{C,P}$, generated by any generator of $\mathfrak{m}_P/\mathfrak{m}_P^2$ in $O_{C,P}/\mathfrak{m}_P^2$.

Any generator of $\mathfrak{m}_P$ in $O_{C,P}$ is called a local parameter or local coordinate for $C$ at $P$.

Proof

Suppose $C = Z(f)\subseteq \mathbb{A}^2$ is a (possibly singular) plane curve and $P\in C$ is a smooth point. Then $x - x(P)\in \mathfrak{m}_P$ is a local parameter at $P$ iff $\pdfd{f}{y}(P) \neq 0$. (Proof)

Corollary (valuation of rational function) open_in_new

Let $C$ be a (possibly singular) curve and $P$ be a smooth point of $C$.

There is a surjective group homomorphism $$ \begin{align*} ν_P: k(C)^\times \to \Z \end{align*} $$ called the valuation or order of vanishing at $P$ (with the convention $ν_P(0) = \infty$), satisfying the following:

  1. for any $f\in k(C)^\times$ and any local parameter $π_P$, $$ \begin{align*} f = u \cdot π_P^{ν_P(f)} \end{align*} $$ where $u$ is a unit in the local ring $O_{C,P}$.

  2. $$ \begin{align*} O_{C,P} ={}& \sb{f\in k(C)^\times}{ν_P(f) \geq 0} \cup \set{0}, \\ \mathfrak{m}_P ={}& \sb{f\in k(C)^\times}{ν_P(f) \geq 1} \cup \set{0}. \end{align*} $$

  3. The ultrametric or non-archimedean triangle inequality holds: $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(f+g) \geq \min\set{ν_P(f), ν_P(g)}, \end{align*} $$ and if $ν_P(f) \neq ν_P(g)$, equality holds.

Proof

Therefore if $f\in k(C)$, then $f$ or $1/f$ is regular at $P$, because $ν_P(1/f) = -ν_P(f)$.

Proposition (degree of morphism) open_in_new

Let $ϕ: X\to Y$ be a non-constant morphism of (possibly affine, possibly singular) curves.

$ϕ$ is dominant, and hence induces a field embedding $ϕ^\ast: k(Y) \hookrightarrow k(X)$. Moreover $k(X)/ϕ^\ast k(Y)$ is a finite field extension.

The degree of $ϕ$ is $[k(X) : ϕ^\ast k(Y)]$, denoted $\deg(ϕ)$.

Proof

If $\deg(ϕ) = 1$, then $ϕ^\ast: k(X) \xrightarrow{\sim} k(Y)$ is an isomorphism, so $ϕ$ is birational. If $X,Y$ are (smooth, projective) curves, then $ϕ$ is an isomorphism.

Definition (ramification degree) open_in_new

Let $ϕ: X\to Y$ be a non-constant morphism of (possibly affine, possibly singular) curves. Let $P\in X$ be smooth on $X$ and $Q := ϕ(P)$ be smooth on $Y$.

The ramification degree of $ϕ$ at $P$ is $$ \begin{align*} e_P := ν_P(ϕ^\ast(π_Q)) \end{align*} $$ where $π_Q$ is any local parameter for $Y$ at $Q$, $ϕ^\ast: k(Y) \hookrightarrow k(X)$ is the pullback, and $ν_P$ is the valuation at $P$.

We say $ϕ$ is unramified at $P$ if $e_P = 1$.

$(ϕ^\ast\mathfrak{m}_Q) O_{V,P} = {\mathfrak{m}_P}^{e_P}$.

If $f\in k(X) \setminus k$ is a rational function and $P\in X$ is unramified and not a pole of $f$, then $f - f(P)$ is a local parameter for $X$ at $P$. (Proof)
