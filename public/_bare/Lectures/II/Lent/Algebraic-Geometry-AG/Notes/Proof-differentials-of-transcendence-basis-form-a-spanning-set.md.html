By the primitive element theorem, let $K = k(x_1, \ldots, x_n, α)$ for some $α\in K$ algebraic over $K_0 := k(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$. As noted before, because any $y\in K$ can be expressed as $y = f(x_1, \ldots, x_n, α)$ for some $f\in k(X_1, \ldots, X_n, A)$, and $$ \begin{align*} \d y = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdfd{f}{X_i}(x_1, \ldots, x_n, α) \d x_i + \pdfd{f}{A} (x_1, \ldots, x_n, α) \d α, \end{align*} $$ $\set{\d x_1, \ldots,\d x_n, \d α}$ spans $Ω_{K/k}$.

To establish a $K$-linear relation of $\d α$ and $\d x_1, \ldots, \d x_n$, let $g\in K_0[A]$ be the minimal polynomial of $α$. Write $G \in k(X_1, \ldots, X_n, A)$ such that $g(A) = G(x_1, \ldots, x_n, A)$. Since $0 = g(α) = G(x_1, \ldots, x_n, α)$, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdfd{G}{X_i}(x_1, \ldots, x_n, α) \d x_i + g'(α) \d α. \end{align*} $$ As $g$ is minimal, $g'(α) \neq 0$, so $\set{\d x_1, \ldots, \d x_n}$ spans $Ω_{K/k}$.
