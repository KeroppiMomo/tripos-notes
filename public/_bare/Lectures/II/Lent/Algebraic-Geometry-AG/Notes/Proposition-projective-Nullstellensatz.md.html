Let $J \lhd k[x_0, \ldots, x_n]$ be a homogeneous ideal. We work over the projective space ($Z$, $I$).

  • If $Z(J)\neq \emptyset$, then $$ \begin{align*} I(Z(J)) = \sqrt{J} \end{align*} $$ where $\sqrt{J}$ is the radical ideal.

  • If $Z(J) = \emptyset$, then $$ \begin{align*} \sqrt{J} = (x_0, \ldots, x_n) \text{ or }(0). \end{align*} $$
