Since $J$ is proper, there exists a maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}\supseteq J$ (obtained by Zorn's lemma, or due to Noetherianity of $k[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$).

By Hilbert's weak Nullstellensatz, $\mathfrak{m} = (x_1 - a_1, \ldots, x_n - a_n)$ for some $a_1, \ldots, a_n\in k$. Because $\set{(a_1, \ldots, a_n)} = Z(\mathfrak{m}) \subseteq Z(J)$, $Z(J)$ is non-empty.
