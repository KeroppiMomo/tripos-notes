Let $R$ be an integral domain, $\mathfrak{p}$ be a prime ideal and $R_\mathfrak{p}$ be its localisation. Consider the functions $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{ideals of $R$}} &\leftrightarrow \set{\text{ideals of $R_\mathfrak{p}$}} \\ I &\mapsto ϕ(I) \cdot R_\mathfrak{p} = \sb{f/g}{f\in I, g\not\in \mathfrak{p}}, \\ \sb{f\in R}{f/1\in J} = ϕ\inv(J) &\mapsfrom J. \end{align*} $$

  1. If $I$ is an ideal of $R$ but $I \not\subseteq \mathfrak{p}$, then $ϕ(I) R_\mathfrak{p} = R_\mathfrak{p}$.

  2. $ϕ(\mathfrak{p}) R_\mathfrak{p}$ is the unique maximal ideal of $R_\mathfrak{p}$.

  3. $ϕ(ϕ\inv(J)) R_\mathfrak{p} = J$ for any ideal $J$ of $R_\mathfrak{p}$.

  4. They are inverses when restricted to $\set{\text{prime ideals of }R\text{ contained by }\mathfrak{p}} \leftrightarrow \set{\text{prime ideals of }R_\mathfrak{p}}$, giving a bijective correspondence.
