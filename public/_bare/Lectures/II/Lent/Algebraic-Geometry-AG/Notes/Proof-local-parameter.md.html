WLOG, by taking an affine patch and applying a coordinate change (which does not affect $O_{C,P}$), suppose $C\subseteq \mathbb{A}^n$ is affine, $P = (0, \ldots, 0)$, and the one-dimensional space $T_{P,C}$ is $\set{x_2 = \ldots = x_n = 0}$.

By definition $$ \begin{align*} \mathfrak{m}_P = x_1 O_{C,P} + x_2 O_{C,P}+ \ldots + x_n O_{C,P}. \end{align*} $$ Recall the natural vector space isomorphism $D: \mathfrak{m}_P/\mathfrak{m}_P^2 \to (T_{C,P})^\ast$ defined as $D(f) = \d f_P|_{T_{C,P}}$, and note $D(x_2), \ldots, D(x_n)$ are all zero. Therefore $x_2, \ldots, x_n\in \mathfrak{m}_P^2$ and hence $$ \begin{align*} \mathfrak{m}_P = x_1 O_{C,P} + \mathfrak{m}_P^2. \end{align*} $$

Apply Nakayama (with $R = O_{C,P}$ a local ring, $J = \mathfrak{m}_P$ the unique maximal ideal, $M = \mathfrak{m}_P$ and $N = x_1 O_{C,P}$) to obtain $$ \begin{align*} \mathfrak{m}_P \subseteq x_1 O_{C,P} \end{align*} $$ so indeed $\mathfrak{m}_P = x_1 O_{C,P}$, where $x_1$ is a generator of $\mathfrak{m}_P/\mathfrak{m}_P^2$ in $O_{C,P}/\mathfrak{m}_P^2$.
