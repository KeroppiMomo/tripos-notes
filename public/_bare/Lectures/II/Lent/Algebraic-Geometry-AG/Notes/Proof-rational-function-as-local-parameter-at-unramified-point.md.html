$f$ extends to a morphism $ϕ = (1:f): V \to \mathbb{P}^1$, and let $t = x_1/x_0$ be the affine coordinates of $\mathbb{P}^1$. $t - t(ϕ(P))$ is a local parameter of $\mathbb{P}^1$ at $ϕ(P) = (1:f(P))$, so $$ \begin{align*} 1 = e_P = ν_P(ϕ^\ast(t)) = ν_P(f - f(P)) \end{align*} $$ and hence $f - f(P)$ is a local parameter of $V$ at $P$.
