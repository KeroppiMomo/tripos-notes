Suppose there is an ascending chain of ideals in $R_\mathfrak{p}$ $$ \begin{align*} J_1 \subseteq J_2 \subseteq \ldots \qquad \subseteq R_\mathfrak{p}. \end{align*} $$ Taking $ϕ\inv$, we obtain an ascending chain of ideals in $R$ $$ \begin{align*} ϕ\inv(J_1) \subseteq ϕ\inv(J_2) \subseteq \ldots \qquad \subseteq R \end{align*} $$ which must stabilise by hypothesis, i.e. $ϕ\inv(J_n) = ϕ\inv(J_k)$ for all $n>k$. Using $J = ϕ(ϕ\inv(J)) R_\mathfrak{p}$, we see $J_n = J_k$ for all $n>k$.
