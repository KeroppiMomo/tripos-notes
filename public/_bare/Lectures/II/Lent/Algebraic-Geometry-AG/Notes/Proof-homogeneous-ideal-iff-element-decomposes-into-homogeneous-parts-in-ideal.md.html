Homogeneous $\implies$ $f_d\in J$ for all $f\in J$

Suppose $J$ is homogeneous, generated by a set $S$ of homogeneous polynomials. Let $f\in J$, meaning $f = \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i g_i$ for $g_i\in S$ and $h_i\in k[x_0, \ldots, x_n]$. Letting $d_i = \deg g_i$, we have $$ \begin{align*} f_d = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (h_i)_{d - d_i} g_i \in J \end{align*} $$ where $(h_i)_{d-d_i}$ is the degree $(d-d_i)$-homogeneous part of $h_i$.

$f_d\in J$ for all $f\in J$ $\implies$ homogeneous

Take $S = \sb{f_d}{f\in J, d\in \Z_{\geq 0}}$ where $f_d$ is the degree $d$-homogeneous part of $f$. Then $J = (S)$.
