Consider the affine cone $C(Z_p(J))$.

$Z_p(J)\neq \emptyset$

Since $C(Z_p(J)) = Z_a(J)$, taking $I_a$ on both sides gives $I_p(Z_p(J)) = I_a(Z_a(J))$ which equals $\sqrt{J}$ by the affine Nullstellensatz.

$Z_p(J) = \emptyset$

Since $Z_a(J) \setminus \set{0} = q\inv(Z_p(J)) = \emptyset$, either $Z_a(J) = \set{0}$ or $\emptyset$, so $\sqrt{J} = I_a(Z_a(J)) = (x_1, \ldots, x_n)$ or $(0)$.
