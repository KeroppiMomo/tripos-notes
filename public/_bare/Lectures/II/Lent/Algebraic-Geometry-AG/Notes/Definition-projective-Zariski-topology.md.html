The Zariski topology on $\mathbb{P}^n$ is, equivalently,

  • the quotient topology induced by $q: \mathbb{A}^{n+1} \setminus \set{0} \twoheadrightarrow \mathbb{P}^n: (x_0, \ldots, x_n) \mapsto (x_0 : \ldots : x_n)$ where $\mathbb{A}^{n+1}$ is endowed with the Zariski topology;

  • the topology such that the closed sets are precisely the projective algebraic sets.

(Proof)
