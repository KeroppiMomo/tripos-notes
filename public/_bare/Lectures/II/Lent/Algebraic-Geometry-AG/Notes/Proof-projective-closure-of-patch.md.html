Let $X_0 = U_0 \cap X$. Since $X \supseteq X_0$, we showed $X \supseteq X_0^\ast$.

Now $X \setminus X_0^\ast \subseteq X \setminus X_0 \subseteq X \cap \set{x_0=0}$, so $$ \begin{align*} X = (X \cap \set{x_0=0}) \cup X_0^\ast, \end{align*} $$ both of which are closed. By hypothesis $X \cap \set{x_0 = 0}$ is proper and $X$ is irreducible, so $X = X_0^\ast$.
