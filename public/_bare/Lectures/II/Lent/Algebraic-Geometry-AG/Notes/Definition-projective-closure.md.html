Let $X\subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ be a projective algebraic set.

The projective closure of $X$ is $X^\ast :={}$$Z$$($$I$$(X)$$^\ast$$)$.

Projective closures are not isomorphism invariant.

If $Y\subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ is any projective algebraic set with $U_0 \cap Y$${}\supseteq X$, then $Y \supseteq X^\ast$. (Proof)

If $X\subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ is an irreducible projective variety and $X \not\subseteq \set{x_0 = 0}$, then $(U_0 \cap X)^\ast = X$. (Proof)
