Let $V$ be a curve.

The space of rational differentials on $V$ is the $k(V)$-vector space $Ω_V :={}$$Ω_{k(V)/k}$.

The module of differentials regular at $P$ is the $O_{V,P}$-module $$ \begin{align*} Ω_{V,P} := \sb{\sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i \d g_i}{f_1, \ldots, f_n, g_1, \ldots, g_n\in O_{V,P}}. \end{align*} $$

$Ω_V$ is spanned by $\d t$ for any $t\in k(V)\setminus k$, because $\op{trdeg}_kk(V) = 1$.
