It suffices to show $Δ_{\mathbb{P}^n}$ is closed in $\mathbb{P}^n \times \mathbb{P}^n$, because then $$ \begin{align*} Δ_{X} = Δ_{\mathbb{P}^n} \cap (X \times X) \end{align*} $$ is closed in $X\times X\subseteq \mathbb{P}^n \times \mathbb{P}^n$ by subspace topology.

Via the Segre embedding $σ_{n,n}: \mathbb{P}^n \times \mathbb{P}^n \to Σ_{n,n}$, a point $P\in Σ_{n,n}$ is in $Δ_{\mathbb{P}^n}$ iff $z_{ij}(P) = z_{ji}(P)$, so $$ \begin{align*} σ_{n,n}(Δ_{\mathbb{P}^n}) = Z(z_{ij} - z_{ji} \mid 0\leq i,j\leq n) \end{align*} $$ which is closed in $Σ_{n,n}$.
