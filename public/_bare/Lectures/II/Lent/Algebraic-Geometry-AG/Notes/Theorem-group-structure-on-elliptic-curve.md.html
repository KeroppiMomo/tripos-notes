Let $E$ be an elliptic curve.

  • Every degree 1 divisor is linearly equivalent to a point.

  • There is a bijection $$ \begin{align*} E &\xrightarrow{\sim} \op{Cl}^0(E) \\ P &\mapsto [P - P_0]. \end{align*} $$

  • This gives $E$ the structure of an abelian group, where $P+Q$ is the unique point linearly equivalent to $P+Q-P_0$.
