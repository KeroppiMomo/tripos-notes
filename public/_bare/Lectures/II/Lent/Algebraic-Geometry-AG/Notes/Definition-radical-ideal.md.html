Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I$ be an ideal in $R$.

The radical ideal of $I$ is $$ \begin{align*} \sqrt{I} := \sb{r\in R}{r^m\in I \text{ for some }m\in \Z_{>0}}. \end{align*} $$

Indeed $\sqrt{I}$ is an ideal. (Proof)

$\set{\text{maximal ideals}}\subseteq \set{\text{prime ideals}} \subseteq \set{\text{radical ideals}}$.
