Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{P}^n$ be a projective variety and suppose $X \not \subseteq \set{x_0 = 0}$. Take the affine patch $X_0 := U_0 \cap X$.

Then the function fields of $X$ and $X_0$ are isomorphic via $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: k(X) &\to k(X_0) \\ \frac{f(x_0, \ldots, x_n)}{g(x_0, \ldots, x_n)} &\mapsto \frac{f(1, x_1, \ldots, x_n)}{g(1, x_1, \ldots, x_n)}. \end{align*} $$ Moreover if $P\in X_0$, then the local rings of $X$ and $X_0$ are also isomorphic: $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: O_{X,P} \to O_{X_0, P}. \end{align*} $$
