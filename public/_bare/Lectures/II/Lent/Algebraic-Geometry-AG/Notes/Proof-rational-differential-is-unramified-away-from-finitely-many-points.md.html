Fix any $g\in k(V) \setminus k$, so $\d g$ spans $Ω_V$. Write $ω = f \d g$ for some $f\in k(V)$. Because $ν_P(ω) = ν_P(f) + ν_P(\d g)$, it suffices to show $ν_P(f)$ and $ν_P(\d g)$ equal zero away from finitely many points. We already showed this for $f$.

$g$ extends to a morphism $ϕ = (1:g): V \to \mathbb{P}^1$. Only finitely many points are ramified for $g$, and $g$ has only finitely many poles. For $P$ unramified and not a pole, we showed $g - g(P)$ is a local parameter of $V$ at $P$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} ν_P(\d g) = ν_P(1 \cdot \d (g - g(P))) = ν_P(1) = 0. \end{align*} $$
