Suppose $T \vDash ϕ$. Then $(ϕ\Rightarrow \bot)$ is false in any model $T$, so $T \cup \set{(ϕ \Rightarrow \bot)}$ is not satisfiable. By the model existence theorem, $T \cup \set{(ϕ \Rightarrow \bot)}$ is not consistent, i.e. $T \cup \set{(ϕ \Rightarrow\bot)} \vdash \bot$. By the deduction theorem, $T \vdash ((ϕ \Rightarrow \bot) \Rightarrow \bot)$. Using $(((ϕ\Rightarrow \bot)\Rightarrow \bot) \Rightarrow ϕ)$ an axiom of type 3, we obtain a $T$-proof of $ϕ$.
