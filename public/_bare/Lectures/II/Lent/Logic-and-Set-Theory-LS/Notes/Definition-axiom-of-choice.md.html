In the language of set theory, let $x$ be a set. $f$ is a choice function for $x$ if it satisfies $$ \begin{align*} \op{cf}(f, x) := \bigl(f: x\to \bigcup x\bigr) \wedge \forall y \bigl(y\in x \wedge y\neq \emptyset \Rightarrow f(y) \in y\bigr). \end{align*} $$

The axiom of choice is the $\in$-formula $$ \begin{align*} \mathsf{AC} := \forall x \exists f\ \op{cf}(f,x). \end{align*} $$
