Let $D$ be a $T$-proof of $ϕ$ and $E$ be a $(T \cup \set{ϕ})$-proof of $ψ$. Then $DE$ is a $(T \cup \set{ϕ})$-proof of $ψ$.

The first occurrence of $ϕ$ is in $D$, so satisfies either $A_T$, $B$ or $C$. Subsequent occurrences of $ϕ$ then satisfy the same condition (as $A_T$ and $B$ depend only on $ϕ$ and $C$ depends only on previous formulas in the proof). Hence $DE$ is a $T$-proof.
