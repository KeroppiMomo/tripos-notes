Let $a$ be a signature, $\mathbf{X}$ be an $a$-structure, and $\mathfrak{I}$ and $\mathfrak{J}$ be $\mathbb{X}$-interpretations.

  1. If $t$ is an $a$-term where $\mathfrak{I}|_{\op{occ}(t)} = \mathfrak{J}|_{\op{occ}(t)}$, then $\mathfrak{J}(t) = \mathfrak{J}(t)$.

  2. If $ϕ$ is an $a$-formula where $\mathfrak{I}|_{\op{free}(ϕ)} = \mathfrak{J}|_{\op{free}(ϕ)}$, then $\mathbf{X}, \mathfrak{I}\vDash ϕ$ iff $\mathbb{X}, \mathfrak{J} \vDash ϕ$.

Proof

If $\op{occ}(t) = \set{v_0, \ldots, v_k}$, denote $$ \begin{align*} t(x_0, \ldots, x_k) := \mathfrak{I}\tfrac{x_0}{v_0} \ldots \tfrac{x_k}{v_k}(t) \end{align*} $$ and if $\op{free}(ϕ) = \set{v_0, \ldots, v_k}$, denote $$ \begin{align*} \mathbf{X} \vDash ϕ(x_0, \ldots, x_k) \quad :\iff \quad \mathbf{X}, \mathfrak{I}\tfrac{x_0}{v_0} \ldots \tfrac{x_k}{v_k} \vDash ϕ. \end{align*} $$ This result shows these do not depend on the choice of interpretation $\mathfrak{I}$.
