Let $v$ be a valuation and $T \subseteq \mathrm{Fml}$ be a subset of formulas.

Say $v$ is a model of $T$ if $v(ϕ) = 1$ for all $ϕ\in T$.

Say $T$ is satisfiable if it has a model.

Say $T$ semantically entails a formula $ϕ\in \mathrm{Fml}$ (denoted $T \vDash ϕ$) if $v(ϕ) = 1$ for all model $v$ of $T$
