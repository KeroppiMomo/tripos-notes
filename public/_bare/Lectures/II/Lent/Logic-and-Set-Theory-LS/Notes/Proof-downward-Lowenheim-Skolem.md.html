We will show the term structure $\mathbf{H}$ of $T_\infty$ we constructed has cardinality $\leq |\mathrm{Fml}^a| =: κ$.

Note $κ \geq \aleph_0$, so if the alphabet $Σ^{a^\ast}$ of some signature has cardinality $κ$, then by Hessenberg, $$ \begin{align*} κ = |Σ^{a^\ast}| \leq |\mathrm{Term}^{a^\ast}| \leq |\mathrm{Fml}^{a^\ast}| \leq |(Σ^{a^\ast})^\ast| = \bigcup_{N\in \N} \sum_{n=0}^{N} \underbrace{|Σ^{a^\ast}|^n}_{κ} = κ. \end{align*} $$

We claim for all $n\geq 1$, $$ \begin{align*} |Σ^{a_n}| = |\mathrm{Term}^{a_n}| = |\mathrm{Fml}^{a_n}| = κ. \end{align*} $$ The signature $a_{n+1}$ is obtained by adding $|\mathrm{Fml}^{a_n} \times V| = κ \times |V| = κ$-many constant symbols (by Hessenberg and induction hypothesis or base case $|\mathrm{Fml}^{a}| = κ$), so $$ \begin{align*} |Σ^{a_{n+1}}| = |Σ^{a_n}| + κ = κ. \end{align*} $$

Finally $$ \begin{align*} |H| \leq |\mathrm{Term}^{a_\infty}| = \Bigl|\bigcup_{n\in \N} \mathrm{Term}^{a_n}\Bigr| \leq \aleph_0 \cdot κ = κ. \end{align*} $$
