Let $K$ be any set. Introduce a new signature $a_+$ by adding $K$-many constant symbols (nullary operations) $$ \begin{align*} \sb{c_k}{k\in K}. \end{align*} $$

Define $$ \begin{align*} ψ_{kl} := \neg\ c_k = c_l, \quad S = \sb{ψ_{kl}}{k,l\in K,k\neq l}. \end{align*} $$

We claim the set $T \cup S$ of $a_+$-sentences is satisfiable. By compactness, we just have to check every finite $T_0 \subseteq T\cup S$ is satisfiable. As $T_0$ only involves finitely many $c_k$'s, say $c_{k_0}, \ldots, c_{k_{n-1}}$. By hypothesis find an $a$-structure $\mathbf{Y}$ which is a model of $T$ of size at least $n$. Expand $\mathbf{Y}$ to an $a_+$-structure $\mathbf{Y}_+$ by interpreting $c_{k_0}, \ldots, c_{k_{n-1}}$ by any $n$ distinct elements of $Y$. Then $\mathbf{Y}_+ \vDash T_0$.

Hence there exists an $a_+$-structure $\mathbf{X}_+$ which is a model of $T \cup S$. As $\mathbf{X}_+ \vDash S$, the cardinality of $X$ must be at least $K$. Moreover the $a$-reduct $\mathbf{X}$ of $\mathbf{X}_+$ is a model of $T$.
