Fix a set $T$ of $a$-formulas and define the equivalence relation on $\mathrm{Term}^a$ by $t \sim t' :\iff T \vdash t = t'$.

The term structure $\mathbf{H}$ of $T$ is an $a$-structure with an underlying set $$ \begin{align*} H = \mathrm{Term}^a/{\sim} \end{align*} $$ and operations and relations $$ \begin{gather*} s^\mathbf{H}([t_0], \ldots, [t_{n-1}]) := [st_0\ldots t_{n-1}], \\ ([t_0], \ldots, [t_{n-1}]) \in r^\mathbf{H} :\iff T\vdash rt_0 \ldots t_{n-1}, \end{gather*} $$ well-defined by congruence properties of $\vdash$.

The term interpretation of $T$ is $$ \begin{align*} \mathfrak{I}^\mathbf{H}(t) := [t]. \end{align*} $$
