Work in a model of $\mathsf{FST}$ + $\mathsf{Repl}$ + $\mathsf{AC}$. For cardinals $κ, λ$, define $$ \begin{align*} κ^λ = \left|\sb{f\in \mathcal{P}(λ\times κ)}{f: λ\to κ}\right|. \end{align*} $$

  • $(κ$${}\cdot{}$$λ)^μ = κ^μ \cdot λ^μ$;

  • $κ^{λ + μ} = κ^λ \cdot κ^μ$;

  • $κ^{λ\cdot μ} = (κ^λ)^{μ}$;

  • If $κ\leq λ$, then $κ^μ \leq λ^μ$ and $μ^κ \leq μ^λ$.

  • $|\mathcal{P}(λ)| = 2^λ$.

  • $|\R| = 2^{\aleph_0}$.
