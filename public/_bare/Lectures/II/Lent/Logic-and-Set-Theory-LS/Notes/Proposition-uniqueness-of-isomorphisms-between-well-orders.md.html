Let $\mathbf{X}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$ be well-orders, and let $A,B \subseteq Y$ be initial segments.

If $f: \mathbf{X} \to \mathbf{A}$ and $g: \mathbf{X} \to \mathbf{B}$ are isomorphisms of well-orders, then $A = B$ and $f = g$.

Proof

This shows a well-order is not isomorphic to any proper initial segment.
