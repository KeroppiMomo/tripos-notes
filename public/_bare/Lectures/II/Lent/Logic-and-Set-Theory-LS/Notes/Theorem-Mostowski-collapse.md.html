Let $\mathbf{X} = (X, <)$ be a well-founded strict partial order. Define by recursion a function $π$ on $X$ $$ \begin{align*} π(x) := \sb{π(y)}{y<x} \end{align*} $$ called the Mostowski collapse, and let $T = \op{ran}(π)$.

If $\mathbf{X}$ is extensional, i.e. $\mathbf{X} \vDash{}$$\mathsf{Ext}$, then $T$ is transitive and $π: \mathbf{X} \to (T, \in)$ is an isomorphism.
