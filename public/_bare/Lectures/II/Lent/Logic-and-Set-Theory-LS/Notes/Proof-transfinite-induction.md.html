Suppose not, so there exists some ordinal $α$ where $ϕ(α)$ does not hold. Either $α$ is the least ordinal where $ϕ(α)$ does not hold, or $\sb{β\in α}{\neg ϕ(β)}$ is non-empty, in which case by well-foundedness its $\in$-minimal element $β$ is the least ordinal where $ϕ(β)$ does not hold.

Both cases cannot happen, because each ordinal is either zero, a successor or a limit, and $ϕ(γ)$ holds for all smaller $γ$.
