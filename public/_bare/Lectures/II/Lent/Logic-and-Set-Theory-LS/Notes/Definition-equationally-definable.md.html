Let $\mathbf{X} = (X, S_0, S_1)$ be an $a$-structure, $S \subseteq S_0$ be a subset of operations and $f: X^n \to X$ be a function.

Say $f$ is $S$-equationally definable over $\mathbf{X}$ if there exists an expression $t(x)$ using only the operations in $X$ such that $f(x) = t(x)$ for all $x\in X^n$.

Let $\mathbf{X}_\ast$ be an $a_\ast$-reduct of $\mathbf{X}$. If all operations in $\mathbf{X}$ are equationally definable over $\mathbf{X}_\ast$, then $\mathbf{X}_\ast$ has all information needed to recover the full structure $\mathbf{X}$.
