Let $f: α \to β$ be an isomorphism. We will prove by induction that $f(γ) = γ$ for all $γ\in α$.

Suppose $f(δ) = δ$ for all $δ\in γ$.

  • $γ \subseteq f(γ)$: if $δ\in γ$, then $f(δ) = δ$ by induction hypothesis, while $f(δ) \in f(γ)$ since $f$ is a homomorphism. Combining gives $δ\in f(γ)$.

  • $f(γ)\subseteq γ$: suppose $η\in f(γ) \in β$. Transitivity of $β$ shows $η \in β$. Since $f$ is surjective, find $δ\in α$ such that $η = f(δ)$. Then since $f(δ) = η \in f(γ)$ and $f$ is an isomorphism, $δ \in γ$. Then induction hypothesis gives $f(δ) = δ$. Together, $η = f(δ) = δ\in γ$.
