Using the proof of upward Löwenheim–Skolem, let $a_+$ be the expanded signature with $|Σ^{a_+}| = κ$, and $T_+ \supseteq T$ saying there exists all $κ$-many new constant symbols are distinct. Then models of $T_+$ are models of $T$ of cardinality at least $κ$.

Apply downward Löwenheim–Skolem to get a model of $T_+$ of cardinality $\leq |\mathrm{Fml}^{a_+}| = |Σ^{a_+}| = κ$. Then this is a model of cardinality exactly $κ$.
