We can choose a triangulation with all edges being geodesics: by compactness, choose finitely many normal neighbourhoods that cover $Σ$. Then pick a triangulation, subdivide so that each triangle is in one normal neighbourhood, remove all edges, and reconnect the old edges with a geodesic.

Each edge is the edge of two triangles and edge face has three edges, so $$ \begin{align*} 3F = 2E. \end{align*} $$ By Gauss's theorem, the sum of internal angles of each triangle $T$ is $\int_{T} K \d A + π$. Each vertex contributes a total internal angles of $2π$, so $$ \begin{align*} 2π\cdot V = \int_{Σ} K \d A + πF. \end{align*} $$ Thus $$ \begin{align*} 2π(V - E + F) = \int_{Σ} K \d A \end{align*} $$ as required.
