Let $\tilde ψ: \tilde V \to \tilde U$ be another parametrisation around $x$. By restricting, WLOG assume $\tilde U = U$. We want $\d \tilde ψ_{\tilde ψ\inv(x)}(\R^k) = \d ψ_{ψ\inv(x)}(\R^k)$.

Note $$ \begin{align*} ψ = \tilde ψ \circ (\tilde ψ\inv \circ ψ) \end{align*} $$ so by the chain rule $$ \begin{align*} \d ψ_{ψ\inv(x)} = \d \tilde ψ_{\tilde ψ\inv(x)} \circ \d(\tilde ψ\inv \circ ψ)_{ψ\inv(x)}. \end{align*} $$

Because $(ψ\inv \circ \tilde ψ) \circ (\tilde ψ\inv \circ ψ) = \id$, we have $\d (ψ\inv \circ \tilde ψ) \circ \d (\tilde ψ\inv \circ ψ) = \d \,\id = \id$, so $\d (\tilde ψ\inv \circ ψ)$ is invertible. Hence $\d ψ_{ψ\inv(x)}$ and $\d \tilde ψ_{\tilde ψ\inv(x)}$ have the same image.
