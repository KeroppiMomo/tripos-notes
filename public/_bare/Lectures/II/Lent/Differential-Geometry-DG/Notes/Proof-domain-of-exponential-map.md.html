Openness is by ODE theory: if $v$ is such that the maximal geodesic $γ_v: [0, T_+(v)]\to S$ with $γ_v(0) = p$, $\dot γ_v(0) = v$ and $T_+(v) > 1$, then there exists $ε>0$ such that $T_+(w) > 1$ if $|w - v| < ε$.

Starlike: note by uniqueness of geodesic $$ \begin{align*} γ_{λv}(t) = γ_v(λt) \end{align*} $$ so if $γ_v(1)$ is defined, $γ_{λv}(1)$ is also defined for all $λ\in [0,1]$.
