Let $f: X\to Y$ be a smooth map between manifolds, and let $Z \subseteq Y$ be a submanifold of $Y$.

Say $f$ is traversal to $Z$ (denoted $f \pitchfork Z$) if for all $x\in f\inv(Z)$, $$ \begin{align*} \im \d f_x + T_{f(x)} Z = T_{f(x)} Y \end{align*} $$ where $\d f_x$ is the differential map.

If $Z \cap f(X) = \emptyset$, then $f$ is transversal to $Z$.

$y\in Y$ is a regular value iff $f$ is transversal to the submanifold $\set{y}\subseteq Y$.
