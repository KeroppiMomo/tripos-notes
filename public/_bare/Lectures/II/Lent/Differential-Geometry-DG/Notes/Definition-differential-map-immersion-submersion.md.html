Let $f: X\to Y$ be a smooth function between manifolds, and $x\in X$.

Choose local parametrisations $ψ_1: V_1\to U_1$ of $X$ around $x$ and $ψ_2: V_2 \to U_2$ of $Y$ around $f(x)$. WLOG assume $f(\mathcal{V}_1) \subset \mathcal{V}_2$ by restricting.

The differential map of $f$ at $x$ is the linear map $$ \begin{align*} \d f_x = \underbrace{\d (ψ_2)_{ψ_2\inv(f(x))}}_{\R^{\tilde k} \to T_{f(x)} Y} \circ \underbrace{\d(ψ_2\inv \circ f \circ ψ_1)_{ψ_1\inv(x)}}_{\R^k \to \R^{\tilde k}} \circ \underbrace{(\d(ψ_1)_{ψ_1\inv(x)})\inv}_{T_x X \to \R^k}: T_x X \to T_y Y \end{align*} $$ between tangent spaces.

$f$ is an immersion at $x\in X$ if $\d f_x$ is an injection. $f$ is an submersion at $x\in X$ if $\d f_x$ is a surjection.

This does not depend on parametrisation.

Chain rule applies: if $f: X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ are smooth maps between manifolds, then $\d(g \circ f)_x = \d g_{f(x)} \circ \d f_{x}$.
