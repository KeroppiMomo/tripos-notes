For a local parametrisation $ϕ: U\to V$, given a smooth function $h: U\to \R$, the normal variation of $V$ determined by $h$ is the family $$ \begin{align*} ϕ^t(u,v) := ϕ(u,v) + t\, h(u,v)\, N(u,v), \qquad t\in (-ε, ε) \end{align*} $$ where $N = \frac{ϕ_u \times ϕ_v}{\|ϕ_u \times ϕ_v\|}$. For $ε$ sufficiently small, $ϕ^t(U)$ is a smooth surface in $\R^3$.

Then the mean curvature $H \equiv 0$ iff $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \dd{t} \op{area}(ϕ^t(U))\Big|_{t=0} \equiv \dd{t} \int_{U} \sqrt{E^t G^t - (F^t)^2} \d A \Big|_{t=0} \end{align*} $$ for all normal variation.
