Let $α(s)$ be a curve parametrised by arc length. Its tangent $t$, normal $n$ and binormal $b$ satisfy the ordinary differential equation (called the Frenet formulae) $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{t'(s) \\ n'(s) \\ b'(s)} = \pmat{& k(s) & \\ -k(s) & & -τ(s) \\ &τ(s)&}\pmat{t(s) \\ n(s) \\ b(s)} \end{align*} $$ where $k$ is the curvature and $τ$ is the torsion.

The right-handed orthonormal basis $(t, n, b)$ is called the Frenet frame associated to $α$.
