Let $α$ be parametrised by arc length, so $α: [0,l]\to \R^2$ with $l = \op{length}(α)$. Write $α(s) = (x(s), y(s))$.

By translating, WLOG assume $\int_{0}^{l} x(s) \d s = 0 = \int_{0}^{l} y(s) \d s$.

$$ \begin{align*} 2\op{area}(A) ={}& \iint_{A} \pdd{x}x + \pdd{y}y \d x\d y \\ ={}& \int_0^l \langle (x,y), n\rangle \d s &&\text{divergence theorem}\\ \leq{}& \int_0^l \|(x,y)\| \d s \\ \leq{}& \left(\int_0^l (x^2 + y^2) \d s \cdot \int_{0}^{l} 1 \d s\right)^{1/2} &&\text{Cauchy-Schwarz} \\ \leq{}& \sqrt{l} \sqrt{\frac{l^2}{4π^2} \int_{0}^{l} \underbrace{(\dot x^2 + \dot y^2)}_{\|\dot α(s)\|^2 = 1} \d s} &&(*) \\ ={}& \frac{l^2}{2π^2}. \end{align*} $$ (Divergence theorem, Cauchy-Schwarz) If equality holds, then Cauchy-Schwarz implies $x^2 + y^2$ is constant, so $α$ is a constant.

$(*)$ is due to Wirtinger's inequality:
