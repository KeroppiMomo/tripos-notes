Compute the first fundamental form: $$ \begin{align*} E^t ={}& \langle ϕ^t_u, ϕ^t_u\rangle \\ ={}& \langle ϕ_u, ϕ_u\rangle + 2th_u \underbrace{\langle ϕ_u, N\rangle}_{0} + 2th\langle ϕ_u, N_u\rangle + O(t^2) \\ ={}& E - 2the + O(t^2) \end{align*} $$ and similarly $$ \begin{align*} F^t ={}& F - 2thf + O(t^2), \\ G^t ={}& G - 2thg + O(t^2). \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} E^tG^t - (F^t)^2 ={}& (EG - F^2) - 2th(eG + 2fF + gE) + O(t^2) \\ ={}& (EG - F^2)(1 - 4thH + O(t^2)) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t} \op{area}(ϕ^t(U))\Big|_{t=0} ={}& \int_{U} \dd{t}((EG - F^2)(1 - 4thH + O(t^2)))^{1/2} \Big|_{t=0}\d A \\ ={}& \int_{U} -2Hh \sqrt{EG - F^2} \d u \d v. \end{align*} $$

Taking $h = H$, we see $\dd{t}\op{area}(ϕ^t(U))|_{t=0}\leq 0$ with equality iff $H = 0$.
