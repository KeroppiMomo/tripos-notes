Let $X$ be a manifold with boundary, $Y$ be a manifold, $f: X\to Y$ be smooth, and $y\in Y$.

If $y$ is a regular value of $f: X\to Y$ and $f|_{\partial X}: \partial X\to Y$, then $f\inv(y)\subseteq X$ (if non-empty) is a manifold with boundary, with $$ \begin{align*} \op{codim}_X f\inv(y) = \dim Y \end{align*} $$ and $$ \begin{align*} \partial f\inv(y) = f\inv(y) \cap \partial X. \end{align*} $$
