$f$ has a convergent Fourier series $$ \begin{align*} f(x) = \sum_{n\in \Z} a_n e^{2πinx/L} \end{align*} $$ so its derivative is $$ \begin{align*} f'(x) = \sum_{n\in \Z} \frac{2πin}{L} a_n e^{2πinx/L}. \end{align*} $$ Since $\int_{0}^{L} f(x) \d x = 0$, we have $a_0 = 0$.

By Parseval, $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{L} \int_{0}^{L} |f|^2 \d x ={}& \sum_{|n|\geq 1} |a_n|^2, \\ \frac{1}{L} \int_{0}^{L} |f'|^2 \d x ={}& \sum_{|n|\geq 1} \frac{4π^2 n^2}{L^2} |a_n|^2 \\ \geq{} & \frac{4π^2}{L^2} \sum_{|n|\geq 1} |a_n|^2 = \frac{4π^2}{L^2} \frac{1}{L}\int_{0}^{L} |f|^2 \d x \end{align*} $$ as required.
