Starting from $$ ϕ_{uu} = Γ_{11}^1 ϕ_u + Γ_{11}^2 ϕ_v + e N, $$ take inner product with $ϕ_u$ and $ϕ_v$ to get $$ \begin{align*} \langle ϕ_{uu}, ϕ_u\rangle ={}& Γ_{11}^1 E + Γ_{11}^2 F, \\ \langle ϕ_{uu}, ϕ_v\rangle ={}& Γ_{11}^1 F + Γ_{11}^2 G. \end{align*} $$

Expand to see $\frac{1}{2}E_{u} = \langle ϕ_{uu}, ϕ_u\rangle$ and $F_u - \frac{1}{2}E_v = \langle ϕ_{uu}, ϕ_v\rangle$.
