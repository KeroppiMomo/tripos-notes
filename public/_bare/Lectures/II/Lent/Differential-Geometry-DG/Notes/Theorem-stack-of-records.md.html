Suppose $f: X\to Y$ is a smooth map between manifolds. Assume

  1. $\dim X = \dim Y$;

  2. $X$ is compact; and

  3. $y\in Y$ is a regular value.

Then there exists $V\subset Y$ open neighbourhood of $y$ such that $f\inv(V) = W_1 \sqcup \ldots\sqcup W_n$ for some disjoint open sets $W_i\subseteq X$ with $f|_{\mathcal{W}_i}: \mathcal{W}_i\to \mathcal{V}$ is a diffeomorphism.
