Since $ψ\inv: U \to V$ is smooth, by definition there exists a smooth extension $Φ: \tilde U\to V$ where $\tilde U \subset \R^N$ is open. It satisfies $$ \begin{align*} Φ \circ ψ = \id_V \end{align*} $$ so chain rule says $$ \begin{align*} \d Φ_{ψ(x)} \circ \d ψ_x = \d (\id_{V})_x = \id_{\R^k} \end{align*} $$ and thus $\dim \im \d ψ_x \geq k$.

But also $\d ψ_x : \R^k\to \R^N$ so $\dim \im \d ψ_x \leq k$. Hence $\dim T_{x}X = k$.
