$\set{ϕ_u/\sqrt{E}, ϕ_v/\sqrt{G}, N}$ form a right-handed orthonormal basis, so $$ \begin{align*} \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}(ϕ_u/\sqrt{E})}{\d t}\right] ={}& \left\langle \dd{t}\frac{ϕ_u}{\sqrt{E}}, \frac{ϕ_v}{\sqrt{G}}\right\rangle \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\sqrt{G}}\left\langle ϕ_u \dd{t}\frac{1}{\sqrt{E}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{E}} \dd{t}ϕ_u, ϕ_v\right\rangle \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\sqrt{EG}} \langle ϕ_{uu}\dot u + ϕ_{uv}\dot v, ϕ_v\rangle \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\sqrt{EG}} \left(\dot u \Bigl(\pdd{u}\underbrace{\langle ϕ_u, ϕ_v\rangle}_{F = 0} - \frac{1}{2}\pdd{v}\underbrace{\langle ϕ_u, ϕ_u\rangle}_{E}\Bigr) + \dot v \cdot \frac{1}{2} \pdd{v}\underbrace{\langle ϕ_v, ϕ_v\rangle}_{G}\right) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2\sqrt{EG}}(G_u \dot v - E_v \dot u). \end{align*} $$
