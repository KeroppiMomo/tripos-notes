A (smooth) $k$-dimensional manifold is a Hausdorff, second countable topological space $X$ together with a collection of maps $ϕ_α: U_α \to X$ satisfying the following:

  1. $U_α \subset \R^k$ open and $ϕ_α: U_α \to ϕ_α(U_α) =: V_α$ is a homeomorphism. (In particular $V_α$ is open.) Define $ψ_α := ϕ_α\inv: V_α \to U_α$, called a chart.

  2. $\bigcup_{α} V_α = X$.

  3. Letting $V_{αβ} := V_α \cap V_{β}$, the map $ϕ_β\inv \circ ϕ_α := ϕ_α\inv(V_{αβ}) \to ϕ_β\inv(V_{αβ})$ between open sets of $\R^k$ is smooth.

  4. The atlas is "maximal": suppose $\tilde ϕ: \tilde U\to X$ is such that $\set{ϕ_α}_α \cup \set{\tilde ϕ}$ still satisfies 1, 2, 3, then $\tilde ϕ = ϕ_α$ for some $α$.

A collection of $ϕ_α$ satisfying 1, 2, 3 is called a smooth atlas.
