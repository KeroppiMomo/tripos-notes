Let $f: X\to Y$ be a smooth map between manifolds.

If $y\in Y$ is a regular value, then $f\inv(y)$ (if non-empty) is a manifold of codimension $\dim Y$.

Moreover, $T_x f\inv(y) = \ker \d f_x$ for all $x\in f\inv(y)$.

Proof

If $\dim X = \dim Y$, then $f\inv(y)$ is a discrete subset of $X$. If $X$ is compact, then $f\inv(y)$ is finite.
