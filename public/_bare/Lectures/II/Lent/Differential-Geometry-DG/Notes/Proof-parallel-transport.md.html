In a local parametrisation $ϕ$, the equation $\mathrm{D}V/\d t = 0$ can be written as $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{\dot a \\ \dot b} = -\pmat{Γ_{11}^1 \dot u + Γ_{12}^1 \dot v & Γ_{12}^1\dot u + Γ_{22}^1 \dot v \\ Γ_{11}^2 \dot u + Γ_{12}^2 \dot v & Γ_{12}^2 \dot u + Γ_{22}^2 \dot v} \pmat{a \\ b} \end{align*} $$ which is a linear system. Therefore by ODE theory, there exists a unique solution $a(t), b(t)$.

If $α$ does not lie on one chart, keep doing on multiple charts.
