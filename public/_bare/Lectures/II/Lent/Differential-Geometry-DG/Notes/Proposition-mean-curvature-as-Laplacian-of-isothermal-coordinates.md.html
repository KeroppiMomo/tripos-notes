Let $S$ be a surface in $\R^3$ and $ϕ: U\to V$ be a local parametrisation.

If $ϕ$ is isothermal (i.e. $E = G = λ^2$ and $F = 0$), then $$ \begin{align*} Δϕ \equiv ϕ_{uu} + ϕ_{v v} = 2λ^2 \v H. \end{align*} $$ where $\v H = HN$ the mean curvature in the normal direction.

Proof

Therefore $S$ is a minimal surface iff $ϕ$ is harmonic.
