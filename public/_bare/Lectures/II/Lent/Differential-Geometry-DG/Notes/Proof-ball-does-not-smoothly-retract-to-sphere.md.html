If $f: D^{n+1} \to S^n$ is a smooth retract, then define $$ \begin{align*} g: S^n \times [0,1] &\to S^n \\ (x, t) &\mapsto f(t\cdot x), \end{align*} $$ which is a smooth homotopy from a constant map $g(\cdot, 0)$ to the identity $g(\cdot, 1)$. However $\deg_2{}$$g(\cdot, 0) = 0$ and $\deg_2 g(\cdot, 1) = 1$, contracting the homotopy lemma.
