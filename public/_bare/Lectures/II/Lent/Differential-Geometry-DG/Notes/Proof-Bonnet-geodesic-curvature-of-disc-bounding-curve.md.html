We have $$ \begin{align*} k_g = \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}\dot α}{\d s}\right] = \dot ψ + \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}(ϕ_u/\sqrt{E})}{\d s}\right] \end{align*} $$ where $ψ$ is a smooth determination of angles from $ϕ_u/\sqrt{E}$ to $\dot α$. From previous computation, we apply Green's theorem to get $$ \begin{align*} \int_{a}^{b} \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}(ϕ_u/\sqrt{E})}{\d s}\right] \d s ={}& \int_{a}^{b} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{EG}}(G_u \dot v - E_v \dot u) \d s \tag{1}\\ ={}& \int_{ϕ\inv \circ α} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{EG}}(G_u \d v - E_v \d u) \\ ={}& \int_D \pdd{u}\frac{G_u}{2\sqrt{EG}} + \pdd{v}\frac{E_v}{2\sqrt{EG}} \d A \\ ={}& \int_{D} -K \sqrt{EG} \d A \tag{2}\\ ={}& -\int_{ϕ\inv(D)} K \d A. \end{align*} $$ (1), (2)

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \int_{a}^{b} k_g \d s + \int_{ϕ\inv(D)} K \d A = ψ(b) - ψ(a) = 2π \end{align*} $$ by the theorem of turning tangents.
