Let $S$ be a surface in $\R^3$.

Suppose $α: [a,b]\to U$ is a simple closed curve parametrised by arc length, lying in a local parametrisation $ϕ: V\to U\subseteq S$. Assume $ϕ$ is orthogonal (i.e. $F = 0$), $ϕ\inv\circ α$ bounds a disc $D$, and $ϕ\inv\circ α$ is positively oriented. Then $$ \begin{align*} \int_{a}^{b} k_g \d s + \int_{ϕ(D)} K \d A = 2π \end{align*} $$ where $K$ is the Gaussian curvature and $k_g$ is the geodesic curvature of $α$.
