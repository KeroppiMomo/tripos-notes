$f\inv((0, \infty))$ is an open set of $X$ so is a $k$-dimensional manifold.

Let $x\in f\inv(0)$, and we continue from the proof of the preimage theorem where we have defined $$ \begin{align*} F: X &\to \R \times \R^{k-1} \\ x &\mapsto (f(x), T(x)) \end{align*} $$ and an open set $U\subseteq X$ such that $F|_U: U\to f(U) \times T(U)$ is a diffeomorphism. Now $$ \begin{align*} ψ = (F|_U)\inv|_{(f(U) \cap \R_{\geq 0}) \times T(U)} : (f(U) \cap \R_{\geq 0}) \times T(U) \to U \cap f\inv([0, \infty)) \end{align*} $$ is a local parametrisation of $f\inv([0,\infty))$ near $x$: it is a diffeomorphism from an open set in $\sb{(x_1, \ldots, x_k)}{x_1 \geq 0}\isom \mathbb{H}^k$ to an open in $f\inv([0,\infty))$.
