Let $S$ be a surface in $\R^3$ and let $α: I\to S$ be a curve parametrised by arc length.

The geodesic curvature of $α$ is the algebraic value of the covariant derivative of $\dot α$: $$ \begin{align*} k_g(t) := \left[\frac{\mathrm{D\dot α}}{\d t}\right](t) \end{align*} $$

$k_g \equiv 0$ iff $α$ is a geodesic.

In orthonormal components, $\ddot α = \langle \dot α, N\rangle N + \langle \dot α, N \times \dot α\rangle(N \times \dot α) ={}$$k_n$$N + k_g (N \times \dot α)$, so the curvature of the curve is $$ \begin{align*} k^2 = k_n^2 + k_g^2. \end{align*} $$
