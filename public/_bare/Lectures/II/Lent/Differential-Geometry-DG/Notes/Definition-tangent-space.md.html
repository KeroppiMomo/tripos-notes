Let $X \subset \R^N$ be a $k$-dimensional manifold, and $x\in X$. Choose a local parametrisation $ψ: V\to U\ni x$ around $x$.

The tangent space is the image $$ \begin{align*} T_x X := \d ψ_{ψ\inv(x)}(\R^k) \subset \R^{N} \end{align*} $$ a vector subspace of $\R^N$, where $\d$ is the differential operator.

This does not depend on parametrisation. (Proof)

$\dim T_x X = k$. (Proof) This shows the dimension of $X$ (at a point $x$) is well-defined.
