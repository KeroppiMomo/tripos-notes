Let $S$ be a surface in $\R^3$ and let $α: I\to S$ be a curve parametrised by arc length. Let $N$ be a normal vector of $S$ along $α$.

For two unit vector fields $V,W$ along $α$, a smooth determination of an angle from $V$ to $W$ is a smooth function $ϕ: I\to \R$ such that $$ \begin{align*} W = (\cos ϕ)\,V + (\sin ϕ)\, N \times V. \end{align*} $$

Then $$ \begin{align*} \dot ϕ = \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}W}{\d t}\right] - \left[\frac{\mathrm{D}V}{\d t}\right]. \end{align*} $$
