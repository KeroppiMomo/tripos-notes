Consider the relation $x\sim x'$ iff there exists a diffeomorphism $X\to X$ sending $x\mapsto x'$ which is smoothly isotopic to the identity. This is an equivalence relation. By connectedness, it suffices to show any equivalence class is open, i.e. for all $x$, there exists a neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ such that $x\sim x'$ for all $x'\in U$.

Taking $U$ to be a chart, it suffices to prove the statement in $\R^k$. For each $|z| < 1$, we will construct a smooth isotopy $H: \R^k \times [0,1] \to \R^k$ with $H(\cdot, 0) = \id_X$, $H(0, 1) = z$ and $H(x,t) = x$ for all $|x|\geq 1$.

Let $V: \R^k \to \R^k$ be any smooth vector field satisfying $V(x) = 0$ for all $|x|\geq 1$ and $V(tz) = z$ for all $t\in [0,1]$. Let $H: \R^k \times [0,1] \to \R^k$ be the flow along $V$, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} H(\cdot, 0) = \id_X, \qquad \pdd{t}H(x, t) = V(H(x,t)). \end{align*} $$ By ODE theory, $H$ is smooth and $H(\cdot, t)$ is a diffeomorphism for all $t$. Moreover by uniqueness, $H(0, t) = tz$, so $H(0,1) = z$.
