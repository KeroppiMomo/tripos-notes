Let $V(t)$ and $\tilde V(t)$ be the parallel transport of $v_0$ and $\tilde v_0$ along $α$ respectively.

By Leibniz's rule, $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t}\langle V(t), \tilde V(t)\rangle = \left\langle \frac{\mathrm{D}V}{\d t}, \tilde V\right\rangle + \left\langle V, \frac{\mathrm{D}\tilde V}{\d t}\right\rangle = 0 \end{align*} $$ because $\mathrm{D}V/\d t = \mathrm{D}\tilde V/\d t$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \langle v_0, \tilde v_0\rangle = \langle V(1), \tilde V(1)\rangle. \end{align*} $$
