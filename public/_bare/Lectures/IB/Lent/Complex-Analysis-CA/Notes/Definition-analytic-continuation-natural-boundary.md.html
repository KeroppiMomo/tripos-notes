Suppose $U\subseteq V\subseteq \C$ are domains, and $f:U \to \C$ and $g: V\to \C$ are holomorphic.

We say $g$ is an analytic continuation of $f$ if $g|_U = f$.

If $f: U\to \C$ has no non-trivial analytic continuation (i.e. $V\neq U$), then the boundary $\partial U$ of $U$ is called the natural boundary of $f$.
