Let $U$ be a domain and $γ: [a,b]\to U$ be a curve.

If $F: U\to \C$ is holomorphic on $U$, then $$ \begin{align*} \int_{γ} F'(z)\d z = F(γ(b)) - F(γ(a)). \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • Since $F$ is holomorphic, $F'$ is continuous, so the integral is defined.

  • If $γ$ is closed, then the integral vanishes: $$ \begin{align*} \oint_γ F' = 0. \end{align*} $$
