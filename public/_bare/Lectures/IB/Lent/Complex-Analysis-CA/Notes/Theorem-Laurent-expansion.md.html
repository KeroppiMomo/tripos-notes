Let $f$ be holomorphic on an annulus $A:= \sb{z\in \C}{r<|z-a|<R}$ for some $a\in \C$ and $0\leq r<R\leq \infty$.

  1. $f$ has a unique convergent expansion about $a$ on $A$ $$ \begin{align*} f(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} c_n(z-a)^n \end{align*} $$ called the Laurent series of $f$ about $a$.

  2. The Laurent series converges uniformly on some slightly shrunk annulus $\sb{z\in \C}{ρ' < |z-a|<ρ}$ (where $r<ρ'\leq ρ<R$).

  3. The coefficients are given by $$ \begin{align*} c_n = \frac{1}{2πi} \int_{|z| = ρ} \frac{f(z)}{(z-a)^{n+1}} \d z \end{align*} $$ for any $r<ρ<R$.
