WLOG assume $w=0$ and replace $γ$ with $γ/|γ|$ to assume $|γ(t)|\equiv 1$.

Divide $[a,b]$ into subintervals where $γ$ misses one point of the unit circle, and then piece all the arguments together.

$γ$ is continuous on a compact interval $[a,b]$ so it is uniformly continuous. Let $ε>0$ such that $|γ(s) - γ(t)|<2$ if $|s-t|\leq ε$. Subdivide $a=a_0 < a_1 < \ldots < a_{n-1} < a_n = b$ such that $a_{j+1} - a_j < 2ε$. Then if $t\in [a_j, a_{j+1}]$, then $|γ(t) - γ(\frac{a_j + a_{j+1}}{2})| < 2$, i.e. $γ(t) \neq -γ(\frac{a_j + a_{j+1}}{2})$. Therefore $γ|_{[a_j, a_{j+1}]}$ lies in a slit plane and so has a continuously defined argument $θ_j$.

For each endpoint $a_j$, $$ \begin{align*} e^{iθ_{j-1}(a_j)} = γ(a_j) = e^{iθ_j(a_j)} \end{align*} $$ so $θ_j(a_j) - θ_{j-1}\in 2π\Z$. Starting from $j=1$, shift $θ_{j+1}$ by the relevant multiple of $2π$ so that $θ_{j+1}(a_j) - θ_j(a_j) = 0$. This defines a continuous $θ$.

Uniqueness

If $θ$ and $\tilde θ$ are two choices of continuous arguments, then $e^{iθ(t)} = e^{i\tilde θ(t)}$ implies $θ(t) - \tilde θ(t) \in 2π\Z$ for all $t\in [a,b]$. Thus $θ-\tilde θ: [a,b]\to 2π\Z$ is a continuous function, which by the connectedness of $[a,b]$, implies $θ-\tilde θ$ is constant.
