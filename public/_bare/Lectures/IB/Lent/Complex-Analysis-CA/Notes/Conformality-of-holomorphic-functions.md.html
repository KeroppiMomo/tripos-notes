Holomorphic functions are conformal, i.e. angle-preserving:

Suppose $U$ is a domain, $w\in \C$ and $f: U \to \C$ holomorphic with $f'(w)\neq 0$. Take two paths $γ_1, γ_2: [-1, 1] \to U$ with $γ_i(0) = w$, and assume $γ_1,γ_2\in C^1$ with $γ_1'(0),γ_2'(0)\neq 0$.

Note that for $i=1,2$, $$ \begin{align*} \arg\bigl((f\circ γ_i)'(0)\bigr) = \arg\bigl(f'(w) \cdot γ_i'(0)\bigr) = \arg(f'(w)) + \arg(γ_i'(0)) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \arg\bigl((f\circ γ_2)'(0)\bigr) - \arg\bigl((f\circ γ_1)'(0)\bigr) = \arg(γ_2'(0)) - \arg(γ_1'(0)). \end{align*} $$
