• If $f$ has a simple pole at $z=a$, then $\Res_{z=a} f(z) = \lim_{z\to a} (z-a) f(z)$.

  • If $f(z) = g(z)/h(z)$ where $g,h$ are holomorphic at $a$, $g(a)\neq 0$, and $h$ has a simple zero at $a$, then $\Res_{z=a}f(z) = g(a)/h'(a)$.

  • If $f(z) = g(z)/(z-a)^k$ where $g$ is holomorphic at $a$, $g(a)\neq 0$, and $k\in \N$, then $\Res_{z=a} f(z) = g^{(k-1)}(a)/(k-1)!$.

Lemma (Jordan's lemma) open_in_new

Let $f: C_R([0,π]) \to \C$ be a continuous function where $C_R: [0,π]\to \C$ is the upper semicircle of radius $R$ given by $C_R(t) = Re^{it}$. Then for any $α>0$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z) \, e^{iαz} \d z\right| \leq \frac{π}{α} \max_{t\in [0,π]} |f(Re^{it})|. \end{align*} $$

Proof
