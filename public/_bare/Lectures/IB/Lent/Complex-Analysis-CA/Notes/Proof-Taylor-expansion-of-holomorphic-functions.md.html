Fix $0<ρ<r$ and $z\in B(a,ρ)$. Apply the series expansion $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{ζ-z} = \frac{1}{ζ-a} \frac{1}{1-\frac{z-a}{ζ-a}} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(z-a)^n}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}}, \end{align*} $$ which is valid on $|z-a|<|ζ-a|$, to the Cauchy integral formula to obtain $$ \begin{align*} f(z) ={}& \frac{1}{2πi} \int_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{ζ-z} \d ζ \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2πi} \int_{|ζ-a|=ρ} f(ζ) \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(z-a)^n}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}} \d ζ \\ ={}& \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{2πi}\int_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}} \d ζ\right)}_{c_n} (z-a)^n \end{align*} $$ where swapping $\sum$ and $\int$ is legal by uniform convergence of the series on $|ζ-a|=ρ$.
