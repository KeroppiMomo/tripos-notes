Let $U\subseteq \C$ be open, and $f_n: U\to\C$ be a sequence of functions.

We say $\set{f_n}$ converges locally uniformly on $U$ to a function $f: U\to \C$ if for all $a\in U$, there exists $B(a,r)\subseteq U$ such that $f_n\tto f$ uniformly on $B(a,r)$, i.e. for all $ε>0$, there exists $N\in \N$ such that for all $n>N$, $|f_n(z) - f(z)| < ε$ for all $z\in B(a,r)$.

  • Locally uniform convergence is equivalent to uniform convergence on any compact subset.
