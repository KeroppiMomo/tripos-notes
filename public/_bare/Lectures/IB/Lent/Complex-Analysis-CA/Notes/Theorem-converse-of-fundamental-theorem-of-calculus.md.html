Let $U\subseteq \C$ be a domain and $f$ be continuous on $U$.

If either

  • $\oint_γ f=0$ for every closed curve $γ$ in $U$; or

  • $U$ is starlike and $\oint_{\partial T} f = 0$ for every triangle $T$ in $U$,

then $f$ has an antiderivative, i.e. there exists a holomorphic function $F$ on $U$ such that $F'=f$.
