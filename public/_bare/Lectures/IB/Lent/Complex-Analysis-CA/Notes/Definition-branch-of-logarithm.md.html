Let $U\subseteq C\setminus \set{0}$ be open.

A branch of the logarithm is a continuous function $λ: U\to \C$ such that $\exp$$(λ(z)) = z$ for all $z\in U$.

For $U = \C \setminus \sb{x\in \R}{x\leq 0}$, we have the principal logarithm $\Log: U \to \C$ $$ \begin{align*} \Log(z) = \ln |z| + i \op{Arg}(z) \end{align*} $$ where $\op{Arg}(z) \in (-π, π)$.
