Definition (complex integral) open_in_new

Define the integral of a continuous complex-valued function $f: [a,b]\to \C$ as $$ \begin{align*} \int_{a}^{b} f(t) \d t := \int_{a}^{b} \Re(f(t)) \d t + i \int_{a}^{b} \Im(f(t)) \d t. \end{align*} $$

  • $\Re(f(t))$ and $\Im(f(t))$ are integrable since they are continuous.

Proposition (sup-norm bound on complex integral) open_in_new

If $f: [a,b]\to \C$ is continuous, then $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{a}^{b} f(t) \d t\right| \overset{(1)}{\leq} \int_{a}^{b} |f(t)| \d t \overset{(2)}{\leq} (b-a) \cdot \sup_{[a,b]} |f|, \end{align*} $$ with equality for (1) iff $\arg(f(t))$ is constant, and equality for (2) iff $|f|$ is constant.

Definition (arc length of a complex curve) open_in_new

The arc length of a curve $γ: [a,b]\to \C$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{length}(γ) = \int_{a}^{b} |γ'(t)| \d t. \end{align*} $$

Definition (integral along curve) open_in_new

Let $U$ be a domain in $\C$, $f: U\to \C$ be a continuous function, and $γ: [a,b]\to U$ be a $C^1$-smooth curve in $U$.

The integral of $f$ along $γ$ is $$ \begin{align*} \int_{γ} f(z) \d z := \int_{a}^{b} f(γ(t))\, γ'(t) \d t. \end{align*} $$

  • Linearity: $\int_{γ} αf(z) + βg(z) \d z = α\int_{γ} f(z) \d z + β\int_{γ} g(z) \d z$.

  • For $a\leq c\leq b$, we have $\int_{γ|_{[a,c]}} f + \int_{γ|_{[c,b]}} f = \int_{γ} f$.

  • $\int_{-γ} f = -\int_{γ} f$ where $(-γ)(t) = γ(-t): [-b,-a]\to U$.

  • Independence of parametrisation: if $ϕ: [a',b'] \to [a,b]$ is $C^1$ with $ϕ(a') = a$ and $ϕ(b') = b$, then by integration by substitution, $$ \begin{align*} \int_{γ\circ ϕ} f = \int_{a'}^{b'} f(γ(ϕ(t))) \, γ'(ϕ(t))\, ϕ'(t) \d t = \int_{a}^{b} f(γ(u))\, γ'(u) \d u = \int_{γ} f. \end{align*} $$

  • If $γ$ is piecewise $C^1$, sum up the integrals of each $C^1$-smooth curve.

Proposition (sup-norm bound on integral along curve) open_in_new

Let $f: U\to \C$ be continuous and $γ: [a,b]\to U$ be a curve. Then $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{γ} f(z) \d z\right| \leq \mathrm{length}(γ) \cdot \sup_{γ} |f|. \end{align*} $$

Proof
