Using $z=Re^{it}$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z)\,e^{iαz} \d z\right| ={}& \left|\int_{0}^{π} f(Re^{it}) \,i\,Re^{it}\,e^{iαRe^{it}} \d t\right| \\ \leq{}& \int_{0}^{π} M e^{-αR\sin t} \d t \\ ={}& 2M\int_{0}^{π} e^{-αR\sin t} \d t \end{align*} $$ where $|zf(z)|\leq M$ on $|z|>r$.

Use the lower bound $\sin t \geq \frac{2}{π}t$ on $t\in [0,π/2]$ to get $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z)\,e^{iαz} \d z\right| \leq{}& 2M \int_{0}^{π} e^{-\frac{2}{π}αRt} \d t \\ \leq{} & \frac{2M}{\frac{2}{π}αR}\left(1 - e^{-2αR}\right) \to 0 \end{align*} $$ as $R\to \infty$.
