Using $z=Re^{it}$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z)\,e^{iαz} \d z\right| ={}& \left|\int_{0}^{π} f(Re^{it}) \,i\,Re^{it}\,e^{iαRe^{it}} \d t\right| \\ \leq{}& K \int_{0}^{π} R e^{-αR\sin t} \d t \\ ={}& 2K \int_{0}^{π/2} Re^{-αR\sin t} \d t. \end{align*} $$ where $K=\max_{t\in [0,π]} |f(Re^{it})|$.

Use the lower bound $\sin t \geq \frac{2}{π}t$ on $t\in [0,π/2]$ to get $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z)\,e^{iαz} \d z\right| \leq{}& 2K \int_{0}^{π} Re^{-\frac{2}{π}αRt} \d t \\ \leq{} & \frac{2K}{\frac{2}{π}α}\left(1 - e^{-2αR}\right) \\ \leq{} & \frac{π}{α}K. \end{align*} $$
