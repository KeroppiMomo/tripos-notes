Let $f: B(a,R)\to \C$ be holomorphic and non-constant, with a local degree $k$ at $z=a$.

For all $r>0$ sufficiently small, there exists $ε>0$ such that for all $w\in B(f(a),ε)^\times$, the equation $f(z) = w$ has exactly $k$ simple solutions (i.e. each with local degree 1) in $B(a,r)$.

Proof

  • If $k=1$, then $f: B(a,r)\to B(a,ε)$ is a conformal equivalence.
