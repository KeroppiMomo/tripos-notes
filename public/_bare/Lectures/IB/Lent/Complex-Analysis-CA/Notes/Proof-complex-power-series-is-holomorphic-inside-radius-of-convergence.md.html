WLOG assume $a=0$.

Radius of convergnce of $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} nc_nz^{n-1}$

Consider the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} nc_n(z-a)^{n-1}$. Since $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |c_n| |z|^{n} \leq \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n|c_n||z|^n$, if $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} nc_nz^{n-1}$ converges absolutely, then so does $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_nz^n$, and thus the radius of convergence is at most $R$ by comparison.

Let $z\in B(0,R)$. Pick some $r$ so that $|z| < r < R$. Then $$ \begin{align*} |nc_n z^{n-1}| = |c_n| r^{n-1} \cdot n \biggl(\frac{|z|}{r}\biggr)^{n-1}. \end{align*} $$ Since $n|z|^{n-1}/r^{n-1} \to 0$ as $n\to \infty$, we can apply the Weierstrass M-test with $M_n = |c_n| r^{n-1}$, for which $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} M_n < \infty$. Thus the radius of convergence is $R$.

Derivative of $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_nz^n$

Define the function $ϕ_N: B(0,R)\times B(0,R) \to \C$ by $$ \begin{align*} ϕ_N(z,w) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} c_n \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} z^j w^{n-1-j}. \end{align*} $$ For $|z|,|w| < R$ with $z\neq w$, pick $r$ such that $|z|,|w| < r< R$. $$ \begin{align*} \left|c_n \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} z^j w^{n-1-j}\right| \leq |nc_n r^{n-1}| \end{align*} $$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |nc_n r^{n-1}|<\infty$, so by the M-test, $ϕ_N(z,w)$ converges uniformly to say $ϕ(z,w)$.

If $z\neq w$, by the geometric sum formula, $$ \begin{align*} ϕ_N(z,w) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} c_n \frac{z^n - w^n}{z-w}. \end{align*} $$ By the uniform limit theorem, $$ \begin{align*} ϕ(w,w) ={}& \lim_{z\to w} ϕ(z,w) \\ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n c_n w^{n-1} ={}& \lim_{z\to w} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} c_n \frac{z^n - w^n}{z-w} \\ ={}& \lim_{z\to w} \frac{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n z^n - \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n w^n}{z-w} \end{align*} $$ so $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n z^n$ is differentiable at $z=w$ with derivative $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} nc_n w^{n-1}$.
