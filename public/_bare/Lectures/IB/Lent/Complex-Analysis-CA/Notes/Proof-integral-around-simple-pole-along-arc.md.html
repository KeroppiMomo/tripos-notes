Since $z=a$ is a simple pole, write $$ \begin{align*} f(z) = \frac{\Res_{z=a} f(z)}{z-a} + g(z) \end{align*} $$ where $g$ is a holomorphic function on $B(a,R)$.

By continuity of $g$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{γ_r} g(z) \d z\right| \leq (β-α)r \cdot \sup_{z\in \overline{B(a,r)}} |g(z)| \to 0 \end{align*} $$ as $r\to 0$ since $g$ is bounded.

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \lim_{r\to 0} \int_{γ_r} f(z) \d z ={}& \Res_{z=a} f(z) \cdot \lim_{r\to 0} \int_{γ_r} \frac{1}{z-a} \d z \\ ={}& \Res_{z=a} f(z) \cdot \lim_{r\to 0} \int_{α}^{β} \frac{ire^{it}}{re^{it}} \d t \\ ={}& \Res_{z=a} f(z)\cdot (β-α)i. \end{align*} $$
