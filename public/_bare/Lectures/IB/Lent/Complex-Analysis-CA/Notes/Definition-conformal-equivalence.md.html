Let $U,V$ be domains in $\C$.

A function $f: U\to V$ is called a conformal equivalence if it is bijective, holomorphic and $f'(u)\neq 0$ for all $u\in U$.

$U$ is said to be conformally equivalent to $V$ if such $f$ exists.

  • This is an equivalence relation.

  • In fact if $f$ is injective and holomorphic on $U$, then $f'(z) \neq 0$ on $U$, so that condition is redundant.
