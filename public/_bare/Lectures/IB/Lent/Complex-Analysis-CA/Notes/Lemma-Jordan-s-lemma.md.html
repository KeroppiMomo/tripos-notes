Let $f: C_R([0,π]) \to \C$ be a continuous function where $C_R: [0,π]\to \C$ is the upper semicircle of radius $R$ given by $C_R(t) = Re^{it}$. Then for any $α>0$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{C_R} f(z) \, e^{iαz} \d z\right| \leq \frac{π}{α} \max_{t\in [0,π]} |f(Re^{it})|. \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • When $|f(z)|\to 0$ as $z\to\infty$, we apply this to conclude $\int_{C_R} f(z) e^{iαz} \d z \to 0$ as $R\to \infty$.

  • (The version stated in lectures: if $f$ is holomorphic on $|z|>r$ for some $r>0$, and $|zf(z)|$ is bounded on $|z|>r$, then for all $α>0$, $\int_{C_R} f(z) \, e^{iα z} \d z \to 0$ as $R\to \infty$. This is so weak!)
