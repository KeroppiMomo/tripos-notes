Suppose $f$ is holomorphic on $|z|>r$ for some $r>0$, and assume $|zf(z)|$ is bounded on $|z|>r$.

Then for all $α>0$, $$ \begin{align*} \int_{C_R} f(z) \, e^{iα z} \d z \to 0 \qquad \text{as }R\to \infty \end{align*} $$ where $C_R: [0,π]\to \C$ is the upper semicircle of radius $R$ given by $C_R(t) = Re^{it}$.
