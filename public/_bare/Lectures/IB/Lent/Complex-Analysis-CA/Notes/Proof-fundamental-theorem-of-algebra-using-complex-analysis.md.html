Suppose $p(z) = a_d z^d + a_{d-1} z^{d-1} + \ldots + a_1 z + a_0$ ($a_d\neq 0$) does not attain 0. Since $p$ is entire, so is $$ \begin{align*} f(z) := \frac{1}{p(z)}. \end{align*} $$

Now $|p(z)| \to \infty$ as $|z|\to \infty$ because $|p(z)/z^d| \to a_d$, so $|f(z)|\to 0$ as $|z|\to \infty$. Therefore $f$ is bounded on $\C \setminus B(0,R)$ for some sufficiently large $R>0$. But $f$ is also bounded on $\overline{B(0,R)}$ by continuity, so it is bounded on the entire $\C$.

Thus by Liouville's theorem, $f$ is constant. Thus $p$ is also constant.
