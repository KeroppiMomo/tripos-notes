Let $f: U\to \C$ be a function on a domain $U$.

  • $f$ is differentiable at $u\in U$ if $$ \begin{align*} f'(u) = \lim_{z\to u} \frac{f(z) - f(u)}{z-u} \end{align*} $$ exists.

  • $f$ is holomorphic at $u\in U$ if there exists $ε>0$ such that $f$ is differentiable at all $z\in B(u,ε)$.

  • $f: \C\to \C$ is entire if it is holomorphic on $\C$.
