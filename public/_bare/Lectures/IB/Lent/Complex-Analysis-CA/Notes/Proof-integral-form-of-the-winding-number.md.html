Write $γ(t) = w + r(t) e^{iθ(t)}$. $γ$ is piecewise $C^1$, and thus so are $r(t)$ and $θ(t)$.

Compute $$ \begin{align*} \int_{γ} \frac{\d z}{z-w} ={}& \int_{a}^{b} \frac{γ'(t)}{γ(t) - w} \d t \\ ={}& \int_{a}^{b} \frac{r'(t)}{r(t)} + iθ'(t) \d t \\ ={}& \left[\ln r(t) + iθ(t)\right]_{a}^b \\ ={}& i(θ(b) - θ(a)) \\ ={}& 2πi\, I(γ; w) \end{align*} $$ as $r(a) = r(b)$.
