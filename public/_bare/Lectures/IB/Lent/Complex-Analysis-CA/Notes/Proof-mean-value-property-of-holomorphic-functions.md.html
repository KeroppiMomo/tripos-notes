Apply the Cauchy's integral formula, $$ \begin{align*} f(a) = \frac{1}{2πi} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{ζ-a} \d ζ. \end{align*} $$ Parametrise the circle by $$ \begin{align*} γ(t) = a + ρ\, e^{2πit}, \qquad t\in [0,1]. \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} f(a) ={}& \frac{1}{2πi} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f(γ(t))}{γ(t) - a} \cdot γ'(t) \d t \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2πi} \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f(a + ρ\, e^{2πit})}{ρ \, e^{2πit}} \cdot ρ \cdot 2πi e^{2πit}\d t \\ ={}& \int_{0}^{1} f(a+ρe^{2πit}) \d t. \end{align*} $$
