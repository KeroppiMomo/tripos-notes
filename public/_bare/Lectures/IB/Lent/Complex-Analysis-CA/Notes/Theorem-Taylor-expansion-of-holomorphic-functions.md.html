Let $f: B(a,r)\to \C$ be holomorphic.

$f$ has a power series representation valid on $D(a,r)$ $$ \begin{align*} f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n (z-a)^n \end{align*} $$ where the coefficients $c_n$ are given by $$ \begin{align*} c_n = \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} = \frac{1}{2πi} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}} \d ζ \end{align*} $$ for any $0<ρ<r$.

Proof

  • If $f$ is holomorphic on a domain $U$ and $z\in U$, then for any $B(z,ε)\subseteq U$ we choose, $f$ has a valid power series expansion on the disk. (But this may not be convergent on all of $U$.)

  • Therefore if $f$ is holomorphic, then $f$ is infinitely differentiable and analytic.
