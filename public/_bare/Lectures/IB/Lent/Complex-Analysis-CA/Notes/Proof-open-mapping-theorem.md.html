Let $V\subseteq U$ be an open set in the domain $U$. For any $a\in V$, the local degree of $f$ at $a$ is at least one, so the local mapping theorem implies that for all $r>0$ sufficiently small, $f(B(a,r)) \supseteq B(f(a), ε)$ for some $ε>0$. Choosing $r$ sufficiently small so that $B(a,r)\subseteq V$, we have $f(a) \in B(f(a), ε) \subseteq f(V)$, so $f(V)$ is open.
