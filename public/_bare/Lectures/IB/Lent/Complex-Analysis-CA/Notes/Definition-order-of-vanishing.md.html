Let $f: B(a,r)\to \C$ be holomorphic, $f(a) = 0$ but $f\not\equiv 0$. The order of vanishing of $f$ at $a$ is the smallest $m>0$ such that the Taylor series coefficient $c_m\neq 0$.

Equivalently, we can write $$ \begin{align*} f(z) = (z-a)^m g(z) \end{align*} $$ where $g$ is holomorphic on $B(a,r)$ and $g(a)\neq 0$.

We say $f$ has a zero of order $m$ at $a$.
