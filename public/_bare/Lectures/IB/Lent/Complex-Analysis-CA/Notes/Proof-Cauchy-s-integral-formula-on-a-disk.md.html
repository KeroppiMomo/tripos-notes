We can replace $f(ζ)$ with $f(z)$: fix $z\in B(a,r)$, and define $$ \begin{align*} g(ζ) := \begin{cases} \dfrac{f(ζ) - f(z)}{ζ-z}, &ζ\neq z. \\ f'(z), &ζ=z. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ $g$ is continuous on $B(a,r)$ and holomorphic on $B(a,r) \setminus \set{z}$, so $$ \begin{align*} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} g(ζ) \d ζ = 0. \end{align*} $$ Since the circle $|ζ-a|=ρ$ does not contain $z$, the integrand is $\frac{f(ζ) - f(z)}{ζ-z}$, so $$ \begin{align*} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{ζ-z} \d ζ = \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(z)}{ζ-z} \d ζ = f(z) \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{1}{ζ-z}\d ζ. \end{align*} $$ To compute the integral, recall the power series expansion $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{1-w} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} w^n \end{align*} $$ has a radius of convergence of 1. So $$ \begin{align*} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{1}{ζ-z} \d ζ ={}& \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{1}{ζ-a} \frac{1}{1 - \frac{z-a}{ζ-a}} \d ζ \\ ={}& \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(z-a)^n}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}} \d ζ \tag{*}\\ ={}& \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (z-a)^n \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{1}{(ζ-a)^{n+1}} \d ζ \\ ={}& \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (z-a)^n \oint_{|ξ|=ρ} \frac{1}{ξ^{n+1}} \d ξ. \end{align*} $$ Since $|\frac{z-a}{ζ-a}| = \frac{ρ}{|ζ-a|} < 1$, the series in $(*)$ converges uniformly on the circle $|ζ-a|=ρ$, so we can swap the integral and sum.

From here, the contour integral is $2πi$ when $n=0$ and 0 otherwise. Therefore we obtain the result $$ \begin{align*} \oint_{|ζ-a|=ρ} \frac{f(ζ)}{ζ-z} \d ζ = 2πi f(z). \end{align*} $$
