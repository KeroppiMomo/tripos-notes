Fix any $z\in \C$. By the Cauchy's integral formula on any disk $B(0,R)$, $$ \begin{align*} |f(z) - f(0)| = \frac{1}{2π} \left|\int_{|ζ|=R} f(ζ)\cdot \left(\frac{1}{ζ-z} - \frac{1}{ζ}\right) \d ζ\right| \end{align*} $$ for any $R>|z|$.

For $R>2|z|$, $$ \begin{align*} \left|\frac{1}{ζ-z}-\frac{1}{ζ}\right| ={}& \left|\frac{z}{ζ(ζ-z)}\right| \leq \frac{|z|}{R\cdot R/2} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} |f(z) - f(0)| \leq{}& \frac{1}{2π}\cdot 2πR\cdot \sup_{|ζ|=R} \left|f(ζ) \left(\frac{1}{ζ-z}-\frac{1}{ζ}\right)\right| \\ \leq{}& R\cdot \sup_{|ζ|=R} |f(ζ)| \cdot \frac{|z|}{R\cdot R/2} \\ \to{}& 0 \quad \text{ as } R\to \infty \end{align*} $$ since $f$ is bounded by assumption.

Thus $f(z) = f(0)$ for all $z\in \C$.
