Let $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n(z-a)^n$ be a complex power series with a radius of convergence $R>0$.

$f$ is holomorphic on $B(a,R)$, with $$ \begin{align*} f'(z) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} nc_n(z-a)^{n-1} \end{align*} $$ which also has a radius of convergence $R$ about $z=a$.

Proof

  • By induction, $f$ has derivatives of all orders on $B(a,R)$ and $f^{(n)}(a) = n!\,c_n$.

  • If $f(z) \equiv 0$ on $B(a,ε)$, then $f(z) \equiv 0$ on $B(a,R)$.
