Let $f$ be meromorphic on a domain $U$ and $γ$ be a closed curve homologous to zero in $U$, not containing any poles of $f$.

Let $a_1, \ldots, a_m$ be the finitely many poles of $f$ that $γ$ winds around (i.e. $I(γ; a_n)$ $\neq 0$). Then $$ \begin{align*} \oint_γ f(z) \d z = 2πi\sum_{n=1}^{m} I(γ; a_n) \Res_{z=a} f(z). \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • This generalises Cauchy's theorem and Cauchy's integral formula.

  • If $γ$ is a closed curve bounding a domain $D$, and $f$ is holomorphic on $D$ and a neighbourhood of $γ$, then $\oint_γ f = 0$ and $f(w) = \frac{1}{2πi}\oint_γ f(z)/(z-w)\d z$ for all $w\in D\setminus γ.$
