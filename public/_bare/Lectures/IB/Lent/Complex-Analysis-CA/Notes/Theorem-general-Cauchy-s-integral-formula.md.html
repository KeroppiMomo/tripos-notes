Let $f: U\to \C$ be holomorphic on a domain $U$, and let $γ$ be a closed curve in $U$.

If $γ$ is homologous to zero in $U$, then $$ \begin{align*} \oint_γ f(z) \d z = 0 \end{align*} $$ and for all $w\in U\setminus γ$, $$ \begin{align*} I(γ; w) \, f(w) = \frac{1}{2πi} \oint_{γ} \frac{f(z)}{z-w}\d z. \end{align*} $$
