Suppose $f$ is holomorphic and non-constant on a open set $V$. Fix $a\in V$. Choose $r>0$ such that $\overline{B(a,r)}^\times$ has no zero of $f(z) - f(a)$ by the principle of isolated zeros. Let $γ=\partial B(a,r)$. Since $γ$ does not contain $f(a)$, choose $0<ε<\min_{z\in γ}|f(z) - f(a)|$.

For any $w\in B(f(a), ε)$ and $z\in γ$, $$ \begin{align*} f(z) - w = \bigl(f(a) - w\bigr) + \bigl(f(z) - f(a)\bigr) \end{align*} $$ and notice $$ \begin{align*} |f(a) - w| < ε < |f(z) - f(a)|. \end{align*} $$ Therefore by Rouché's, $f(z) - w$ and $f(z)-f(a)$ have the same number of zeros in $B(a,r)$, which is at least one. Thus $B(f(a), ε) \subseteq f(V)$.
