At any point on a charged surface, let $\v n$ be the unit normal vector, pointing from region 1 to 2.

A surface current $\v K$ (current per unit length), e.g. of the form $J_ϕ(r) = K_ϕ δ(r-R)$ in a solenoid, results in discontinuity in the magnetic field $$ \begin{align*} [\v n \times \v B] ={}& μ_0 \v K, \\ [\v n\cdot \v B] ={}& 0. \end{align*} $$
