Time-independent circuit

If $C(t) = C$ is static, integrate (M3) and apply Stokes' theorem to obtain $$ \begin{align*} \oint_{C} \v E\cdot \d \v x = \int_{S} (∇\times \v E)\cdot \d \v S = -\dd{t}\int_{S} \v B\cdot \d \v S. \end{align*} $$

Time-dependent circuit

Let $V$ be the volume swept out by $S(t)$ in the time interval $δt$. Its boundary is $\partial V =S(t+δt) - S(t) + Σ$, where $Σ$ is the surface swept out by $C(t)$ in time $δt$.

$$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(t + δt) - \mathcal{F}(t) ={}& \int_{S(t+δt)} \v B(\v x, t+δt)\cdot \d \v S - \int_{S(t)} \v B(\v x,t) \cdot \d \v S \\ ={}& \int_{S(t+δt)} \Bigl(\v B(\v x,t) + δt \pdfd{\v B}{t} + O(δt^2)\Bigr) \cdot \d \v S - \int_{S(t)} \v B(\v x,t)\cdot \d \v S \\ ={}& \int_{S(t+δt)-S(t)} \v B(\v x,t)\cdot \d \v S + δt \int_{S(t)} \pdfd{\v B}{t}\cdot \d \v S + O(δt^2) \\ ={}& \int_{\partial V} \v B\cdot \d \v S - \int_{Σ} \v B\cdot \d \v S - δt \int_{S(t)} (∇\times \v E)\cdot \d \v S + O(δt^2). \end{align*} $$

The first term vanishes because of divergence theorem and $∇\cdot \v B = 0$: $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\partial V} \v B\cdot \d\v S = \int_{V} ∇\cdot \v B \d^3\v x = 0. \end{align*} $$

For the second term, each point on $C(t)$ travels $\v v \,δt$ in time $δt$, so each length element $\d \v x$ of $C(t)$ sweeps out an outward pointing area element $$ \begin{align*} \d \v S = \d \v x \times \v v\, \delta t. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} -\int_{Σ} \v B \cdot \d \v S ={}& -\int_{C} \v B\cdot (\d \v x \times \v v\,δt) + O(δt^2) \\ ={}& -δt \int_{C} (\v v \times \v B)\cdot \d \v x + O(δt^2). \end{align*} $$

The third term can be rewritten by Stokes' theorem as $$ \begin{align*} -δt \int_{C} \v E\cdot \d \v x. \end{align*} $$

Thus $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{F}(t+δt) - \mathcal{F}(t) ={}& -δt \int_{C} (\v E + \v v\times \v B) \cdot \d \v x + O(δt^2) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \dfd{\mathcal{F}}{t} = -\int_{C} (\v E + \v v\times \v B)\cdot \d \v x. \end{align*} $$
