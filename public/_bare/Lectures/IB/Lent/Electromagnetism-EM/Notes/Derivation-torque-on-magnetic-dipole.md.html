Recall the magnetic force on a volume element is $\v J\times \v B \d^3\v x$, so the total torque is $$ \begin{align*} τ = \int_{V} \v x \times (\v J\times \v B) \d^3 \v x = \int_{V} (\v x \cdot \v B)\v J - (\v x\cdot \v J) \v B \d^3 \v x. \end{align*} $$ Since $\v B$ slowly changes throughout $V$, approximate $\v B(\v x) \approx \v B(\v 0)$ to zeroth order to get $$ \begin{align*} τ_j \approx B_i(\v 0) \int_{V} x_iJ_j \d^3 \v x - B_j(\v 0) \int_{V} x_i J_i \d^3 \v x. \end{align*} $$

The magnetic dipole moment satisfies $$ \begin{align*} \int_{V} x_i J_j \d^3 \v x = ε_{ijk} m_k \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} τ_j \approx B_i(\v 0) ε_{ijk} m_k - 0 = \v m \times \v B. \end{align*} $$
