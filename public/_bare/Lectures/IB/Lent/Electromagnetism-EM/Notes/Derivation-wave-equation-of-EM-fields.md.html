With $ρ=0$, $\v J = \v 0$, Maxwell's equations imply $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd[2]{\v E}{t} &= \frac{1}{μ_0 ε_0} \pdd{t}(∇\times \v B) \\ &= \frac{1}{μ_0ε_0} ∇\times \pdfd{\v B}{t} \\ &= -\frac{1}{μ_0 ε_0} ∇\times (∇\times \v E) \\ &= -\frac{1}{μ_0 ε_0} \bigl(∇\underbrace{(∇\cdot \v E)}_{0} - ∇^2\v E\bigr) \\ &= \frac{1}{μ_0 ε_0} ∇^2\v E. \end{align*} $$

Similarly $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd[2]{\v B}{t} &= -\pdd{t}(∇\times \v E) \\ &= -∇\times \pdfd{\v E}{t} \\ &= -\frac{1}{μ_0ε_0}∇\times (∇\times \v B) \\ &= -\frac{1}{μ_0ε_0}\bigl(∇\underbrace{(∇\cdot \v B)}_{0} - ∇^2\v B\bigr) \\ &= \frac{1}{μ_0ε_0} ∇^2 \v B. \end{align*} $$
