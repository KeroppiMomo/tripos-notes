For a general charge distribution $ρ(\v x)$ contained within $V = \sb{\v x}{|\v x|<R}$, the potential is, at large $|\v x|\gg R$, $$ \begin{align*} Φ(\v x) = \frac{1}{4πε_0}\left(\frac{Q}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v p\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3} + \frac{1}{2} \frac{Q_{ij} x_i x_j}{|\v x|^5} + \ldots\right) \end{align*} $$ where the first three multipole moments are

  • total charge (electric monopole moment, scalar) $$ \begin{align*} Q = \int_{V} ρ(\v x) \d^3 \v x, \end{align*} $$

  • electric dipole moment (vector) $$ \begin{align*} \v p = \int_{V} \v x\,ρ(\v x) \d^3 \v x, \end{align*} $$

  • electric quadruple moment (traceless second-order symmetric tensor) $$ \begin{align*} Q_{ij} = \int_{V} (3x_i x_j - |\v x|^2 δ_{ij})\,ρ(\v x) \d^3 \v x. \end{align*} $$
