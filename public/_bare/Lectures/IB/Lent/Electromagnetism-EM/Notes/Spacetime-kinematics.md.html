The proper time $τ$ is the time experienced by the particle. The world line parametrised in $τ$ has constant speed $-c^2$, i.e. $\d X \cdot \d X = -c^2 \d τ^2$.

The 4-velocity of a massive particle is $$ \begin{align*} U = \dfd{X}{τ} = γ_\v u(c,\v u) \end{align*} $$ where $\v u = \dfd{\v x}{t}$ is the 3-velocity. We have $$ \begin{align*} U\cdot U = -c^2. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

The 4-momentum of a massive particle with rest mass $m>0$ is $$ \begin{align*} P^μ = mU^μ = mγ_\v u(c, \v u) = \left(\frac{E}{c}, \v p\right) \end{align*} $$ where $E$ is the energy and $\v p = mγ_\v u \v u$ is the 3-momentum. Computing its square $P\cdot P = -m^2c^2$ gives $$ \begin{align*} E^2 = (mc^2)^2 + (|\v p|c)^2. \end{align*} $$ Massless particles such as photons travel at the speed of light and they have $$ \begin{align*} E = |\v p|c. \end{align*} $$

The relativistic equation of motion for a massive particle is $$ \begin{align*} \dfd{P^μ}{τ} = f^μ \end{align*} $$ where $f^μ$ is the 4-force. Since $P\cdot P$ is constant, we must have $$ \begin{align*} f\cdot P = 0. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)
