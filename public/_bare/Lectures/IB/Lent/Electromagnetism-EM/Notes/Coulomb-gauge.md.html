We can always assume the Coulomb gauge $$ \begin{align*} ∇\cdot \v A = 0 \end{align*} $$ for the magnetic vector potential $\v A$ by making a gauge transformation $\v A = \tilde {\v A} + ∇χ$ with $χ$ the solution of the Poisson equation $-∇^2χ = ∇\cdot \tilde {\v A}$.

Then using the identity $∇\times (∇\times \v A) = ∇(∇\cdot \v A) - ∇^2 \v A$, (M4') becomes the Poisson equation $$ \begin{align*} -∇^2 \v A = μ_0 \v J. \end{align*} $$
