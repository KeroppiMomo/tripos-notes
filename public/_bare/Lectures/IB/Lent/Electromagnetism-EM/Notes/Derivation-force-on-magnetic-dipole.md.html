Total force on the current distribution is $$ \begin{align*} \v F = \int_{V} \v J \times \v B \d^3 \v x. \end{align*} $$ Taylor expand $$ \begin{align*} \v B(\v x) = \v B(\v 0) + (\v x\cdot ∇)\v B(\v 0) + O(|\v x|^2) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} F_i &\approx ε_{ijk} \int_{V} J_j (B_k(\v 0) + x_l\partial_l B_k(\v 0)) \d^3 \v x \\ &= ε_{ijk} B_k(\v 0) \underbrace{\int_{V} J_j \d^3 \v x}_{0} + ε_{ijk} \partial_l B_k(\v 0) \underbrace{\int_{V} x_l J_j \d^3 \v x}_{ε_{ljn} m_n} \\ &= \partial_i B_k(\v 0) m_k - \underbrace{\partial_k B_k(\v 0)}_{∇\cdot \v B = 0} m_i \\ &= \partial_i(\v m\cdot \v B)(\v 0), \end{align*} $$ where we have used results from here.
