(In what follows, $\v A\cdot \v B = A_1 B_1 + A_2 B_2 + A_3 B_3$ and $|\v A|^2 = A_1 \overline{A_1} + A_2 \overline{A_2} + A_3 \overline{A_3}$.)

We use the identity $$ \begin{align*} \Re(A) \Re(B) = \frac{A+\bar{A}}{2} \frac{B + \bar{B}}{2} = \frac{AB + A\bar{B} + \bar{A}B + \bar{A} \bar{B}}{4} = \Re\left(\frac{AB + A\bar B}{2}\right). \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} w &= \frac{ε_0}{2} (\Re(\v E_0 e^{i(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt)}))^2 + \frac{1}{2μ_0} (\Re(\v B_0 e^{i(\v k\cdot \v x-ωt)}))^2 \\ &= \frac{ε_0}{4}\Re\left(\v E_0\cdot \v E_0 e^{2i(\v k\cdot \v x-ωt)} + |\v E_0|^2\right) +\frac{1}{4μ_0}\Re\left(\v B_0\cdot \v B_0 e^{2i(\v k\cdot \v x-ωt)} + |\v B_0|^2\right). \end{align*} $$ The terms involving $e^{2i(\v k\cdot \v x-ωt)}$ average to zero over time or space, leaving the average energy density $$ \begin{align*} \langle w\rangle = \frac{ε_0}{4}|\v E_0|^2 + \frac{1}{4μ_0}|\v B_0|^2. \end{align*} $$ Since $\v B_0 = \frac{1}{c}\vhat k\times \v E_0$ and $\vhat k\cdot \v E_0 = 0$, the two contributions are equal and so $$ \begin{align*} \langle w\rangle = \frac{1}{2}ε_0 |\v E_0|^2. \end{align*} $$

The Poynting vector is $$ \begin{align*} \v S ={}& \frac{1}{μ_0} \v E\times \v B \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2μ_0}\Re\left(\v E_0\times \v B_0 e^{2i(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt)} +\v E_0\times \overline{\v B_0}\right) \end{align*} $$ with the first term averaging to zero. So $$ \begin{align*} \langle \v S\rangle &= \frac{1}{2μ_0c}|\v E_0|^2 \vhat k \\ &= \frac{1}{2}ε_0|\v E_0|^2 c \vhat k \\ &=\langle w\rangle c \vhat k. \end{align*} $$
