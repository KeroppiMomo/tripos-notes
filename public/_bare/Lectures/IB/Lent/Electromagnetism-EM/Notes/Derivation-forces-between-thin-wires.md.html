Apply the Biot-Savart law and the Lorentz force: $$ \begin{align*} \v F_{ij} ={}& I_i \int_{C_i} \d \v x_i \times \v B_j(\v x_i) \\ ={}& \frac{μ_0 I_i I_j}{4π} \int_{C_i} \int_{C_j} \d \v x_i \times \frac{\d \v x_j \times (\v x_i - \v x_j)}{|\v x_i - \v x_j|^3}. \end{align*} $$ To see the second expression, use the vector triple product formula $$ \begin{align*} \v F_{ij} ={}& \frac{μ_0I_i I_j}{4π} \int_{C_{i}} \int_{C_j} \left(\d \v x_i \cdot \frac{\v x_i - \v x_j}{|\v x_i - \v x_j|^3}\right) \d \v x_j - \frac{\v x_i - \v x_j}{|\v x_i - \v x_j|^3} (\d \v x_i \cdot \d \v x_j) \\ ={}& \frac{μ_0I_i I_j}{4π} \Biggl(\int_{C_j} \underbrace{\left(\int_{C_i} ∇\Bigl(\frac{1}{|\v x_i - \v x_j|}\Bigr)\cdot \d \v x_i\right)}_{0} \d \v x_j + \int_{C_i} \int_{C_j} \frac{\v x_j - \v x_i}{|\v x_j - \v x_i|^3} (\d \v x_i \cdot \d \v x_j)\Biggr) \\ ={}& \frac{μ_0I_i I_j}{4π} \int_{C_i} \int_{C_j} \frac{\v x_j - \v x_i}{|\v x_j - \v x_i|^3} (\d \v x_i \cdot \d \v x_j) \end{align*} $$ because $C_i$ is closed or infinitely long.
