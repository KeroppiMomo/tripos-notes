For the plane wave solution here, the averaged (over time or space) energy density and flux density are $$ \begin{align*} \langle w\rangle &= \frac{1}{2}ε_0 |\v E_0|^2, \\ \langle \v S\rangle &= \langle w\rangle c\vhat k, \end{align*} $$ implying the wave transports energy at speed $c$ in the direction $\vhat k$.
