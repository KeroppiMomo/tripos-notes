For a cylindrically symmetric charge distribution $ρ(r)$ contained within $r\leq R$ (in cylindrical polars), $$ \begin{align*} \v E(r) = \frac{λ}{2πε_0 r}, \qquad r>R, \end{align*} $$ where $λ$ is the charge per unit length. (Derivation)

In the limit $R\to 0$, we have a line charge $λ$, corresponding to $$ \begin{align*} ρ(\v x) = λ\,δ(x)\,δ(y). \end{align*} $$
