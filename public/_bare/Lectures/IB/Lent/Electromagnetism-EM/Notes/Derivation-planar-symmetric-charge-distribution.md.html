$\v E$ also has planar symmetry, so $\v E = E(z) \v e_z$. This satisfies $∇\times \v E= \v 0$. Reflectional symmetry requires $E(-z) = E(z)$.

For $z>d$, apply Guass's law to a Gaussian pillbox of height $2z$ and arbitrary area $A$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \frac{σ A}{ε_0} = \int_{S} \v E\cdot \d \v S = E(z)\, A - E(-z)\, A = 2E(z)\, A. \end{align*} $$
