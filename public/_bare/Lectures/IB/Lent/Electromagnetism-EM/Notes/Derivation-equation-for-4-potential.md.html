Substituting $$ \begin{align*} F_{μν} = \partial_μ A_ν - \partial_ν A_μ, \end{align*} $$ the second equation becomes $$ \begin{align*} ε^{μνρσ} \partial_ν F_{ρσ} = ε^{μνρσ}\partial_ν\partial_ρ A_σ - ε^{μνρσ} \partial_ν \partial_σA_ρ = 0, \end{align*} $$ where each term vanishes because $ε^{μνρσ}$ is antisymmetric but the derivatives are symmetric.

The first equation is $$ \begin{align*} \partial_ν \partial^μ A^ν - \partial_ν \partial^ν A^μ = μ_0 J^μ. \end{align*} $$ Using the Lorentz gauge $\partial_ν A^ν = 0$, we get $$ \begin{align*} -\partial_ν \partial^ν A^μ = -\square A^μ = μ_0 J^μ. \end{align*} $$
