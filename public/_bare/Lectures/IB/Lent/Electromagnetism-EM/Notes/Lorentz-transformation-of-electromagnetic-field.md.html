If an inertial frame $S'$ moves at a velocity $\v v$ relative to $S$, then the components of $\v E$ and $\v B$ parallel and perpendicular to $\v v$ transform as $$ \begin{align*} \v E_\parallel' &= \v E_\parallel, & \v E_\perp' &= γ(\v E_\perp + \v v\times \v B_\perp), \\ \v B_\parallel' &= \v B_\parallel, & \v B_\perp' &= γ(\v B_\perp - \frac{1}{c^2}\v v\times \v E_\perp). \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)
