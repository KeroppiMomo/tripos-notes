Suppose the current distribution is contained within a volume $V$. $$ \begin{align*} ∇\cdot \v A &= \frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_V ∇\cdot \left(\frac{\v J(\v x')}{|\v x-\v x'|}\right) \d^3\v x' \\ &= \frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_V \v J(\v x') \cdot ∇\left(\frac{1}{|\v x-\v x'|}\right) \d^3\v x' \\ &= -\frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_V \v J(\v x') \cdot ∇'\left(\frac{1}{|\v x-\v x'|}\right) \d^3 \v x' \\ &= -\frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_V ∇'\cdot \left(\frac{\v J(\v x')}{|\v x-\v x'|}\right) - \frac{1}{|\v x-\v x'|}\underbrace{∇'\cdot \v J(\v x')}_{0} \d^3\v x' \\ &= -\frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_{\partial V} \frac{\v J(\v x')}{|\v x-\v x'|} \cdot \d \v S' \\ &= 0. \end{align*} $$ This also holds if $\v J$ decays sufficiently quickly as $|\v x|\to \infty$.
