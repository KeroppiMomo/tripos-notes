For a spherically symmetric charge distribution $ρ(r)$ contained within $r\leq R$ (in spherical polars), $$ \begin{align*} \v E(r) = \frac{Q}{4πε_0 r^2}, \qquad r>R. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

In the limit $R\to 0$, we have a point charge $Q$, corresponding to $$ \begin{align*} ρ(\v x) = Q\, δ(\v x). \end{align*} $$

The Coulomb force is the corresponding Lorentz force. $$ \begin{align*} \v F =q\v E = \frac{Qq}{4πε_0 r^2} \v e_r, \end{align*} $$ similar to gravity, but it can be both repulsive and attractive, and is much stronger.
