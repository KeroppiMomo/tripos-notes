A 4-tensor of type $(r,s)$ is an object whose $4^{r+s}$ components transform as $$ \begin{align*} {T'^{μ_1\ldots μ_r}}_{ν_1\ldots ν_s} = {Λ^{μ_1}}_{ρ_1} \ldots {Λ^{μ_r}}_{ρ_r}\, {(Λ\inv)^{σ_1}}_{ν_1}\ldots{(Λ\inv)^{σ_s}}_{ν_s} \, {T^{ρ_1 \ldots ρ_r}}_{σ_1 \ldots σ_s} \end{align*} $$ under a Lorentz transformation $Λ$.
