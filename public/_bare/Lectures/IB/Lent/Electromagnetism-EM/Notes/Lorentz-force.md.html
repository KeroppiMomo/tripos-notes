Charged particles interact by generating electric fields $\v E(\v x, t)$ and magnetic fields $\v B(\v x, t)$ around them that are experienced by other charged particles.

The Lorentz force on a particle of charge $q$ and velocity $\v v$ is $$ \begin{align*} \v F = q(\v E + \v v\times \v B). \end{align*} $$ For a continuous charge and current densities, the Lorentz force per unit volume is $$ \begin{align*} ρ \v E + \v J \times \v B. \end{align*} $$
