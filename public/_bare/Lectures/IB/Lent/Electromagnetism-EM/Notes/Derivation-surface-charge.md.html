Normal component

Take a Gaussian pillbox parallel to the surface, and take $A \to 0$ and $h\to 0$. This gives $$ \begin{align*} \frac{σA}{ε_0} = \int_{S} \v E\cdot \d \v S \sim [\v nA\cdot \v E] \implies [\v n\cdot \v E] = \frac{σ}{ε_0}. \end{align*} $$

Tangential component

For any direction $\vhat v$ tangential to $\v n$, consider a closed rectangular curve, and take $L\to 0$ and $h\to 0$. By Stokes' theorem and $∇\times \v E = \v 0$, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \int_{C} \v E\cdot \d \v l \sim [\vhat vL\cdot \v E] \implies [\vhat v\cdot \v E]=0. \end{align*} $$
