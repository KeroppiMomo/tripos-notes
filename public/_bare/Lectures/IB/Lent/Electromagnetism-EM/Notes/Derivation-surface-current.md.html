Tangential field

Consider any direction $\vhat v$ perpendicular to $\v n$. Applying Ampere's law to a rectangular loop with $L\to 0$, $h\to 0$, $$ \begin{align*} μ_0 (L\vhat v \times \v n)\cdot \v K \sim \oint_{C} \v B\cdot \d \v x \sim [\v B\cdot L\vhat v] \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} [\v n\times \v B] = μ_0\v K. \end{align*} $$

Normal field

Take a small pillbox with $A\to 0$ and $h\to 0$. By $∇\cdot \v B = 0$ and the divergence theorem, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \oint_S \v B\cdot \d \v S \sim [\v B\cdot A\v n] \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} [\v n\cdot \v B] = 0. \end{align*} $$
