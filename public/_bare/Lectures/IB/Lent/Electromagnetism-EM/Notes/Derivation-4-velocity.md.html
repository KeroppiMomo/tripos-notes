By definition of proper time, $$ \begin{align*} U\cdot U = -c^2. \end{align*} $$ But $X^μ = (ct, \v x)$, so $$ \begin{align*} U^μ = \dfd{t}{τ}\Bigl(c, \dfd{\v x}{t}\Bigr) \end{align*} $$ and so $$ \begin{align*} U\cdot U = \left(\dfd{t}{τ}\right)^2 \left(-c^2 + |\v u|^2\right). \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \dfd{t}{τ} = \frac{c}{\sqrt{c^2 - |\v u|^2}} = γ_\v u. \end{align*} $$
