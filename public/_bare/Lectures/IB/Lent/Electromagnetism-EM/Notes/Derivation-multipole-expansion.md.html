Taylor expand: $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{|\v x-\v x'|} ={}& \frac{1}{|\v x|} - \v x' \cdot ∇\left(\frac{1}{|\v x|}\right) + \frac{1}{2}(\v x'\cdot ∇)(\v x'\cdot ∇)\left(\frac{1}{|\v x|}\right) + O(|\v x'|^3) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v x'\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3} - \frac{1}{2}\v x' \cdot ∇\left(\frac{\v x'\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3}\right) + O(|\v x'|^3) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v x'\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3} + \frac{1}{2}\v x'\cdot \left(\frac{3(\v x'\cdot \v x)\v x}{|\v x|^5} - \frac{\v x'}{|\v x|^3}\right) + O(|\v x'|^3) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v x'\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3} + \frac{1}{2}\frac{3(\v x'\cdot \v x)^2 - |\v x'|^2|\v x|^2}{|\v x|^5} + O(|\v x'|^3) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v x'\cdot \v x}{|\v x|^3} + \frac{1}{2}\frac{3x_i x'_i x_j x'_j - x_i x_i x'_j x'_j}{|\v x|^5} + O(|\v x'|^3) \end{align*} $$

So the solution to Poisson's equation becomes $$ \begin{align*} Φ(\v x) ={}& \frac{1}{4πε_0} \int_{V} \frac{ρ(\v x')}{|\v x-\v x'|} \d^3 \v x' \\ ={}& \frac{1}{4πε_0} \left(\frac{\int_{V} ρ(\v x') \d^3 \v x'}{|\v x|} + \frac{\v x \cdot \int_{V} \v x' ρ(\v x') \d ^3 \v x'}{|\v x|^3} + \frac{1}{2}\frac{x_i x_j \int_{V} (3x_i' x_j' - |\v x'|^2 δ_{ij}) ρ(\v x') \d^3 \v x'}{|\v x|^5} + \ldots\right). \end{align*} $$
