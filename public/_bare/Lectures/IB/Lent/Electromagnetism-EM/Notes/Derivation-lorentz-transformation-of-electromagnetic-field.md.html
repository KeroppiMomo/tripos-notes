Consider a Lorentz boost in the standard configuration, with frame $S'$ travelling at $v$ in the $+x$ direction relative to $S$: $$ \begin{align*} Λ = \pmat{γ & -γβ \\ -γβ & γ \\ & &1\\ & & & 1} \iff Λ\inv = \pmat{γ & γβ \\ γβ & γ \\ & &1\\ & & & 1}. \end{align*} $$

$F_{μν}$ transforms as $$ \begin{align*} F'_{μν} = {(Λ\inv)^ρ}_μ {(Λ\inv)^σ}_ν F_{ρσ}. \end{align*} $$ In matrix form, $$ \begin{align*} F' = (Λ\inv)^T F (Λ\inv). \end{align*} $$

Multiply out to see $$ \begin{align*} F' = \pmat{0 & -E_x/c & γ(-E_y/c + βB_z) & γ(-E_z/c - βB_y) \\ E_x/c & 0 & γ(B_z - βE_y/c) & γ(-B_y - βE_z/c) \\ γ(E_y/c - βE_z) & γ(-B_z + βE_y/c) & 0 & B_x \\ γ(E_z/c + βE_y) & γ(B_y + βE_z/c) & -B_x & 0}. \end{align*} $$
