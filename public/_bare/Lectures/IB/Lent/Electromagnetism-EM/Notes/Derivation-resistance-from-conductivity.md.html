Suppose a wire is along a unit vector $\v n$. For a uniform electric field $\v E = E\v n$, Ohm's law implies $$ \begin{align*} \v J = σE\v n. \end{align*} $$ So the total current is $$ \begin{align*} I = σEA. \end{align*} $$ The voltage is then $$ \begin{align*} V = \int \v E\cdot \d \v x = EL = \frac{LI}{σA}. \end{align*} $$
