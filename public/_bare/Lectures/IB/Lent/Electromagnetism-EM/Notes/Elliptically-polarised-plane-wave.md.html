For $\v E_0 = \v a - i\v b$, $\v B_0 = \v a' - i\v b'$, we have $$ \begin{align*} \v E &= \v a \cos(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt) + \v b \sin(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt), \\ \v B &= \v a' \cos(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt) + \v b' \sin(\v k\cdot \v x - ωt), \\ \end{align*} $$ with $|\v a'| = |\v a|/c, |\v b'| = |\v b|/c$ and $\v k,\v a,\v a'$ and $\v k,\v b,\v b'$ being right-handed orthogonal triads. The fields trace out an ellipse and the wave is elliptically polarised.

Circular polarisation occurs when $|\v b| = |\v a|$ and $\v a\cdot \v b = 0$. It is right-handed if $\v b = \vhat k\times \v a$ and left-handed if $\v b = -\vhat k\times \v a$.
