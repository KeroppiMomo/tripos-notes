The solution to the Poisson equation governing $\v A$ is (assuming $\v A \to \v 0$ as $|\v x|\to \infty$) $$ \begin{align*} \v A(\v x) = \frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_{\R^3} \frac{\v J(\v x')}{|\v x-\v x'|} \d^3 \v x', \end{align*} $$ which satisfies the Coulomb gauge condition (Derivation).

The magnetic field is given by the Biot-Savart law $$ \begin{align*} \v B(\v x) = \frac{μ_0}{4π} \int_{\R^3} \frac{\v J(\v x')\times (\v x - \v x')}{|\v x - \v x'|^3} \d^3 \v x'. \end{align*} $$
