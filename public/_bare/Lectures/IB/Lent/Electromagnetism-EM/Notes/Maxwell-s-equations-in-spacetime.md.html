Maxwell's equations can be written in covariant form $$ \begin{align*} \partial_ν F^{μν} = μ_0 F^μ \end{align*} $$ and $$ \begin{align*} ε^{μνρσ} \partial_ν F_{ρσ} = 0 \iff \partial_μ F_{νρ} + \partial_ν F_{ρμ} + \partial_ρ F_{μν} = 0. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

They agree with charge conservation. (Derivation)

In the Lorentz gauge, the second equation is automatically satisfied, and the first becomes $$ \begin{align*} -\square A^μ = μ_0 J^μ. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)
