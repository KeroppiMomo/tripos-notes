First fundamental form agrees $\implies$ locally isometric

Let $f = σ' \circ σ\inv: U\to U'$, which is a diffeomorphism. For a curve $γ(t) = σ(u(t), v(t))$ on $Σ$, its image is $f\circ γ(t) = σ'(u(t), v(t))$. Since the first fundamental form agrees, their lengths are the same.

Locally isometric $\implies$ first fundamental form

We will show lengths of curves determine the first fundamental form. WLOG let $σ: V\to U$ be an allowable parametrisation with $V=B(0,δ)\subseteq \R^2$ and $σ(0) = p$.

Using the curve $$ \begin{align*} γ_ε: [0,ε]&\to U \\ t &\mapsto σ(t, 0), \end{align*} $$ we can determine $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{length}(γ_ε) = \int_{0}^{ε} \sqrt{E(t,0)} \d t \implies \sqrt{E(0,0)} = \dd{ε} \mathrm{length}(γ_ε)\Big|_0. \end{align*} $$

Similarly $$ \begin{align*} χ_ε: [0,ε]&\to U \\ t &\mapsto σ(0, t), \end{align*} $$ determines $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{length}(χ_ε) = \int_{0}^{ε} \sqrt{G(0,t)} \d t \implies \sqrt{G(0,0)} = \dd{ε} \mathrm{length}(γ_ε)\Big|_0. \end{align*} $$

Finally using $$ \begin{align*} λ_ε: [0,ε]&\to U \\ t &\mapsto σ(t, t), \end{align*} $$ we have $$ \begin{align*} &\mathrm{length}(λ_ε) = \int_{0}^{ε} \sqrt{E(t,t) + 2F(t,t) + G(t,t)} \d t \\ \implies & \sqrt{E(0,0) + 2F(0,0) + G(0,0)} = \dd{ε}\mathrm{length}(λ_ε)\Big|_0. \end{align*} $$

Therefore the first fundamental form at $p=σ(0)$ is determined by lengths of curves.
