Equip $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ with triangulations, with number of vertices $V_1, V_2$, edges $E_1, E_2$, faces $F_1, F_2$. We can form the connect sum by taking closed faces $D_1 \subseteq Σ_1$ and $D_2 \subseteq Σ_2$, and identifying the boundary circles, matching vertices and edges.

Then $Σ_1 \# Σ_2$ inherits a subdivision, with $V_1 + V_2 - 3$ vertices (3 vertices overlapping), $E_1 + E_2 - 3$ edges (3 edges overlapping), and $F_1 + F_2 - 2$ faces (one face removed from each surface). Thus $$ \begin{align*} χ(Σ_1 \# Σ_2) = χ(Σ_1) + χ(Σ_2) - 2. \end{align*} $$
