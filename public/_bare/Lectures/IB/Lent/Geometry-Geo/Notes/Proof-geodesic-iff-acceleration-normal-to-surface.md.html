This is a local statement, so work in an allowable parametrisation $σ$ for $Σ$, and let $γ(t) = σ(u(t), v(t))$.

Since the tangent plane $T_p Σ$ is spanned by $σ_u$ and $σ_v$, the acceleration $\ddot γ(t) = \dd{t}(σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v)$ is normal to $Σ$ iff $$ \begin{align*} \left\langle \dd{t}(σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v), \; σ_u\right\rangle ={}& 0, \\ \left\langle \dd{t}(σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v), \; σ_v\right\rangle ={}& 0. \end{align*} $$

The first equation is equivalent to $$ \begin{align*} 0 ={}& \dd{t}\langle σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v, \; σ_u\rangle - \left\langle σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v,\; \dd{t}σ_u\right\rangle \\ ={}& \dd{t}\bigl(\langle σ_u, σ_u\rangle \dot u + \langle σ_u, σ_v\rangle \dot v\bigr) - \langle σ_u \dot u + σ_v \dot v, \; σ_{uu}\dot u + σ_{uv} \dot v \rangle \\ ={}& \dd{t}\bigl(E\dot u + F\dot v\bigr) - \frac{1}{2}\left(E_u \dot u^2 + 2F_u \dot u \dot v + G_u \dot v^2\right) \end{align*} $$ which is the first geodesic equation. Similarly for the second.

For the last statement, note $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t}\|\dot γ\|^2 = \dd{t}\langle \dot γ, \dot γ\rangle = 2\langle \dot γ, \ddot γ\rangle = 0 \end{align*} $$ since $\dot γ\in T_pΣ$.
