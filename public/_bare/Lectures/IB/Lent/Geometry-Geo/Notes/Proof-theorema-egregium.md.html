Fix a point $p\in Σ_1$. Pick an allowable parametrisation $σ$ with first fundamental form $$ \begin{align*} \d u^2 + G(u,v)\d v^2. \end{align*} $$

Since $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ are locally isometric near $p$ and $f(p)$, $f\circ σ$ is an allowable parametrisation on $Σ_2$ near $f(p)$ with the same first fundamental form.

By the above lemma, they have the same Gauss curvature.
