The real projective plane is $\mathbb{RP}^2 = S^2/{\sim} = S^2 / (\pm 1) = S^2 / (\Z/2)$, where the equivalence relation $\sim$ identifies $x$ with $-x$ for all $x\in S^2$.

  • On the level of sets, $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is the set of straight lines in $\R^3$ through the origin.

  • $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is Hausdorff. (Proof)

  • $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is second countable. (Proof)

  • $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is locally homeomorphic to an open disc. (Proof)

Thus $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is a topological surface.

  • $\mathbb{RP}^2$ can also be viewed as a polygon quotient.
