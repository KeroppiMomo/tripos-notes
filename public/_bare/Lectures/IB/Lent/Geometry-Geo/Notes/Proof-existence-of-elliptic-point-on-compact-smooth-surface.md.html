Let $Σ$ be a compact smooth surface in $\R^3$. Since $Σ$ is compact, the function $Σ\to \R$ sending $p\mapsto \|p\|$ attains a maximum $R>0$ at say $p\in Σ$. Then $Σ \subseteq \overline{B(0,R)}$. WLOG rotate the axes so that $p = (0,0,R)$.

Locally near $p$, $Σ$ can be viewed as the graph of a function over one of the coordinate planes. But $Σ \cap \set{z>R} = \emptyset$, so it must be over the $xy$-plane. Let $V\subseteq \R^2$ be an open set and $f: V\to \R$ such that $Σ = \sb{(x,y,f(x,y))}{(x,y)\in V}$, with $f(0,0)=(0,0,R)$.

Now on $(x,y)\in V$, we have $$ \begin{align*} f(x,y) \leq \sqrt{R^2 - x^2 - y^2}. \end{align*} $$ Noting that $f$ attains a local maximum at $(0,0)$ so $f_x(0,0) = 0 = f_y(0,0)$, Taylor expansion gives $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{2}\underbrace{(f_{xx}(0,0) x^2 + 2f_{xy}(0,0)xy + f_{yy}(0,0)y^2)}_{\II_p((x,y),(x,y))} \leq -\frac{1}{2R}(x^2 + y^2) + O(x^3, y^3). \end{align*} $$ The second order terms are bilinear forms, so $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{L & M \\ M & N} \leq -\frac{1}{R}\pmat{1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $\pmat{L & M \\ M & N}$ is negative definite. Thus $$ \begin{align*} κ(p) = \det \pmat{L & M \\ M & N}\biggr|_0 > 0. \end{align*} $$
