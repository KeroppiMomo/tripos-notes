Lemma (right-angled hyperbolic hexagon) open_in_new

For each $l_α, l_β, l_γ>0$, there exists a right-angled hyperbolic hexagon with side lengths $l_α, *, l_β, *, l_γ, *$ in cyclic order ($*$ are unspecified lengths). Moreover it is unique up to isometry.

Proof

Definition (pair of pants) open_in_new

A pair of pants is a topological space homeomorphic to the complement of three open discs in $S^2$, where the discs have disjoint closures.

Illustration

We can glue together two isometric right-angled hyperbolic hexagons with side lengths $l_α, *, l_β, *, l_γ, *$ to form a hyperbolic pair of pants. The lengths of the boundary circles are then $2l_α, 2l_β, 2l_γ$, which we can freely choose. The boundary circles are geodesics on the pair of pants, in the sense that they are hyperbolic geodesics under a chart.

With two hyperbolic pants with a boundary circle of common length, we can glue them together via a choice of isometry between the boundary circles. (Illustration)

By gluing hyperbolic pants together, we can obtain hyperbolic surfaces $Σ_g$ of genus $g\geq 2$. The boundary being glued together are geodesics.

Pant decomposition is not unique, giving a space of hyperbolic metrics on $Σ_g$. This gives rise to the moduli space $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{hyperbolic metric on $Σ_g$}} / (\text{diffeomorphisms of this abstract smooth surface}). \end{align*} $$
