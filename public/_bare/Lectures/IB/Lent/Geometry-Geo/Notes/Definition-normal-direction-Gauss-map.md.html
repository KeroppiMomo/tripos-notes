Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$ and $p\in Σ$ be a point.

The normal direction to $Σ$ at $p$ is the Euclidean orthogonal complement $(T_p Σ)^\perp\subseteq \R^3$ of the tangent plane.

A unit vector in $(T_pΣ)^\perp$ is a unit normal vector.

The Gauss map $n: U\to S^2$ defined on a subset $U\subseteq Σ$ is a continuous map that sends a point to the unit normal vector.
