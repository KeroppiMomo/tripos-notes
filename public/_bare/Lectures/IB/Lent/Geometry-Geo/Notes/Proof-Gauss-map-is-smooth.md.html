Since smoothness is local, it suffices to consider $U$ as the image of an allowable parametrisation $σ: V\to U$. Here, $n$ is given by $$ \begin{align*} (n\circ σ)(u,v) = \frac{σ_u \times σ_v}{\|σ_u \times σ_v\|}, \end{align*} $$ which is a smooth function from $V \subseteq \R^2$ to $\R^3$. Compose with a stereographic projection $π$ (which is smooth viewed as open set in $\R^3 \to \R^2$) to see $π\circ n\circ σ$ is smooth $V\to \R^2$. Therefore $n$ is smooth by definition.
