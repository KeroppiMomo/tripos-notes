$\mathrm{Möb}(D)$ is generated by $z\mapsto e^{iθ} z$ and $z\mapsto \frac{z - a}{1 - \bar a z}$.

Since $g_\text{hyp}$ only depends on $|z|$, rotation preserves this metric.

Let $w = \frac{z-a}{1-\bar az}$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \d w = \frac{(1 - \bar az) - (z-a)(-\bar a)}{(1 - \bar az)^2} \d z = \frac{1 - |a|^2}{(1 - \bar az)^2} \d z. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\left|\d w\right|}{1 - |w|^2} ={}& \frac{\left|\d z\right|}{|1 - \bar az|^2} \frac{1 - |a|^2}{1 - \left|\frac{z-a}{1 - \bar a z}\right|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{\left|\d z\right| (1 - |a|^2)}{|1 - \bar az|^2 - |z-a|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{\left|\d z\right|}{1 - |z|^2}. \end{align*} $$
