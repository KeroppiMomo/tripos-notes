Check that $T$ and $T\inv$ are indeed inverse diffeomorphisms.

Compute $$ \begin{align*} T'(w) = \frac{(w+i) - (w-i)}{(w+i)^2} = \frac{2i}{(w+i)^2}. \end{align*} $$ Then with $z = T(w)$, $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\left|\d z\right|}{1 - |z|^2} ={}& \frac{|T'(w)| \left|\d w\right|}{1 - |T(w)|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{2 \left|\d w\right|}{|w+i|^2 (1 - \left|\frac{w-i}{w+i}\right|^2)} \\ ={}& \frac{\left|\d w\right|}{2\Im(w)}. \end{align*} $$ So the Riemannian metrics agree.
