Definition (orientation-preserving diffeomorphism on R2) open_in_new

Let $\GL^+(2, \R) \leq \GL(2, \R)$ be the subgroup of $2\times 2$ real matrices with positive determinants.

A diffeomorphism $f: V\to V'$ between open sets $V,V'\subseteq \R^2$ is said to be orientation-preserving if $Df|_x \in \GL^+(2,\R)$ for all $x\in V$.

Definition (orientable surface, orientation) open_in_new

An abstract smooth surface $Σ$ is orientable if it admits an atlas $\set{(U_i, ϕ_i)}_{i\in I}$ such that all transition maps are orientation-preserving.

The choice of such an atlas is called an orientation.

  • There is a definition of orientability for a topological surface, but it requires algebraic topology. We here simply define a topological surface to be orientable if it contains no subset homeomorphic to a Möbius band.

Lemma (diffeomorphisms preserve orientability) open_in_new

Let $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ be abstract smooth surfaces.

If $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ are diffeomorphic, then $Σ_1$ is orientable iff $Σ_2$ is orientable.

Proof

Definition (tangent plane of smooth surface in R3) open_in_new

Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$. Fix an allowable parametrisation $σ: V\to U$ with $σ(0)=p$.

  • The tangent plane at $p$ is $$ \begin{align*} T_p Σ = \im Dσ|_0 \subseteq \R^3. \end{align*} $$

  • The affine tangent plane at $p$ is $p+T_pΣ \subseteq \R^3$.


  • $T_p Σ$ does not depend on the choice of $σ$. (Proof)

Lemma (tangent plane as derivative of all curves) open_in_new

The tangent plane of a smooth surface $Σ$ in $\R^3$ at $p$ is $$ \begin{align*} T_p Σ = \sb{γ'(0)}{\text{smooth }γ: (-ε, ε)\to Σ, \; γ(0)=p}. \end{align*} $$

Proof

Lemma (derivative of map between surfaces preserves tangent spaces) open_in_new

Let $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$.

If $A: \R^3 \to \R^3$ is smooth and $A: Σ_1 \to Σ_2$, then $DA|_p:$ $T_pΣ_1$ $\to T_{A(p)}Σ_2$.

Proof

Definition (normal direction, Gauss map) open_in_new

Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$ and $p\in Σ$ be a point.

The normal direction to $Σ$ at $p$ is the Euclidean orthogonal complement $(T_p Σ)^\perp\subseteq \R^3$ of the tangent plane.

A unit vector in $(T_pΣ)^\perp$ is a unit normal vector.

The Gauss map $n: U\to S^2$ defined on a subset $U\subseteq Σ$ is a continuous map that sends a point to the unit normal vector.

Definition (two-sided surface) open_in_new

A smooth surface in $\R^3$ is two-sided if it admits a global continuous choice of unit normal vector, i.e. a globally well-defined Gauss map.

Lemma (Gauss map is smooth) open_in_new

The Gauss map $n: U \to S^2$ is smooth.

Proof

Lemma (orientable iff two-sided for smooth surface in R3) open_in_new

Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$.

$Σ$ is orientable (as an abstract smooth surface) iff $Σ$ is two-sided.
