Suppose $α\in \mathrm{Isom}^+(\mathbb{H}) \setminus \set{\id}$ be an orientation-preserving isometry of the hyperbolic plane. We say $α$ is

  1. elliptic if it fixes $p\in \mathbb{H}$;

  2. parabolic if it fixes a unique point on $\partial\mathbb{H}$;

  3. hyperbolic if it fixes two points on $\partial\mathbb{H}$.

Every $α\in \mathrm{Isom}^+(\mathbb{H}) \setminus \set{\id}$.
