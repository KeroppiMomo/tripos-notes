Fix an allowable parametrisation $σ: V \to U$ near $p=σ(0)$, and WLOG assume all $U_i \subseteq U$. Define $V_i = σ\inv(U_i)$, which shrinks to $0$ as $i\to \infty$.

The area of $A_i$ is given by $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{area}_Σ(A_i) = \int_{V_i} \sqrt{\det \I} \d u \d v = \int_{V_i} \|σ_u \times σ_v\| \d u \d v. \end{align*} $$

For $n: Σ\to S^2$ the Gauss map, $n_u = \pdd{u}(n\circ σ) = Dn(σ_u) = -\mathbb{S}σ_u$ and similarly $n_v = -\mathbb{S}σ_v$. Since $κ(p) \neq 0$, we have $$ \begin{align*} \|n_u \times n_v\| = |\det \mathbb{S}| \|σ_u \times σ_v\| = |κ|\|σ_u \times σ_v\| \neq 0 \end{align*} $$ on some open neighbourhood $\tilde V$ of $0$. Thus $n\circ σ$ is an allowable parametrisation of $S^2$ near $n(p)$.

So for sufficiently large $i$ (so that $V_i \subseteq \tilde V$), $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{area}_{S^2}(n(A_i)) ={}& \int_{V_i} \|n_u \times n_v\| \d u \d v \\ ={}& \int_{V_i} |κ| \|σ_u \times σ_v\| \d u \d v \end{align*} $$

Now it is simply continuity: for any $ε>0$, $|κ(q) - κ(p)| < ε$ for all $q\in U_i$ and $i$ sufficiently large. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} |\mathrm{area}_{S^2}(n(A_i)) - |κ(p)| \, \mathrm{area}_{Σ}(A_i)| ={}& \left|\int_{V_i} (|κ(σ(u,v))| - κ(p)|) \|σ_u \times σ_v\|\d u \d v\right| \\ \leq{} & ε \cdot \mathrm{area}_Σ (A_i). \end{align*} $$ So $$ \begin{align*} |κ(p)| - ε \leq \frac{\mathrm{area}_{S^2}(n(A_i))}{\mathrm{area}_Σ(A_i)} \leq |κ(p)| + ε, \end{align*} $$ as claimed.
