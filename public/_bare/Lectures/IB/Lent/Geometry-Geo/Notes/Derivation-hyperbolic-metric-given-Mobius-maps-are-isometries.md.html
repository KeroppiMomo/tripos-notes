Look for a metric (in the sense of a metric space) on $D = \sb{z\in \C}{|z|<1}$ $$ \begin{align*} d: D\times D \to \R_{\geq 0} \end{align*} $$ such that

  1. $\mathrm{Möb}(D)$ acts by isometries, i.e. $d(Tx, Ty) = d(x,y)$ for all $x,y\in D$, $T\in \mathrm{Möb}(D)$; and

  2. $\R \cap D$ is length-minimising, i.e. if $0<a<b$ then $d(0,b) = d(0,a) + d(a,b)$.

Let $a\in \R$ and $p(a) = d(0,a)$. Then using the Möbius transformation $z\mapsto \frac{z-a}{1-\bar az}$ to send $a\mapsto 0$, $$ \begin{align*} p(b) = p(a) + p\left(\frac{b-a}{1-\bar a b}\right). \end{align*} $$ Assuming $p$ is differentiable, take derivative with respect to $b$ to obtain $$ \begin{align*} p'(b) ={}& p'\left(\frac{b-a}{1-\bar ab}\right) \cdot \frac{(1-\bar ab) + \bar a(b-a)}{(1-\bar ab)^2} \\ ={}& p'\left(\frac{b-a}{1-\bar ab}\right)\cdot \frac{1-|a|^2}{(1-\bar ab)^2}. \end{align*} $$ Take $b=a$ to see $$ \begin{align*} &p'(a) = \frac{p'(0)}{1 - a^2} \\ \implies & p(a) = \text{const.} \cdot \tanh\inv(a). \end{align*} $$ This was $d_\text{hyp}(0,a)$ for a particular value of constant, chosen so that $κ\equiv -1$.
