Let $σ: V\to U\subseteq Σ$ be an allowable parametrisation near $p$.

For any smooth curve $γ: (-ε, ε) \to Σ$ with $γ(0)=p$, by shrinking $ε$ we can assume $γ: (-ε, ε)\to U$. Apply the inverse of $σ$ to write $γ(t) = σ(u(t), v(t))$ for some map $(u(t), v(t)): (-ε, ε) \to V$. Then $$ \begin{align*} γ'(t) = σ_u u'(t) + σ_v v'(t) \in \mathrm{span}\set{σ_u, σ_v} = \im(Dσ). \end{align*} $$ Thus $γ'(0) \in \im(Dσ|_0) = T_pΣ$.

Conversely, for any $aσ_u(0) + bσ_v(0) \in T_p Σ$, consider the curve $$ \begin{align*} γ(t) = σ(at, bt) \end{align*} $$ for $t\in (-ε,ε)$ where $ε$ is chosen so that $(at,bt)\in V$ throughout. By the above $γ'(0)=aσ_u(0) + bσ_v(0)$.
