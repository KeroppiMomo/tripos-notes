Orientable $\implies$ two-sided

Suppose $Σ$ is equipped with oriented atlas.

For every point $p\in Σ$, take $σ: V\to U$ be an allowable parametrisation at $p$ ($V\subseteq \R^2$, $U\subseteq Σ$ open) so that the associated chart belongs to our oriented atlas. Define the positive unit normal over $U$ by $$ \begin{align*} n_σ = \frac{σ_u \times σ_v}{\|σ_u \times σ_v\|}. \end{align*} $$

We will show $n_σ$ for all $σ$ glue together to form a global map. If $\tilde σ: \tilde V\to\tilde U$ is another allowable parametrisation at $p$ in our oriented atlas (WLOG take $σ(0) = p = \tilde σ(0)$), then $σ = \tilde σ\circ ϕ$ where the transition map $ϕ=\tilde σ\inv\circ σ$ is positively-oriented, i.e. $\det Dϕ > 0$. Then $$ \begin{align*} &σ_u \times σ_v = \underbrace{(\det Dϕ|_0)}_{>0} (\tilde σ_u\times \tilde σ_v) \tag{*}\\ \implies & n_σ(p) = n_{\tilde σ}(p). \end{align*} $$

To see the first line, write $Dϕ|_0 = \pmat{α&β\\γ&δ}.$ Then by chain rule, $σ_u = α\tilde σ_u + γ\tilde σ_v$ and $σ_v = β\tilde σ_u + δ\tilde σ_v$. Finally expand $σ_u \times σ_v$.

Thus we have a global choice of normal vector over $Σ$.

Because locally $n_σ$ is a continuous function on $V$, the global Gauss map is also continuous.

Two-sided $\implies$ orientable

Let $n(p)$ be a global Gauss map.

For every point $p\in Σ$, pick an allowable parametrisation $σ: V\to U$ at $p$, with $σ(0) = p$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \frac{σ_u \times σ_v}{\|σ_u \times σ_v\|} = \pm n(p). \end{align*} $$ If the sign is negative, then swap coordinates $(u,v)\in V \mapsto (v,u)\in V'$ to get a different allowable parametrisation $σ': \tilde V\to U$ gives a positive sign.

Form an atlas with these parametrisations. By $(*)$, the transition map $ϕ = \tilde σ\inv \circ σ$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} &σ_u \times σ_v = (\det Dϕ|_0) (\tilde σ_u \times \tilde σ_v) \\ \implies& \det Dϕ_0 = \frac{\|σ_u \times σ_v\|}{\|\tilde σ_u \times \tilde σ_v\|} > 0. \end{align*} $$
