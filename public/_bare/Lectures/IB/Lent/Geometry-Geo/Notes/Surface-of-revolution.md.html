Let $η(t) = (f(t), 0, g(t))$ for $t\in [a,b]$ be a smooth curve in the $xz$-plane. Assume $η$ is injective, $f(t)>0$ and $η'(t)\neq 0$.

The surface of revolution is obtained by spinning $η$ around the $z$-axis. Consider the parametrisation $$ \begin{align*} σ(u,v) = (f(u) \cos u, f(u) \sin v, g(u)) \end{align*} $$ where $u\in (a,b)$ and $v\in (θ, θ+δ)$ with $0<δ<2π$.

Useful computation: $$ \begin{align*} &σ_u \times σ_v = (-f\, g' \cos v, -f \, g' \sin v, f\, f') \\ \implies & \|σ_u \times σ_v\|^2 = f^2\bigl((f')^2 + (g')^2\bigr) \neq 0. \end{align*} $$ so $σ$ is an allowable parametrisation.

This is a smooth surface in $\R^3$, as we can vary $θ$ to cover all of the surface.

First fundamental form: $$ \begin{align*} \I = \bigl((f')^2 + (g')^2\bigr) \d u^2 + f^2 \d v^2. \end{align*} $$
