Take an regular $4g$-gon $G(l)$ in $(D^2, g_{\text{hyp}})$ with vertices at $le^{2πik/4g}$ for all $k=0,1,\ldots,4g-1$. Letting $β(l)$ be the internal angle of $G(l)$, the area of $G(l)$ is $$ \begin{align*} A(l) = \mathrm{area}(G(l)) = (4g-2)π - 4gβ(l). \end{align*} $$

The ideal $4g$-gon has area $A(1) = (4g-2)π$ since $β(1)=0$. Moreover as $l\to 0$, $A(l)\to 0$, so $$ \begin{align*} β(l) = \frac{(4g-2)π - A(l)}{4g} \to \frac{4g-2}{4g}π > \frac{2π}{4g}. \end{align*} $$ Thus by continuity of $A$ (or $β$), $β$ attains $2π/4g$ for some $l$.
