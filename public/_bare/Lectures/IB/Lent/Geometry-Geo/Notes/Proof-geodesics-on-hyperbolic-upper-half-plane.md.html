These follows from the facts in the hyperbolic disc and the isometry. Möbius transformations preserve circle/lines, and the unit circle corresponds to the real line.

The distance is $$ \begin{align*} d_{(h,\text{hyp})}(p,q) = d_{(D, \text{hyp})}\left(\frac{p-i}{p+i}, \frac{q-i}{q+i}\right) = 2\tanh\inv(R) \end{align*} $$ where $$ \begin{align*} R ={}& \left|\frac{\frac{p-i}{p+i} - \frac{q-i}{q+i}}{1 - \frac{\bar p+i}{\bar p-i} \frac{q-i}{q+i}}\right| \\ ={}& \left|\frac{\bar p-i}{p+i}\frac{(q+i)(p-i) - (q-i)(p+i)}{(\bar p-i)(q+i) - (\bar p+i)(q-i)}\right| \\ ={}& \left|\frac{p-q}{\bar p-q}\right|. \end{align*} $$
