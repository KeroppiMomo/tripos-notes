Let$Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ be topological surfaces, and choose embeddings (continuous injection) $i_1: \overline{B(0, δ)}\hookrightarrow Σ_1$ and $i_2: \overline{B(0,δ)} \hookrightarrow Σ_2$ of the closed disc $\overline{B(0,δ)}$ into $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$.

The connect sum $Σ_1\# Σ_2$ of $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ (with respect to the embeddings $i_1$ and $i_2$) is defined as $$ \begin{align*} \biggl(\Bigl(Σ_1 \setminus i_1(B(0,δ))\Bigr) \cup \Bigl(Σ_2 \setminus i_2(B(0,δ))\Bigr)\biggr) \bigg/ \sim \end{align*} $$ where $\sim$ identifies $i_1(δe^{iθ})$ and $i_2(δe^{iθ})$ for all $θ\in [0,2π]$.

  • It is a topological surface. We construct disc neighbourhoods of points on the image of $S^1(0,δ)$ in $Σ_1\#Σ_2$ by gluing half-discs together.

  • If $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ are connected, then up to homeomorphism, $Σ_1\#Σ_2$ depends only on $Σ_1$ and $Σ_2$ and not on the embeddings $i_1$ and $i_2$.
