Let $γ$ be a smooth curve on a smooth surface in $\R^3$.

  1. If $γ$ has constant speed $\|\dot γ\|$ and locally minimises length among paths with the same endpoints, then $γ$ is a geodesic.

  2. If $γ$ globally minimises energy among paths with the same endpoints (and so is a geodesic), then $γ$ also globally minimises length and has constant speed.
