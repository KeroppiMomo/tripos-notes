If $T$ is a Möbius map, since it sends circles/lines to circles/lines and is conformal, $z,z'\in \hat \C$ are inverse for $Γ$ iff $Tz, Tz'$ are inverses for $T(Γ)$.

Let $T$ be a Möbius transformation mapping $\R\cup \set{\infty} \to Γ$. Then $w = T\inv z$ and $w' = T\inv z'$ are inverses for $\R\cup \set{\infty}$. $w'$ has to be on the vertical line through $w$, and drawing any circle through $w$ orthogonal to $\R\cup \set{\infty}$ uniquely determines $w' = \bar w$.
