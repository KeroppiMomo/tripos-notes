The torus is $S^1 \times S^1$ where $S^1 = \sb{z\in \C}{|z|=1}$.

  • $S^1\times S^1 \subseteq \C\times \C = \R^4$, so we can see it is compact and Hausdorff.

  • The map $$ \begin{alignat}{1} e:\quad && \R^2 &\to S^1 \times S^1 \\ && (s,t) &\mapsto (e^{2πis}, e^{2πit}) \end{alignat} $$ induces a homeomorphism $\R^2/\Z^2 \to S^1 \times S^1$. (Proof)

  • $\R^2/\Z^2$ is locally homeomorphic to an open disc. (Proof)

  • We can also view $\R^2/\Z^2$ as the quotient $[0,1]^2/{\sim}$ where $(x,0)\sim(x,1)$ and $(0,y)\sim(1,y)$ for all $0\leq x\leq 1$ and $0\leq y\leq 1$. (Proof)
