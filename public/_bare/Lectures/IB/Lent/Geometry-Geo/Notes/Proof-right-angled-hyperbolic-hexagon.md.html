Among all geodesics perpendicular to $\color{royalblue}γ$, exactly one is parallel to $L_α$. Let $w(l_α)$ the distance between $x$ and its intersection point with $γ$.

For any $λ>0$, at a distance $w(l_α) + λ + w(l_β)$ from $x$ along $\color{royalblue}γ$, construct $\color{yellowgreen}γ_β$ and $\color{orange}L_β$ similarly. $\color{orange}L_α$ and $\color{orange}L_β$ are ultraparallel, so there exists a common perpendicular. As $γ$ varies from $0$ to $\infty$, the length of the perpendicular also ranges from $0$ to $\infty$ continuously. Thus there exists some $λ$ where it has length $l_γ$.
