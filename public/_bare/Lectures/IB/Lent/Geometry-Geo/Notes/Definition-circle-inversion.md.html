Let $Γ\subseteq \hat \C = \C\cup \set{\infty}$ be a circle/line.

Points $z,z'\in \hat \C$ are inverses for $Γ$ if every circle/line through $z$ and orthogonal to $Γ$ passes through $z'$.

Let $J_Γ$ denote the inversion map, sending $z$ to its inverse with respect to $Γ$.

  • For every $Γ$ and $z$, there exists a unique point inverse to $z$ with respect to $Γ$. (Proof)
