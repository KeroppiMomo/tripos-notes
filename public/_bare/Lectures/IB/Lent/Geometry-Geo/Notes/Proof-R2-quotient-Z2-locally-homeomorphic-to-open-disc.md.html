Let $π: \R^2 \to \R^2/\Z^2$ be the quotient map.

Let $π(p) \in \R^2/\Z^2$ and $\overline{D}$ be a sufficiently small closed disc neighbourhood of $p\in \R^2$ such that $\overline{D} \cap ((n,m) + \overline{D}) = \emptyset$ for all $(n,m) \in \Z^2\setminus \set{(0,0)}$. Then $π|_{\overline{D}}: \overline{D} \to π(\overline{D})$ is injective, surjective, and continuous mapping a compact space to a Hausdorff space, so it is a homeomorphism. Thus $π(D)$ is a neighbourhood of $π(p)$ homeomorphic to a disc.
