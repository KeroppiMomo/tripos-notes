Write $C: z\mapsto \bar z$ for inversion in $\R\cup \set{\infty}$. For $Γ_1, Γ_2\subseteq \hat \C$, the inversion map is $J_{Γ_i} = T_i \circ C \circ T_i\inv$ where $T_i$ is a Möbius transformation sending $\R\cup \set{\infty}$ to $Γ_i$, for $i=1,2$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} J_{Γ_1} \circ J_{Γ_2} = T_1 \circ C \circ \underbrace{T_1\inv \circ T_2}_{μ} \circ C \circ T_2\inv \end{align*} $$ where $μ$ is a Möbius transformation. We can directly compute $C\circ μ \circ C$: $$ \begin{align*} C\circ μ\circ C(z) = \frac{\bar az+ \bar b}{\bar cz + \bar d}\in \mathrm{Möb}. \end{align*} $$
