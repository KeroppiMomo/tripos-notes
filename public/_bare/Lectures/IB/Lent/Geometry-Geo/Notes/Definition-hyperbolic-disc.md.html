Define the hyperbolic disc $(D, g_\text{hyp})$ as the unit disc $D = B(0,1)\subseteq \R^2 \isom \C$ equipped with the abstract Riemannian metric $$ \begin{align*} g_{\text{hyp}} = \frac{4(\d u^2 + \d v^2)}{(1 - u^2 - v^2)^2} \equiv \frac{4\left|\d z\right|^2}{(1 - |z|^2)^2}. \end{align*} $$ for $z = u + iv$.

  • Lengths of curves are then defined as $$ \begin{align*} \mathrm{length}_\text{hyp}(γ) = 2\int_{a}^{b} \frac{|\dot γ(t)|}{1 - |γ(t)|^2} \d t. \end{align*} $$

  • $E \equiv G$ and $F \equiv 0$, so the metric is conformal, and the angles agree with Euclidean angles.
