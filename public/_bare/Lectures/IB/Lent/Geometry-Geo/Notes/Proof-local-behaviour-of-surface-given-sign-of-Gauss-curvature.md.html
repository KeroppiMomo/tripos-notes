Take an allowable parametrisation $σ: V\to U$ near $p = σ(0)$. Since $\I = \pmat{E & F \\ F & G}$ is positive definite, $\det \I > 0$, so $\det \II = κ \cdot \det \I$ has the same sign as $κ$.

Recall that the signed distance of $σ(x)\in U$ to $p+T_pΣ$ is $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{2} \II_p(x, x) + O(\|x\|^3). \end{align*} $$

If $p$ is elliptic, $\det \II_p > 0$ so the matrix is positive definite or negative definite. Hence the signed distance only take one sign locally.

If $p$ is hyperbolic, $\det \II_p < 0$ so $\II_p$ has eigenvalues of opposite sign. Thus the bilinear form takes both signs locally.
