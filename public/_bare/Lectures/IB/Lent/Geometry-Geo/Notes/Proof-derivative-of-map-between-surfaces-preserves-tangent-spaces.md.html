We characterise the tangent space by derivative of smooth curves. Any smooth curve $γ: (-ε, ε)\to Σ_1$ through $γ(0) = p$ is sent to a smooth curve $A\circ γ: (-ε, ε)\to Σ_2$ through $(A\circ γ)(0) = A(p)$. Now notice $$ \begin{align*} DA|_p(γ'(t)) = (A\circ γ)'(t) \in T_{A(p)}Σ_2 \end{align*} $$ so $DA|_p : T_p Σ_1 \to T_{A(p)} Σ_2$.
