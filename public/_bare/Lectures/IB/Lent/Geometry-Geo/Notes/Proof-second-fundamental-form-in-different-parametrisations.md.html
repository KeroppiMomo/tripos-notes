Define $$ \begin{align*} n_σ(u,v) = \frac{σ_u \times σ_v}{\|σ_u \times σ_v\|}, \qquad n_{\tilde σ}(\tilde u, \tilde v) = \frac{\tilde σ_{\tilde u} \times \tilde σ_{\tilde v}}{\|\tilde σ_{\tilde u} \times \tilde σ_{\tilde v}\|}. \end{align*} $$ Write $σ = \tilde σ \circ ϕ$ where $ϕ = \tilde σ\inv \circ σ$ is the transition map.

As before, we have $$ \begin{align*} σ_u \times σ_v |_{(u,v)} = (\det Dϕ)(\tilde σ_{\tilde u} \times \tilde σ_{\tilde v})|_{(\tilde u, \tilde v)} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} n_σ(u,v) = \sign(\det Dϕ) \cdot n_{\tilde σ}(\tilde u, \tilde v) \implies n_σ = \sign(\det Dϕ)\cdot n_{\tilde σ} \circ ϕ. \end{align*} $$

Thus by chain rule, $$ \begin{align*} \II = -(Dn_σ)^T (Dσ) ={}& -\sign(\det Dϕ) \cdot (Dn_{\tilde σ} \circ Dϕ)^T (D\tilde σ\circ Dϕ) \\ ={}& \sign(\det Dϕ) \cdot (Dϕ)^T \tilde \II (Dϕ). \end{align*} $$
