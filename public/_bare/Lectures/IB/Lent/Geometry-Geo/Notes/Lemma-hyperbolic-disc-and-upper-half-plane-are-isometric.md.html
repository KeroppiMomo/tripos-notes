The hyperbolic disc $(D, g_\text{hyp})$ and the hyperbolic upper half plane $(h, g_\text{hyp})$ are isometric via $$ \begin{align*} T: h &\to D & T\inv : D &\to h\\ w &\mapsto \frac{w - i}{w + i}, & z &\mapsto i \left(\frac{1 + z}{1 - z}\right). \end{align*} $$

We write $\mathbb{H}$ for the hyperbolic plane without specifying a model.
