Suppose $σ: V\to U$ and $\tilde σ: \tilde V\to U$ are both allowable parametrisations. Write $σ = \tilde σ \circ ϕ$ where $ϕ=\tilde σ\inv \circ σ$ is the transition map. We have shown $$ \begin{align*} I = (Dϕ)^T \tilde I (Dϕ), \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \int_{V} \sqrt{\det I} \d u\d v = \int_{V} \sqrt{\det \tilde I} \cdot \det Dϕ \d u \d v = \int_{\tilde V} \sqrt{\det \tilde I} \d \tilde u \d \tilde v. \end{align*} $$
