Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$, $p\in Σ$ and $v \in$ $T_p Σ$.

There exists some $ε>0$ and a geodesic $γ: (-ε, ε)\to Σ$ such that $γ(0)=p$ and $γ'(0)=v$. Moreover $γ$ depends smoothly on the initial condition $(p,v)$.

$γ$ is unique in the following sense: if $\tilde γ: (-\tilde ε, \tilde ε)\to Σ$ is another such geodesic, then $\tilde γ = γ$ on $(-ε, ε) \cap (-\tilde ε, \tilde ε)$.

Therefore there exists a unique maximal geodesic $γ: (T_-, T_+)\to S$.
