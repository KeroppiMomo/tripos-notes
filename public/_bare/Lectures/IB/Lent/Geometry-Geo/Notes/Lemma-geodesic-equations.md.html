Let $Σ$ be a smooth surface in $\R^3$ and $σ: V\to U$ be an allowable parametrisation.

A smooth curve $γ: [a,b]\to U$ is a geodesic iff it satisfies the geodesic equations $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t}(E\dot u + F\dot v) ={}& \frac{1}{2}(E_u \dot u^2 + 2F_u \dot u \dot v + G_u \dot v^2), \\ \dd{t}(F\dot u + G\dot v) ={}& \frac{1}{2}(E_v \dot u^2 + 2F_v \dot u \dot v + G_v \dot v^2). \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • These are the Euler-Lagrange equations.
