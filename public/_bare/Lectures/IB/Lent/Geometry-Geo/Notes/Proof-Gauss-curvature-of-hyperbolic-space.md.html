Under a change of variable $$ \begin{align*} U = e^v \tanh u, \quad V = e^v \op{sech} u, \end{align*} $$ the first fundamental form of the hyperbolic upper half plane is related by $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\d U^2 + \d V^2}{V^2} = \d u ^2 + \cosh^2(u) \d v^2. \end{align*} $$ From before, the Gauss curvature is $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\cosh(u)_{uu}}{\cosh(u)} = -1. \end{align*} $$

Then apply theorema egregium for the hyperbolic disc.
