Write locally $γ(t) = σ(u(t), v(t))$, and WLOG assume $γ$ is parametrised by arc length. The angle $θ(t)$ with the parallel $s \mapsto σ(f(u(t)), s)$ (which has a tangent vector $σ_v$) is $$ \begin{align*} \cos θ = F\dot u + G\dot v = f^2 \dot v. \end{align*} $$

But the second geodesic equation is $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t}(f^2 \dot v) = 0 \end{align*} $$ so we get our result.
