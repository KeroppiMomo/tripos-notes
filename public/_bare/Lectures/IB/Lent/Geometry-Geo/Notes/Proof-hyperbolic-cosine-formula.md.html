Using an isometry, move vertex $c$ to $0$ and rotate so that vertex $a$ is at $A$.

Recalling the formula for hyperbolic distance, we have $$ \begin{align*} a = \tanh(A/2), \qquad b = e^{iγ} \tanh(B/2) \end{align*} $$ and $$ \begin{align*} \left|\frac{b-a}{1-\bar a b}\right| = \tanh(C/2). \end{align*} $$

Using hyperbolic half angle formula $$ \begin{align*} \tanh(λ/2) = t, \qquad \cosh λ = \frac{1 + t^2}{1 - t^2}, \qquad \sinh λ = \frac{2t}{1-t^2}, \end{align*} $$ we have $$ \begin{gather} \cosh A = \frac{1 + a^2}{1 - a^2}, \qquad \cosh B = \frac{1 + |b|^2}{1 - |b|^2}, \qquad \cosh C = \frac{|1-\bar a b|^2 + |b-a|^2}{|1-\bar ab|^2 - |b-a|^2}, \\ \sinh A = \frac{2a}{1 - a^2}, \qquad \sinh B = \frac{2|b|}{1 - |b|^2}. \end{gather} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \cosh C ={}& \frac{(1 + |a|^2)(1 + |b|^2) - 2(\bar a b + a\bar b)}{(1 - |a|^2)(1 - |b|^2)} \\ ={}& \frac{(1 + |a|^2)(1 + |b|^2)}{(1 - |a|^2)(1 - |b|^2)} - \frac{2a}{1 - |a|^2} \frac{2|b| \cos γ}{1-|b|^2} \\ ={}& \cosh A \cosh B - \sinh A \sinh B \cos γ. \end{align*} $$
