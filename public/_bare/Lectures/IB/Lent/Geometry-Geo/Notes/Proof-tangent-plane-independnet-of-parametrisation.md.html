Suppose $σ: V\to U\subseteq Σ$ and $\tilde σ : \tilde V \to \tilde U\subseteq Σ$ are two allowable parametrisations of open neighbourhoods of $p\in Σ$.

The transition map $σ\inv\circ \tilde σ$ is a diffeomorphism of open sets in $\R^2$, so its derivative $D(σ\inv\circ \tilde σ)$ is invertible. Applying chain rule to $\tilde σ = σ\circ (σ\inv\circ \tilde σ)$, $$ \begin{align*} D\tilde σ|_0 = Dσ|_0 \circ D (σ\inv \circ \tilde σ)|_0 \end{align*} $$ so $D\tilde σ|_0$ and $Dσ|_0$ have the same image.
