Let $π: S^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^2$ be the quotient map.

$S^2$ is second countable, so we can find a countable base $\mathcal{U} = \set{U_i}_{i\in \N}$ for the topology on $S^2$.

Let $\mathcal{V} = \set{π(U_i)}_{i\in \N}$ be a countable collection of sets in $\mathbb{RP}^2$. Since $π\inv(π(U_i)) = U_i\cup (-U_i)$, $\mathcal{V}$ is a collection of open sets.

For any $V\subseteq \mathbb{RP}^2$ open, $π\inv(V) \subseteq S^2$ is open, so there exists $I\subseteq \N$ finite such that $$ \begin{align*} π\inv(V) = \bigcup_{i\in I} U_i. \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} V = π(π\inv(V)) = \bigcup_{i\in I} π(U_i) \end{align*} $$ and hence $\mathcal{V}$ is a countable base of $\mathbb{RP}^2$.
