Let $f: Σ_1 \to Σ_2$ be a diffeomorphism, and suppose $Σ_2$ is orientable, equipped with an oriented atlas $\set{(U_i, ϕ_i)}_{i\in I}$.

Equip $Σ_1$ with the atlas $\set{(f\inv(U_i), ϕ_i \circ f|_{f\inv(U_i)})}_{i\in I}$. The transition maps are $$ \begin{align*} (ϕ_i \circ f) \circ (ϕ_j \circ f)\inv = ϕ_i \circ ϕ_j\inv, \end{align*} $$ the same as with $Σ_2$ and so are orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms.
