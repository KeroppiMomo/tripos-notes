A chart for a topological surface $Σ$ is a pair $(U, ϕ)$ where $U\subseteq Σ$ is open and $ϕ: U\to V$ is a homeomorphism to an open set $V\subseteq \R^2$.

The inverse $σ = ϕ\inv: V \hookrightarrow Σ$ is called a local parametrisation of $Σ$.

An atlas for $Σ$ is a collection $\set{(U_i, ϕ_i)}_{i\in I}$ of charts such that $\bigcup_{i\in I} U_i = Σ$.

  • Every topological surface by definition admits an atlas: every point has an open neighbourhood homeomorphic to an open disc.
