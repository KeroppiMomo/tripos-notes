A topological surface is a topological space $Σ$ such that

  1. for all $p\in Σ$, there exists an open neighbourhood $U\subseteq Σ$ of $p$ such that $U$ is homeomorphic to an open disc in $\R^2$; and

  2. $Σ$ is Hausdorff and second countable.


  • $\R^2$ is homeomorphic to $B(0,1)$ so we could also say $U$ is homeomorphic to $\R^2$.
