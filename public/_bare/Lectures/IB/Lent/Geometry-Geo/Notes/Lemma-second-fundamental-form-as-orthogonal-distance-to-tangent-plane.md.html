The orthogonal distance from $σ(u,v)$ to the affine tangent plane $p + T_pΣ$ is given by $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{2}(Lu^2 + 2Muv + Nv^2)\big|_0 + O(u^3, v^3) = \frac{1}{2}\II((u,v), (u,v))\big|_0 + O(u^3, v^3) \end{align*} $$ where $\II(x,y)=x^T \pmat{L&M\\M&N} y$ is the second fundamental form.
