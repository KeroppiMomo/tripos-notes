$c=c_α$ is chosen so that $$ \begin{align*} \sup_{θ\in Θ_0} \prob_θ(Λ(X) \geq c_α) = α \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} c_α = \inf\sb{c}{\sup_{θ\in Θ_0} \prob_θ(Λ(\tilde X) > c) \leq α}. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} T(X) = 1 \iff Λ(X) > c_α \iff P = \sup_{θ\in Θ_0} \prob_θ(Λ(\tilde X) > Λ(X)\mid X) \leq α. \end{align*} $$
