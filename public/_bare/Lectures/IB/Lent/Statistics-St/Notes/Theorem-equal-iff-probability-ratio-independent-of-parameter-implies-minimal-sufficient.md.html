Suppose $T$ is a statistic, satisfying that $T(x) = T(y)$ iff $p(x; θ)/p(y; θ)$ does not depend on $θ$. Then $T$ is minimal sufficient.

Proof

  • If the range of $X$ depends on $θ$, the condition means $p(x;θ) = c(x,y)p(y;θ)$ for some $c(x,y)$.
