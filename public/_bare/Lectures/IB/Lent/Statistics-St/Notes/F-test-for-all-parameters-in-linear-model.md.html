The F-test for testing $H_0: β = β_0$ against $H_1: β\neq β_0$ is to reject $H_0$ when $$ \begin{align*} \frac{\|X(\hat β_{\text{MLE}} - β_0)\|^2/p}{\hat σ^2} > F_{p,n-p}(α) \end{align*} $$ where $\hat σ^2 = \frac{1}{n-p}\|Y - X\hat β_{\text{MLE}}\|$ is the unbiased estimator for $σ^2$.

This gives a $(1-α)$-confidence set for $β$ $$ \begin{align*} \sb{β\in \R^n}{\frac{\|X(\hat β_{\text{MLE}} - β)^2\|}{p\hat σ^2} \leq F_{p,n-p}(α)}, \end{align*} $$ which is an ellipsoid.

This is the F-test for nested models.
