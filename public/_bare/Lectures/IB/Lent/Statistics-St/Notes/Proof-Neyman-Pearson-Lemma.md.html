Let $T$ be any test $T(X)$ with size $β(T; θ_0) \leq α = β(T^\ast; θ_0)$. Define $R=\sb{x\in \mathcal{X}}{T(X)=1}$ be the rejection region of the test, and similarly $R^\ast$ for $T^\ast$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} β(T^\ast, θ_1) - β(T, θ_1) ={}& \int_{R^\ast} p(x; θ_1) \d x - \int_{R} p(x; θ_1) \d x \\ ={}& \int_{R^\ast \setminus R} p(x;θ_1) \d x - \int_{R\setminus R^\ast} p(x;θ_1) \d x \\ ={}& \int_{R^\ast\setminus R} \underbrace{\frac{p(x; θ_1)}{p(x; θ_0)}}_{Λ(x)>c \text{ inside } R^\ast} p(x;θ_0) \d x - \int_{R\setminus R^\ast} \underbrace{\frac{p(x; θ_1)}{p(x; θ_0)}}_{Λ(x)\leq c \text{ outside }R^\ast} p(x; θ_0) \d x \\ \geq {}& c\left(\int_{R^\ast\setminus R} p(x; θ_0) \d x - \int_{R\setminus R^\ast} p(x; θ_0) \d x\right) \\ ={}& c\left(\int_{R^\ast} p(x ;θ_0) \d x - \int_{R} p(x; θ_0) \d x\right) \\ ={}& c(β(T^\ast, θ_0) - β(T, θ_0)) \\ \geq{}& 0. \end{align*} $$
