(In the following, "$\propto$" hides quantities independent of $μ$).

Prior distribution $$ \begin{align*} π(μ) \propto \exp\left(-\frac{λ_0}{2}(μ-μ_0)^2\right). \end{align*} $$

Likelihood function $$ \begin{align*} p(X\mid μ) \propto{}& \prod_{i=1}^{n} \exp\left(-\frac{λ}{2}(X_i-μ)^2\right) \\ \propto{}& \exp\left(-\frac{nλ}{2}(-2\overline{X}μ + μ^2)\right) \\ \propto{}& \exp\left(-\frac{nλ}{2}(\overline{X}-μ)^2\right) \end{align*} $$

Posterior distribution $$ \begin{align*} π(μ\mid X) \propto{}& p(X\mid μ)\, π(μ) \\ \propto{}& \exp\left(-\frac{λ_0}{2}(μ-μ_0)^2 - \frac{nλ}{2}(\overline{X} - μ)^2\right) \\ \propto{}& \exp\left(-\frac{λ_0 + nλ}{2}μ^2 + (λ_0 μ_0 + nλ\overline{X})μ\right) \\ \propto{}& \exp\left(-\frac{λ_0 + nλ}{2}\left(μ - \frac{λ_0μ_0 + nλ\overline{X}}{λ_0 + nλ}\right)^2\right) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} μ\mid X \sim N\biggl(\frac{λ_0 μ_0 + nλ\overline{X}}{λ_0 + nλ}, \frac{1}{λ_0 + nλ}\biggr). \end{align*} $$
