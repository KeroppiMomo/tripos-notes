The likelihood of $θ$ at the observed data $X$ is $L_X: θ \mapsto p(X; θ)$, viewed as a function of $θ$.

The maximum likelihood estimator (MLE) of $θ$ is the value of $θ$ that maximises $L_X$, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \hat θ_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{θ\in Θ} L_X(θ), \end{align*} $$ which is an estimator for $θ$ (a function of $X$).
