Let $F(t) = \prob_{θ_0}(Λ(X) \leq t)$ be the distribution function of $Λ$ under $θ_0$. Then $$ \begin{align*} P = \prob_{θ_0}(Λ(\tilde X) \geq Λ(X) \mid X) = 1 - F(Λ(X)). \end{align*} $$ So $$ \begin{align*} \prob_{θ_0}(P \leq u) ={}& \prob_{θ_0}(F(Λ(X)) \geq 1-u) \\ ={}&\prob_{θ_0}(Λ(X) \geq F\inv(1-u)) \\ ={}& 1 - F(F\inv(1-u)) \\ ={}& 1-(1-u) \\ ={}& u. \end{align*} $$
