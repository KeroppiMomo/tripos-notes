Compute $$ \begin{align*} \op{MSE}(\hat θ) ={}& \expect_θ((\hat θ-θ)^2) \\ ={}& \expect_θ\Bigl(\bigl((\hat θ - \expect_θ(\hat θ)) + \underbrace{(\expect_θ(\hat θ) - θ)}_{\text{deterministic}}\bigr)^2\Bigr) \\ ={}& \expect_θ\bigl((\hat θ - \expect_θ(\hat θ))^2\bigr) + 2(\expect_θ(\hat θ) - θ) \cdot \underbrace{\expect_θ\bigl(\hat θ - \expect_θ(\hat θ) \bigr)}_{0} + \bigl(\expect_θ(\hat θ) - θ\bigr)^2 \\ ={}& \var(\hat θ) + \op{bias}(\hat θ)^2. \end{align*} $$
