We know $\overline{X} \sim N(μ, σ^2/n)$, so the distribution of $\sqrt{n}(\overline{X}-μ)/σ \sim N(0,1)$ does not depend on $μ$.

Let $z_{α_1}$ be the upper $α_1$ quantile of $N(0,1)$ and similarly for $z_{α_2}$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \prob\Bigl(-z_{α_1} \leq \frac{\sqrt{n}(\overline{X} - μ)}{σ} \leq z_{α_2}\Bigr) = 1-α_1-α_2. \end{align*} $$ Rearranging gives $$ \begin{align*} \prob\Bigl(\overline{X} - \frac{σz_{α_1}}{\sqrt{n}} \leq μ \leq \overline{X} + \frac{σz_{α_2}}{\sqrt{n}}\Bigr) = 1-α_1-α_2. \end{align*} $$
