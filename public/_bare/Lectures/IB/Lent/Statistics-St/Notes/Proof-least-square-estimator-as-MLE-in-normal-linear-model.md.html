The likelihood function is $$ \begin{align*} L_{Y\mid X}(β, σ^2) = (2πσ^2)^{-n/2} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2σ^2} \|Y - Xβ\|^2\right). \end{align*} $$ Log-likelihood $$ \begin{align*} l_{Y\mid X}(β, σ^2) = -\frac{n}{2}\log σ^2 - \frac{1}{2σ^2} \|Y-Xβ\|^2. \end{align*} $$

$(\hat β_{\text{MLE}}, \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}})$ maximises $l_{Y\mid X}(β, σ^2)$, so $$ \begin{align*} \hat β_{\text{MLE}} = \arg\max_{β\in \R^p}\left(-\frac{n}{2}\log \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}} - \frac{1}{2\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}}\|Y-Xβ\|^2\right) = \arg\min_{β\in \R^p} \|Y-Xβ\|^2. \end{align*} $$ Also $$ \begin{align*} &0 = \pdfd{l_{Y\mid X}}{σ^2}\bigg|_{\hat β_{\text{MLE}}, \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}} = -\frac{n}{2\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}} + \frac{1}{2(\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}})^2} \|Y - X\hat β_{\text{MLE}}\|^2 \\ \implies& \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}} = \frac{1}{n}\min_{β\in \R^p} \|Y-Xβ\|^2 := \frac{\mathrm{RSS}}{n}. \end{align*} $$

The maximum likelihood is then $$ \begin{align*} L_{Y\mid X}(\hat β_{\text{MLE}}, \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}) = \left(\frac{2πe}{n}\mathrm{RSS}\right)^{-n/2}. \end{align*} $$
