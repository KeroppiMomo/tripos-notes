Write $$ \begin{align*} Y = Xβ + ε \end{align*} $$ where $ε\mid X \sim N(\v 0, σ^2 I_n)$. Also let $$ \begin{align*} P = X(X^T X)\inv X^T \end{align*} $$ be the orthogonal projection matrix onto the columns space of $X$.

Since $\hat β_{\text{MLE}}$ solves the least squares problem and $X$ has full rank, $$ \begin{align*} \hat β_{\text{MLE}} ={}& (X^T X)\inv X^T Y \\ ={}& (X^T X)\inv X^T (Xβ + ε) \\ ={}& β + (X^T X)\inv X^T ε, \end{align*} $$ which has mean and variance $$ \begin{align*} \expect(\hat β_{\text{MLE}}) ={}& β + (X^TX)\inv X^T \expect(ε) = β, \\ \var(\hat β_{\text{MLE}}) ={}& (X^T X)\inv X^T \var(ε) \,X(X^TX)\inv \\ ={}& σ^2 (X^T X)\inv X^T X (X^T X)\inv \\ ={}& σ^2 (X^T X)\inv. \end{align*} $$ This is an affine transformation of the Gaussian $ε$, so $$ \begin{align*} \hat β_{\text{MLE}} \sim N(β, σ^2(X^T X)\inv). \end{align*} $$

Moreover since $PX = X$ (since $P$ fixes the image of $X$), we have $X^T = X^T P^T = X^T P$. So $$ \begin{align*} \hat β_{\text{MLE}} = β + (X^T X)\inv X^T Pε \end{align*} $$ is a function of $Pε$.

Meanwhile $$ \begin{align*} \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}} ={}& \frac{1}{n}\|(I_n-P)Y\|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{1}{n}\|(I_n-P)(Xβ + ε)\|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{1}{n}\|\underbrace{(I_n-P)X}_{0} β + (I_n-P)ε\|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{1}{n}\|(I_n-P)ε\|^2 \end{align*} $$ is a function of $(I_n-P)ε$.

By the lemma, $Pε$ and $(I_n-P)ε$ are independent, and thus so is $\hat β_{\text{MLE}}$ and $\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}$. Moreover $$ \begin{align*} n\,\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}/σ^2 = \|(I-P)ε\|^2/σ^2 \sim χ_{\mathrm{rank}(I_n-P)}^2 = χ_{n-p}^2. \end{align*} $$
