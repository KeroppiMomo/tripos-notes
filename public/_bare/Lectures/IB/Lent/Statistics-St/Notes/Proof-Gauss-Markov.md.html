The least square estimator is given by $$ \begin{align*} \hat β = (X^T X)\inv X^TY, \end{align*} $$ which is linear in $Y$.

$\hat β$ is unbiased, since $$ \begin{align*} \expect(\hat β\mid X) ={}& (X^T X)\inv X^T \expect(Y\mid X) \\ ={}& (X^T X)\inv X^T Xβ \\ ={}& β. \end{align*} $$

Now suppose $\tilde β = CY$ is a another linear estimator in $Y$. Write $$ \begin{align*} C = (X^T X)\inv X^T + A. \end{align*} $$ Note $$ \begin{align*} \expect(\tilde β \mid X) = ((X^T X)\inv X^T + A)(Xβ) = β + AXβ \end{align*} $$ so if $\tilde β$ is unbiased, then $AXβ = 0$ for all $β$ and thus $AX = 0$.

Now $$ \begin{align*} \var(\tilde β\mid X) ={}& C \var(Y\mid X)\, C^T \\ ={}& \bigl((X^T X)\inv X^T + A\bigr) (σ^2 I_n) \bigl((X^T X)\inv X^T + A\bigr)^T \\ ={}& σ^2 \Bigl((X^T X)\inv X^T X (X^T X)\inv + \underbrace{AX}_{0}(X^T X)\inv + (X^TX)\inv \underbrace{X^T A^T}_{0} + AA^T\Bigr) \\ ={}& σ^2\bigl((X^T X)\inv + AA^T\bigr) \\ ={}& \var(\hat β \mid X) + σ^2 AA^T. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $\var(\tilde β\mid X) - \var(\hat β \mid X)$ is a positive semidefinite matrix, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \var(a^T \tilde β\mid X) ={}& a^T \var(\tilde β\mid X)\, a\\ ={}& a^T \var(\hat β\mid X)\, a + σ^2 a^T A A^T a \\ ={}& \var(a^T \hat β\mid X) + σ^2 \|A^T a\|^2 \\ \geq{} & \var(a^T \hat β\mid X). \end{align*} $$
