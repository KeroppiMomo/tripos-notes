Likelihood function $$ \begin{align*} L_X(μ, θ) = (2πσ^2)^{-n/2} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2σ^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - μ)^2\right). \end{align*} $$ Log-likelihood $$ \begin{align*} l_X(μ, θ) = \log L_X(μ, θ) = -\frac{n}{2}\log σ^2 - \frac{1}{2σ^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - μ)^2 + \text{const.}. \end{align*} $$

The maximum likelihood estimator $\hat μ_{\text{MLE}}$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \pdfd{l_X}{μ}\bigg|_{\hat μ_{\text{MLE}}} = \frac{1}{σ^2}\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i - n \hat μ_{\text{MLE}}\Bigr) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \hat μ_{\text{MLE}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i = \overline{X}. \end{align*} $$

The maximum likelihood estimator $\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \pdfd{l_X}{σ^2}\bigg|_{\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}} = -\frac{n}{2\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}} + \frac{1}{2(\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}})^2} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - \hat μ^2_{\text{MLE}})^2 \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i - \overline{X})^2 = \frac{S_{XX}}{n}. \end{align*} $$

From here, $\hat μ_{\text{MLE}} \sim N(μ, σ^2/n)$ is unbiased since $\expect(\hat μ_{\text{MLE}}) = μ$. Moreover $S_{XX}/σ^2 \sim χ_{n-1}^2$, so $\expect(\hat σ^2_{\text{MLE}}) = \frac{n-1}{n}σ^2$.
