The Bayes loss is $$ \begin{align*} R_X(\tilde θ) ={}& \int_{\R} |\tilde θ - θ|\,π(θ\mid X) \d θ \\ ={}& \int_{-\infty}^{\tilde θ} (\tilde θ - θ)\,π(θ\mid X) \d θ + \int_{\tilde θ}^{\infty} (θ - \tilde θ)\, π(θ\mid X) \d θ. \end{align*} $$ The minimiser $\hat θ$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \left.\dfd{R_X}{\tilde θ}\right|_{\hat θ} ={}& \int_{-\infty}^{\hat θ} π(θ\mid X) \d θ - \int_{\hat θ}^{\infty} π(θ\mid X) \d θ \\ \implies \int_{-\infty}^{\hat θ} π(θ\mid X) \d θ ={}& \int_{\hat θ}^{\infty} π(θ\mid X) \d θ \\ \prob_π(θ < \hat θ\mid X) ={}& \prob_π(θ >\tilde θ \mid X). \end{align*} $$
