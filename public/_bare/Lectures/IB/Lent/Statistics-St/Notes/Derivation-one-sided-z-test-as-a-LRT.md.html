Likelihood ratio is $$ \begin{align*} Λ = \frac{L_X(μ_1)}{L_X(μ_0)} ={}& \frac{(2πσ^2)^{-n/2} \exp(-\frac{1}{2σ^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i-μ_1)^2)}{(2πσ^2)^{-n/2} \exp(-\frac{1}{2σ^2}\sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_i-μ_0)^2)} \\ ={}& \exp\left(\frac{(μ_1 - μ_0)\,n\overline{X}}{σ^2} - \frac{n(μ_1^2 - μ_0^2)}{2σ^2}\right), \end{align*} $$ which is an increasing function of $\overline{X}$ since $μ_1>μ_0$.

Therefore the likelihood ratio test is to reject $H_0$ if $Λ>\text{const.} \iff \overline{X}>\text{const.} \iff Z = \frac{\sqrt{n}(\overline{X} - μ_0)}{σ}>z$ for some constant $z$.

For a size $α$-test, we require $\prob_{μ_0}(Z > z) = α$. Since $Z \sim N(0,1)$, we take $z$ to be the upper $α$ quantile of $N(0,1)$.
