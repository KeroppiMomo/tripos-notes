For a least square polynomial fitting problem, let $(Q_k)_{k=0}^n$ be the orthogonal polynomials.

Then the least square approximant is given by $$ \begin{align*} p^\ast = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\langle f,Q_k\rangle}{\|Q_k\|^2} Q_k \in \mathcal{P}_n \end{align*} $$ with error $$ \begin{align*} \|f-p^\ast\|^2 = \|f\|^2 - \|p^\ast\| = \|f\|^2 - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\langle f,Q_k\rangle^2}{\|Q_k\|^2}. \end{align*} $$
