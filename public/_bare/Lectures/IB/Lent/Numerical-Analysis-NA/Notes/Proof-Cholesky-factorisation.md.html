Factorise $A = LDL^T$. Then $$ \begin{align*} A = \underbrace{LD^{1/2}}_{\tilde L} \cdot \underbrace{D^{1/2} L^T}_{\tilde L^T}. \end{align*} $$

To see uniqueness, note every lower triangular matrix can be expressed uniquely as a product of a lower unitriangular matrix and a diagonal matrix. Then apply the uniqueness of $LDL^T$ factorisation.
