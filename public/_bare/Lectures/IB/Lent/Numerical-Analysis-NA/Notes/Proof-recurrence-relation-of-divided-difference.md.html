Suppose $q_0\in \mathcal{P}_{k-1}$ interpolates $f$ on $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_{k-1}$ while $q_1 \in \mathcal{P}_{k-1}$ interpolates $f$ on $x_1, \ldots, x_{k-1}, x_k$.

The polynomial $$ \begin{align*} p(x) = \frac{x-x_0}{x_k - x_0} q_1 + \frac{x_k - x}{x_k - x_0} q_0 \in \mathcal{P}_k \end{align*} $$ interpolates $f$ on $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_{k-1}, x_k$.

The leading coefficients of $p, q_0, q_1$ are $f[x_0, \ldots, x_k], f[x_0, \ldots, x_{k-1}], f[x_1, \ldots, x_k]$ respectively, so $$ \begin{align*} f[x_0, \ldots, x_k] = \frac{f[x_1, \ldots, x_k] - f[x_0, \ldots, x_{k-1}]}{x_k - x_0}. \end{align*} $$
