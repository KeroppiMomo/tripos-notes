We can write any upper triangular matrix uniquely as the product of a diagonal matrix and an upper unitriangular matrix: $$ \begin{align*} U = \pmat{u_{11} \\& u_{22} \\ &&\ddots \\&&& u_{nn}} \pmat{1 & u_{12}/u_{11} & u_{13}/u_{11} & \ldots & u_{1n}/u_{11} \\ & 1 & u_{23}/u_{22} & \ldots & u_{2n}/u_{22} \\ && \ddots & & \vdots \\ &&&&1}. \end{align*} $$ Now apply existence and uniqueness of LU decomposition.
