The choice $ρ(w)$ $= w^s - w^{s-1} = w^{s-1}(w-1)$ corresponds to Adams methods.

  • Adams-Bashforth methods are explicit, and has order $p=s$.

  • Adams-Moulton methods are implicit, and has order $p=s+1$.

Proposition (coefficients of Adams methods as integrals of polynomials) open_in_new

The coefficients $b_m$ in an $s$-step Adams method are given by $$ \begin{align*} b_m = \int_{s-1}^{s} l_m(t) \d t \end{align*} $$ where $l_m(t) = ω(t)/(t-m)ω'(m)$ are the Lagrange fundamental polynomials:

  • for an Adams-Moulton (explicit) method, the nodes are $0,1,\ldots,s$, i.e. $ω(t) = \prod_{m=0}^{s} (t-m)$;

  • for an Adams-Bashforth (implicit) method, the nodes are $0,1,\ldots,s-1$, i.e. $ω(t) = \prod_{m=0}^{s-1} (t-m)$.
