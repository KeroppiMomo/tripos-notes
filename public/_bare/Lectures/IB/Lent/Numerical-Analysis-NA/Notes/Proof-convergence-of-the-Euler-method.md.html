It suffices to prove for the case $d=1$. Taylor expand the exact solution: $$ \begin{align*} y(t_{n+1}) ={}& y(t_n) + h y'(t_n) + \frac{1}{2}h^2 y''(τ_n) \\ ={}& y(t_n) + hf\bigl(t_n, y(t_n)\bigr) + \frac{1}{2}h^2 y''(τ_n) \end{align*} $$ for some $τ_n \in (t_n, t_{n+1})$. The Euler method is $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+1} = y_n + hf(t_n, y_n). \end{align*} $$

Subtracting, $$ \begin{align*} e_{n+1} = e_n + h\Bigl(f\bigl(t_n, y(t_n)\bigr) - f\bigl(t_n, y_n\bigr)\Bigr) + \frac{1}{2}h^2 y''(τ_n). \end{align*} $$

To see the order, assume $y_n = y(t_n)$, so $e_n = 0$ and $e_{n+1} = η_{n+1}$. Then $$ \begin{align*} η_{n+1} = \frac{1}{2}h^2y''(τ_n) \leq \frac{1}{2}\|y''\|_\infty h^2 = O(h^2) \end{align*} $$ so the method has order 1.

To see convergence, we make use of the Lipschitz condition to have $$ \begin{align*} |e_{n+1}| \leq{} & |e_n| + hλ\,|y(t_n) - y_n| + \frac{1}{2}h^2 \|y''\|_\infty \\ ={}& (1+hλ) \, |e_n| + \frac{1}{2}h^2 \|y''\|_\infty. \end{align*} $$ Solving this recurrence, $$ \begin{align*} |e_n| \leq{} & (1+hλ)^n \, \underbrace{|e_0|}_{0} + \frac{1}{2}h^2 \|y''\|_\infty \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} (1+hλ)^k \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2}h^2 \|y''\|_\infty \cdot \frac{(1+hλ)^n-1}{hλ} \\ \leq{} & \frac{\|y''\|_\infty}{2λ}h e^{λnh} &&1+x \leq e^x \\ \leq{}& \frac{\|y''\|_\infty}{2λ} e^{λT} h \\ ={}& O(h). \end{align*} $$
