The coefficients $b_m$ in an $s$-step Adams method are given by $$ \begin{align*} b_m = \int_{s-1}^{s} l_m(t) \d t \end{align*} $$ where $l_m(t) = ω(t)/(t-m)ω'(m)$ are the Lagrange fundamental polynomials:

  • for an Adams-Bashforth (explicit) method, the nodes are $0,1,\ldots,s$, i.e. $ω(t) = \prod_{m=0}^{s} (t-m)$;

  • for an Adams-Moulton (implicit) method, the nodes are $0,1,\ldots,s-1$, i.e. $ω(t) = \prod_{m=0}^{s-1} (t-m)$.
