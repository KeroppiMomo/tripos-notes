Since $L_i$ and $U_i$ are invertible, we have $$ \begin{align*} L_2\inv L_1 = U_2\inv U_1. \end{align*} $$ LHS is lower triangular and RHS is upper triangular (recall invertible lower/upper triangular matrices form a group), so this quantity is diagonal. Moreover since $L_1$ and $L_2\inv$ have unit diagonals, so does $L_2\inv L_1$, and hence $L_2\inv L_1 = U_2 \inv U_1 = I$. Thus $L_1 = L_2$ and $U_1 = U_2$.
