The Chebyshev polynomial $T_n$ on $[-1,1]$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} T_n(x) = \cos(n\cos\inv x), \qquad x\in [-1,1]. \end{align*} $$

  • Trigonometric identity implies the recurrence relation $$ \begin{align*} T_0 ={}& 1, \\ T_1 ={}& x, \\ T_{n+1}(x) ={}& 2x\, T_n(x) - T_{n-1}(x) \end{align*} $$ so $T_n$ are indeed polynomials.

  • The highest degree term in $T_n$ is $2^{n-1} x^n$.
