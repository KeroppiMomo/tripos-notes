Euler method

For an initial value problem, $$ \begin{align*} \v y_{n+1} = \v y_n + h \v f(t_n, \v y_n). \end{align*} $$

Theorem (convergence of the Euler method) open_in_new

For an initial value problem, suppose

  • $\v y''$ is bounded on $[0,T]$; and

  • $\v f$ is Lipschitz in $\v y$, i.e. $\|\v f(t,\v x) - \v f(t, \v y)\| \leq λ\|\v x - \v y\|$ for all $0\leq t\leq T$ and $\v x,\v y\in \R^d$.

Then the Euler method has order 1 and is convergent, with global error $\v e_n := \v y(t_n) - \v y_n = O(h)$.
