An explicit two-stage Runge-Kutta has the form $$ \begin{align*} \v k_1 ={}& \v f(t_n, \v y_n), \\ \v k_2 ={}& \v f(t_n + c_2 h, \v y_n + c_2 h \v k_1), \\ \v y_{n+1} ={}& \v y_n + h(b_1 \v k_1 + b_2 \v k_2). \end{align*} $$

(We omit evaluation at $(t_n, \v y_n)$ to simplify notation from now on.)

Taylor expand $\v k_2$ about $(t_n, \v y_n)$ to give $$ \begin{align*} \v k_2 ={}& \v f + c_2h \,\v f_t + c_2h\,(\v k_1 \cdot ∇_\v y) \v f + O(h^2) \\ ={}& \v f + c_2h(\v f_t + (\v f\cdot ∇_\v y) \v f) + O(h^2). \end{align*} $$ Note that $$ \begin{align*} \v y' = \v f(t, \v y) \implies \v y'' = \v f_t + (\v y' \cdot ∇_y)\v f = \v f_t + (\v f\cdot ∇_\v y) \v f \end{align*} $$ so assuming $\v y_n = \v y(t_n)$, $$ \begin{align*} \v k_1 = \v y', \qquad \v k_2 = \v y' + c_2h \v y'' + O(h^2). \end{align*} $$

Hence $$ \begin{align*} \v y_{n+1} ={}& \v y + h(b_1+b_2) \v y' + h^2 b_2 c_2 \v y'' \end{align*} $$ while $$ \begin{align*} \v y(t_{n+1}) ={}& \v y + h\v y' + \frac{1}{2}h^2 \v y'' + O(h^3). \end{align*} $$

Therefore the method is of order 2 if $$ \begin{align*} b_1 + b_2 = 1, \qquad b_2c_2 = \frac{1}{2}. \end{align*} $$
