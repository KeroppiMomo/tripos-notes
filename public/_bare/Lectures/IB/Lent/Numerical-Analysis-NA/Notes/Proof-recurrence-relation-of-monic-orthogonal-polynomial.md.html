Expand $$ \begin{align*} xQ_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n+1} c_k \,Q_k(x), \qquad c_k = \frac{\langle xQ_n, Q_k\rangle}{\langle Q_k, Q_k\rangle} = \frac{\langle Q_n, xQ_k\rangle}{\langle Q_k, Q_k\rangle}. \end{align*} $$

  • For $k=n+1$, monicity implies $c_{n+1} = 1$.

  • For $k=n$, $c_n = a_n$.

  • For $k=n-1$, $xQ_{n-1} - Q_n\in \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$ by monicity, so $$ \begin{align*} \langle Q_n, xQ_{n-1}\rangle = \langle Q_n, Q_n\rangle - \underbrace{\langle Q_n, Q_{n-1}-Q_n\rangle}_{0} = \langle Q_n, Q_n\rangle \end{align*} $$ and hence $c_{n-1} = b_n$.

  • For $k<n-1$, $xQ_{k} \in \mathcal{P}_{n-1}$, so $c_k = 0$.

Hence $$ \begin{align*} xQ_n = Q_{n+1} + a_n Q_n + b_nQ_{n-1}. \end{align*} $$
