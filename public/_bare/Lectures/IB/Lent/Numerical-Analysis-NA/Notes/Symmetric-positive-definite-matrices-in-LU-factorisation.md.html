Theorem (LDLT factorisation of SPD matrix) open_in_new

$A\in \R^{n\times n}$ is symmetric positive definite iff it admits a factorisation $A=LDL^T$ where $L$ is lower unitriangular and $D$ is diagonal with positive entries.

Moreover the factorisation is unique.

Proof

Corollary (Cholesky factorisation) open_in_new

$A\in \R^{n\times n}$ is symmetric positive definite iff it admits a factorisation $A = LL^T$ where $L$ is lower triangular with a positive diagonal.

Moreover the factorisation is unique.

Proof
