Interpolating $\implies$ exact

Using linearity, $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i f(x_i) = λ\biggl(\underbrace{\sum_{i=0}^{n} l_i(x) f(x_i)}_{p(x)}\biggr) \end{align*} $$ where $p\in \mathcal{P}_n$ interpolates $f$ on $x_0, \ldots, x_n$. Therefore when $f\in \mathcal{P}_n$, $f=p$ and thus the formula is exact.

Exact $\implies$ interpolating

Take $l_j \in \mathcal{P}_n$ to see $$ \begin{align*} λ(l_j) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i \underbrace{l_j(x_i)}_{δ_{ij}} = a_j. \end{align*} $$
