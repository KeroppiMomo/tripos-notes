Consider the implicit two-stage Runge-Kutta method $$ \begin{align*} k_1 ={}& f(t_n, y_n + \tfrac{1}{4}h(k_1 - k_2)), \tag{1}\\ k_2 ={}& f(t_n + \tfrac{2}{3}h, y_n + \tfrac{1}{12}h(3k_1 + 5k_2)), \tag{2} \\ y_{n+1} ={}& y_n + \tfrac{1}{4}h(k_1 + 3k_2). \tag{3} \end{align*} $$

We'll simplify our analysis by considering the autonomous system $y' = f(y)$ instead.

(We omit evaluation at $t_n$ for simplicity.)

Expand (1) and (2) to obtain $$ \begin{align*} k_1 ={}& f + \tfrac{1}{4}hf_y(k_1-k_2) + \tfrac{1}{32}h^2 f_{yy}(k_1-k_2)^2 + O(h^3), \tag{4} \\ k_2 ={}& f + \tfrac{1}{12}hf_y(3k_1 + 5k_2) + \tfrac{1}{288} h^2 f_{yy}(3k_1 + 5k_2)^2 + O(h^3). \tag{5} \end{align*} $$ Substitute $k_1 = f + O(h)$ and $k_2 = f + O(h)$ back into (4) and (5) to get $$ \begin{align*} k_1 = f + O(h^2), \qquad k_2 = f + \tfrac{2}{3}hf_y f + O(h^2). \end{align*} $$ Substitute these back into (4) and (5) again to gain one more order, $$ \begin{align*} k_1 ={}& f - \tfrac{1}{6}h^2 f_y^2 f + O(h^3), \\ k_2 ={}& f + \tfrac{2}{3}h f_y f + h^2(\tfrac{5}{18} f_{y}^2 f + \tfrac{2}{9} f_{yy} f^2) + O(h^3). \end{align*} $$

Substitute these into (3) to get $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+1} = y_n + hf + \tfrac{1}{2}h^2 f_y f + \tfrac{1}{6}h^3 (f_y^2 f + f_{yy}y^2) + O(h^4). \end{align*} $$ Note that $$ \begin{align*} y' = f(y) \implies y'' = f_y y' = f_y f \implies y''' = f_y^2 f + f_{yy} f^2. \end{align*} $$ Hence $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+1} = y_n + hy' + \tfrac{1}{2}h^2y'' + \tfrac{1}{6}h^2 y''' + O(h^4). \end{align*} $$ So the method has order at least 3.
