Take $s=2$, $ρ(w) = w(w-1)$. We'll follow the construction here.

Let $w = ξ+1$. Expand about $w=1$ i.e. $ξ=0$: $$ \begin{align*} \frac{ρ(w)}{\log w} ={}& \frac{ξ + ξ^2}{\log(1+ξ)} \\ ={}& \frac{ξ+ξ^2}{ξ - \frac{1}{2}ξ^2 + \frac{1}{3}ξ^3 + O(ξ^4)} \\ ={}& (1 + ξ)\bigl(1 -\tfrac{1}{2}ξ + \tfrac{1}{3}ξ^2 + O(ξ^3)\bigr)\inv \\ ={}& (1+ξ)\bigl(1 - (-\tfrac{1}{2}ξ + \tfrac{1}{3}ξ^2) + (-\tfrac{1}{2}ξ + \tfrac{1}{3}ξ^2)^2 + O(ξ^3)\bigr) \\ ={}& (1+ξ)\bigl(1 + \tfrac{1}{2}ξ - \tfrac{1}{12}ξ^2 + O(ξ^3)\bigr) \\ ={}& 1 + \tfrac{3}{2}ξ + \tfrac{5}{12}ξ^2 + O(ξ^3) \\ ={}& {-\tfrac{1}{12}} + \tfrac{2}{3} w + \tfrac{5}{12}w^2 + O(|w-1|^3) \\ ={}& {-\tfrac{1}{2}} + \tfrac{3}{2}w + O(|w-1|^2). \end{align*} $$

  • For an implicit method, take $σ(w) = -\frac{1}{12} + \frac{2}{3}w + \frac{5}{12}w^2 \in \mathcal{P}_2$. So the 2-step 3rd-order Adams-Moulton method is $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+2} - y_{n+1} = h\left(\frac{5}{12}f_{n+2} + \frac{2}{3}f_{n+1} - \frac{1}{12}f_n\right). \end{align*} $$

  • For an explicit method, take $σ(w) = -\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{2}w \in \mathcal{P}_1$. So the 2-step 2nd-order Adams-Bashforth method is $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+2} - y_{n+1} = h\left(\frac{3}{2}f_{n+1} - \frac{1}{2} f_n\right). \end{align*} $$
