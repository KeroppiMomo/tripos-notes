Solves least squares $\implies$ normal equations

$\v c^\ast$ minimises $$ \begin{align*} \|\v y - A\v c\|^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{m} \Bigl(y_i - \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij} c_j\Bigr)^2. \end{align*} $$ Take derivative for each $k=1,2,\ldots,m$, $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \pdfd{\|\v y - A\v c\|^2}{c_k}\bigg|_{\v c^\ast} = -2\sum_{i=1}^{m} \Bigl(y_i - \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}c^\ast_j\Bigr)a_{ik} \end{align*} $$ so in matrix notation, $$ \begin{align*} A^T (\v y - A\v c^\ast) = 0. \end{align*} $$

Nomral equations $\implies$ solves least squares

If $A^T(A\v c^\ast - \v y) = \v 0$, then for all $\v c\in \R^m$, write $\v c = \v c^\ast + \v d$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \|A\v c - \v y\|^2 ={}& \bigl((A\v c^\ast - \v y) + A\v d\bigr)^T \bigl((A\v c^\ast - \v y) + A\v d\bigr) \\ ={}& \|A\v c^\ast - \v y\|^2 + \|A\v d\|^2 + 2\v d^T \underbrace{A^T(A\v c^\ast - \v y)}_{\v 0} \\ \geq{}& \|A\v c^\ast - \v y\|^2. \end{align*} $$ Equality holds iff $A\v d = \v 0$. If $A$ has full rank, this is equivalent to $\v d=0$.
