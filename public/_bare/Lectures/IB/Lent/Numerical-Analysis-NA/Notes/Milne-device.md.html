For multistep methods, we use a pair of methods of the same order $p$:

  • the predictor: explicit, lower computational cost; and

  • the corrector: implicit, higher accuracy.

The predictor provides a good initial guess in the solution of the implicit corrector equations, and allows for error estimation:

If the local truncation errors are $$ \begin{align*} \v y(t_{n+1}) - \v y_{n+1}^{\text{P}} ={}& c_{\text{P}} h^p \v y^{(p)}(t_n) + O(h^{p+1}), \\ \v y(t_{n+1}) - \v y_{n+1}^{\text{C}} ={}& c_{\text{C}} h^p \v y^{(p)}(t_n) + O(h^{p+1}), \end{align*} $$ then we estimate the error as $$ \begin{align*} \v y(t_{n+1}) - \v y_{n+1}^\text{C} \approx -\frac{c_{\text{C}}}{c_\text{P} - c_\text{C}}(\v y_{n+1}^\text{P} - \v y_{n+1}^{\text{C}}). \end{align*} $$
