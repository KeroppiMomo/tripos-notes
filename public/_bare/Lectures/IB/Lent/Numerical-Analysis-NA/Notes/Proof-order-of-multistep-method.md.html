The idea is to Taylor expand everything.

Proof for 1

$$ \begin{align*} &\sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m \v y(t_{n+m}) - h\sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m \v y'(t_{n+m}) \\ ={}& \sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(mh)^k}{k!} \v y^{(k)}(t_n) - h \sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(mh)^{k-1}}{(k-1)!} \v y^{(k)}(t_n) \\ ={}& \left(\sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m\right)\v y(t_n) + \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{h^k}{k!} \left(\sum_{m=0}^{s} m^k a_m - km^{k-1}b_m\right) \v y^{(k)}(t_n). \end{align*} $$ The method has order $p$ iff this expression is $O(h^{p+1})$, which occurs iff all coefficients of $h^k$ vanish for $k=0,1,\ldots,p$, as claimed.

Proof for 2

Write $$ \begin{align*} \v y(t_{n+m}) = \v Q(t_{n+m}) + O(h^{p+1}) \end{align*} $$ where $\v Q\in \mathcal{P}_p$.

The multistep method has order $p$ iff $$ \begin{align*} &\sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m \v y(t_{n+m}) = h\sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m \v y'(t_{n+m}) + O(h^{p+1}) \\ \iff& \sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m \v Q(t_{n+m}) = h\sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m \v Q'(t_{n+m}) \end{align*} $$ for any $\v Q$.

Proof for 3

$$ \begin{align*} ρ(e^z) - zσ(e^z) ={}& \sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m e^{mz} - z\sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m e^{mz} \\ ={}& \sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k!} m^k z^k - z \sum_{m=0}^{s} b_m \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k!} m^k z^k \\ ={}& \left(\sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m\right) + \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{k!} \left(\sum_{m=0}^{s} a_m m^k - kb_m m^{k-1}\right) z^k \end{align*} $$ which is $O(h^{p+1})$ iff 1 holds.

This has identical proof as 1 because Taylor expansion is informally $f(x+h) = e^{hD} f(x)$ where $D = \dd{x}$.
