Any $p\in \mathcal{P}_n$ can be expanded as $p=\sum_{k=0}^{n} c_k Q_k$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \|f-p\|^2 ={}& \left\langle f-\sum_{k=0}^{n} c_k Q_k,\; f - \sum_{k=0}^{n} c_kQ_k\right\rangle \\ ={}& \|f\|^2 - 2\sum_{k=0}^n c_k \langle f,Q_k\rangle + \sum_{k=0}^{n} \sum_{j=0}^{n} c_{k} c_j \underbrace{\langle Q_k, Q_j\rangle}_{\|Q_k\|^2 δ_{jk}} \\ ={}& \|f\|^2 + \sum_{k=0}^{n} \Bigl(\|Q_k\|^2 c_k ^2 - 2\langle f,Q_k\rangle c_k\Bigr) \\ ={}& \|f\|^2 - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\langle f,Q_k\rangle^2}{\|Q_k\|^2} + \sum_{k=0}^{n} \left(\|Q_k\|c_k - \frac{\langle f,Q_k\rangle}{\|Q_k\|}\right)^2 \\ \geq{}& \|f\|^2 - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{\langle f,Q_k\rangle^2}{\|Q_k\|^2} \end{align*} $$ with equality iff $c_k = \langle f,Q_k\rangle/\|Q_k\|^2$.
