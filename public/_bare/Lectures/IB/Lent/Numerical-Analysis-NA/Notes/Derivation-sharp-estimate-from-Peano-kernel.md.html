We apply the inequality $$ \begin{align*} \left|\int_{a}^{b} K_λ(t) f^{(n+1)}(t) \d t\right| \leq \int_{a}^{b} |K_λ(t)| |f^{(n+1)}(t)| \d t \leq \int_{a}^{b} |K_λ(t)| \d t \cdot \max_{[a,b]} |f^{n+1}|. \end{align*} $$ Equality occurs iff $f^{(n+1)}(t) \equiv c\sign K_λ(t)$ for some constant $c$.

Let $f_0$ such that $f_0^{(n+1)}=\sign K_λ$. Although $f_0$ is not $C^{(n+1)}$, we can approximate it with $f_ε$ which linearly interpolates between the discontinuities over an interval of length $ε$. This shows sharpness.
