A general explicit Runge-Kutta method is $$ \begin{align*} \v k_i ={}& \v f\Bigl(t_n + c_i h, \v y_n + h \sum_{j=1}^{i-1} a_{ij} \v k_j\Bigr), && c_s = \sum_{j=1}^{i-1} a_{ij} \\ \v y_{n+1} ={}& \v y_n + h \sum_{i=1}^{s} b_i \v k_i, && 1 = \sum_{i=1}^{s} b_i. \end{align*} $$ Applying this to $f(t,y) = λy$, we get $$ \begin{align*} hk_i = hλ \left(y_n + \sum_{j=1}^{i-1} a_{ij}hk_j\right), \qquad y_{n+1} = y_n + \sum_{i=1}^{s} b_i hk_i. \end{align*} $$ By induction we see that $hk_i = q_i(λ)\cdot y_n$ for some polynomial $q_i\in \mathcal{P}_i$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} y_{n+1} = \underbrace{\left(1 + \sum_{i=1}^{s} b_i q_i(λh)\right)}_{p_s(λh)\in \mathcal{P}_s}y_n. \end{align*} $$ The linear stability domain is $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{D} = \sb{z\in \C}{|p_s(z)|<1}. \end{align*} $$ But $p_s$ is a polynomial so $p_s(z)\to \pm\infty$ as $z\to -\infty$, and thus $\mathbb{C}^-\not\subseteq \mathcal{D}$.
