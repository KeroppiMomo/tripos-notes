Let $p_k\in \mathcal{P}_k$ interpolates $f$ on $x_0, \ldots, x_k$ (which exists uniquely). Since $p_k - p_{k-1}\in \mathcal{P}_k$ has roots $x_0, \ldots, x_{k-1}$ and the leading coefficient is the leading coefficient of $p_k$ i.e. $f[x_0, \ldots, x_k]$, so $$ \begin{align*} p_k(x) - p_{k-1}(x) = f[x_0, \ldots, x_k] \prod_{i=0}^{k-1} (x-x_i). \end{align*} $$

Thus $$ \begin{align*} p_n ={}& p_0 + (p_1 - p_0) + \ldots + (p_n - p_{n-1}) \\ ={}& f[x_0] + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} f[x_0, \ldots, x_k] \prod_{i=0}^{k-1} (x-x_i). \end{align*} $$
