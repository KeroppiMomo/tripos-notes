Write $f = c(f)\, \tilde f$ and $g= c(g)\, \tilde g$ where $\tilde f$ and $\tilde g$ are primitive. Then $$ \begin{align*} &fg = c(f) \, c(g) \, \tilde f\, \tilde g \\ \implies & c(fg) = c(f) \, c(g) \, c(\tilde f\, \tilde g). \end{align*} $$ Since $\tilde f\, \tilde g$ is primitive, $c(\tilde f\,\tilde g)$ is a unit, as required.
