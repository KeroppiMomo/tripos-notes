We will show $\mathrm{Fit}_k(A)$ is unchanged by row and column operations. Since determinants of submatrices stay unchanged under transpose, $\mathrm{Fit}_k(A) = \mathrm{Fit}_k(A^T)$ so it suffices to check row operations.

Let $C$ be a $k\times k$ submatrix of $A$, and consider adding $c$ times row $i$ to row $j$ of $A$ to form $B$.

  • If both rows lie inside $C$, then the determinant is unchanged by properties of determinants.

  • If row $j$ lie outside $C$, then $C$ is unchanged.

  • Suppose row $j$ lie inside $C$ but row $i$ is not. Let the new submatrix be $C'$. Expanding the determinant along row $j$, we have $$ \begin{align*} \det C' = \det C + c\det D \end{align*} $$ where $D$ is obtained from $C$ by replacing row $j$ with row $i$. But $D$ is a $k\times k$ submatrix of $A$, so $\det C' \in \mathrm{Fit}_k(A)$.

Therefore $\mathrm{Fit}_k(B) \subseteq \mathrm{Fit}_k(A)$. But row operation is invertible so $\mathrm{Fit}_k(B) = \mathrm{Fit}_k(A)$.

Multiplying a unit to a row alters determinants of submatrices by up to a unit, and swapping two rows alters by up to a sign change, which doesn't change the Fitting ideals.
