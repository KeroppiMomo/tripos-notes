Note $ϕ$ is the restriction of the usual $\C$-norm $z\mapsto |z|^2$, so in particular $ϕ(ab) = ϕ(a) ϕ(b)$ for all $a,b\in \Z[i]$. If $b\neq 0$, then $ϕ(b) \geq 1$, so $ϕ(ab) \geq ϕ(a)$.

To perform the division algorithm, consider $a,b\in \Z[i]$ with $b\neq 0$. There is a point $q\in \Z[i]$ with distance $<1$ from $a/b\in \C$, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \left|\frac{a}{b} - q\right| < 1. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} a = bq + r \end{align*} $$ where $r\in \Z[i]$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} ϕ(r) = \left|a - bq\right|^2 = |b|^2 \left|\frac{a}{b}-q\right| < ϕ(b), \end{align*} $$ as required.
