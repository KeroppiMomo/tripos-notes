A constant is either zero or a unit in $\C$, and so not a prime.

Any polynomial of degree $\geq 2$ has a linear factor by the fundamental theorem of algebra, and so is not a prime.

Any linear polynomial is irreducible (since one of the factors must have degree 0, or note $\C[X]/(X-λ)\isom \C$ is a field and so $(X-λ)$ is maximal).
