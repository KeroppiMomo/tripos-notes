Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $\mathbb{F}$, and fix an endomorphism $α$, i.e. a $V\to V$ linear map.

Define $V_α$ as the $\mathbb{F}[X]$-module $V$ with operation $$ \begin{align*} \mathbb{F}[X] \times V &\to V \\ (f, v) &\mapsto (f(α))(v), \end{align*} $$ where $f(α)$ involves self-composition, scaling and sums.
