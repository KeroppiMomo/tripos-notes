Definition (Noetherian ring) open_in_new

A ring $R$ is Noetherian if every chain of ideals $$ \begin{align*} I_1 \subseteq I_2 \subseteq I_3 \subseteq \ldots \end{align*} $$ eventually stabilises, i.e. there exists $N\in \N$ such that $I_n = I_{n+1}$ for all $n\geq N$. This is known as the ascending chain condition.

Proposition (Noetherian iff all ideals are finitely generated) open_in_new

A ring is Noetherian iff all ideals are finitely generated.

Proof

  • This shows all principal ideal domains are Noetherian.
