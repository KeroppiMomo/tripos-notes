Define the function $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: S &\to R / I \\ s &\mapsto s + I. \end{align*} $$ This is a ring homomorphism.

The kernel is $$ \begin{align*} \ker(ϕ) = \sb{s\in S}{ϕ(s) = 0_{R/I}} = \sb{s\in S}{s\in I} = S\cap I. \end{align*} $$ The image is $$ \begin{align*} \im(ϕ) = \sb{s + I}{s\in S} = (S+I)/I. \end{align*} $$ Apply the first isomorphism theorem.
