Define an action $G\actson G/H$ by $$ \begin{align*} g * g_1H = gg_1 H. \end{align*} $$ We get a permutation representation $ϕ: G\to \Sym(G/H)$.

Consider $G_X = \ker(ϕ)$. $$ \begin{align*} k\in G_X &\iff kg H = gH &&\forall g\in G \\ &\iff g\inv k g \in H &&\forall g\in G \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} G_X = \bigcap_{g\in G} gHg\inv. \end{align*} $$

In particular, $G_X \leq H$ and $G_X \lhd G$.

If $K\lhd G$ and $K\leq H$, then $gkg\inv \in K \subseteq H$ for all $k\in K$, $g\in G$ so $k\in \bigcap_{g\in G} gHg\inv = G_X$. Thus $G_X$ is the largest normal subgroup contained in $H$.

If $G$ is finite and $|G:H| = n$, then by the first isomorphism theorem, $G/G_X = G/\ker(ϕ) \isom \im(ϕ) \leq \Sym(G/H) \isom S_n$, and so $|G/G_X| \mid n!$ by Lagrange. Moreover since $G_X \leq H$, $|G/G_X| \geq |G/H| = n$.
