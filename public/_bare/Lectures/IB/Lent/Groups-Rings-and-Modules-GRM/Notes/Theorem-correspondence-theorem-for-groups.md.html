Let $N\lhd G$ be groups. The sets $\mathcal{G} = \sb{\text{subgroups }H\leq G}{N\lhd H}$ and $\mathcal{N} = \set{\text{subgroups } S\leq G/N}$ have the "same structure", in the following way:

Define the homomorphism $p: G\to G/N$ by $p(g) = gN$. There exists a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{G} &\xleftrightarrow{\quad} \mathcal{N} \\ H &\shortmid\!\xrightarrow{\quad} p(H) = H/N \\ \sb{g\in G}{gN \in S}= p\inv(S) &\xleftarrow{\quad}\!\shortmid S. \end{align*} $$ Moreover, $N\lhd H\lhd G$ iff $G/H \lhd G/N$.
