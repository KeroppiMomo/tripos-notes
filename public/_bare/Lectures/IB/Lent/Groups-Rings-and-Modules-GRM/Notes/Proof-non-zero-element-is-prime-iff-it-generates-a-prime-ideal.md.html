Prime ideal $\implies$ prime element

Suppose $(r)$ is a prime ideal. By definition, $(r)\neq R$, so $r$ is not a unit.

Let $r\mid xy$. Then $xy\in (r)$, which is prime, so $x\in (r)$ or $y\in (r)$, which means $r \mid x$ or $r\mid y$.

Prime element $\implies$ prime ideal

Suppose $r$ is a prime element. Since $r$ is not a unit, $1\neq (r)$, so $(r)\neq R$.

If $xy\in (r)$, then $r\mid xy$. Since $r$ is prime, $r\mid x$ or $r\mid y$, which means $x\in (r)$ or $y\in (r)$.
