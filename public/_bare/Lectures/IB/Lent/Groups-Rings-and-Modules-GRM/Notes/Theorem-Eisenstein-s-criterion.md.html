Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain and $f=a_0 + a_1 X + \ldots + a_n X^n\in R[X]$ be primitive with $a_n \neq 0$.

If there exists an irreducible $p\in R$ such that

  1. $p\not\mid a_n$;

  2. $p\mid a_i$ for all $i=0,1,\ldots,n-1$; and

  3. $p^2\not\mid a_0$,

then $f$ is irreducible in $R[X]$.
