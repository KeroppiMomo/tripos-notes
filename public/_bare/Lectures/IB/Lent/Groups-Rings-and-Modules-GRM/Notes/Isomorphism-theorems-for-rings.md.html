Theorem (first isomorphism theorem for rings) open_in_new

Let $ϕ: R\to S$ be a ring homomorphism.

Then $\ker ϕ$ $\lhd$ $R$, and $$ \begin{align*} f:\quad R/\ker(ϕ) &\to \im(ϕ) \leq S \\ r + \ker(ϕ) &\mapsto ϕ(r) \end{align*} $$ is a well-defined ring isomorphism.

Proof

Theorem (second isomorphism theorem for rings) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring, $S\leq R$ be a subring, and $I\lhd R$ be an ideal. Then

  • $S + I\leq R$ is a subring of $R$;

  • $S\cap I \lhd S$ is an ideal of $S$; and

  • $(S+I)/I \isom S/(S\cap I)$.

Proof

Theorem (correspondence theorem for rings) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring and $I\lhd R$ be an ideal. There is a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{subrings of $R$ containing $I$}} &\xleftrightarrow{\quad} \set{\text{subrings of $R/I$}} \\ \end{align*} $$ and a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{ideals of $R$ containing $I$}} &\xleftrightarrow{\quad} \set{\text{ideals of $R/I$}} \\ \end{align*} $$ given by $$ \begin{align*} S &\shortmid\!\xrightarrow{\quad} S/I \\ \sb{r\in R}{r+I \in L} &\xleftarrow{\quad}\!\shortmid L. \end{align*} $$

Theorem (third isomorphism theorem for rings) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring, $I\lhd R$ and $J\lhd R$ with $I\subseteq J$. Then $$ \begin{align*} ^{\large (R/I)}/_{\large (J/I)} \isom R/J. \end{align*} $$
