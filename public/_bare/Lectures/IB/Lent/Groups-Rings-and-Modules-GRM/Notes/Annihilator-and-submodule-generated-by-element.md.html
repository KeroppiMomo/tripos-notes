Definition (annihilator) open_in_new

Let $M$ be an $R$-module.

For an element $m\in M$, its annihilator is $$ \begin{align*} \op{Ann}(m) = \sb{r\in R}{r\cdot m = 0.} \end{align*} $$

For a subset $S\subseteq M$, its annihilator is $$ \begin{align*} \op{Ann}(S) = \sb{r\in R}{r\cdot s = 0 \ \forall s\in S} = \bigcap_{s\in S} \op{Ann}(s). \end{align*} $$

  • $\op{Ann}(S)\lhd R$ is an ideal.

Definition (submodule generated by element) open_in_new

Let $M$ be an $R$-module.

For an element $m\in M$, the submodule generated by $m$ is $$ \begin{align*} Rm = \sb{r\cdot m}{r\in R} \leq M. \end{align*} $$

Proposition (quotient of annihilator is isomorphic to submodule generated) open_in_new

Let $M$ be an $R$-module.

For any element $m\in M$, there is an $R$-module isomorphism $R/$$\mathrm{Ann}$$(m) \isom$ $Rm$.
