Let $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{F}$, and fix an endomorphism $α$, giving an $\mathbb{F}[X]$-module $V_α$.

If $V$ is finite-dimensional, then $$ \begin{align*} V_α \isom \C[X]/((X-λ_1)^{a_1}) \oplus \ldots \oplus \C[X]/((X - λ_r)^{a_r}) \end{align*} $$ where $λ_i\in \C$ may not be distinct.

This gives a basis for $V$ where the matrix of $α$ is in Jordan canonical form $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{J_{a_1}(λ_1) \\ &\ddots \\ &&J_{a_r}(λ_r)}. \end{align*} $$
