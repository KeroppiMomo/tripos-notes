Suppose $xZ(G)$ generate $G/Z(G)$. Since cosets partition, any $g\in G$ can be written as $x^n z$ for some $n\in \Z$ and $z\in Z(G)$.

Thus for any $g=x^n z, g'=x^{n'}z' \in G$, $$ \begin{align*} gg' ={}& x^n z x^{n'} z' \\ ={}& x^n x^{n'} z z' \\ ={}& x^{n'} x^n z' z \\ ={}& x^{n'} z' x^n z = g'g. \end{align*} $$
