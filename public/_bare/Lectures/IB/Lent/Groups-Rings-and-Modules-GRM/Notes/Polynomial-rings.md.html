Definition (polynomial ring) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring.

The polynomial ring $R[X]$ is the set of all polynomials in $X$ with coefficients in $R$ $$ \begin{align*} R[X] = \sb{\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i X^i}{n\geq 0, \, a_i\in R} \end{align*} $$ where $X^i$ are formal symbols, with $0_{R[X]} = 0_R$, $1_{R[X]} = 1_R$ and the usual operations $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i X^i + \sum_{i=0}^{m} b_i X^i ={}& \sum_{i=0}^{\max\set{n,m}} (a_i + b_i )X^i, \\ \left(\sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i X^i\right) \cdot \left(\sum_{i=0}^{m} b_i X^i\right) ={}& \sum_{i=0}^{n+m} \left(\sum_{j=0}^{i} a_j b_{i-j}\right) X^i. \\ \end{align*} $$

The polynomial ring of two variables $R[X,Y] \isom (R[X])[Y]$ is constructed similarly, etc.

Definition (ring of formal power series) open_in_new

The ring of formal power series in $X$ with coefficients in $R$ is $$ \begin{align*} R[[X]] = \sb{\sum_{i=0}^{\infty} r_i X^i}{r\in R} \end{align*} $$ with the usual operations.

Proposition (polynomial ring over a field is a Euclidean domain) open_in_new

Let $K$ be a field. The polynomial ring $K[X]$ is a Euclidean domain, with Euclidean function $ϕ = \deg$.

Proof

Keep in mind $K[X]$ has all the nice properties you get in a Euclidean domain.

Definition (content of polynomial, primitive polynomial) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain.

The content of $f = a_0 + a_1 X + \ldots + a_n X^n \in$ $R[X]$ is $$ \begin{align*} c(f) = \op{gcd}(a_0, a_1, \ldots, a_n) \in R, \end{align*} $$ defined up to associates.

We say $f$ is primitive if $c(f)$ is a unit.

Lemma (product of primitive polynomials is primitive) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain.

If $f,g\in R[X]$ are primitive, then so is $f\cdot g$.

Lemma (Gauss's lemma, reducibility of polynomials in UFD and its field of fractions) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain and $F$ be its field of fractions.

For a primitive polynomial $f\in R[X]$, $f$ is irreducible in $R[X]$ iff $f$ is irreducible in $F[X]$.

Proof

Proposition (divisibility of polynomials in UFD and its field of fractions) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain and $F$ be its field of fractions.

For a primitive polynomial $g\in R[X]$, a polynomial $f\in R[X]$ is divisible by $g$ in $R[X]$ iff it is divisible by $g$ in $F[X]$.

Equivalently, if $J=(g)\lhd R[X]$ and $I=(g)\lhd F[X]$, then $J = I \cap R[X]$.

Proof

Theorem (Eisenstein's criterion) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a unique factorisation domain and $f=a_0 + a_1 X + \ldots + a_n X^n\in R[X]$ be primitive with $a_n \neq 0$.

If there exists an irreducible $p\in R$ such that

  1. $p\not\mid a_n$;

  2. $p\mid a_i$ for all $i=0,1,\ldots,n-1$; and

  3. $p^2\not\mid a_0$,

then $f$ is irreducible in $R[X]$.
