Definition (maximum ideal) open_in_new

An ideal $I\lhd R$ of a ring is called maximal if

  1. $I\neq R$; and

  2. for any $J\lhd R$, $$ \begin{align*} I\subseteq J\subseteq R \implies J=I \quad \text{or} \quad J=R. \end{align*} $$

  • A maximal ideal always exists for any ring, a consequence of Zorn's lemma.

Definition (prime ideal) open_in_new

An ideal $I\lhd R$ of a ring is called prime if

  1. $I\neq R$; and

  2. for all $a,b\in R$, $$ \begin{align*} ab\in I \implies a\in I \quad \text{or} \quad b\in I. \end{align*} $$

Proposition (ideal is prime iff quotient is an integral domain) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring and $I\lhd R$ be an ideal.

$I$ is prime iff $R/I$ is a integral domain.

Proof

Corollary (maximal ideals are prime) open_in_new

If $R$ is a ring and $I\lhd R$ is maximal, then $I$ is prime.

Proof: $I$ is maximal $\iff$ $R/I$ is a field $\iff$ $R/I$ is an integral domain $\iff$ $I$ is prime.

Definition (division, associates) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring, and let $a,b\in R$ be elements.

We say $b$ divides $a$, written as $b\mid a$, if equivalently

  • $a = bc$ for some $c\in R$;

  • $(a)$ $\subseteq (b)$.

We say $a,b$ are associates if equivalently

  • $a=bc$ for some unit $c\in R$;

  • $a\mid b$ and $b\mid a$;

  • $(a)=(b)$.

Definition (irreducible) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring. An element $a\in R$ is said to be irreducible if

  1. $a\neq 0$;

  2. $a$ is not a unit; and

  3. for all $x,y\in R$, if $a=xy$, then $x$ or $y$ is a unit.

Definition (prime element) open_in_new

Let $R$ be a ring. An element $p\in R$ is said to be prime if

  1. $p\neq 0$;

  2. $p$ is not a unit; and

  3. for all $x,y\in R$, if $p\mid xy$, then $p\mid x$ or $p\mid y$.

Let $R$ be a ring, and $r\in R$ be an element.

If $r\neq 0$, then $r$ is a prime element iff $(r)$ is a prime ideal.
