Let $I\lhd R$ be a maximal ideal of $R$ so that $R/I$ is a field.

Consider $$ \begin{align*} IM = \sb{\sum_{i} r_i m_i}{\text{finitely many }r_i\in I,\, m_i\in M} \leq M. \end{align*} $$ Then $M/IM$ is an $R/I$-module with operation $$ \begin{align*} R/I \times M/IM &\to M/IM \\ (r+I, m+IM) &\mapsto rm+IM, \end{align*} $$ which is well-defined: if $r'+I = r=I$ and $m+IM = m' + IM$, then by letting $r' = r+s$, $s\in I$ and $m'=m+n$, $n\in IM$, we get $$ \begin{align*} r'm' + IM = rm + \underbrace{rn + s m + sn}_{\in IM} + IM = rm+IM. \end{align*} $$

By taking $M=R^n$ and noting $IR^n = \sb{(r_1, \ldots, r_n)}{r_i\in I} = I^n$, we see $R^n/IR^n = R^n/I^n$ $\isom$ $(R/I)^n$ is a module (vector space) over the field $R/I$. Similarly for $R^m$. Thus $$ \begin{align*} (R/I)^n \isom R^n/IR^n \isom R^m/IR^m \isom (R/I)^m \end{align*} $$ is an isomorphism between vector spaces, so $n=m$.
