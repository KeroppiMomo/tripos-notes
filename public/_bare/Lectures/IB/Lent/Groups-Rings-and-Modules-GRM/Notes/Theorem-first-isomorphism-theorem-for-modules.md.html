Let $ϕ: M\to N$ be a homomorphism of $R$-modules. Then

  1. $\ker(ϕ) \leq M$ is a $R$-submodule;

  2. $\im(ϕ) \leq N$ is a $R$-submodule; and

  3. there is an $R$-module isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} f: \quad M/\ker(ϕ) &\to \im(ϕ) \\ m + \ker(ϕ) &\mapsto ϕ(m). \end{align*} $$

Proof: follows from the first isomorphism theorem for groups.
