Theorem (Sylow) open_in_new

Let $G$ be a finite group of order $p^a m$, where $p$ is prime and $p\not\mid m$.

  1. The set of Sylow $p$-subgroups $\mathrm{Syl}_p(G) = \sb{P\leq G}{|P| = p^a}$ is non-empty.

  2. Any two Sylow $p$-subgroups $H, H' \in \mathrm{Syl}_p(G)$ are conjugate, i.e. $H' = gHg\inv$ for some $g\in G$.

  3. The number $n_p = |\mathrm{Syl}_p(G)|$ of Sylow $p$-subgroups satisfies

    • $n_p = |G :$ $N_G(P)$$|$ for any $P\in \mathrm{Syl}_p(G)$, and so $n_p \mid |G|$;

    • $n_p \equiv 1 \pmod p$, and so $n_p \mid m$.

Proof

Corollary (unique Sylow subgroup is normal) open_in_new

Let $G$ be a finite group.

If $\mathrm{Syl}_p(G)$ $= \set{P}$ has size 1, then $P\lhd G$.

Proof

Corollary (non-abelian simple group and number of Sylow subgroups) open_in_new

Let $G$ be a finite group.

If $G$ is non-abelian and simple, then for each prime $p\mid |G|$, we have $|G| \mid$ $n_p$$!/2$ and $n_p \geq 5$.
