By definition $r\neq 0$ and $r$ is not a unit.

Suppose $r=xy$. Since $r\mid r= xy$, by primality, $r\mid x$ or $r\mid y$. WLOG assume $r\mid x$.

Then $x = rz$ for some $z\in R$. Substituting, $$ \begin{align*} r = xy = ryz \implies yz = 1 \end{align*} $$ as we are working in an integral domain. Hence $y$ is a unit.
