Let $R$ be an Euclidean domain and $N\leq R^m$ be a submodule.

There exists $v_1, \ldots, v_m\in R^m$ such that

  1. $\set{v_1, \ldots, v_m}$ is a basis for $R^m$; and

  2. $\set{d_1 v_1, \ldots, d_r v_r}$ is a basis for $N$, for some $0\leq r\leq m$ and $d_i\in R$ satisfying $d_i \mid d_{i+1}$.

Proof

  • This shows any submodule of $R^m$ is free.
