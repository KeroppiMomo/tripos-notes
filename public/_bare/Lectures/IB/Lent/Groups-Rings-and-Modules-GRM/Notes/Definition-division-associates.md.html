Let $R$ be a ring, and let $a,b\in R$ be elements.

We say $b$ divides $a$, written as $b\mid a$, if equivalently

  • $a = bc$ for some $c\in R$;

  • $(a)$ $\subseteq (b)$.

We say $a,b$ are associates if equivalently

  • $a=bc$ for some unit $c\in R$;

  • $a\mid b$ and $b\mid a$;

  • $(a)=(b)$.
