Let $M$ be an $R$-module and $S = \set{m_1, \ldots, m_k}\subseteq M$ be a finite subset. The following are equivalent:

  1. $S$ generates $M$ freely;

  2. $S$ generates $M$ and $S$ is linearly independent;

  3. every $m\in M$ can be uniquely expressed as $m=r_1 m_1 + \ldots + r_k m_k$ for $r_i\in R$.
