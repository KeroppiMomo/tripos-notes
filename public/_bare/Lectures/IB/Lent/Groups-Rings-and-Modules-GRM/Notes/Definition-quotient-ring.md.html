Let $I\lhd R$ be an ideal.

The quotient ring $R/I = \sb{r+I}{r\in R}$ is the set of cosets with $0_{R/I} = 0_R + I$, $1_{R/I} = 1_R + I$ and operations $$ \begin{align*} (r_1 + I) + (r_2 + I) ={}& (r_1 + r_2) + I, \\ (r_1 + I) \cdot (r_2 + I) ={}& (r_1 \cdot r_2) + I. \end{align*} $$

Proof that this is a ring

  • By definition, the function $f: R\to R/I$ sending $r\mapsto r+I$ is a ring homomorphism.
