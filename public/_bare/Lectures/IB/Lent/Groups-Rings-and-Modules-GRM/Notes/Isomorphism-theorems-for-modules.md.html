Theorem (first isomorphism theorem for modules) open_in_new

Let $ϕ: M\to N$ be a homomorphism of $R$-modules. Then

  1. $\ker(ϕ) \leq M$ is a $R$-submodule;

  2. $\im(ϕ) \leq N$ is a $R$-submodule; and

  3. there is an $R$-module isomorphism $$ \begin{align*} f: \quad M/\ker(ϕ) &\to \im(ϕ) \\ m + \ker(ϕ) &\mapsto ϕ(m). \end{align*} $$

Proof: follows from the first isomorphism theorem for groups.

Theorem (second isomorphism theorem for modules) open_in_new

Let $L,K\leq M$ be $R$-submodules. Then

  1. $L+K$ is an $R$-submodule of $K$; and

  2. $(L+K)/K \isom L/(L\cap K)$.

Proof: follows from the second isomorphism theorem for groups.

Theorem (correspondence theorem for modules) open_in_new

Let $N\leq M$ be submodules. There is a bijection $$ \begin{align*} \set{\text{submodules of $M$ containing $N$}} &\xleftrightarrow{\quad} \set{\text{submodules of $M/N$}}. \end{align*} $$

Theorem (third isomorphism theorem for modules) open_in_new

Let $N\leq L\leq M$ be submodules. Then $$ \begin{align*} ^{\large (M/N)}/_{\large (L/N)} \isom M/L. \end{align*} $$

Proof: follows from the third isomorphism theorem for groups.
