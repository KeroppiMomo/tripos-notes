Theorem (finite p-group has non-trivial centre) open_in_new

If a group $G$ is non-trivial and has order $p^n$ where $p$ is prime, then $Z(G)$ is non-trivial.

Proof

Corollary (group of size prime squared is abelian) open_in_new

A group of order $p^2$ where $p$ is prime is abelian.

Proof

Theorem (finite p-group has subgroup of all prime power orders) open_in_new

Let $G$ be a finite group.

If $G$ has order $p^n$ where $p$ is prime, then for all $0\leq k\leq n$, $G$ has a subgroup of order $p^k$.
