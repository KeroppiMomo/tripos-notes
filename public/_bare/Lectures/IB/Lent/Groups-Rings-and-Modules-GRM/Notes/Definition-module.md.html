Let $R$ be a ring.

An $R$-module is a quadruple $(M, +, 0_M, \cdot)$ where

  • $(M, +, 0_M)$ is an abelian group; and

  • $\cdot: R\times M \to M$

satisfying, for all $m_1, m_2, m\in M$ and $r_1, r_2, r\in R$,

  1. $(r_1 + r_2) \cdot m = r_1\cdot m + r_2 \cdot m$;

  2. $r\cdot (m_1 + m_2) = r\cdot m_1 + r\cdot m_2$;

  3. $r_1(r_2\cdot m) = (r_1 r_2)\cdot m$;

  4. $1_R \cdot m = m$.
