Define a map $$ \begin{align*} ϕ: G/K &\to G/L \\ gK &\mapsto gL. \end{align*} $$

  • $ϕ$ is well-defined since if $gK = g'K$, then $g\inv g' \in K \subseteq L$, so $gL = g'L$.

  • $ϕ$ is a homomorphism.

  • The kernel is $$ \begin{align*} \ker(ϕ) = \sb{gK \in G/K}{gL = L} =\sb{gK\in G/K}{g\in L} = L/K. \end{align*} $$

  • $ϕ$ is surjective.

Result follows from the first isomorphism theorem.
