Recall Schrodinger's equation $i\hbar \pdfd{\psi}{t} = \hat H\psi$. $$ \begin{align*} \dd{t}\langle O\rangle_\psi ={}& \dd{t} \int_{\R^3} \psi^\ast \hat O\psi \d V \\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} \left(\pdfd{\psi^\ast}{t} O \psi + \psi^\ast \dfd{\hat O}{t} \psi + \psi^\ast O \pdfd{\psi}{t}\right) \d V \\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} \frac{1}{i\hbar}\left(-(\hat H\psi)^\ast \hat O \psi + \psi^\ast \hat O \hat H \psi\right) \d V + \Big\langle \dfd{\hat O}{t}\Big\rangle_\psi\\ ={}& \frac{1}{i\hbar} \int_{\R^3} (\psi^\ast \hat O \hat H \psi - \psi^\ast \hat H \hat O \psi)\d V + \Big\langle \dfd{\hat O}{t}\Big\rangle_\psi\\ ={}& \frac{1}{i\hbar} \langle [\hat O, \hat H]\rangle_\psi + \Big\langle \dfd{\hat O}{t}\Big\rangle_\psi. \end{align*} $$
