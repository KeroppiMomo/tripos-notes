For eigenfunctions $\psi_1, \psi_2$ with real eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$, $$ \begin{align*} (\lambda_2 - \lambda_1) \int_{\R^3} \psi_1^\ast \psi_2 \d V ={}& \int_{\R^3} \psi_1^\ast (\lambda_2 \psi_2) \d V - \int_{\R^3} (\lambda_1 \psi_1)^\ast \psi_2 \d V \\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} \psi_1^\ast \hat O \psi_2 \d V - \int_{\R^3} (\hat O\psi_1)^\ast \psi_2 \d V \\ ={}& 0 \end{align*} $$ and since $\lambda_1 \neq \lambda_2$, $\psi_1$ and $\psi_2$ are orthogonal.
