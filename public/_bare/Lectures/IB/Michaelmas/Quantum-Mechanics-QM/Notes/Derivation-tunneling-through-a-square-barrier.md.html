We look for stationary state wave function obeying $$ \begin{align*} -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \dfd[2]{\chi}{x} + U(x) \chi = E\chi. \end{align*} $$ Defining $$ \begin{align*} k = \frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar}, \qquad \kappa = \frac{\sqrt{2m(U_0-E)}}{\hbar}, \end{align*} $$ we have the solution $$ \begin{align*} \chi(x) = \begin{cases} \exp(ikx) + A\exp(-ikx), &x<0, \\ B\exp(-\kappa x) + C\exp(\kappa x), &0<x<a, \\ D\exp(ik x), &x>a \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ where we have normalised the incident way to have amplitude 1.

Enforcing that $\chi$ and $\chi'$ are continuous at $x=0$ and $x=a$, $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} 1 + A = B + C, \\ ik - ikA = -\kappa B + \kappa C, \\ B\exp(-\kappa a) + C\exp(\kappa a) = D\exp(ika), \\ -\kappa B\exp(-\kappa a) + \kappa C\exp(\kappa a) = ikD\exp(ika). \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ We can solve this (or use a computer since it's horrible): $$ \begin{align*} A ={}& B + C - 1, \\ B ={}& \frac{(ik + \kappa)C - 2ik}{\kappa - ik}, \\ C ={}& \frac{2ik\exp(-\kappa a) + (\kappa - ik)\exp(ika) D}{(ik+\kappa)\exp(-\kappa a) + (\kappa - ik)\exp(\kappa a)}, \\ D ={}& \frac{4ik\kappa}{\exp(ika) \,\big((\kappa+ik)^2 \exp(-\kappa a) - (\kappa-ik)^2 \exp(\kappa a)\big)}. \end{align*} $$

The transmission probability is $$ \begin{align*} T ={}& \frac{j_t}{j_i} \\ ={}& \frac{(\hbar k/m) |D|^2}{(\hbar k/m)} \\ ={}& |D|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{16k^2\kappa^2}{\Big|(\kappa^2-k^2)\big(\exp(-\kappa a) - \exp(\kappa a)\big) + 2ik\kappa\big(\exp(-\kappa a) + \exp(\kappa a)\big)\Big|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{4k^2\kappa^2}{\big|(k^2-\kappa^2)\sinh(\kappa a) + 2ik\kappa\cosh(\kappa a)\big|^2} \\ ={}& \frac{4k^2\kappa^2}{(k^4 - 2k^2\kappa^2 + \kappa^4) \sinh^2(\kappa a) + 4k^2\kappa^2\cosh^2(\kappa a)} \\ ={}& \frac{4k^2\kappa^2}{(k^4 - 2k^2\kappa^2 + \kappa^4) \sinh^2(\kappa a) + 4k^2\kappa^2(1+\sinh^2(\kappa a))} \\ ={}& \frac{4k^2\kappa^2}{(k^2+\kappa^2)^2 \sinh^2(\kappa a) + 4k^2\kappa^2}. \end{align*} $$

Consider a low energy particle scattering on a very tall barrier ($U_0 - E \gg \hbar^2/2ma^2$) so that $\kappa a\gg 1$. Then $$ \begin{align*} T \approx \frac{8k^2\kappa^2}{(k^2+\kappa^2)^2} \exp(-2\kappa a) = f\left(\frac{E}{U_0 - E}\right) \exp\left(-\frac{2a}{\hbar}\sqrt{2m(U_0 - E)}\right). \end{align*} $$
