The potential $$ \begin{align*} U(x) = \begin{cases} 0, &0 \leq x \leq a \\ \infty, &\text{otherwise} \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ has stationary state solutions, for each $n=1,2,3,\ldots$, $$ \begin{align*} \chi(x) = \sqrt{\frac{2}{a}}\sin kx, \qquad k = \frac{n\pi}{a}, \qquad E = \frac{\hbar^2\pi^2}{2ma^2} n^2 \end{align*} $$

(Derivation)

  • The ground state energy $E_1 = \hbar^2\pi^2/2ma^2$ is non-zero, which differs from classical mechanics. This is related to the uncertainty principle: if the particle is confined, it must have some momentum.

  • As $n\to \infty$, the probability density approaches a constant, which corresponds to the classical result. This is an example of the correspondence principle.
