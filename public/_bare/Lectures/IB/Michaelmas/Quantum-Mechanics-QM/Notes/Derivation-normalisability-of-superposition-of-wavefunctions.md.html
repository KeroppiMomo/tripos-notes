Suppose $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\R^3} |\psi_1|^2 \d V = N_1<\infty, \qquad \int_{\R^3} |\psi_2|^2 \d V = N_2 < \infty. \end{align*} $$ Note that $$ \begin{align*} |z_1 + z_2|^2 &\leq (|z_1| + |z_2|)^2 \\ &= |z_1|^2 + 2|z_1||z_2| + |z_2|^2 \\ &\leq 2|z_1|^2 + 2|z_2|^2 &&\text{since } (|z_1| - |z_2|)^2 \geq 0. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\R^3} |\alpha\psi_1 + \beta\psi_2|^2 \d V &\leq \int_{\R^3} 2|\alpha|^2|z_1|^2 + 2|\beta|^2|z_2|^2 \d V \\ &\leq 2|\alpha|^2 N_1 + 2|\beta|^2 N_2 \\ &<\infty. \end{align*} $$
