Consider $U(\v x)=0$. Solving the time-independent Schrodinger's equation gives the plane wave solution $$ \begin{align*} \chi(\v x) = A\exp(i\v k\cdot \v x), \qquad E = \frac{\hbar^2 |\v k|^2}{2m}. \end{align*} $$

However this is not normalisable.

Gaussian wave packet open_in_new

A superposition of the plane wave solution to the Schrodinger's equation can be normalisable: in 1D, take $$ \begin{align*} \psi(x,t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \mathcal{A}(k) \exp i\big(kx - \frac{\hbar k^2}{2m} t\big) \d k, \qquad \mathcal{A}(k) = \exp\left(-\frac{\sigma}{2}(k-k_0)^2\right). \end{align*} $$

After normalising, we get $$ \begin{align*} \rho(x,t) = |\psi(x,t)|^2 = \sqrt{\frac{\sigma}{\pi}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \hbar^2 t^2/m^2}} \exp\left(-\frac{\sigma(x - \hbar k_0 t/m)}{\sigma^2 + \hbar^2 t^2/m^2}\right). \end{align*} $$

(Derivation)

Beam interpretation of free particle wave function open_in_new

We can interpret the free particle wave function $$ \begin{align*} \psi_\v k(\v x,t) = A \exp i\big(\v k\cdot \v x - \frac{\hbar |\v k|^2}{2m}t\big) \end{align*} $$ as a beam of particles with

  • momenutm $\v p = \hbar \v k$;

  • energy $E = |\v p|^2/2m = \hbar\omega$;

  • average density of particles $\rho=|\psi|^2 = |A|^2$;

  • probability current $\v j=|A|^2 \times (\hbar \v k)/m =$ average density $\times$ velocity.
