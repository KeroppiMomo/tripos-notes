For any eigenfunction $\psi$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, $$ \begin{align*} (\lambda - \lambda^\ast) \int_{\R^3} \psi^\ast \psi \d V ={}& \int_{\R^3} \psi^\ast (\lambda\psi) \d V - \int_{\R^3} (\lambda\psi)^\ast \psi \d V \\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} \psi^\ast \hat O\psi \d V - \int_{\R^3} (\hat O\psi)^\ast \psi \d V \\ ={}& 0 \end{align*} $$ and since $\psi\not\equiv 0$, $\lambda = \lambda^\ast$.
