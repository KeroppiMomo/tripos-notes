The position-momentum uncertainty follows from the general case and $[\hat x, \hat p] = i\hbar$.

Define $$ \begin{align*} \hat P ={}& \hat A - \langle A\rangle, \\ \hat Q ={}& \hat B - \langle B\rangle. \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} \langle P^2\rangle ={}& \big\langle (A - \langle A\rangle)^2\big\rangle = (\Delta A)^2, \\ \langle Q^2\rangle ={}& \big\langle (B - \langle B\rangle)^2\big\rangle = (\Delta B)^2, \\ [\hat P, \hat Q] ={}& [\hat A, \hat B] - [\langle A\rangle, \hat B] - [\hat A, \langle B\rangle] + [\langle A\rangle, \langle B\rangle] = [\hat A, \hat B]. \\ \end{align*} $$ Hence it suffices to prove that $\langle P^2\rangle\langle Q^2\rangle \geq \frac{1}{4}|\langle [\hat P, \hat Q]\rangle|^2$.

Proof 1

The inequality $$ \begin{align*} 0 \leq{}& \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} ((\hat P + i\lambda \hat Q)\psi)^\ast \, ((\hat P + i\lambda \hat Q)\psi) \d x \\ ={}& \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^\ast (\hat P - i\lambda \hat Q)(\hat P + i\lambda\hat Q) \psi \d x &\text{$\hat P$, $\hat Q$ Hermitian}\\ ={}& \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^\ast (\hat P^2 + i\lambda[\hat P, \hat Q] + \lambda^2 \hat Q) \psi\d x \\ ={}& \langle \hat P^2\rangle + i\lambda \langle [\hat P, \hat Q]\rangle + \lambda^2 \langle \hat Q^2\rangle \end{align*} $$ holds for all real $\lambda$, so $$ \begin{align*} (i\langle [\hat P, \hat Q]\rangle)^2 - 4\langle \hat P^2\rangle\langle \hat Q^2\rangle \leq{} &0 \\ \langle P^2\rangle\langle Q^2\rangle \geq{} &\frac{1}{4}\big|\langle [\hat P, \hat Q]\rangle\big|. \end{align*} $$

Proof 2

Using Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, $$ \begin{align*} \langle P^2\rangle \langle Q^2\rangle ={}& \left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^\ast \hat P^2 \psi \d x\right)\left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^\ast \hat Q^2 \psi \d x\right) \\ ={}& \left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat P\psi)^\ast (\hat P\psi) \d x\right)\left(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat Q\psi)^\ast(\hat Q\psi) \d x\right) \\ \geq{} & \left|\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat P\psi)^\ast (\hat Q\psi) \d x\right|^2 \\ \geq{} & \left(\Im\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat P\psi)^\ast (\hat Q\psi) \d x\right)^2 \\ ={} & \frac{1}{4}\left|\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat P\psi)^\ast (\hat Q\psi) \d x - \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} (\hat Q\psi)^\ast (\hat P\psi) \d x\right|^2 \\ ={} & \frac{1}{4}\left|\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi^\ast (\hat P\hat Q - \hat Q\hat P)\psi\right|^2 \\ ={}& \frac{1}{4} \big|\langle [\hat P, \hat Q]\rangle\big|^2. \end{align*} $$
