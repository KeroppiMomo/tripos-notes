For any normalisable functions $f$ and $g$, $f,g\to 0$ as $|\v x|\to 0$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\R^3} f^\ast\, \vhat p g\d V ={}& -i\hbar \int_{\R^3} f^\ast\, \nabla g \d V \\ ={}& -i\hbar\bigg(\underbrace{\int_{\partial \R^3} f^\ast g \d \v S}_{0} - \int_{\R^3} \nabla f^\ast \,g\d V\bigg) &\text{by parts}\\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} (-i\hbar \nabla f)^\ast \, g \d V \\ ={}& \int_{\R^3} (\vhat pf)^\ast g \d V. \end{align*} $$
