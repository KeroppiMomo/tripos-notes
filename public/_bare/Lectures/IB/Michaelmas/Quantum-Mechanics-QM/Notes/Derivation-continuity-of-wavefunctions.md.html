Assume $U$ has a finite discontinuity at $x_0$. For $\epsilon>0$, integrating the Schrodinger's equation from $x_0-\epsilon$ to $x_0+\epsilon$ gives $$ \begin{align*} \int_{x_0-\epsilon}^{x_0+\epsilon} (E-U(x))\chi(x) \d x ={}& \int_{x_0-\epsilon}^{x_0+\epsilon} -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \dfd[2]{\chi}{x} \d x \\ ={}& -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(\dfd{\chi}{x}\Big|_{x_0+\epsilon} - \dfd{\chi}{x}\Big|_{x_0 - \epsilon}\right) \end{align*} $$ $(E-U(x))\chi(x)$ is bounded, so letting $\epsilon\to 0$ shows that $\dfd{\chi}{x}$ is continuous, and therefore $\chi$ is continuous.
