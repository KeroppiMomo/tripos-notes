From IA Vector Calculus, $$ \begin{alignat}{1} \v e_r ={}& (\sin\theta\cos\phi) \v e_1 &&+ (\sin\theta\sin\phi) \v e_2 &&+ (\cos\theta) \v e_3, \\ \v e_\theta ={}& (\cos\theta\cos\phi) \v e_1 &&+ (\cos\theta\sin\phi) \v e_2 &&+ (-\sin\theta) \v e_3, \\ \v e_\phi ={}& (-\sin\phi) \v e_1 &&+ (\cos\phi) \v e_2 &&+ (0) \v e_3. \\ \end{alignat} $$ and $$ \begin{align*} \v e_1 \pdd{x_1} + \v e_2 \pdd{x_2} + \v e_3 \pdd{x_3} = \nabla = \v e_r \pdd{r} + \v e_\theta\frac{1}{r} \pdd{\theta} + \v e_\phi\frac{1}{r\sin\theta} \pdd{\phi}, \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \hat L_1 ={}& -i\hbar\left(x_2 \pdd{x_3} - x_3 \pdd{x_2}\right) \\ ={}& -i\hbar \bigg((r\sin\theta\sin\phi)\left(\cos\theta \pdd{r} - \frac{\sin\theta}{r} \pdd{\theta}\right) \\ &\qquad -(r\cos\theta)\left(\sin\theta\sin\phi \pdd{r} + \frac{\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r} \pdd{\theta} + \frac{\cos\theta}{r\sin\theta} \pdd{\phi}\right)\bigg) \\ ={}& i\hbar\left(\sin\phi \pdd{\theta} + \cot\theta\cos\phi \pdd{\phi}\right), \\ \hat L_2 ={}& -i\hbar\left(x_3 \pdd{x_1} - x_1 \pdd{x_3}\right) \\ ={}& -i\hbar \bigg((r\cos\theta)\left(\sin\theta\cos\phi \pdd{r} + \frac{\cos\theta\cos\phi}{r} \pdd{\theta} - \frac{\sin\phi}{r\sin\theta} \pdd{\phi}\right) \\ &\qquad -(r\sin\theta\cos\phi)\left(\cos\theta \pdd{r} - \frac{\sin\theta}{r} \pdd{\theta}\right)\bigg) \\ ={}& i\hbar\left(-\cos\phi \pdd{\theta} + \cot\theta\sin\phi \pdd{\phi}\right), \\ \hat L_3 ={}& -i\hbar\left(x_1 \pdd{x_2} - x_2 \pdd{x_1}\right) \\ ={}& -i\hbar \bigg((r\sin\theta \cos\phi)\left(\sin\theta\sin\phi \pdd{r} + \frac{\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r} \pdd{\theta} + \frac{\cos\phi}{r\sin\theta} \pdd{\phi}\right) \\ &\qquad -(r\sin\theta\sin\phi)\left(\sin\theta\cos\phi \pdd{r} + \frac{\cos\theta \cos\phi}{r} \pdd{\theta} - \frac{\sin\phi}{r\sin\theta} \pdd{\phi}\right)\bigg) \\ ={}& -i\hbar \pdd{\phi}. \end{align*} $$
