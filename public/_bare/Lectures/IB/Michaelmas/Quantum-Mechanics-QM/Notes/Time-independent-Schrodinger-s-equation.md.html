We perform a separation of variables $$ \begin{align*} \psi(\v x,t) = \chi(\v x) e^{-i\omega t}, \qquad E = \hbar \omega \end{align*} $$ to obtain the time-independent Schrodinger's equation $$ \begin{align*} -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \nabla^2 \chi(\v x) + U(\v x) \chi(\v x) = E\chi(\v x). \end{align*} $$
