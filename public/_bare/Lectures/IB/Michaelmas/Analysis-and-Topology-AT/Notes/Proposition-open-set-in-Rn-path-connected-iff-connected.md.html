Let $U\subseteq \R^n$ be an open set. Then $U$ is connected iff it is path-connected.

Proof

  • This is false for closed sets: the topologist's sine wave $$ \begin{align*} X = \Big\{\big(x, \sin \frac{1}{x}\big)\;\Big|\; x>0 \Big\} \cup \bigl(\set{0} \times [-1,1]\bigr) \subseteq \R^2. \end{align*} $$ is connected but not path-connected. (Proof)
