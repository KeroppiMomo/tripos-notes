Since $(X,d)$ and $(X,d')$ are (\)-continuous, $\mathrm{id}: (X,d)\to(X,d')$ and $\mathrm{id}\inv: (X,d')\to(X,d)$ are (\)-continuous. Also $f: (X,d)\to (Y,d'')$ is (\*)-continuous, so $\tilde f = f\circ \mathrm{id}\inv: (X,d')\to (Y,d'')$ is also (\*)-continuous by composition of (\*)-continuous functions. (continuity, uniform continuity, Lipschitz continuity)
