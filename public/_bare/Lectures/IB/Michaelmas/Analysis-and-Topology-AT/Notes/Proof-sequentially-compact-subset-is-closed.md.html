Let $\set{y_n}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence in $Y$ that converges to $x\in X$.

Since $Y$ is sequentially compact, $\set{y_n}_{n=1}^\infty$ has a subsequence $\set{y_{n_k}}_{k=1}^\infty$ that converges to some $y\in Y$.

But a subsequence must converge to the same limit as the sequence, so $x=y\in Y$.

Hence $Y$ is sequentially closed and therefore closed.
