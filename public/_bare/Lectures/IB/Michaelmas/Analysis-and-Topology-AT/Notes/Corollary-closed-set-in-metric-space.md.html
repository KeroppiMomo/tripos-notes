Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space. Then

  1. $\emptyset$ and $X$ are closed.

  2. For any family $\set{U_i}_{i\in I}$ where $U_i$ is closed, the intersection $\bigcap_{i\in I} U_i$ is closed.

  3. For any finite family $\set{U_i}_{i\in I}$ where $U_i$ is closed, the union $\bigcup_{i\in I} U_i$ is closed.

Proof: take complements in the properties of a topology.
