Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space, and $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $X$. Define

  • the quotient space $Q=X/{\sim} = \sb{[x]}{x\in X}$, where $[x] = \sb{y\in X}{y\sim x}$, as the set of all equivalence classes;

  • the quotient map $q: X\to Q$ by $x\mapsto [x]$;

  • the quotient topology $$ \begin{align*} \tau_\sim = \sb{V\subseteq Q}{q\inv(V) \in \tau}. \end{align*} $$

Proof that the quotient topology is a topology

  • $\tau_\sim$ is defined this way so that $q: (X,\tau) \to (Q,\tau_\sim)$ is continuous: the preimage of an open set (in $\tau_\sim$) is open (in $\tau$).
