• $\emptyset\in \tau_\sim$ since $q\inv(\emptyset) = \emptyset \in \tau$.

  • $Q\in \tau_\sim$ since $q\inv(Q) = X\in \tau$.

  • For a collection $\sb{V_i}{i\in I} \subseteq \tau_\sim$, $$ \begin{align*} q\inv\left(\bigcup_{i\in I} V_i\right) = \bigcup_{i\in I} q\inv(V_i) \in \tau \end{align*} $$ since it is an union of open sets, so $\bigcup_{i\in I} V_i \in \tau_\sim$.

  • For a finite collection $\sb{V_i}{i\in I} \subseteq \tau_\sim$, $$ \begin{align*} q\inv\left(\bigcap_{i\in I} V_i\right) = \bigcap_{i\in I} q\inv(V_i) \in \tau \end{align*} $$ since it a finite intersection of open sets, so $\bigcap_{i\in I} V_i \in \tau_\sim$.
