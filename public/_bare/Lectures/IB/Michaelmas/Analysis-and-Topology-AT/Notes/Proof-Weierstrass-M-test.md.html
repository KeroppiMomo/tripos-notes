Let $g_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i$. We will show that $g_n$ is uniformly Cauchy.

Fix $ε>0$. Since $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} M_i$ converges, there exists $N\in \N$ such that $|M_n - M_m| = \sum_{i=m+1}^{n} M_i<ε$ for all $n\geq m\geq N$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \sup_U \|g_n - g_m\| ={}& \sup_U \left\|\sum_{i=m+1}^{n} f_i\right\| \\ \leq {}& \sup_U \sum_{i=m+1}^{n} \|f_i\| \\ \leq{} & \sum_{i=m+1}^{n} \sup_U \|f_i\| \\ \leq {}& \sum_{i=m+1}^{n} M_i \\ <{}&ε. \end{align*} $$
