Let $f: U\to\R^m$ with $U\subseteq \R^n$ and $x_0\in U$.

If $\partial_i f, \partial_j f,$ $\partial_{ij}^2$$f$ exist on $U$ and $\partial_{ij}^2 f$ is continuous at $x_0$, then $\partial_{ji} f(x_0)$ exists and $\partial_{ji} f(x_0) = \partial_{ij} f(x_0)$.
