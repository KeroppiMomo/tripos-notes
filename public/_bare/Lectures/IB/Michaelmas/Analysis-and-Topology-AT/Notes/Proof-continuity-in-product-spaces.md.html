$f$ continuous $\implies$ $\pi_X\circ f$ and $\pi_Y\circ f$ continuous

$\pi_X$ and $\pi_Y$ are continuous, and composition preserves continuity.

$\pi_X\circ f$ and $\pi_Y\circ f$ continuous $\implies$ $f$ continuous

Let $U\times V$ be any element of the base of the product topology. $$ \begin{align*} f\inv(U\times V) ={}& f\inv((U\times Y) \cap (X\times V)) \\ ={}& f\inv(U\times Y) \cap f\inv(X\times V) \\ ={}& f\inv(\pi_X\inv(U)) \cap f\inv(\pi_Y\inv(V)) \\ ={}& (\pi_X \circ f)\inv(U) \cap (\pi_Y \circ f)\inv(V). \end{align*} $$ This is the intersection of two open sets since $\pi_X\circ f$ and $\pi_Y\circ f$ are continuous, and so $f\inv(U\times V)$ is open. Thus $f$ is continuous.
