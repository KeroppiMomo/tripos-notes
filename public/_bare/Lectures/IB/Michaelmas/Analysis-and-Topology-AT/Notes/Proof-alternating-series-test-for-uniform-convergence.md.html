By the alternating series test for real series, $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} (-1)^i f_i$ converges pointwise to say $f$.

We will show that $g_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (-1)^i f_i$ is uniformly Cauchy. For $n\geq m$, $$ \begin{align*} \sup_U \|g_n - g_m\| ={}& \sup_U \left\|\sum_{i=m+1}^{n} (-1)^i f_i\right\| = \sup_U \|f_{m+1}\| \to 0 \end{align*} $$ as $m\to \infty$ since $f_{n} \tto 0$.
