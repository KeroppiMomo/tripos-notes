It is enough to prove the product of two compact spaces is compact.

Suppose $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ are compact, and let $\mathcal{W}$ be an open cover of $X\times Y$.

For each $(x,y)\in X\times Y$, there exists $W_{x,y}\in \mathcal{W}$ such that $(x,y)\in W_{x,y}$. By the product topology, there exists open sets $U_{x,y}\subseteq X$ and $V_{x,y}\subseteq Y$ such that $(x,y)\in U_{x,y}\times V_{x,y}\subseteq W_{x,y}$.

Now for each $x\in X$, $\sb{V_{x,y}}{y\in Y}$ is an open cover of $Y$. So by compactness of $Y$, there exists a finite subcover $\sb{V_{x,y}}{y\in Y'_x}$ where $Y'_x\subseteq Y$ is a finite subset.

Define $$ \begin{align*} U_x = \bigcap_{y\in Y'_x} U_{x,y}. \end{align*} $$ $U_x$ is open since it is a finite intersection of open sets. Then $\sb{U_x}{x\in X}$ is an open cover of $X$. By compactness of $X$, there exists a finite subcover $\sb{U_x}{x\in X'}$ where $X'\subseteq X$ is a finite subset.

We have obtained an finite open cover $\sb{U_x\times V_{x,y}}{x\in X',\,y\in Y'_x}$ of $X\times Y$. And since $W_{x,y} = U_{x,y} \times V_{x,y} \supseteq U_x \times V_{x,y}$, we have $\sb{W_{x,y}}{x\in X',y\in Y_x'}$ a finite subcover of $\mathcal{W}$.
