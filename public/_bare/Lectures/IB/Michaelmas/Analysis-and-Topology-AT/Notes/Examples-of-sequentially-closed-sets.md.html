• Let $Y\subseteq \R$ and $C_b(Y) = (\set{\text{continuous, bounded, real-valued functions on }Y}, \text{sup metric})$. This is a subset of $L^\infty(Y) = (\set{\text{bounded real-valued functions on }Y}, \text{sup metric})$, and in fact it is sequentially closed (and therefore closed) by the uniform limit theorem.
