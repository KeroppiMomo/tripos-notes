Fix $\epsilon>0$. By continuity, for each $x\in X$, there exists some $\delta_x > 0$ such that for all $y\in X$, $$ \begin{align*} d(x,y) < 2\delta_x \implies d'(f(x), f(y)) < \epsilon. \end{align*} $$

Take an open cover of $X$ $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{U} = \sb{B_{\delta_x}(x)}{x\in X}. \end{align*} $$ By compactness of $X$, there exists $x_1, \ldots, x_N$ such that $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{U}' = \set{B_{\delta_{x_1}}(x_1), \ldots, B_{\delta_{x_n}}(x_n)} \end{align*} $$ is an open cover of $X$. Let $$ \begin{align*} \delta = \min\set{\delta_{x_1}, \ldots, \delta_{x_n}}. \end{align*} $$

Suppose $x, y\in X$ and $d(x,y)<\delta$. Since $\mathcal{U}'$ is an open cover, $x\in B_{\delta_{x_i}}(x_i)$ for some $i=1,2,\ldots,n$. Then since $$ \begin{align*} d(x_i, x) &< \delta_{x_i} < 2\delta_{x_i}, \\ d(x_i, y) &\leq d(x_i, x) + d(x,y) < \delta_{x_i} + \delta \leq 2\delta_{x_i}, \end{align*} $$ we use continuity at $x_i$ to conclude $$ \begin{align*} d'(f(x), f(y)) \leq d'(f(x_i), f(x)) + d'(f(x_i),f(y)) < \epsilon + \epsilon = 2\epsilon. \end{align*} $$
