Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces, and $f: X\to Y$. The following are equivalent:

  • $f$ is continuous;

  • for all open set $V$ in $(Y,\rho)$, the preimage $f\inv(V)$ is open in $(X,\tau)$;

  • for all closed set $V$ in $(Y,\rho)$, the preimage $f\inv(V)$ is closed in $(X,\tau)$.
