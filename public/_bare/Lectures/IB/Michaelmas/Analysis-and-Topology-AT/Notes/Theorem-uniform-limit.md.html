Let $(X,d)$ and $(Y,d')$ be a metric spaces and $\set{f_n}_{n\geq 1}^\infty$ of functions $X\to Y$, and let $f: X\to Y$.

If $f_n$ $\tto$ $f$ and $f_n$ are continuous, then $f$ is also continuous, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \lim_{x\to x_0} \lim_{n\to \infty} f_n(x) = \lim_{n\to \infty} \lim_{x\to x_0}f_n(x). \end{align*} $$

Consequently, the metric space $C_b(X) = (\set{\text{bounded, continuous functions } X\to\R}, \text{sup metric})$ is (sequentially) closed in $L^\infty(X)$, and is therefore complete.
