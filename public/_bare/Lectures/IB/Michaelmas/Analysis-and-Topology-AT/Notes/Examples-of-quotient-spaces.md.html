Circle

Let $X = [0,2\pi]$. Define the equivalence relation $\sim$ by $0\sim 2\pi$ and all other points are not equivalent to each other.

$Q = X/{\sim}$ is homeomorphic to $Y=\mathbb{S}^1$.

To see this, let $$ \begin{align*} f: [0,2\pi] &\to \mathbb{S}^1 \\ \theta &\mapsto e^{i\theta}. \end{align*} $$ This generates a homeomorphism $\tilde f: Q\to Y$.

Torus

Let $X = [0,2\pi]^2$ with the equivalence relation $(0, \phi) \sim (2\pi, \phi)$, $(\theta, 0) \sim (\theta, 2\pi)$, and all other points not equivalent to each other.

$X/{\sim} = Q$ is homeomorphic to the torus: $$ \begin{align*} f: [0,2\pi]^2 &\to \mathbb{S}^1 \times \mathbb{S}^1 \\ (\theta, \phi) &\mapsto (e^{i\theta}, e^{i\phi}) \end{align*} $$ generates a homeomorphism $\tilde f$.
