Let $Y$ be a set, $(Z,d)$ be a complete metric space and $\set{f_n}_{n\geq 1}^\infty$ be a sequence of functions $Y\to Z$.

$\set{f_n}$ is uniformly convergent iff $\set{f_n}$ is uniformly Cauchy.

Consequently, the metric space $L^\infty(Y; Z) = (\set{\text{bounded functions } Y\to Z},$ $\text{sup metric}$$)$ is complete.

Proof

  • This applies to $Z=\R$ since $(\R, |\cdot|)$ is complete.
