Suppose not. Let $p\in \bigcap_{α} A_α$, and $U\cup V = \bigcup_{α} A_α$ be a separation. WLOG assume $p\in U$.

For each $α$, since $A_α$ is connected, either $A_α\cap U= \emptyset$ or $A_α\cap V=\emptyset$, but since $p\in A_α \cap U$, we must have $A_α\cap V = \emptyset$.

Then $V = V\cap \bigcup_{α} A_α = \bigcup_{α} (V\cap A_α) = \emptyset$, which is a contradiction.
