Let $(X, d)$ and $(\tilde X, \tilde d)$ be metric space. The following are metrices on $X\times \tilde X$:

  • $d_p((x,\tilde x), (y, \tilde y)) = \sqrt[p]{d(x,y)^p + \tilde d(\tilde x, \tilde y)^p}$ for some $p\geq 1$;

  • $d_\infty((x,\tilde x), (y, \tilde y)) = \max\set{d(x,y), \tilde d(\tilde x, \tilde y)}$.
