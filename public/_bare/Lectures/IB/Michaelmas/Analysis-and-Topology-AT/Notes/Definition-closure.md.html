Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and let $A\subseteq X$. Define the closure of $A$ as the smallest closed set containing $A$: $$ \begin{align*} \overline{A} = \op{cl}(A) = \bigcap_{\substack{U \supseteq A \\ U \text{ closed}}} U. \end{align*} $$

  • By definition, $A$ is closed iff $A = \overline{A}$.
