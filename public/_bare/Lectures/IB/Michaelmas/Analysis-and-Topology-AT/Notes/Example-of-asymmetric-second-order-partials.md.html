Let $$ \begin{align*} f(x,y) ={}& \begin{cases} xy \dfrac{x^2-y^2}{x^2 + y^2}, &(x,y)\neq (0,0) \\ 0, &(x,y)=(0,0). \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

First order partials $$ \begin{align*} \partial_x f(x,y) ={}& \frac{y(x^4 + 4x^2 y^2 - y^4)}{(x^2 + y^2)^2}, \\ \partial_y f(x,y) ={}& \frac{x(x^4 - 4x^2 y^2 - y^4)}{(x^2+y^2)^2} \end{align*} $$ which is continuous in $\R^2$, and so $f$ is first differentiable.

In particular $$ \begin{align*} Df(0,0) = (0,0), \qquad Df((h,0)) = (0, h), \qquad Df((0,h)) = (-h,0). \end{align*} $$ Thus the second order mixed partials are $$ \begin{align*} \partial_{xy}^2 f((0,0)) = 1, \qquad \partial_{yx}^2 f((0,0)) = -1. \end{align*} $$
