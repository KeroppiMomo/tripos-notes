Subspace topology open_in_new

Definition (subspace topology) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and $Y\subseteq X$.

The subspace topology on $Y$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} \sb{U\cap Y}{U\in \tau}. \end{align*} $$

Proof that this is a topology

Lemma (continuity relative to subspace topology) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces, and $A\subseteq X$ with the subspace topology.

If $f:X\to Y$ is continuous, then so is $f|_A$.

Proof

Product topology open_in_new

Definition (product topology) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces.

The product topology is the topology on $X\times Y$ which admits $\mathcal{B} = \sb{U\times V}{U\in \tau, V\in \rho}$ as a base.

This can be extended to finite products.

Lemma (projection map is continuous) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces.

The projection maps $$ \begin{align*} \pi_X: X\times Y&\to X &\pi_Y: X\times Y&\to Y \\ (x,y) &\mapsto x & (x,y) &\mapsto y \end{align*} $$ are continuous relative to the product topology.

Proof

  • The definition of the product topology is the coarsest possible topology such that this lemma is true.

Proposition (continuity in product spaces) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$, $(Y,\rho)$ and $(Z,\sigma)$ be topological spaces.

A function $f: Z\to X\times Y$ is continuous iff both $\pi_X$${}\circ f$ and $\pi_Y \circ f$ are continuous.

Proof

Quotient topology open_in_new

Definition (quotient space, quotient map, quotient topology) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space, and $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $X$. Define

  • the quotient space $Q=X/{\sim} = \sb{[x]}{x\in X}$, where $[x] = \sb{y\in X}{y\sim x}$, as the set of all equivalence classes;

  • the quotient map $q: X\to Q$ by $x\mapsto [x]$;

  • the quotient topology $$ \begin{align*} \tau_\sim = \sb{V\subseteq Q}{q\inv(V) \in \tau}. \end{align*} $$

Proof that the quotient topology is a topology

  • $\tau_\sim$ is defined this way so that $q: (X,\tau) \to (Q,\tau_\sim)$ is continuous: the preimage of an open set (in $\tau_\sim$) is open (in $\tau$).

Proposition (continuity on quotient space) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces, $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $X$, $Q = X/{\sim}$ be the quotient space and $q: X\to Q$ be the quotient map.

A function $f: Q\to Y$ is continuous iff $f\circ q$ is continuous.

Proof

Proposition (continuity of functions agreeing on equivalence class) open_in_new

Let $(X,\tau)$ and $(Y,\rho)$ be topological spaces, $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $X$, $Q = X/{\sim}$ be the quotient space and $q: X\to Q$ be the quotient map.

If $f:X\to Y$ is such that $x\sim y\implies f(x) = f(y)$, then there exists a unique $\tilde f: Q\to Y$ such that $f = \tilde f \circ q$.

In this case,

  1. if $f$ is continuous, then so is $\tilde f$;

  2. if for all $U\subseteq Y$, we know $f\inv(U)\in \tau\implies U\in \rho$, then $\tilde f(U) \in \tau_\sim \implies U\in \rho$;

  3. if both $f$ and $f\inv$ are continuous, $f$ is surjective, and $f(x) = f(y) \iff x\sim y$, then $\tilde f$ is a homeomorphism.

Proof
