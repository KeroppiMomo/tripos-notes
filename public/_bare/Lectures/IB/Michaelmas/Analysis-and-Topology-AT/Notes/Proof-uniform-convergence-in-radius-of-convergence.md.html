$$ \begin{align*} \sup_{x\in B_r[a]} |c_n (x-a)^n| \leq |c_n| r^n = |c_n|\bigl((a+r) - a\bigr)^n \end{align*} $$ and since $a+r$ is within the radius of convergence, $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} |c_n| ((a+r) - a)^n$ converges.

Thus by the Weierstrass M-test, the series converges uniformly absolutely on $B_r[a]$.
