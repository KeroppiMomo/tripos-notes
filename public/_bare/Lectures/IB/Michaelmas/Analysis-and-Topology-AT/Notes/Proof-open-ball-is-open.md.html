Let $y\in B_r(x)$. We want to fit another open ball $B_\epsilon(y)$ inside $B_r(x)$.

Let $\epsilon = r - d(x,y)$. Then for all $z \in B_\epsilon(y)$, $$ \begin{align*} d(x,z) &\leq d(x,y) + d(y,z) \\ &< d(x,y) + \epsilon \\ &= r. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $z \in B_r(x)$ so $B_\epsilon(y) \subseteq B_r(x)$.
