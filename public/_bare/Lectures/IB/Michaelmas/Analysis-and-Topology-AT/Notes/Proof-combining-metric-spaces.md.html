$d_\infty$

Let $(x,\tilde x), (y,\tilde y), (z,\tilde z) \in X\times\tilde X$. Triangle inequality holds because $$ \begin{align*} &d_\infty((x,\tilde x), (y,\tilde y)) + d_\infty((y,\tilde y), (z,\tilde z)) \\ ={}& \max\set{d(x,y), \tilde d(\tilde x,\tilde y)} + \max\set{d(y,z), \tilde d(\tilde y,\tilde z)} \\ \geq{}& \max \set{d(x,y) + d(y,z), \tilde d(\tilde x, \tilde y) + \tilde d(\tilde y,\tilde z)} &&\text{maximum}\\ \geq{}& \max \set{d(x,z), \tilde d(\tilde x,\tilde z)} &&\text{$d$ and $\tilde d$ are metrics}\\ ={}& d_\infty((x,\tilde x), (z,\tilde z)). \end{align*} $$
