Let $Y = [a,b]$, a sequence $\set{f_n}_{n\geq 1}^\infty$ of Riemann integrable functions $Y\to \R$, and $f: Y\to \R$.

If $f_n$ $\tto$ $f$, then $f$ is also Riemann integrable, and $$ \begin{align*} \int_{a}^{b} f(x) \d x \equiv \int_{a}^{b} \lim_{n\to \infty} f_n(x) \d x = \lim_{n\to \infty} \int_{a}^{b} f_n(x) \d x. \end{align*} $$
