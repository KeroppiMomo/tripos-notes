Let $(X,\tau)$ be a topological space and $X\neq\emptyset$.

If $f: X\to\R$ is continuous and $(X,\tau)$ is compact, then $f$ is bounded and there exists $p,q\in X$ such that $$ \begin{align*} f(p) &= \sup_{x\in X} f(x) &\text{and}&& f(q) = \inf_{x\in X} f(x). \end{align*} $$

Proof

  • Consequently, $C(X; Y) = (\set{\text{continuous functions }X\to Y}, \text{sup metric}) =$ $C_b(X; Y)$ is well-defined for any metric space $Y$.

  • In particular if $Y$ is complete, then so is $C(X; Y)$.
