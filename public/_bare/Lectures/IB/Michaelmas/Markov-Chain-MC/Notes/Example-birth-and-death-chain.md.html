Let $\ouline{X} = (X_n)_{n\geq 0}$ be a Markov chain on $I = \set{0,1,2,\ldots}$ where $X_n$ represents the size of the population at time $n$. Assume for $i\geq 1$, $$ \begin{align*} P(i, i+1) &= p_i, \\ P(i,i-1) &= q_i = 1 - p_i, \\ P(0, 0) &= 1. \end{align*} $$

Let $A = \set{0}$ and consider the probability of extinction $h_i^A$ $= \prob(T_A < \infty|X_0 = i) = \prob(\text{eventual extinction}|X_0 = i)$.

Hitting probabilities are the minimal non-negative solution to $$ \begin{align*} h_0^A &= 1, \\ h_i^A &= p_i \, h_{i+1}^A + q_i \, h_{i-1}^A, \qquad i\geq 1. \end{align*} $$ Rearranging, $$ \begin{align*} p_i(h_{i+1}^A - h_i^A) = q_i(h_i^A - h_{i-1}^A). \end{align*} $$

By letting $u_i = h_i^A - h_{i-1}^A$, we have $$ \begin{align*} u_{i+1} = \frac{q_i}{p_i} u_i = \ldots = \underbrace{\bigg(\prod_{j=1}^i \frac{q_j}{p_j}\bigg)}_{\gamma_i} u_1. \end{align*} $$ Define $\gamma_0 = 1$ and $\gamma_i = \prod_{j=1}^i \frac{q_j}{p_j}$.

Combining, $$ \begin{align*} h_i^A &= h_0^A + \sum_{j=1}^{i} u_i \\ &= 1 + u_1 \sum_{j=1}^{i} \gamma_{j-1} \\ &= 1 + (h_1^A - 1) \sum_{j=0}^{i-1} \gamma_j. \end{align*} $$ To have $h_i^A\geq 0$, we require $$ \begin{align*} h_1^A \geq 1 - \frac{1}{\sum_{j=0}^{i-1} \gamma_j} \end{align*} $$ and by taking $i\to \infty$, $$ \begin{align*} h_1^A \geq 1 - \frac{1}{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j} \end{align*} $$

For the minimal solution, take $$ \begin{align*} h_1^A = 1-\frac{1}{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j}. \end{align*} $$

  • If $\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j < \infty$, then $$ \begin{align*} h_0^A = 1, \qquad h_i^A = 1 - \frac{\sum_{j=0}^{i-1} \gamma_j}{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j} = \frac{\sum_{j=i}^{\infty} \gamma_j}{\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j}. \end{align*} $$

  • If $\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \gamma_j = \infty$, then $h_i^A = 1$ for all $i$.
