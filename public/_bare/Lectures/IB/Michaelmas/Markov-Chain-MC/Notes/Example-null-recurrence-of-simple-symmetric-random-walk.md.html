The simple random symmetric walk on $\Z$, i.e. the Markov chain on $I$ with transition matrix $P$ given by $$ \begin{align*} P(x,x+1) = P(x,x-1) = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad x\in \Z \end{align*} $$ is not positive recurrent.

To see this, consider an invariant measure $\pi$, which satisfies the invariance equations $$ \begin{align*} \pi_i = \frac{1}{2}\pi_{i-1} + \frac{1}{2}\pi_{i+1}, \qquad i\in \Z. \end{align*} $$ $\pi_i=1$ satisfies the equations.

But this chain is irreducible and recurrent, so all invariant measures are multiples of one another. Since $\pi_i$ cannot be normalised, there is no invariant distribution, and thus this chain is not positive recurrent.
