Suppose $\ouline{X} = (X_n)_{n\geq 0} \sim$ $\mathrm{Markov}(\lambda, P)$ on $I$. Let $T$ be a stopping time and $i\in I$.

Then conditional on $\set{T<\infty} \cap \set{X_T = i}$, the process $(X_{T+n})_{n\geq 0}\sim \mathrm{Markov}(\delta_i, P)$ and is independent of $(X_0, X_1, \ldots, X_T)$.
