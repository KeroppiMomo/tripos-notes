Satisfies system

By definition, if $i\in A$, then $k_i^A = 0$.

If $i\not\in A$, $$ \begin{align*} k_i^A &= \expect(T_A | X_0 = i) \\ &= \sum_{n\geq 0} \prob(T_A > n | X_0 = i) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} \prob(X_1, \ldots, X_n \not \in A | X_0 = i) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} \sum_{j\not\in A} \prob(X_1 = j,\, X_2,\ldots,X_n\not\in A|X_0 = i) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{n\geq 1} \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j) \prob(X_2, \ldots, X_n \not \in A|X_1 = j) \\ &= 1+ \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j) \sum_{n\geq 1} \prob(X_2, \ldots, X_n \not \in A | X_1 = j) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j) \sum_{n\geq 1} \prob(X_0, \ldots, X_{n-1} \not \in A | X_0 = j) &&\text{simple Markov property}\\ &= 1 + \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j) \sum_{n\geq 0} \prob(T_A > n|X_0 = j) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j) \expect(T_A | X_0 = i) \\ &= 1 + \sum_{j\not\in A} P(i,j)\, k_i^A. \end{align*} $$

Minimality

Suppose $k_i$ is also a non-negative solution to the linear system. For $i\in A$, $k_i = 0 = k_i^A$.

If $i\not\in A$, then $$ \begin{align*} k_i ={}& 1 + \sum_{j_1\not\in A} P(i,j_1) \, k_{j_1} \\ ={}& \prob(T_A > 0|X_0 = i) + \sum_{j_1\not\in A} P(i,j_1) + \sum_{j_1,j_2\not\in A} P(i,j_1)P(j_1, j_2) k_{j_2} \\ ={}& \prob(T_A > 0|X_0 = i) + \prob(T_A > 1|X_0 = i) \\ &+\sum_{j_1,j_2\not\in A} P(i,j_1)P(j_1, j_2) + \sum_{j_1,j_2,j_3\not\in A} P(i,j_1) P(j_1, j_2) P(j_2, j_3) k_{j_3} \\ ={}&\ldots \\ ={}&\prob(T_A > 0|X_0 = i) + \ldots + \prob(T_A > n|X_0 = i) \\ &+\sum_{j_1,\ldots,j_{n+1}\not\in A} P(i,j_1) P(j_1, j_2) \ldots P(j_n, j_{n+1}) k_{j_{n+1}} \\ \geq {}& \prob(T_A > 0|X_0 = i) + \ldots + \prob(T_A > n|X_0 = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{m=0}^{n} \prob(T_A > m) \qquad \text{for all }n. \end{align*} $$ Taking the limit as $n\to\infty$, $$ \begin{align*} k_i \geq \sum_{m\geq 0} \prob(T_A > m|X_0 = i) = \expect(T_A | X_0 = i) = k_i^A. \end{align*} $$
