The chain is irreducible, so it suffices to show that 0 is recurrent by showing $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00}(n) = \infty. \end{align*} $$

Note that $X_n=0$ if $n$ is even and we take $n/2$ steps to the right and $n/2$ steps to the left. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) ={}& \pmat{2n \\ n} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2n} \\ ={}& \frac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2} \frac{1}{2^{2n}} \\ \sim{}& \frac{\sqrt{4\pi n}\, (2n)^{2n} e^{-2n}}{\big(\sqrt{2\pi n}\, n^{-n} e^{-n}\big)^2} \frac{1}{2^{2n}} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi n}} \end{align*} $$ where we have used Stirling's formula.

So for $n\geq n_0$ sufficiently large, we can put a lower bound $p_{00}(2n) \geq 1/2\sqrt{\pi n}$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00}(n) ={}& \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00}(2n) \\ \geq {}&\sum_{n\geq n_0} p_{00}(2n) \\ \geq {}& \sum_{n\geq n_0} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi n}} \\ ={}& \infty. \end{align*} $$
