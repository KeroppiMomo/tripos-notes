Satisfies system

If $i\in A$, then $h_i^A = 1$ by definition.

Otherwise suppose $i\not\in A$. We can write $$ \begin{align*} h_i^A &= \prob(T_A < \infty | X_0 = i) \\ &= \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \prob(T_A = n | X_0 = i) \\ &= \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \prob(T_A = n | X_0 = i) &&i\not\in A \\ &= \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \prob(X_0, X_1, \ldots, X_{n-1}\not\in A \text{ but } X_n \in A|X_0 = i) \tag{*}\\ &= \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \prob(X_1, \ldots, X_{n-1}\not\in A \text{ but } X_n \in A|X_0 = i) && i\not\in A.\\ \end{align*} $$

Now we split into cases for $X_1$, and use the probabilities in a Markov chain (1) and the simple Markov property (2): $$ \begin{align*} &h_i^A \\ ={}& \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \sum_{j\in I} \prob(X_1=j,\ X_1, \ldots, X_{n-1}\not\in A \text{ but } X_n\in A|X_0 = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sum_{j\in I} P(i,j) \prob(X_1, \ldots, X_{n-1}\not\in A \text{ but }X_n\in A | X_0 = i, X_1 = j) &&\text{using (1)}\\ ={}& \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sum_{j\in I} P(i,j) \prob(X_1, \ldots, X_{n-1}\not\in A \text{ but }X_n\in A | X_1 = j) &&\text{using (2)}\\ ={}& \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sum_{j\in I} P(i,j) \prob(X_0, \ldots, X_{n-2}\not\in A \text{ but }X_{n-1}\in A | X_0 = j) &&\text{using (2)}\\ ={}& \sum_{j\in I} P(i,j) h_j^A &&\text{using (*)}. \end{align*} $$

Minimality

Suppose $(h_i)_{i\in I}$ also satisfy the given system of equations and $h_i\geq 0$ for all $i\in I$. For $i\in A$, we have $1=h_i \geq h_i^A = 1$.

If $i\not\in A$, then using the equations it satisfies, $$ \begin{align*} h_i ={}& \sum_{j_1\in I} P(i,j_1) h_{j_1} \\ ={}& \sum_{j_1 \in A} P(i,j_1)\underbrace{h_{j_1}}_{1} + \sum_{j_1\not\in A} P(i,j_1) h_{j_1} \\ ={}& \prob(T_A = 1|X_0 = i) + \sum_{j_1 \not \in A,\ j_2 \in I} P(i,j_1) P(j_1, j_2) h_{j_2} \\ ={}& \prob(T_A = 1|X_0 = i) + \sum_{j_1\not\in A,\ j_2\in A} P(i,j_1) P(j_1, j_2) h_{j_2} + \sum_{j_1, j_2\not\in A} P(i,j_1) P(j_1,j_2) h_{j_2} \\ ={}& \prob(T_A = 1|X_0 = i) + \prob(T_A = 2|X_0 = i) + \sum_{j_1, j_2\not\in A, j_3 \in I} P(i,j_1) P(j_1,j_2) P(j_2, j_3)h_{j_3} \\ ={}& \ldots \\ ={}& \prob(T_A = 1|X_0 = i) + \prob(T_A = 2|X_0 = i) + \ldots + \prob(T_A = n|X_0 = i) \\ &+ \sum_{j_1,\ldots,j_n \not\in A} P(i,j_1) P(j_1, j_2) \ldots P(j_{n-1}, j_n) \underbrace{h_{j_n}}_{\geq 0} \\ \geq {}& \prob(T_A \leq n|X_0 = i). \end{align*} $$ And taking $n\to \infty$, $$ \begin{align*} h_i \geq \prob(T_A < \infty|X_0 = i) = h_i^A. \end{align*} $$
