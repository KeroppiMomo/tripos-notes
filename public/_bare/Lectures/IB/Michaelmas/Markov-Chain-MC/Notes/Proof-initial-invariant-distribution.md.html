Suppose $X_n \sim \pi$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \prob(X_{n+1} = j) ={}& \sum_{i\in I} \prob(X_{n+1} = j, X_n = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{i\in I} \prob(X_n = i) \prob(X_{n+1} = j | X_n = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{i\in I} \pi_i P(i,j) \\ ={}& \pi_j \end{align*} $$ by the definition of an invariant distribution.

The result follows from induction.
