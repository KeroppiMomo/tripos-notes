Definition (simple symmetric random walk) open_in_new

A simple symmetric random walk on $\Z^d$ is the Markov chain with transition probabilities $$ \begin{align*} P(x, x + e_i) = P(x, x-e_i) = \frac{1}{2d} \end{align*} $$ for all $i=1,2,\ldots,d$ where $\set{e_1, \ldots, e_d}$ is the standard basis of $\R^d$.

Theorem (recurrence and transience of simple symmetric random walk) open_in_new

The simple symmetric random walk on $\Z^d$ is recurrent for $d=1,2$ and transient for $d\geq 3$.

Proposition (asymmetric random walk on Z is transient) open_in_new

The Markov chain on $\Z$ with transition probabilities, for some $p\in (0,1)$ and $p\neq 1/2$, $$ \begin{align*} P(x,x+1) = p, \qquad P(x,x-1) = 1-p \end{align*} $$ is transient.
