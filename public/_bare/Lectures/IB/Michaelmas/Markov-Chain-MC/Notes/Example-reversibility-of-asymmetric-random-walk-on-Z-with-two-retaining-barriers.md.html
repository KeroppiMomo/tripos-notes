Consider a random walk on $\Z_n = \set{0,1,2,\ldots,n-1}$ with transition probabilities $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} P(i,i+1) = 2/3, &0\leq i\leq n-2, \\ P(n-1,n-1) = 2/3, \\ P(i,i-1) = 1/3, &1\leq i\leq n-1, \\ P(0,0) = 1/3. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

The detailed balance equations are $$ \begin{align*} \frac{2}{3} \pi_i = \frac{1}{3}\pi_{i+1}, \quad 0\leq i\leq n-2, \end{align*} $$ which has the normalised solution $$ \begin{align*} \pi_i = \alpha\cdot 2^i \quad \text{where } \alpha = \left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} 2^{i}\right)\inv. \end{align*} $$ Therefore the chain is reversible.
