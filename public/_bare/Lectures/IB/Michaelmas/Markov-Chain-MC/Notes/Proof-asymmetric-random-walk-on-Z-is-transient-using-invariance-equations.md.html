Consider an invariant measure $\pi_i$ on $\Z$, which satisfies the invariance equation $$ \begin{align*} \pi_i = p\pi_{i-1} + q\pi_{i+1}. \end{align*} $$ This has solutions $$ \begin{align*} \pi_i = A + B\left(\frac{p}{q}\right)^i. \end{align*} $$ Therefore two invariant measures may not be multiples of each other. Since $P$ is irreducible, no state can be recurrent.
