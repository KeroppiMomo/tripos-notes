$x\to y\iff p_{xy}(n) > 0$

Note that $$ \begin{align*} \set{X_n = y\text{ for some }n \geq 0} = \bigcup_{n\geq 0}\set{X_n = y} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} x\to y &\iff \prob(X_n = y \text{ for some } n\geq 0 | X_0 = x) > 0\\ &\iff \prob(X_n = y) > 0 \text{ for some } n\geq 0. \end{align*} $$

$p_{xy}(n) > 0 \iff$ possible sequence exists

Notice $$ \begin{align*} p_{xy}(n) &= \prob(X_n = y|X_0 = x) \\ &= \sum_{x_1, \ldots, x_{n-1}} \prob(X_n = y, X_{n-1} = x_{n-1}, \ldots, X_1 = x_1 | X_0 = x) \\ &= \sum_{x_1, \ldots, x_{n-1}} P(x,x_1) P(x_1, x_2) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, y) \end{align*} $$ so the sum is positive iff one of the terms is positive.
