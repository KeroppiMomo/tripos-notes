The chain is irreducible, so it suffices to show that 0 is transient by showing $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00}(n) < \infty. \end{align*} $$

Note that $p_{00}(n)$ is only non-zero for even $n$, and to return to 0 after $2n$ steps, we need to take $i$ steps left, $i$ steps right, $j$ steps forward, $j$ steps backward, $k$ stpes up, and $k$ steps down, such that $i+j+k=n$.

Then $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) ={}& \sum_{\substack{i,j,k\geq 0, \\i+j+k=n}} \pmat{2n \\ i,i,j,j,k,k} \left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2n} \\ ={}& \pmat{2n \\ n} \left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2n} \sum_{\substack{i,j,k\geq 0, \\i+j+k=n}} \pmat{n \\ i,j,k}^2. \end{align*} $$ If $n=3m$, we can bound $$ \begin{align*} \pmat{n\\i,j,k} \leq \pmat{n\\m,m,m} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) = \pmat{2n\\n} \pmat{n\\m,m,m}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2n} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n}\sum_{\substack{i,j,k\geq 0, \\i+j+k=n}} \pmat{n\\i,j,k} \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^n. \end{align*} $$ The sum equals 1, because that is the probability of putting $n$ items in 3 boxes at random. $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) ={}& \frac{(2n)!}{(n!)^2} \frac{n!}{(m!)^3} \frac{1}{2^{2n} 3^n} \\ ={}& \frac{(2n)!}{n!(\frac{n}{3}!)^3} \frac{1}{2^{2n} 3^{n}} \\ \sim{}& \frac{\sqrt{4\pi n} \, (2n)^{2n} e^{-2n}}{\sqrt{2\pi n} \, n^{n} e^{-n} \big(\sqrt{2\pi \frac{n}{3}} (\frac{n}{3})^{n/3} e^{-n/3}\big)^3} \frac{1}{2^{2n} 3^{n}} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi^{3/2}} \frac{1}{n^{3/2}} \end{align*} $$ so there exists some constant $C$ (say $C = 1/\pi^{3/2}$) such that for sufficiently large $n\geq N$, $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) \leq \frac{C}{n^{3/2}}. \end{align*} $$

For $n\neq 3m$, we can bound $p_{00}(n)$ by $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(6m) &\geq p_{00}(2) p_{00}(6m-2) \geq \left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2} p_{00}(6m-2), \\ p_{00}(6m) &\geq p_{00}(4) p_{00}(6m-4) \geq \left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{4} p_{00}(6m-4), \\ \end{align*} $$ so for sufficiently large $n\geq N$, $$ \begin{align*} p_{00}(2n) \leq \frac{C'}{n^{3/2}} \end{align*} $$ for some constant $C' = 6^4\cdot C$.

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00} ={}& \sum_{n\geq 0} p_{00}(2n) \\ \leq{} & \sum_{n<N} p_{00}(2n) + \sum_{n\geq N} \frac{C'}{n^{3/2}} \\ <{}&\infty. \end{align*} $$
