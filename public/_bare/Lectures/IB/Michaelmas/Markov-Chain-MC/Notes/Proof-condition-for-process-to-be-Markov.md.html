Markov chain have such probabilities

Suppose $\ouline{X} \sim \mathrm{Markov}(\lambda, P)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} &\prob(X_0 = x_0, \ldots, X_{n-1} = x_{n-1}, X_n = x_n) \\ ={}& \prob(X_0 = x_0, \ldots, X_{n-1} = x_{n-1}) \underbrace{\prob(X_n = x_n | X_{n-1} = x_{n-1}, \ldots, X_0 = x_0)}_{P(x_{n-1}, x_n)} \\ ={}& \prob(X_0 = x_0, \ldots, X_{n-2} = x_{n-2}) \underbrace{\prob(X_{n-1} = x_{n-1} | X_{n-2} = x_{n-2}, \ldots, X_0=x_0)}_{P(x_{n-2}, x_{n-1})} P(x_{n-1}, x_n) \\ \vdots & \\ ={}& \prob(X_0 = x_0) P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, x_n) \\ ={}& \lambda_{x_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, x_n). \end{align*} $$

Processes having such probabilities are Markov chains

Suppose $\ouline{X}$ satisfies $\prob(X_0 = x_0, \ldots, X_n = x_n) = \lambda_{x_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, x_n)$ for all $n\geq 0$ and $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_n \in I$.

Then $\prob(X_0 = x_0) = \lambda_{x_0}$ for all $x_0$, and for all $n\geq 0$ and $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_n \in I$, $$ \begin{align*} \prob(X_{n+1} = x_{n+1} | X_{n} = x_{n}, \ldots, X_0 = x_0) &= \frac{\prob(X_{n+1} = x_{n+1}, \ldots, X_0 = x_0)}{\prob(X_n = x_n, \ldots, X_0 = x_0)} \\ &= \frac{\lambda_{x_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, x_n) P(x_n, x_{n+1})}{\lambda_{x_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{n-1}, x_n)} \\ &= P(x_n, x_{n+1}) \end{align*} $$ so $\ouline{X} \sim \mathrm{Markov}(\lambda, P)$.
