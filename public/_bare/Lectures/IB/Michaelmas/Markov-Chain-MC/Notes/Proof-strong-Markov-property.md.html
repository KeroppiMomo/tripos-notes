For all $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_m\in I$ and finite-length paths $\omega \in \bigcup_{k=0}^\infty I^k$, $$ \begin{align*} &\prob(X_T = x_0,\ldots X_{T+m} = x_m, (X_0,\ldots,X_T) = \omega, X_T = i, T<\infty) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \prob(X_k = x_0,\ldots X_{k+m} = x_m, (X_0,\ldots,X_k) = \omega, X_k = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \prob(X_k=x_0,\ldots,X_{k+m} = x_m | (X_0, \ldots, X_k) = \omega, X_k = i) \\ &\qquad \qquad \times \prob((X_0, \ldots, X_k) = \omega, X_k = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \prob(X_k = x_0, \ldots, X_{k+m}=x_m | X_k = i) \times \prob((X_0, \ldots, X_k) = \omega, X_k = i) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \delta_{ix_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{m-1}, x_m) \times \prob((X_0, \ldots, X_k) = \omega, X_k = i) \\ ={}& \delta_{ix_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{m-1}, x_m) \prob((X_0, \ldots, X_T) = \omega, X_T = i, T<\infty). \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{multline*} \prob(X_T = x_0, \ldots, X_{T+m} = x_m, (X_0, \ldots, X_T) = \omega|X_T = i, T<\infty) \\ = \delta_{ix_0} P(x_0, x_1) \ldots P(x_{m-1}, x_m) \prob((X_0, \ldots, X_T)=\omega|X_T = i, T<\infty) \end{multline*} $$ and so $(X_{T+n})_{n\geq 0} \sim \mathrm{Markov}(\delta_i, P)$ and is independent of $(X_0, X_1, \ldots, X_T)$.
