Let $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,V)$ where $V$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{F}$-vector space.

$\alpha$ is diagonalisable iff

  • the minimal polynomial $m_\alpha$ is a product of distinct linear factors; or equivalently

  • there exists a polynomial $p\in F[t]$ such that $p\neq 0$, $p(\alpha) = 0$, and $p$ is a product of distinct linear factors.
