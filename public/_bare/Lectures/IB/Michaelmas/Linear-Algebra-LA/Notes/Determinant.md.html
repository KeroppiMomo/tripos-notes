Definition (volume form) open_in_new

An $n$-dimensional volume form is a function $F: M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F}) \to \mathbb{F}$ satisfying:

  1. multilinear in columns: for any $\v v_1, \ldots, \v v_n \in \mathbb{F}$, the function $$ \begin{align*} \mathbb{F}^n &\to \mathbb{F} \\ {\color{yellow}\v v} &\mapsto F\big(\pmat{\v v_1 & \ldots & \v v_{i-1} & {\color{yellow}\v v} & \v v_{i+1} & \ldots \v v_n}\big) \end{align*} $$ is a linear map for all $1\leq i\leq n$.

  2. alternating: if $A$ has two columns that are the same, then $F(A) = 0$.

Lemma (properties of volume forms) open_in_new

Let $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ and $F$ be an $n$-dimensional volume form on $M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$.

  1. If $A$ has a zero column, then $F(A) = 0$.

  2. $F(A\cdot$ $T_{ij}$$) = -F(A)$.

  3. $F(A\cdot$ $M_{i,\lambda}$$) = \lambda F(A)$.

  4. $F(A\cdot$ $C_{i,j,\lambda}$$) = F(A)$.

Proof

Theorem (determinant as unique volume form) open_in_new

For each $n\in \N$, there is a unique $n$-dimensional volume form $\det$ on $M_{n\times n(\mathbb{F})}$ satisfying $F(I_n) = 1$, given by $$ \begin{align*} \det(A) = \sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \sign(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^n a_{\sigma(i), i} \end{align*} $$ called the $n$-dimensional determinant. Here $S_n$ is the $n$-th symmetric group and $\sign: S_n \to (\set{\pm 1}, \cdot)$ is the sign homomorphism.

This is called Leibniz formula for determinants.

Proof

Lemma (determinant of transpose) open_in_new

For all $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$, $\det$$(A^T) = \det(A)$.

Proof

Proposition (determinant of products) open_in_new

For all $A,B\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$, $\det(AB) = \det(A) \det(B)$.

Proof

This shows that $\det(A\inv) = \det(A)\inv$ and similar matrices have equal determinants.

Definition (determinant of linear maps) open_in_new

Let $V$ be finite-dimensional vector space and $\alpha: V\to V$ be a linear map.

Define the determinant of $\alpha$ as $\det(\alpha) =$ $\det$$($$[A]^B_B$$)$, where $B$ is a basis for $V$.

$\det(\alpha)$ does not depend on the basis chosen. (Derivation)

Corollary (equivalent conditions for invertible linear maps) open_in_new

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space and $\alpha: V\to V$ be a linear map. The following are equivalent:

  1. $\op{rk}$$(\alpha) = \dim(V)$;

  2. $\alpha$ is invertible;

  3. $\alpha$ is non-singular, i.e. $\det$$(\alpha) \neq 0$.

Proof

Proposition (determinant of block-triangular matrix) open_in_new

Let $A\in M_{k\times k}(\mathbb{F})$ and $B\in M_{l\times l}(\mathbb{F})$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \det\pmat{A & * \\ 0 & B} = \det(A) \det(B). \end{align*} $$

Proof

By extension, for square matrices $A_1, \ldots, A_k$, $$ \begin{align*} \det\pmat{A_1 & * & \ldots & * \\ 0 & A_2 & \ldots & * \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \ldots & A_k} = \det(A_1) \det(A_2) \ldots \det(A_k). \end{align*} $$

Here $*$ means any block matrices of the right size.

Definition (minor) open_in_new

Let $A\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$, $1\leq i\leq m$ and $1\leq j\leq n$.

The $(i,j)$-minor matrix $A_{\hat{ij}}\in M_{(m-1)\times(n-1)}(\mathbb{F})$ of $A$ is obtained from $A$ by deleting $i$ and column $j$.

Lemma (Laplace expansion) open_in_new

Let $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$.

  1. For any fixed column $j$, $$ \begin{align*} \det(A) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{ij} (-1)^{i+j} \det(A_{\hat{ij}}). \end{align*} $$

  2. For any fixed row $i$, $$ \begin{align*} \det(A) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij} (-1)^{i+j} \det(A_{\hat{ij}}). \end{align*} $$

Proof

Definition (adjugate matrix) open_in_new

Let $A\in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$.

The adjugate matrix $\op{adj}(A) \in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ is defined by $$ \begin{align*} \op{adj}(A)_{i,j} = (-1)^{i+j} \det(A_{\hat{ji}}). \end{align*} $$

Theorem (adjugate and inverses) open_in_new

Let $A \in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F})$.

  1. $A \op{adj}(A) = \op{adj}(A)\, A = \det(A) \, I_n$.

  2. If $A$ is invertible, the $A\inv = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \op{adj}(A)$.
