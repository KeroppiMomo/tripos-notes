• For $t\in\R$, $$ \begin{align*} \Big\{\pmat{x\\y\\z}\in\R^3 \mid x + y + z = t\Big\} \end{align*} $$ is a subspace of $\R^3$ iff $t=0$.

  • $\R^\R > C(\R) > C^\infty(\R) > P(\R)$, where

    • $\R^\R$ is all functions $\R\to\R$;

    • $C(\R)$ is all continuous functions $\R\to\R$;

    • $C^\infty(\R)$ is all infinitely differentiable functions $\R\to\R$;

    • $P(\R)$ is all polynomial functions $x\mapsto a_d x^d + a_{d-1} x^{d-1} + \ldots + a_1 x + a_0$ for some $a_i\in\R$.
