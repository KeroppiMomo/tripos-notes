Let $V$ be an $\mathbb{F}$-vector space and $S\subseteq V$.

  • A linear combination of $S$ is an expression of the form $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{s\in S}' \lambda_s s. \end{align*} $$ We assume $\lambda_s = 0$ for all but finitely many $s\in S$ ($\sum'$ denotes finite sum).

  • Define the span of $S$ in $V$ be the set of all linear combinations of $S$ $$ \begin{align*} \langle S\rangle = \Big\{\sum_{s\in S}' \lambda_s s \ \Big|\ \lambda_s \in \mathbb{F}\Big\}. \end{align*} $$ We define $\langle \emptyset\rangle = \set{\v 0_V}$ by convention.

  • If $\langle S\rangle = V$, then we say $S$ spans $V$, or $S$ is a spanning set for $V$.
