Let $\op{c-rk}(A) = r$. There exists $P\in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F})$ and $Q \in \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb{F})$ such that $$ \begin{align*} PAQ = \pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0} \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F}). \end{align*} $$

Taking transpose on both sides, $$ \begin{align*} Q^T A^T P^T = \pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0} \in M_{n\times m}(\mathbb{F}). \end{align*} $$

Transpose of an invertible matrix is also invertible, so $A^T$ is also equivalent to $\pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0}$. Therefore $\op{r-rk}(A) = \op{c-rk}(A^T) = r$.
