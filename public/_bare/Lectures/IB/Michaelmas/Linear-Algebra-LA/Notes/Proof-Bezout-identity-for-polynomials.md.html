Define $$ \begin{align*} S = \Big\{\deg\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{k} g_i(t) f_i(t)\Big) \,\Big|\, g_i \in \mathbb{F}[t]\Big\} \setminus \set{-\infty}. \end{align*} $$ $S\subseteq \N$ and is non-empty, so there exists a minimum element $d$, and let $p_i\in \mathbb{F}[t]$ such that $$ \begin{align*} \deg (F) = d \quad \text{ where } F(x) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} p_i(t) f_i(t). \end{align*} $$

Using Euclidean algorithm, dividing $f_i$ by $F$ gives $q,r\in \mathbb{F}[t]$ with $\deg(r) < d$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} f_i = q\cdot F + r \implies r = f_i - \sum_{j=1}^{k} p_j q f_j \end{align*} $$ so $\deg(r) \in S\cup \set{-\infty}$. But $\deg(r) < d = \min S$, so $r=0$. Therefore $F \mid f_i$.

Since $F$ is a common factor of all $f_i$, $F$ cannot have a degree at least 1, so $F$ is a constant. Divide $p_i$ by $F$ to get the result.
