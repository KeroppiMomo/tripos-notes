Let $n=\dim(V)$. Let $B_\lambda$ be a basis for the eigenspace $V_\lambda$ $\leq V$. Extend it to a basis $B$ for $V$.

Then $[\alpha]^B_B$ has the form $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha]^B_B = \pmat{\lambda I_{g_\lambda} & * \\ 0 & C}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore by the determinant of a block-triangular matrix, $$ \begin{align*} \chi_\alpha(t) ={}& \det(t I_n - [\alpha]^B_B) \\ ={}& \det\pmat{(t-\lambda)I_{g_\lambda} & * \\ 0 & tI_{n-g_\lambda} - C} \\ ={}& (t-\lambda)^{g_\lambda} \chi_{C}(t). \end{align*} $$ So $\lambda$ has multiplicity of at least $g_\lambda$ as a root of $\chi_\alpha$.
