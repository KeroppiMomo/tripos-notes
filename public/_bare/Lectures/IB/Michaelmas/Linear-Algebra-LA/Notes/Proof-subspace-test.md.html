If $U\leq V$, then clearly 1. and 2. are satisfied.

Suppose 1. and 2. are satsified. Take $\lambda=-1$ in 2., then for all $u,w\in V$, we have $u-w \in U$. Since $U$ is non-empty by 1., the subgroup criterion implies that $(U,+)$ is a subgroup of $(V,+)$.

Now that $U$ is a subgroup, setting $u=\v 0\in U$ in 2. gives $\lambda w = \v 0 + \lambda w \in U$ for all $\lambda\in\mathbb{F}$ and $w\in U$, as required.
