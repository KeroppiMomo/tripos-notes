Consider the $n\times n$ $\lambda$-Jordan matrix $$ \begin{align*} A = \pmat{\lambda & 1 \\ & \lambda & 1 \\ &&\ddots & \ddots \\ &&&\lambda & 1 \\ &&&&\lambda} \in M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F}). \end{align*} $$

  • Algebraic multiplicity $a_\lambda = n$, since $$ \begin{align*} \chi_A(t) = (t-\lambda)^n. \end{align*} $$

  • Geometric multiplicity $g_\lambda = 1$, since $$ \begin{align*} A - \lambda I_n = \pmat{0 & 1 \\ & 0 & 1 \\ &&\ddots & \ddots \\ &&&0 & 1 \\ &&&&0} \end{align*} $$ has the kernel $V_\lambda = \langle e_1\rangle$.

  • Multiplicity in minimal multiplicity $c_\lambda = n$, since $$ \begin{align*} (A - \lambda I)^{n-1} ={}& \pmat{0 & \ldots & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & \ldots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & \ldots & 0 & 0} \neq 0, \\ (A - \lambda I)^{n} ={}& 0. \end{align*} $$ Because $m_A \mid \chi_A$, we obtain $$ \begin{align*} m_A(t) = (t-\lambda)^n. \end{align*} $$
