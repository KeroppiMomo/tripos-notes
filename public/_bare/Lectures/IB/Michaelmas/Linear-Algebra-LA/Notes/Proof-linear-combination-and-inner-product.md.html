Let $v\in \langle E\rangle$. Write $$ \begin{align*} v = \sum_{e\in E} ' \lambda_e e. \end{align*} $$

For all $d\in E$, $$ \begin{align*} \langle v,d\rangle ={}& \left\langle \sum_{e\in E} ' \lambda_e e, d\right\rangle \\ ={}& \sum_{e\in E} ' \lambda_e \langle e, d\rangle \\ ={}& \lambda_d \langle d,d\rangle \end{align*} $$ since $\langle e,d\rangle = 0$ for $e\neq d$.

Moreover since $d\neq \v 0$, $$ \begin{align*} \lambda_d = \frac{\langle v,d\rangle}{\langle d,d\rangle}. \end{align*} $$

Set $v=\v 0$ to see $\lambda_d=0$, so $E$ is linearly independent.
