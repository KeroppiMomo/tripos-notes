• For any $n\in \Z_{>0}$,

    • $\mathbb{F}^n$ is the vector space of column vectors of length $n$ with entries in $\mathbb{F}$.

    • $\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{F}^1$ is a vector space over itself.

    • $\C^n$ is a complex vector space (i.e. a vector space over $\C$). It is also a real vector space.

  • For any set $X$, $\mathbb{F}^X$ is the vector space of all functions $X\to\mathbb{F}$, with vector addition and scalar multiplication defined by $$ \begin{align*} (f_1 + f_2)(x) &= f_1(x) + f_2(x) \\ (\lambda f)(x) &= \lambda f(x). \end{align*} $$

  • $M_{n\times m}(\mathbb{F})$ is the vector space of matrices with $n$ rows and $m$ columns with entries in $\mathbb{F}$.
