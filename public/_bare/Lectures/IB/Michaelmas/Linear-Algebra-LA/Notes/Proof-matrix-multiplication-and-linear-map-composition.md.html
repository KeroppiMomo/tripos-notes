$[\beta\circ\alpha]^B_D$ is the unique matrix satisfying $$ \begin{align*} [\beta\circ\alpha]^B_D \,[v]_B = [(\beta\circ\alpha)(v)]_D \end{align*} $$ for all $v\in V$.

However $$ \begin{align*} [\beta]^C_D\, [\alpha]^B_C \,[v]_B &= [\beta]^C_D \, [\alpha(v)]_C = [\beta(\alpha(v))]_D \end{align*} $$ so by uniqueness, the two matrices are the same.
