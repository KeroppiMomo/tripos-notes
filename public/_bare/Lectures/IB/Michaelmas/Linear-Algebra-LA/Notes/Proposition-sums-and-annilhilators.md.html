Let $V$ be a vector space, and $U,W\leq V$.

  1. $(U$ $+$ $W)$$^0$ $= U^0 \cap W^0$.

  2. $U^0 + W^0 \leq (U\cap W)^0$.

  3. If $V$ is finite-dimensional, then we have equality in 2., i.e. $U^0 + W^0 = (U\cap W)^0$.

Proof

  • Equality holds in 2. for any vector space if we assume the axiom of choice.
