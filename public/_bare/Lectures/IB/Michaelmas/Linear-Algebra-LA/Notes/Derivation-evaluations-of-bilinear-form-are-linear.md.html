For any $u,u'\in U, \lambda\in \mathbb{F}, v\in V$, $$ \begin{align*} \phi_L(u + \lambda u')(v) ={}& \phi(u+\lambda u', v) \\ ={}& \phi(u,v) + \lambda \phi(u', v) \\ ={}& \phi_L(u)(v) + \lambda \phi_L(u')(v) \\ ={}& \big(\phi_L(u) + \lambda\phi_L(u')\big)(v) \end{align*} $$ so $\phi_L(u+\lambda u') = \phi_L(u) + \lambda \phi_L(u')$. Same for $\phi_R$.
