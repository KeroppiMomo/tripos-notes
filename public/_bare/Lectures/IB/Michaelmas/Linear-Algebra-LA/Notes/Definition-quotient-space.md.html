Let $V$ be an $\mathbb{F}$-vector space and $U\leq V$.

The quotient space of $V$ by $U$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} V/U := \sb{v + U}{v\in V}, \end{align*} $$ the set of left cosets of $(U,+)$ in $(V,+)$.

$V/U$ is an $\mathbb{F}$-vector space under the operations $$ \begin{align*} (v_1 + U) + (v_2 + U) &= (v_1+v_2) + U, \\ \lambda(u_1 + U) &= (\lambda u_1) + U. \end{align*} $$ Proof
