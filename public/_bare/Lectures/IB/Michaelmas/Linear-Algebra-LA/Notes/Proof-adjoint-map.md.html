Let $B=\set{b_1, \ldots, b_n},C = \set{c_1, \ldots, c_m}$ be orthonormal bases for $V,W$.

Existence

Define $\alpha^\star: W\to V$ by $$ \begin{align*} \alpha^\star(c_j) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \overline{([\alpha]^B_C)_{ji}}\, b_i \end{align*} $$ so that $[\alpha^\star]^C_B = \overline{[\alpha]^B_C}^T$.

Note that $[\langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_V]_B = I_n$ and $[\langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_W]_C = I_m$. Then for $v\in V$ and $w\in W$, $$ \begin{align*} \langle \alpha(v), w\rangle_W ={}& ([\alpha(v)]_C)^T \, \overline{[w]_C} \\ ={}& ([\alpha]^B_C \, [v]_B)^T \, \overline{[w]_C} \\ ={}& ([v]_B)^T \, ([\alpha]^B_C)^T \, \overline{[w]_C} \\ ={}& ([v]_B)^T \, \overline{\overline{[\alpha]^B_C}^T \, [w]_C} \\ ={}& ([v]_B)^T \, \overline{[\alpha^\star]^C_B\, [w]_C} \\ ={}& ([v]_B)^T \, \overline{[\alpha^\star(w)]_B} \\ ={}& \langle v, \alpha^\star(w)\rangle_V. \end{align*} $$

Uniqueness

Suppose $\alpha': W\to V$ also satisfies $\langle \alpha(v),w\rangle_W = \langle v, \alpha'(w)\rangle_V$ for all $v\in V$, $w\in W$. Then $$ \begin{align*} ([\alpha]^B_C)_{ji} ={}& ([\alpha]^B_C \,\v e_i)^T \v e_j \\ ={}& ([\alpha]^B_C \, [b_i]_B)^T [c_j]_C \\ ={}& \langle \alpha(b_i), c_j\rangle_W \\ ={}& \langle b_i, a'(c_j)\rangle_V \\ ={}& \v e_i^T \, \overline{[\alpha']^C_B\, \v e_j} \\ ={}& \overline{([\alpha']^C_B)_{ij}} \end{align*} $$ so $[\alpha']^C_B = [\alpha^\star]^C_B$ and hence $\alpha' = \alpha^\star$.
