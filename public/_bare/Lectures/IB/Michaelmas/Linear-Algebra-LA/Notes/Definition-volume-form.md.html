An $n$-dimensional volume form is a function $F: M_{n\times n}(\mathbb{F}) \to \mathbb{F}$ satisfying:

  1. multilinear in columns: for any $\v v_1, \ldots, \v v_n \in \mathbb{F}$, the function $$ \begin{align*} \mathbb{F}^n &\to \mathbb{F} \\ {\color{yellow}\v v} &\mapsto F\big(\pmat{\v v_1 & \ldots & \v v_{i-1} & {\color{yellow}\v v} & \v v_{i+1} & \ldots \v v_n}\big) \end{align*} $$ is a linear map for all $1\leq i\leq n$.

  2. alternating: if $A$ has two columns that are the same, then $F(A) = 0$.
