1. For all $\theta\in V^\star$, $$ \begin{align*} \theta\in (U+W)^0 &\iff \theta(u+w) = 0 &&\text{for all } u\in U, w\in W \\ &\iff \theta(u) = 0 = \theta(w) &&\text{for all } u\in U, w\in W \\ &\iff \theta\in U^0 \cap W^0. \end{align*} $$

  2. Since $U\cap W\leq U$ and $W$, we have $U^0$ and $W^0 \leq (U\cap W)^0$. Therefore $U^0 + W^0 \leq (U\cap W)^0$.

  3. Let $n=\dim(V)$. Then by the sum-intersection formula and the dimension of annihilators, $$ \begin{align*} \dim(U^0 + W^0) ={}& \dim(U^0) + \dim(W^0) - \dim(U^0 \cap W^0) \\ ={}& \dim(U^0) + \dim(W^0) - \dim((U+W)^0) \\ ={}& \big(n-\dim(U)\big) + \big(n-\dim(W)\big) - \big(n - \dim(U+W)\big) \\ ={}& n-\big(\dim(U) + \dim(V) - \dim(U+W)\big) \\ ={}& n-\dim(U\cap W) \\ ={}& \dim((U\cap W)^0). \end{align*} $$
