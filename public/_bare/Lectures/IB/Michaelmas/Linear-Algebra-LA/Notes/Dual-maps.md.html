Definition (dual map) open_in_new

Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces, and $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,W)$. The dual map of $\alpha$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} \alpha^\star: W^\star &\to V^\star \\ \theta &\mapsto \theta\circ\alpha. \end{align*} $$

Illustration

Lemma (properties of dual maps) open_in_new

Let $U,V,W$ be vector spaces, $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,W)$ and $\beta\in \mathcal{L}(U,V)$.

  1. $\alpha^\star$ is linear.

  2. The function $\mathcal{L}(V,W) \to \mathcal{L}(W^\star, V^\star)$ given by $\gamma\mapsto \gamma^\star$ is linear.

  3. $(\alpha\circ\beta)^\star = \beta^\star\circ\alpha^\star$.

  4. If $\alpha$ is an isomorphism, then so is $\alpha^\star$, and $(\alpha^\star)\inv = (\alpha\inv)^\star$.

Proof

Proposition (matrix of dual maps) open_in_new

Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces, and $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,W)$. Further let $B$ be a basis for $V$ and $C$ be a basis for $W$. Then $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha^\star]^{C^\star}_{B^\star} = \big([\alpha]^B_C\big)^T. \end{align*} $$

Proof

Corollary (rank of dual map) open_in_new

Let $\alpha$ as in here. Then $\op{rk}($$\alpha^\star$$) = \op{rk}(\alpha)$.

Proof

Proposition (kernel and image of dual maps) open_in_new

Let $V$ and $W$ be $\mathbb{F}$-vector spaces and $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,W)$. Then

  1. $\ker(\alpha^\star) = \im(\alpha)^0$;

  2. $\im(\alpha^\star) \leq \ker(\alpha)^0$;

  3. If $V$ and $W$ are finite-dimensional, then we have equality in 2., i.e. $\im(\alpha^\star) = \ker(\alpha)^0$.
