Let $A = \pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_n}$, and for $1\leq i\leq n$, $$ \begin{align*} f_i : \mathbb{F}^n &\to \mathbb{F} \\ \v v &\mapsto F\big(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_{i-1} & \v v & \v c_{i+1} & \ldots & \v c_n}\big). \end{align*} $$ By definition, $f_i$ is linear and $$ \begin{align*} f_i(\v c_j) = \delta_{ij} F(A) \end{align*} $$ (since if $j\neq i$, then two columns are equal and so $f_i(\v c_j) = 0$, and if $j=i$, then that is simply $F(A)$).

  1. If $\v c_i=\v 0$, then $F(A) = f_i (\v c_i) = f_i(\v 0) = \v 0$.

  2. WLOG assume $i<j$. Note that $$ \begin{align*} &F(A) + f_j(\v c_i) + F(A \cdot T_{i,j}) + f_i(\v c_j) \\ ={}& F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_i & \ldots & \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ &+ F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_i & \ldots & \v c_i & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ &+ F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_i & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ &+ F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ ={}& F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_i & \ldots & \v c_i + \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ &+ F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_i + \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ ={}& F(\pmat{\v c_1 & \ldots & \v c_i + \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_i + \v c_j & \ldots & \v c_n}) \\ ={}& 0 \end{align*} $$ but $f_j(\v c_i) = f_i(\v c_j) = 0$, so we obtain $F(AT_{i,j}) = -F(A)$.

  3. $F(A\cdot M_{i,\lambda}) = f_i(\lambda \v c_i) = \lambda f_i(\v c_i) = \lambda F(A)$.

  4. $F(A\cdot C_{i,j,\lambda}) = f_j(\v c_j + \lambda \v c_i) = f_j(\v c_j) + \lambda f_j(\v c_i) = F(A) + 0 = F(A)$.
