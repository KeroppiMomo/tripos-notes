By similarity, WLOG assume $A$ is in its Jordan normal form, and write $$ \begin{align*} A = \pmat{J_{n_1}(\lambda_1) \\ & J_{n_2}(\lambda_2) \\ && \ddots \\ &&& J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)}. \end{align*} $$

$a_\lambda(A)$

Note that by the determinant of block-triangular matrix, $$ \begin{align*} \chi_A(t) ={}& \det(t I_n - A) \\ ={}& \det\pmat{tI_{n_1} - J_{n_1}(\lambda_1) \\ & \ddots \\ && tI_{n_k} - J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)} \\ ={}& \prod_{i=1}^{k} \det(t I_{n_i} - J_{n_i}(\lambda_i)) \\ ={}& \prod_{i=1}^{k} (t-\lambda_i)^{n_i}. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} a_\lambda(A) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_\lambda\big(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)\big) \end{align*} $$ equals the sum of sizes of the $\lambda$-Jordan blocks.

$g_\lambda(A)$

Consider $$ \begin{align*} A - \lambda I_n = \pmat{J_{n_1}(\lambda_1) - \lambda I_{n_1} \\ & \ddots \\ && J_{n_k}(\lambda_k) - \lambda I_{n_k}}. \end{align*} $$ By the rank-nullity theorem, $$ \begin{align*} g_\lambda(A) ={}& \ker(A - \lambda I_n) \\ ={}& n - \op{rk}(A - \lambda I_n) \\ ={}& \left(\sum_{i=1}^{k} n_i\right) - \left(\sum_{i=1}^{k} \op{rk}(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k) - \lambda I_{n_k})\right) \\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{k} \Big(n_i - \op{rk}\big(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k) - \lambda I_{n_k}\big)\Big) \\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{k} g_\lambda\big(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)\big). \end{align*} $$ $g_\lambda(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)) = 1$ if $\lambda_k = \lambda$ and 0 otherwise, so $g_\lambda(A)$ is the number of $\lambda$-Jordan blocks for $A$.

$c_\lambda(A)$

For any $p\in \mathbb{F}[t]$, $$ \begin{align*} p(A) = \pmat{p\big(J_{n_1}(\lambda_1)\big) \\ & \ddots\\ & & p\big(J_{n_k}(\lambda_k)\big)}, \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} p(A) = 0 &\iff p\big(J_{n_i}(\lambda_i)\big) = 0 &\text{for all } i=1,2,\ldots,k \\ &\iff m_{J_{n_i}(\lambda_i)} \mid p &\text{for all } i=1,2,\ldots,k. \end{align*} $$ Therefore the minimal polynomial $m_A = \op{lcm}(m_{J_{n_1}(\lambda_1)}, \ldots, m_{J_{n_1}(\lambda_1)})$, and thus $c_\lambda(A) = \max_i\big(c_\lambda(J_{n_i}(\lambda_i))\big)$.

Since $c_\lambda(J_{n}(\lambda)) = n$, $c_\lambda(A)$ is the maximum size of any $\lambda$-Jordan block of $A$.
