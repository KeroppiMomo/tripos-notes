Surjectivity is clear.

For linearity, let $v,w\in V$ and $\lambda\in\mathbb{F}$. $$ \begin{align*} \pi_U(v + \lambda w) &= (v + \lambda w) + U \\ &= (v + U) + ((\lambda w) + U) \\ &= (v + U) + \lambda(w + U) \\ &= \pi_U(v) + \lambda\pi_U(w). \end{align*} $$

For $v\in V$, $v\in\ker(\pi_U)$ iff $$ \begin{align*} v + U = \pi_U(v) = \v 0_{V/U} = \v 0_V + U \end{align*} $$ which is equivalent to $v \in U$.
