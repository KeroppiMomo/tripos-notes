Using this and $\langle e,e\rangle=1$ for $e\in E$, $$ \begin{align*} \langle u,v\rangle ={}& \Big\langle \sum_{e\in E}' \langle u,e\rangle e, \; \sum_{d\in E}' \langle v,d\rangle d \Big\rangle \\ ={}& \sum_{d,e\in E} ' \langle u,e\rangle \, \overline{\langle v,d\rangle} \,\langle e,d\rangle \\ ={}& \sum_{e\in E} ' \langle u,e\rangle \, \overline{\langle v,e\rangle}. \end{align*} $$
