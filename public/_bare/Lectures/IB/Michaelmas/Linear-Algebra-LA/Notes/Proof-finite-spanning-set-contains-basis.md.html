If $S$ is linearly independent, then take $S' = S$ and we are done.

Otherwise if $S$ is linearly dependent, then let $\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_n\in\mathbb{F}$ and $v_1, \ldots, v_n\in S$ distinct be such that $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i v_i = \v 0 \end{align*} $$ and WLOG assume $\lambda_n \neq 0$. Then $$ \begin{align*} v_n = -\frac{1}{\lambda_n} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \lambda_i v_i \in \langle S \setminus \set{v_n}\rangle. \end{align*} $$ So $S\setminus \set{v_n}$ spans $V$. Replace $S$ with $S\setminus \set{v_n}$ and repeat this argument. This terminates after at most $|S|$ steps.
