If $\op{rk}(A)$ or $\op{rk}(B) < n$, then $\op{rk}(AB)<n$. So both sides are 0.

If $\op{rk}(A) = \op{rk}(B)$, then we can write $A$ and $B$ as a product of elementary matrics $$ \begin{align*} A = E_1 \ldots E_m, \qquad B=E_1'\ldots E_k'. \end{align*} $$ and we know $$ \begin{align*} \det(A) = \prod_i \det(E_i), \qquad \det(B) = \prod_i \det(E_i'). \end{align*} $$

Therefore since $AB = E_1 \ldots E_m E_1' \ldots E_k'$, $$ \begin{align*} \det(AB) = \prod_i \det(E_i) \prod_i \det(E_i') = \det(A) \det(B). \end{align*} $$
