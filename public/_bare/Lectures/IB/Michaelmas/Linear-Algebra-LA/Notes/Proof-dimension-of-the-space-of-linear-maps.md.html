Let $\dim(V) = n$ and $\dim(W) = m$.

From the previous theorem, we know that $\mathcal{L}(V,W)\isom M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$. And since $M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ has the standard basis $\set{E_{rs}}$ given by $$ \begin{align*} (E_{rs})_{ij} = \delta_{ri} \delta_{sj}, \qquad 1\leq r\leq m, \ 1\leq s\leq n, \end{align*} $$ i.e. zero matrices with the $(r,s)$ entry being 1. Therefore $\dim(M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})) = mn$.
