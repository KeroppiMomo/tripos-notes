What are the possible Jordan normal forms in $M_{n\times n}(\C)$?

Let $\lambda_i$ be distinct.

$n=2$

Jordan normal form $A$

Minimal polynomial $m_A(t)$

Characteristic polynomial $\chi_A(t)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 \\ & \lambda_2}$

$(t-\lambda_1) (t-\lambda_2)$

$(t-\lambda_1)(t-\lambda_2)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 & \\ & \lambda_1}$

$(t-\lambda_1)$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 & 1 \\ & \lambda_1}$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2$

$n=3$

Jordan normal form $A$

Minimal polynomial $m_A(t)$

Characteristic polynomial $\chi_A(t)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 \\ & \lambda_2 \\ & & \lambda_3}$

$(t-\lambda_1)(t-\lambda_2)(t-\lambda_3)$

$(t-\lambda_1)(t-\lambda_2)(t-\lambda_3)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 \\ & \lambda_1 \\ & & \lambda_2}$

$(t-\lambda_1)(t-\lambda_2)$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2 (t-\lambda_2)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 & 1 \\ & \lambda_1 \\ & & \lambda_2}$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2(t-\lambda_2)$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2 (t-\lambda_2)$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 \\ & \lambda_1 \\ & & \lambda_1}$

$(t-\lambda_1)$

$(t-\lambda_1)^3$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 & 1 \\ & \lambda_1 \\ & & \lambda_1}$

$(t-\lambda_1)^2$

$(t-\lambda_1)^3$

$\pmat{\lambda_1 & 1 \\ & \lambda_1 & 1\\ & & \lambda_1}$

$(t-\lambda_1)^3$

$(t-\lambda_1)^3$

For $n\leq 3$, the Jordan normal form is uniquely determined by knowing $n$, $m_A$ and $\chi_A$.

Just $n$ and $m_A$ are not enough: $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\lambda_1 \\&\lambda_1 \\&&\lambda_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\lambda_1 \\ &\lambda_2 \\ &&\lambda_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ have the same minimal polynomial $(t-\lambda_1)(t-\lambda_2)$.

This is not true for $n\geq 4$.
