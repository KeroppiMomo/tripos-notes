Let $V$ and $W$ be $\mathbb{F}$-vector spaces.

Define $\mathcal{L}(V,W)$ be the set of linear maps $V\to W$.

$\mathcal{L}(V,W)$ is an $\mathbb{F}$-vector space under the operations $$ \begin{align*} (\alpha + \beta) (v) &= \alpha(v) + \beta(v), \\ (\lambda \alpha)(v) &= \lambda \alpha(v). \end{align*} $$
