Full rank $\iff$ invertible

$$ \begin{align*} \op{rk}(\alpha) = \dim(V) &\iff \op{rk}(\alpha) = \dim(V) \text{ and }\op{n}(\alpha)=0 &&\text{rank-nullity} \\ &\iff \alpha \text{ is surjective and injective} \\ &\iff \alpha \text{ is invertible.} \end{align*} $$

Full rank $\iff$ non-singular

Let $B$ be a basis for $V$. By equivalent conditions for invertible matrices, $$ \begin{align*} \op{rk}(\alpha) = \op{rk}([\alpha]^B_B) = \dim(V) \iff \det(\alpha) = \det([\alpha]_B^B) \neq 0. \end{align*} $$
