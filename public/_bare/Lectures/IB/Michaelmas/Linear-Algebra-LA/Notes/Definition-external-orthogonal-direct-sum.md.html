Let $(V_1, \langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_1)$ and $(V_2, \langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_2)$ be inner product spaces.

Define an inner product on the external direct sum $V_1 \oplus V_2$ via $$ \begin{align*} \big\langle (v_1, v_2), (v_1', v_2')\big\rangle = \langle v_1,v_1'\rangle_1 + \langle v_2, v_2'\rangle_2 \end{align*} $$
