Let $B$ be a basis for $V$ and $C$ be a basis for $W$. Then by matrix of dual maps under dual basis and the fact that row rank equals column rank , $$ \begin{align*} \op{rk}(\alpha^\star) ={}& \op{rk}([\alpha^\star]^{C^\star}_{B^\star}) \\ ={}& \op{rk}\big(([\alpha]^B_C)^T\big) \\ ={}& \op{rk}([\alpha]^B_C) \\ ={}& \op{rk}(\alpha). \end{align*} $$
