If $\dim(V) = \dim(W)$, then using the isomorphisms to $\mathbb{F}^n$, $$ \begin{align*} V \isom \mathbb{F}^{\dim(V)} = \mathbb{F}^{\dim(W)} \isom W. \end{align*} $$

If $V\isom W$, then let $\alpha:V\to W$ be an isomorphism. $\ker(\alpha) = \set{\v 0}$ since $\alpha$ is injective, and $\im(\alpha) = W$ since $\alpha$ is surjective. Then by the rank-nullity theorem, $$ \begin{align*} \dim(V) = \dim(\ker(\alpha)) + \dim(\im(\alpha)) = 0 + \dim(W) = \dim(W). \end{align*} $$
