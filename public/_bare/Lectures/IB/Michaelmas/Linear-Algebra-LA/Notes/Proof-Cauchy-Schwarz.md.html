If $v=0$, then we are done.

Notice $$ \begin{align*} 0 \leq{} & \Big\langle u - \frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2} v,\; u - \frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2}v\Big\rangle \\ ={}& \|u\|^2 - \frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2} \overline{\langle u,v\rangle} - \frac{\overline{\langle u,v\rangle}}{\|v\|^2} \langle u,v\rangle + \frac{|\langle u,v\rangle|^2}{\|v\|^4} \|v\|^2 \\ ={}& \|u\|^2 - \frac{|\langle u,v\rangle|^2}{\|v\|^2} \end{align*} $$ and rearranging yields the result.

Equality holds iff $v=0$ or $u = \frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2}v$.
