Let $n=\dim(V)$ and $m=\dim(W)$. Also let $B = \set{b_1, \ldots, b_n}$ and $C=\set{c_1,\ldots,c_n}$.

$A = [\alpha]^B_C$ has entries $a_{ij}$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} \alpha(b_i) = \sum_{k=1}^{m} a_{ki} c_k, \end{align*} $$ and $A' = [\alpha^\star]^{C^\star}_{B^\star}$ has entries $a_{ij}'$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} \alpha^\star(c_j^\star) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_{ij}' b_i^\star. \end{align*} $$

Then for each $i=1,\ldots,n$, applying $b_i$ to both sides to get $$ \begin{align*} a_{ij}' ={}& \alpha^\star(c_j^\star)(b_i) \\ ={}& (c_j^\star \circ \alpha)(b_i) \\ ={}& c_j^\star\left(\sum_{k=1}^{m} a_{ki}c_k\right) \\ ={}& a_{ji}. \end{align*} $$
