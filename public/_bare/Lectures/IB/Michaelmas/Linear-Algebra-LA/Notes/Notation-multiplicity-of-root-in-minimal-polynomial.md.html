Let $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,V)$ where $V$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{F}$-vector space, and let $\lambda\in \mathbb{F}$.

Define $c_\lambda$ as the multiplicity of $\lambda$ as a root of the minimal polynomial $m_\alpha$.

  • If $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $\alpha$, then $1\leq c_\lambda \leq a_\lambda$. (First inequality, second inequality)
