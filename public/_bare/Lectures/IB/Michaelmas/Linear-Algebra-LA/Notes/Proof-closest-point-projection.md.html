Basically Pythagoras theorem:

$$ \begin{align*} \|v-w\|^2 ={}& \|\underbrace{(v-\pi_W(v))}_{\in W^\perp} + \underbrace{(\pi_W(v) - w)}_{\in W}\|^2 \\ ={}& \|v - \pi_W(v)\|^2 + \langle v-\pi_W(v), \pi_W(v) - w\rangle+ \overline{\langle v-\pi_W(v), \pi_W(v) - w\rangle} + \|\pi_W(v) - w\|^2 \\ ={}& \|v - \pi_W(v)\|^2 + \|\pi_W(v) - w\|^2 \\ \geq{} & \|v - \pi_W(v)\|^2 \end{align*} $$ with equality iff $\|\pi_W(v) - w\| = 0$.
