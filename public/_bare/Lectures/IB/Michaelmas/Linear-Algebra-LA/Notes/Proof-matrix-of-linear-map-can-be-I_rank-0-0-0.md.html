Let $\dim(V) = n$ and $\dim(W) = m$.

Existence of bases

Non-constructive proof

By the rank-nullity theorem, $\dim(\ker(\alpha)) = n-r$. Let $\set{v_{r+1}, v_{r+2}, \ldots, v_n}$ be a basis for $\ker(\alpha)$. Extend it to a basis $B = \set{v_1, \ldots, v_r, v_{r+1}, \ldots, v_n}$. Then $\set{\alpha(v_1), \ldots, \alpha(v_r)}$ spans $\im(\alpha)$ and has size $\leq r = \op{rk}(\alpha)$, so it is a basis of $\im(\alpha)$.

Constructive proof

Let $A\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ be a matrix.

Let $E_1, \ldots, E_l$ be elementary matrices such that $E_1 \ldots E_l A$ is in row-reduced echleon form.

Apply elementary column operations $E_1',\ldots,E_k'$ of type 1 to move all pivot columns to the left: $$ \begin{align*} (E_1\ldots E_l) A (E_1' \ldots E_k') = \pmat{I_r & * \\ 0 & 0} \end{align*} $$ where $r$ is the rank of $A$.

Use elementary column operations $E_1'', \ldots, E_j''$ of type 3 to purge the top-right block: $$ \begin{align*} \underbrace{(E_1\ldots E_l)}_{P} A \underbrace{(E_1' \ldots E_k') (E_1'' \ldots E_j'')}_{Q} = \pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0} \end{align*} $$ where $P, Q$ are invertible.

Extend this to a basis $\set{w_1 = \alpha(v_1), \ldots, w_r = \alpha(v_r), w_{r+1}, \ldots, w_n}$ for $W$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \alpha(v_j) = \begin{cases} w_j, &j\leq r, \\ \v 0, &j > r, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha]^B_C = \pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0}. \end{align*} $$

Size of identity matrix equals rank

Let $B' = \set{v_1', \ldots, v_n'}$ and $C' = \set{w_1', \ldots, w_m'}$, and suppose $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha]^{B'}_{C'} = \pmat{I_{r'} & 0 \\ 0 & 0}, \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \alpha(v_j') = \begin{cases} w_j', & j\leq r', \\ \v 0, & j > r'. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Since $\alpha(v_1') = w_1', \ldots, \alpha(v_{r'}') = w_{r'}'$ spans $\im(\alpha)$ and is linearly independent (as it is a subset of $C'$), it is a basis of $\im(\alpha)$, and therefore $\op{rk}(\alpha) = r'$.
