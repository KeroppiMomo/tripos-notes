Let $B_V$ be a basis of $V$ and $B_W$ be a basis of $W$. Set $$ \begin{align*} B_{V\oplus W} = \sb{(v,\v 0)}{v\in B_V} \cup \sb{(\v 0, w)}{w\in B_W} \subseteq V\oplus W. \end{align*} $$

To see this is linearly independent, suppose $$ \begin{align*} (\v 0, \v 0) &= \sum_{v\in B_V} \lambda_v (v, \v 0) + \sum_{w\in B_W} \mu_w (\v 0, w) \\ &= \left(\sum_{v\in B_V} \lambda_v v, \sum_{w\in B_W} \mu_w w\right) \\ \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{v\in B_V} \lambda_v v = \v 0, \qquad \sum_{w\in B_W} \mu_w w = \v 0 \end{align*} $$ and since $B_V$ and $B_W$ are linearly independent, all $\lambda_v = \mu_w = 0$.

To see $B_{V\oplus W}$ spans $V\oplus W$, for any $(v',w')\in V\oplus W$, write $$ \begin{align*} v' = \sum_{v\in B_v} \lambda_v v, \qquad w' = \sum_{w\in B_W} \mu_w w \end{align*} $$ then $$ \begin{align*} (v',w') = \sum_{v\in B_V} \lambda_v(v,\v 0) + \sum_{w\in B_W} \mu_w (\v 0, w). \end{align*} $$
