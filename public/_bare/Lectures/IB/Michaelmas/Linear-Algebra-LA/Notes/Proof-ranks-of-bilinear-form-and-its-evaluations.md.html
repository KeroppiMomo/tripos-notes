Let $B$ be a basis for $U$ and $C$ be a basis for $V$. Using the matrices of $\phi_L$ and $\phi_R$ and that row rank equals column rank, $$ \begin{align*} \rk(\phi_L) ={}& \rk([\phi_L]^B_{C^\star}) \\ ={}& \rk(([\phi]_{B,C})^T) \\ ={}& \rk([\phi]_{B,C}) = \rk(\phi) \\ ={}& \rk([\phi_R]^{C}_{B^\star}) = \rk(\phi_R). \end{align*} $$
