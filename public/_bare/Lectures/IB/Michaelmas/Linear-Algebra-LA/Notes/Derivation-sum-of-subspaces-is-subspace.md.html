Apply the subspace test:

  1. $\v 0\in U$ and $\v 0\in W$, so $\v 0 = \v 0 + \v 0\in U+W$.

  2. Let $v_1, v_2\in U+W$ and $\lambda\in\mathbb{F}$. We can write $v_1 = u_1 + w_1$ and $v_2 = u_2 + w_2$ where $u_1, u_2 \in U$ and $w_1, w_2 \in W$. Then $$ \begin{align*} v_1 + \lambda v_2 &= (u_1 + w_1) + \lambda (u_2 + w_2) \\ &= \underbrace{(u_1 + \lambda u_2)}_{\in U} + \underbrace{(w_1 + \lambda w_2)}_{\in W} \\ &\in U + W. \end{align*} $$
