Let $U\leq V$. $U$ is finite-dimensional, so let $B_U$ be a basis for $U$. Extend $B_U$ to a basis $B_V$ for $V$.

Let $W = \langle B_V\setminus B_U\rangle$. Then $$ \begin{align*} V = \langle B_V\rangle = \langle B_U \cup (B_V \setminus B_U)\rangle = \langle B_U\rangle + \langle B_V \setminus B_U\rangle = U + W. \end{align*} $$

And by the sum-intersection formula, $$ \begin{align*} \dim(U\cap W) &= \dim(U+W) - \dim(U) - \dim(W) \\ &= \dim(V) - \dim(U) - \dim(W) \\ &= |B_V| - |B_U| - |B_V \setminus B_U| \\ &= 0 \end{align*} $$ so $U\cap W = \set{\v 0}$. (Or just use linear independence of the bases.)
