Suppose $\alpha$ is injective. Let $v\in\ker(\alpha)$. Since $$ \begin{align*} \alpha(v) = \v 0_W = \alpha(\v 0_V), \end{align*} $$ by injectivity $v=\v 0_V$. So $\ker(\alpha) = \set{\v 0_V}$.

Suppose $\ker(\alpha) = \set{\v 0_V}$. Let $u,v\in V$ and suppose $\alpha(u) = \alpha(v)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \alpha(u-v) = \alpha(u) - \alpha(v) = \v 0_W. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $u-v\in\ker(\alpha) = \set{\v 0_V}$ and so $u=v$.
