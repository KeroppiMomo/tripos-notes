Putting $\big(\begin{array}{c|c}A & \v b\end{array}\big)$ into row-reduced echelon form entails finding $P \in \mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb{F})$ such that $$ \begin{align*} \big(\begin{array}{c|c}A' & \v b'\end{array}\big) = P \big(\begin{array}{c|c}A' & \v b'\end{array}\big) = \big(\begin{array}{c|c}PA & P\v b\end{array}\big). \end{align*} $$ Since $P$ is invertible, $\sb{\v x\in \mathbb{F}^n}{A\v x = \v b} = \sb{\v x\in \mathbb{F}^n}{PA\v x = P\v b}$.
