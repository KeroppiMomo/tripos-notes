Let $B = \set{v_1, \ldots, v_n}$. Define $U = \langle v_1, \ldots, v_p\rangle$ and $W = \langle v_{p+1}, \ldots, v_n\rangle$. $U$ has dimensions $p$ and makes $\phi|_U$ positive definite, and $\phi|_W$ is negative semidefinite.

Let $U' \leq V$ be a subspace with $\dim(U') > p$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \dim(U') + \dim(W) > n \geq \dim(U' + W) \end{align*} $$ so by the sum-intersection formula, $U'\cap W\neq \set{\v 0}$. So for $v\in (U'\cap W)\setminus \set{\v 0}$, $\phi(v,v)\leq 0$, so $U'$ is not positive definite.
