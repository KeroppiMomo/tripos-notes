Suppose not, and let $\sum_{i=1}^{n} \mu_i v_i = \v 0$ with WLOG $\mu_1\neq 0$.

Let $$ \begin{align*} \beta = \prod_{i=2}^{n} (\alpha - \lambda_i\,\id_V). \end{align*} $$ Then for $j=1,2,\ldots,n$, $$ \begin{align*} \beta(v_j) = \left(\prod_{i=2}^{n} (\lambda_j - \lambda_i)\right) v_j \end{align*} $$ which is non-zero only for $j=1$. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \v 0 = \beta\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \mu_i v_i\right) = \mu_1 \left(\prod_{i=2}^{n} (\lambda_1 - \lambda_i)\right) v_1 \end{align*} $$ so $\mu_1 = 0$, which is a contradiction.
