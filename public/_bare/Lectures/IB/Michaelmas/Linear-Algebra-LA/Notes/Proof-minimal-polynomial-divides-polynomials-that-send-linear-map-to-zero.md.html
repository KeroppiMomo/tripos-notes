Applying the Euclidean algorithm for polynomials, we divide $f$ by $m_\alpha$: there exists $q,r\in \mathbb{F}[t]$ such that $\deg(r) < \deg(m_\alpha)$ and $$ \begin{align*} f(t) = q(t) \cdot m_\alpha(t) + r(t). \end{align*} $$ Then $$ \begin{align*} r(\alpha) = \underbrace{f(\alpha)}_{0} - q(\alpha) \cdot \underbrace{m_\alpha(\alpha)}_{0} = 0. \end{align*} $$ Therefore $r\in I_\alpha$ but $\deg(r) < \deg(m_\alpha)$, and so $r=0$.
