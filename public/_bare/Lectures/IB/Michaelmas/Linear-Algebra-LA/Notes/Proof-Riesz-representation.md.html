Linearity

For all $u,v,v'\in V$ and $\lambda\in \R$, $$ \begin{align*} \phi_{\R, V}(\lambda v + v')(u) ={}& \langle u, \lambda v+ v'\rangle \\ ={}& \lambda\langle u,v\rangle + \langle u,v'\rangle \\ ={}& \lambda\phi_{\R,V}(v)(u) + \phi_{\R,V}(v')(u) \\ ={}& (\lambda \phi_{\R,V}(v) + \phi_{\R,V}(v'))(u) \end{align*} $$ so $\phi_{\R,V}(\lambda v + v') = \lambda\phi_{\R,V}(v) + \phi_{\R,V}(v')$.

Injectivity

If $v\in \ker(\phi_{\R,V})$, then $\langle u,v\rangle = 0$ for all $u\in V$. In particular $$ \begin{align*} \langle v,v\rangle = 0 \end{align*} $$ so $v=\v 0$.

Surjectivity

Since $\dim(V) = \dim(V^\star)$, surjectivity follows from the rank-nullity theorem.
