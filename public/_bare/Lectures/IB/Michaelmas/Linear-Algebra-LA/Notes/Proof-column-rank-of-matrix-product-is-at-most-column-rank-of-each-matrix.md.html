Define linear maps $$ \begin{align*} \alpha: \mathbb{F}^k &\to \mathbb{F}^n & \beta: \mathbb{F}^m &\to \mathbb{F}^k \\ v &\mapsto Av, & v &\mapsto Bv. \end{align*} $$ Note that $$ \begin{align*} \im(\alpha\circ\beta) = (\alpha\circ \beta)(\mathbb{F}^n) = \alpha(\im(\beta)). \end{align*} $$

  • This is a subspace of $\im(\alpha)$, so $\op{c-rk}(AB) = \dim(\im(\alpha\circ\beta)) \leq \dim(\im(\alpha)) = \op{c-rk}(A)$.

  • Apply the rank-nullity theorem to $\alpha$ restricted on $\im(\beta)$, we have $$ \begin{align*} \op{c-rk}(AB) = \dim(\alpha(\im(\beta))) \leq \dim(\im(B)) = \op{c-rk}(B). \end{align*} $$
