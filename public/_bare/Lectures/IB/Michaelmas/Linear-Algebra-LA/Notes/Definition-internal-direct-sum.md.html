Let $V$ be a vector space, and $U,W$ $\leq$ $V$.

We say $V$ is the (internal) direct sum of $U$ and $W$ (denoted as $U\oplus W = V$) if, equivalently,

  • $U$ $+$ $W = V$ and $U\cap W = \set{\v 0}$;

  • the linear map $\phi: U$ $\oplus$ $W \to V$ given by $\phi(u,w) = u+w$ is an isomorphism;

  • every element of $V$ can be uniquely expressed as $u+w$ where $u\in U$ and $w \in W$.
