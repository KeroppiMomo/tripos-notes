Let $\lambda_{1}, \ldots, \lambda_k$ be the eigenvalues of $\alpha$.

Since $g_\lambda \leq a_\lambda$, $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{i=1}^{k} g_{\lambda_i} \leq \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{\lambda_i} \end{align*} $$ with equality iff $g_\lambda = a_\lambda$ for all $\lambda\in \C$.

But the scalar field is $\C$ so $\alpha$ is triangularisable, and thus $\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{\lambda_i} = \dim(V)$.

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} g_\lambda = a_\lambda \text{ for all }\lambda\in \C \iff & \sum_{i=1}^{k} g_{\lambda_i} = \dim(V) \\ \iff &\alpha \text{ is diagonalisable}. \end{align*} $$
