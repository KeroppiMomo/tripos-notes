There are three types of elementary matrices:

  1. identity with row $i$ and $j$ swapped $$ \begin{align*} T_{i,j} = \pmat{1 \\ &\ddots \\ && 0 && 1 \\ &&& \ddots \\ && 1 && 0 \\ &&&&&\ddots \\ &&&&&&1}, \end{align*} $$

  2. identity with the $i$-th diagonal entry replaced with $\lambda\neq 0$ $$ \begin{align*} E_{i,\lambda} = \pmat{1 \\ &\ddots \\ &&1 \\ &&&\lambda \\ &&&& 1 \\ &&&&& \ddots \\ &&&&&& 1}, \end{align*} $$

  3. identity with $\lambda$ in an off-diagonal entry $(i,j)$ $$ \begin{align*} C_{i,j,\lambda} = \pmat{1 \\ &\ddots \\ &&1 \\ &&& \ddots \\ &&\lambda &&1 \\ &&&&& \ddots \\ &&&&&& 1}. \end{align*} $$
