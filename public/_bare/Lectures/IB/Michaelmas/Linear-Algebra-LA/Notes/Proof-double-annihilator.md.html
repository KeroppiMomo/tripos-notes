$\mathcal{E}(U) \leq U^{00}$

Let $u\in U$ and $\theta\in U^0$. $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{E}(u)(\theta) = \theta(u) = 0 \end{align*} $$ so $\mathcal{E}(u) \in U^{00}$.

$\mathcal{E}(U) = U^{00}$

We compare the dimensions: $$ \begin{align*} \dim(U^{00}) ={}& \dim(V^\star) - \dim(U^0) \\ ={}& \dim(V) - \big(\dim(V) - \dim(U)\big) \\ ={}& \dim(U) \\ ={}& \dim(\mathcal{E}(U)) \end{align*} $$ where we have used the dimension of annihilators and the fact that $\mathcal{E}$ is an isomorphism.
