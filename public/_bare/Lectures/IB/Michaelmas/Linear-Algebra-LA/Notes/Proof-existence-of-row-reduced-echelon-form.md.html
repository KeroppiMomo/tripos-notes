Let the columns of $A \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ be $\v c_1, \v c_2, \ldots, \v c_n$. Proceed by induction on $n$.

If $\v c_1=\v 0$, then put $\pmat{\v c_2 & \ldots & \v c_n}$ into row-reduced echelon form using elementary row operations.

Otherwise, suppose $a_{i1} \neq 0$. Then apply elementary row operations:

  • swap row $i$ and row $1$ so that the $(1,1)$ entry is non-zero;

  • multiply the first row by a scalar so that the $(1,1)$ entry is 1; and then

  • use elementary row operation of type 3 to purge the remaining non-zero entries in $\v c_1$.

We get $$ \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{c|ccccc} 1 & * & * & \ldots & * & * \\\hline 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots &&& A' \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) \end{align*} $$

Put $A'$ into row-reduced echelon form using elementary row operations on rows $2$ to $m$, leaving row 1 unchanged. We obtain $$ \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{c|ccccc} 1 & * & * & \ldots & * & * \\\hline 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots &&&\op{RRE}(A')\\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) \end{align*} $$ Apply type 3 operations to purge non-zero entries in row 1 in pivot columns $\geq 2$.
