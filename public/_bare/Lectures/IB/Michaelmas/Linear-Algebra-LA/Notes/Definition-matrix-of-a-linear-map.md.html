Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional $\mathbb{F}$-vector spaces, and $B=\set{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_n}$ be a basis for $V$ and $C=\set{w_1, w_2, \ldots, w_m}$ be a basis for $W$. Further let $\alpha\in$ $\mathcal{L}(V,W)$.

Define $[\alpha]^B_C = (a_{ij}) \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ be the matrix of $\alpha$ with respect to $B$ and $C$, where $a_{ij}$ are given by $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \alpha(v_1) &= a_{11}w_1 + a_{21}w_2 + \ldots + a_{m1}w_m, \\ \alpha(v_2) &= a_{12}w_1 + a_{22}w_2 + \ldots + a_{m2}w_m, \\ \vdots \\ \alpha(v_n) &= a_{1n}w_1 + a_{2n}w_2 + \ldots + a_{mn}w_m. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ (This means $[\alpha(v_j)]_C$ is the $j$-th column of $[\alpha]^B_C$.)
