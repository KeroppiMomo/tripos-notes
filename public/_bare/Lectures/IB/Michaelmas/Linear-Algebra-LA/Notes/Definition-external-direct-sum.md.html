Let $V$ and $W$ be $\mathbb{F}$-vector spaces.

The external direct sum of $V$ and $W$ (denoted as $V\oplus W$) is defined as the $\mathbb{F}$-vector space with the underlying set $V\times W$ with operations $$ \begin{align*} (v_1,w_1) + (v_2,w_2) &= (v_1 + v_2, w_1+w_2) \\ \lambda(v,w) &= (\lambda v, \lambda w). \end{align*} $$ We similarly define $V_1\oplus \ldots\oplus V_n$.
