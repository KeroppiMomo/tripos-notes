Let $B = \set{b_1, \ldots, b_n}$ and $C = \set{c_1, \ldots, c_m}$. Since $$ \begin{align*} \phi_L(b_i)(c_j) = \phi(b_i, c_j) = ([\phi]_{B,C})_{i,j}, \end{align*} $$ we have $$ \begin{align*} \phi_L(b_i) = \sum_{j=1}^{m} ([\phi]_{B,C})_{i,j} c_j^\star \end{align*} $$ by uniquness of linear extensions. Hence $([\phi_L]^B_{C^\star})_{j,i} = ([\phi]_{B,C})_{i,j}$. Similar for $\phi_R$.
