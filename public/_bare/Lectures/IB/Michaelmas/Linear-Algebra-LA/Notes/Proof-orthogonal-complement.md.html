Clearly $\langle w,u\rangle=0$ for all $w\in W$ and $u\in W^\perp$.

Let $E_W$ be an orthonormal basis for $W$. Extend to an orthonormal basis $E_V$ for $V$. We have $E_V \setminus E_W \subseteq W^\perp$, so $$ \begin{align*} V = W + \langle E_V \setminus E_W\rangle \leq W + W^\perp \leq V \end{align*} $$ and therefore $W+W^\perp = V$.
