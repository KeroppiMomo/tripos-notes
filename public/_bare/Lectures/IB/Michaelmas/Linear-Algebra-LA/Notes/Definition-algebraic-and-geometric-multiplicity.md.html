Let $\lambda\in \mathbb{F}$ be an eigenvalue of $\alpha\in \mathcal{L}(V,V)$ where $V$ is a finite-dimensional vector space. Define

  • the algebraic multiplicity $a_\lambda$ of $\lambda$ as the multipliicty of $\lambda$ as a root of $\chi_\alpha(t)$; and

  • the geometric multiplicity $g_\lambda$ of $\lambda$ as $g_\lambda = \dim($$V_\lambda$$)$.

It is common to define $a_\lambda = 0 = g_{\lambda}$ if $\lambda$ is not an eigenvalue of $\alpha$.
