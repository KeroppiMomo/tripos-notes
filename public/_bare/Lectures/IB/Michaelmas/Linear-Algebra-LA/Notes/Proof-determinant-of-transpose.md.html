Using the definition of determinants, $$ \begin{align*} \det(A^T) ={}& \sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \sign(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n} \sigma_{i,\sigma(i)} \\ ={}& \sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \sign(\sigma\inv) \prod_{i=1}^{n} \sigma_{i,\sigma\inv(i)} &&\sigma\mapsto \sigma\inv \text{ is bijective} \\ ={}& \sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \sign(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{\sigma(i),\sigma\inv\sigma(i)} &&\text{rearranging order} \\ ={}&\sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \sign(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{\sigma(i),i} \\ ={}& 0. \end{align*} $$
