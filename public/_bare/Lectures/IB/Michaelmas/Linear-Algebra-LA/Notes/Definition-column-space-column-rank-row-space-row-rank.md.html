Let $A \in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$.

  • The column space $\op{Col}(A)$ of $A$ is the subspace of $\mathbb{F}^m$ spanned by the columns of $A$.

  • The column rank of $A$ is $\op{c-rk}(A) = \dim(\op{Col}(A))$.

Similarly for row space $\op{Row}(\alpha) \leq \mathbb{F}^n$ and row rank $\op{r-rk}(A)$.
