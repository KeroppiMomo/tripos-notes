We can assume $A$ is in Jordan normal form, since for each $P\in \mathrm{GL}_n(\C)$, $$ \begin{align*} P(A - \lambda I_n)^k P\inv = \big(P(A-\lambda I_n) P\inv\big)^k = (PAP\inv - \lambda I_n)^k \end{align*} $$ so $R_{\lambda,r}(A) = R_{\lambda,r}(PAP\inv)$.

Notice $$ \begin{align*} J_l(\lambda) - \lambda I_l = \pmat{0 & 1 \\ & 0 & 1 \\ &&\ddots & \ddots \\ &&&0 & 1 \\ &&&&0} \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} (J_l(\lambda) - \lambda I_l)^2 = \pmat{0 & 0 & 1 \\ & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ &&\ddots & \ddots & \ddots \\ &&&0 & 0 & 1 \\ &&&&0 & 0 \\ &&&&&0} \end{align*} $$ and so on. Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \op{rk}\big((J_l(\lambda) - \lambda I_l)^k\big) = \begin{cases} l-k, &k\leq l, \\ 0, &k\geq l. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

Moreover for $\mu\neq \lambda$, $$ \begin{align*} J_l(\mu) - \lambda I_l = J_l(\mu - \lambda) = \pmat{\mu - \lambda & 1 \\ & \mu - \lambda & 1 \\ &&\ddots & \ddots \\ &&&\mu - \lambda & 1 \\ &&&&\mu - \lambda} \end{align*} $$ which is non-singular since the determinant is $(\mu-\lambda)^l\neq 0$, and thus so is $(J_l(\mu) - \lambda I_l)^k$ for all $k$.

Hence $$ \begin{align*} \op{rk}\left((A - \lambda I_n)^p\right) ={}& \op{rk}\left(\pmat{J_{n_1}(\lambda_1) - \lambda I_{n_1} \\ & \ddots \\ && J_{n_1}(\lambda_k) - \lambda I_{n_k}}^p\right) \\ ={}& \op{rk}\pmat{(J_{n_1}(\lambda_1) - \lambda I_{n_1})^p \\ & \ddots \\ && (J_{n_1}(\lambda_k) - \lambda I_{n_k})^p} \\ ={}& \sum_{i=1}^{k} \op{rk}\big((J_{n_i}(\lambda_i) - \lambda I_{n_i})^p\big) \\ ={}& n - p \times \#\set{\text{$\lambda$-Jordan blocks of size $\geq p$}} \\ & -\sum \text{sizes of $\lambda$-Jordan blocks of size at most $p-1$} \end{align*} $$ which yields the result.

Since $R_{\lambda,r}(A) - R_{\lambda,r+1}(A)=$ the number of $\lambda$-Jordan blocks of size $r$ in any Jordan normal form of $A$, all Jordan normal forms of $A$ are the same up to rearrangement of the Jordan blocks.
