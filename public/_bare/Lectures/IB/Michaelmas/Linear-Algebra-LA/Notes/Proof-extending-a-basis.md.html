Let $B'$ be a basis of $V$. Applying Steinitz exchange lemma by taking $B_U$ as the linearly independent set and $B'$ as the spanning set, there exists $B''\subseteq B'$ with size $|B''| = |B'| - |B_U|$ such that $B_V := B''\cup B_U$ spans $V$. Since also $|B_V| \leq |B''| + |B_U| = |B'| = \dim(V)$, $B_V$ is a basis for $V$.
