Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces and $\alpha: V\to W$ be a linear map.

There exists bases $B$ for $V$ and $C$ for $W$ such that $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha]_C^B = \pmat{I_r & 0 \\ 0 & 0} \equiv \pmat{1 & 0 & \ldots & 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & \ldots & 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \ldots & 1 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0 & 0 & \ldots & 0}. \end{align*} $$ where $r =$ $\op{rk}$$(\alpha)$.

Conversely, if for some $r'$ and bases $B', C'$ for $V, W$, $$ \begin{align*} [\alpha]^{B'}_{C'} = \pmat{I_{r'} & 0 \\ 0 & 0}, \end{align*} $$ then $r' = \op{rk}(\alpha)$.
