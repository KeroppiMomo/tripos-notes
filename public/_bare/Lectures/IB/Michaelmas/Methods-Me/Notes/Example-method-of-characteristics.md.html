Solve $$ \begin{align*} (1+x^2) \pdfd{u}{x} + \pdfd{u}{y} = u+ 1, \qquad u(0,y) = f(y). \end{align*} $$

Here $C = \sb{(x,y)}{x=0}$.

The non-characteristic condition is satisfied because here $\v n = (1,0)$ and $$ \begin{align*} (1+x^2, 1) \cdot (1,0) \big|_C = (1+x^2)|_C = 1 \neq 0. \end{align*} $$

The characteristics are $$ \begin{align*} \dot x = 1+x^2, \qquad \dot y = 1, \qquad (x(0, s) , y(0,s)) = (0, s) \end{align*} $$ which have solutions $$ \begin{align*} x = \tan t, \qquad y=t + s. \end{align*} $$

Let $z(t,s) = u(x,y)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \dot z = z + 1, \qquad z(0, s) = f(s) \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} z(t,s) = (f(s) + 1)e^t - 1. \end{align*} $$

Now invert, $$ \begin{align*} t = \tan\inv x, \qquad s = y - \tan\inv x \end{align*} $$ so finally $$ \begin{align*} u(x,y) = (f(y - \tan\inv x) + 1) e^{\tan\inv x} - 1. \end{align*} $$
