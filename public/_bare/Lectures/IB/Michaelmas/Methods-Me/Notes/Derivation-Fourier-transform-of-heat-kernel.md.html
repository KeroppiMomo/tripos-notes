Recall that $$ \begin{align*} \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-x^2/2} e^{-i\lambda x} \d x = e^{-\lambda^2/2}. \end{align*} $$

Introduce a change of variable $\v x' = \v x/\sqrt{2\kappa t}$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \hat K_t(\v \lambda) ={}& \frac{1}{(4\pi \kappa t)^{n/2}} \int e^{-i\v \lambda\cdot \v x} e^{-|\v x|^2/4\kappa t} \d ^n \v x \\ ={}& \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n/2}} \int e^{-i(\sqrt{2\kappa t}\v \lambda) \cdot \v x'} e^{-|\v x'|^2/2} \d ^n \v x' \\ ={}& \left(\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-i\sqrt{2\kappa t} \lambda_1 x_1'} e^{-x_1'^2/2} \d x_1'\right) \ldots \left(\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-i\sqrt{2\kappa t} \lambda_n x_n'} e^{-x_n'^2/2} \d x_n'\right) \\ ={}& e^{-(\sqrt{2\kappa t} \lambda_1)^2/2} \ldots e^{-(\sqrt{2\kappa t} \lambda_n )^2/2} \\ ={}& e^{-\kappa t |\v \lambda|^2}. \end{align*} $$
