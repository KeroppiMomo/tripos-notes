From here, we want to solve $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \Delta \psi + \mu \psi = 0, &\Omega, \\ \psi = 0, &\partial \Omega. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

Let $\psi(x,y) = X(x) Y(y)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} \frac{X''}{X} + \frac{Y''}{Y} + \mu = 0. \end{align*} $$ Since $x$ and $y$ are independent, there exists a constant $\lambda$ such that $$ \begin{align*} X'' + \lambda X = 0, \qquad Y'' + (\mu - \lambda) Y = 0. \end{align*} $$ Using the boundary conditions $X(0) = X(L) = 0 = Y(0) = Y(L)$, $$ \begin{align*} X = X_n(x) ={}& \sin \frac{n\pi x}{L}, &\lambda = \lambda_n ={}& \left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2, &n={}& 1,2,3,\ldots \\ Y = Y_m(x) ={}& \sin \frac{m\pi y}{L}, &\mu - \lambda ={}& \left(\frac{m\pi}{L}\right)^2, &m={}&1,2,3,\ldots \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \mu_{m,n} = \left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{m\pi}{L}\right)^2. \end{align*} $$

By linearity, $$ \begin{align*} \phi(x,y,t) = \sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty} A_{m,n} e^{-\kappa\mu_{m,n} t} \sin \frac{n\pi x}{L} \sin \frac{m\pi y}{L} \end{align*} $$ solves the heat equation and satisfies the boundary conditions $\phi=0$ on $\partial \Omega$.

To satisfy the initial condition $$ \begin{align*} f(x,y) = \sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty} A_{m,n} \sin \frac{n\pi x}{L} \sin \frac{m\pi y}{L}, \end{align*} $$ we make use of orthogonality relations $$ \begin{align*} &\int_{0}^{L} \int_{0}^{L} f(x,y) \sin \frac{k\pi x}{L} \sin \frac{l\pi y}{L}\d x \d y \\ ={}& \sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty}A_{m,n} \left(\int_{0}^{L} \sin \frac{n\pi x}{L} \sin \frac{k\pi x}{L} \d x\right) \left(\int_{0}^{L} \sin \frac{m\pi y}{L} \sin \frac{l\pi y}{L} \d y\right) \\ ={}& \sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty} A_{m,n} \left(\frac{L}{2} \delta_{nk}\right) \left(\frac{L}{2} \delta_{ml}\right) \\ ={}& \frac{L^2}{4} A_{k,l}. \end{align*} $$
