Let $f:\R\to\C$.

  1. Derivatives and powers:

    • $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}$$\left[\left(\dd{x}\right)^k f(x)\right] = (i\lambda)^k \hat f(\lambda)$;

    • $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[x^k f(x)\right] = \left(i \dd{\lambda}\right)^k \hat f(\lambda)$.

  2. Translation and exponentials:

    • $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[f(x-a)\right] = e^{-i\lambda a} \hat f(\lambda)$;

    • $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[e^{-iax}f(x)\right] = \hat f(\lambda+a)$.
