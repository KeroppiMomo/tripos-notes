Compute $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \hat X_k e^{2\pi ink/N} ={}& \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} \left[X_m e^{-2\pi imk/N}\right] e^{2\pi ink/N} \\ ={}& \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} \left(\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} e^{2\pi i(n-m)k/N}\right) X_m. \end{align*} $$

By geometric sum, $$ \begin{align*} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} e^{2\pi i(n-m)k/N} ={}& \begin{cases} \dfrac{1 - e^{2\pi i(n-m)k}}{1-e^{2\pi i(n-m)k/N}}, &n\neq m \\ N, & n=m \end{cases} \\ ={}& N\, \delta_{nm}. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \hat X_k e^{2\pi ink/N} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{m=0}^{N-1} N\,X_m \,\delta_{nm} = X_n. \end{align*} $$
