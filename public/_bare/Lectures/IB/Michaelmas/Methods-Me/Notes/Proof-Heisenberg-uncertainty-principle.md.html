Start by integrating the normalisation condition by parts, $$ \begin{align*} 1 ={}& \int \psi(x) \, \overline{\psi(x)} \d x \\ ={}& -\int x\big(\psi(x) \, \overline{\psi'(x)} + \psi'(x) \, \overline{\psi(x)}\big) \d x \\ 1 ={}& \left|\int x\big(\psi(x) \, \overline{\psi'(x)} + \psi'(x) \, \overline{\psi(x)}\big) \d x\right| \\ \leq{} & 2 \int |x|\, |\psi(x)|\, |\psi'(x)| \d x \\ \leq{} & 2 \left(\int x^2 |\psi(x)|^2 \d x\right)^{1/2} \left(\int |\psi'(x)|^2 \d x\right)^{1/2} \end{align*} $$ by Cauchy-Schwartz inequality.

Now by Parseval's theorem, $$ \begin{align*} \int |\psi'(x)|^2 \d x ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi} \int |\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}(\psi'(x))|^2 \d \lambda \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi} \int |i \lambda \hat \psi(\lambda)|^2 \d \lambda \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi} \int \lambda^2 |\hat \psi(\lambda)|^2 \d \lambda \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} 1 \leq 2\left(\int x^2|\psi(x)|^2 \d x\right)^{1/2} \left(\frac{1}{2\pi} \int \lambda^2 |\hat \psi(\lambda)|^2 \d \lambda\right)^{1/2} \end{align*} $$ which gives the result.
