Writing $\v X, \v Y, \vhat X, \vhat Y$ as here, $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \hat X_k \, \overline{\hat Y_k} ={}& \frac{1}{N} (\mathcal{F} \v Y)^\dagger (\mathcal{F} \v X) \\ ={}& \frac{1}{N}\v Y^\dagger \mathcal{F}^\dagger \mathcal{F} \v X \\ ={}& \v Y^\dagger \v X \\ ={}& \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} X_n\, \overline{Y_n} \end{align*} $$ where we have used the result for the inverse discrete Fourier transform.
