For $n$ sufficiently large, $x\mapsto$ $\delta_n$$(x-\xi) \in V$. Then we can write $$ \begin{align*} \delta_n(x-\xi) ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \langle \delta_n(x-\xi), Y_k\rangle_w Y_k(x) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left(\int_{a}^{b} \delta_n(t-\xi) Y_k(t) w(t) \d t\right) Y_k(x). \end{align*} $$ Taking the limit $n\to\infty$ on both sides, $$ \begin{align*} \delta(x-\xi) ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left(\int_{a}^{b} \delta(t-\xi)Y_k(t)w(t) \d t\right) Y_k(x) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} Y_k(\xi) w(\xi) Y_k(x) \\ ={}& w(\xi) \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} Y_k(\xi) Y_k(x). \end{align*} $$

We check that for $f\in V$, $$ \begin{align*} f(x) ={}& \int_{a}^{b} \delta(x-\xi)f(\xi) \d \xi \\ ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left(\int_{a}^{b} f(\xi) Y_k(\xi) w(\xi) \d \xi\right) Y_k(x) \\ ={}& \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \hat f_k Y_k(x). \end{align*} $$
