We use the identity $\delta(x-y) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int e^{i\lambda(x-y)} \d \lambda$ to compute $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{2\pi} \int \hat f(\lambda)\, \overline{\hat g(\lambda)} \d \lambda ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi} \int \bigg(\int e^{-i\lambda x}\, f(x) \d x\bigg)\bigg(\int e^{i\lambda y}\, \overline{g(y)}\d y\bigg) \d \lambda \\ ={}& \iint f(x) \, \overline{g(y)} \, \bigg(\int e^{i\lambda(y-x)} \d \lambda\bigg) \d x \d y \\ ={}& \iint f(x) \, \overline{g(y)} \, \delta(y-x) \d x \d y \\ ={}& \int f(x) \, \overline{g(x)} \d x. \end{align*} $$
