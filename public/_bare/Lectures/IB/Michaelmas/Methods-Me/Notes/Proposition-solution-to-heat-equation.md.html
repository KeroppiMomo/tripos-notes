Consider the initial value problem of the heat equation $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} u_t - \kappa \Delta u = F(\v x, t), &\R^n \times (0,\infty), \\ u(\v x, 0) = f(\v x), &\R^n. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

  • For zero forcing $F=0$, the solution is $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x,t) = (K_t * f)(\v x) = \int K_t(\v x - \v y) \,f(\v y) \d ^n \v y. \end{align*} $$

  • For zero initial condition $f=0$, the solution is $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x,t) = \int_{0}^{t} \int K_{t-s}(\v x-\v y) \, F(\v y,s) \d ^n \v y \d s \\ \end{align*} $$ Equivalently, if we set $F(\v x,t) = 0$ for $t<0$, then $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x, t) ={}& \iint G(\v x,t; \v y, s) \, F(\v y, s) \d^n \v y \d s \end{align*} $$ where $$ \begin{align*} G(\v x, t; \v y, s) = H(t-s) \, K_{t-s}(\v x-\v y) \end{align*} $$ is the Green's function for heat equation on $\R^n$ with zero initial conditions.
