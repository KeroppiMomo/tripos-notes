For $\v x\in\R^3$, assuming $\phi$ is symmetric about the $z$-axis, i.e. $\phi=\phi(r,\theta)$, $$ \begin{align*} \Delta \phi = 0, \qquad r_1 < |\v x| < r_2 \end{align*} $$ has separation of variables solutions in spherical polar coordinates $$ \begin{align*} \phi(r,\theta) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(a_n r^n + \frac{\beta_n}{r^{n+1}}\right) P_n(\cos\theta) \end{align*} $$ where $P_n$ is the $n$-th Legendre polynomial.

(Derivation)

Use the completeness of the Legendre polynomials to satisfy boudnary conditions. (Example)
