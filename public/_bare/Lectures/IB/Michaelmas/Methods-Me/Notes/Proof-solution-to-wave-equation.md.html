Taking the Fourier transform, $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \hat u_{tt} + c^2|\v \lambda|^2 \hat u = \hat F(\v \lambda, t), &\R^n\times (0,\infty), \\ \hat u(\v \lambda, 0) = \hat f(\v \lambda), & \R^n, \\ \hat u_t(\v \lambda, 0) = \hat g(\v \lambda), & \R^n. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

Zero forcing

Solving the ODE $\hat u_{tt} + c^2|\v \lambda|^2 \hat u = 0$ in $t$, $$ \begin{align*} \hat u(\v \lambda, t) = A(\v \lambda) \sin(c|\v \lambda|t) + B(\v \lambda) \cos(c|\v \lambda|t). \end{align*} $$ Initial conditions give $B(\v \lambda) = \hat f(\v \lambda)$ and $A(\v \lambda) = \hat g(\v \lambda)/c|\v \lambda|$, so $$ \begin{align*} \hat u(\v \lambda, t) ={}& \hat g(\v \lambda) \frac{\sin(c|\v \lambda|t)}{c|\v \lambda|} + \hat f(\v \lambda) \pdd{t} \bigg(\frac{\sin(c|\v \lambda|t)}{c|\v \lambda|}\bigg) \\ ={}& \hat g(\v \lambda) \hat \Phi_t(\v \lambda) + \pdd{t}(\hat f(\v \lambda) \hat \Phi_t(\v \lambda)). \end{align*} $$ By the convolution theorem, $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x,t) = (\Phi_t*g)(\v x) + \pdd{t}((\Phi_t*f)(\v x)). \end{align*} $$

Zero initial conditions

First we construct the Green's function $G(t; s)$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} G_{tt} + c^2 |\v \lambda|^2 G = \delta(t-s), \\ G(0; s) = 0, \\ G_t(0; s) = 0, \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ where $s>0$. It is $$ \begin{align*} G(t; s) = \begin{cases} 0, &0<t<s, \\ \dfrac{\sin\big(c|\v \lambda|(t-s)\big)}{c|\v \lambda|}, &t>s. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Therefore $$ \begin{align*} \hat u(\v \lambda, t) ={}& \int_{0}^{t} \frac{\sin \big(c|\v \lambda|(t-s)\big)}{c|\v \lambda|} \, \hat F(\v \lambda, s) \d s \\ ={}& \int_{0}^{t} \hat \Phi_{t-s}(\v \lambda) \, \hat F(\v \lambda, s) \d s \end{align*} $$ so by the convolution theorem, $$ \begin{align*} u(\v \lambda, t) = \int_{0}^{t} \int \Phi_{t-s}(\v x-\v y)\, F(\v y, s) \d^n \v y \d s. \end{align*} $$
