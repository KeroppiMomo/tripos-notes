Let $f_\alpha(\v x) = |\v x|^{-\alpha}$. Its Fourier transform is $$ \begin{align*} \hat f_{\alpha}(\v \lambda) = \int e^{-i\v \lambda\cdot \v x} |\v x|^{-\alpha} \d ^n \v x. \end{align*} $$

Note that $\hat f_\alpha$ is rotation invariant: for any $R\in \mathrm{SO}(n)$, we use the substitution $\v x = R^T \v x'$ to see $$ \begin{align*} \hat f_\alpha(\v \lambda) ={}& \int e^{-i\v \lambda\cdot (R^T \v x')} |R^T \v x'|^{-\alpha} \, (\det R) \d ^n \v x' \\ ={}& \int e^{-i(R \v \lambda)\cdot \v x'} |\v x'|^{-\alpha} \d ^n \v x' \\ ={}& \hat f_\alpha(R\v \lambda). \end{align*} $$

Let $\v \lambda = |\v \lambda| \v m$ where $|\v m|=1$. We know $\hat f_\alpha(\v \lambda)$ will not depend on $\v m$. Now $$ \begin{align*} \hat f_\alpha(\v \lambda) = \int e^{-i\v m\cdot |\v \lambda| \v x} |\v x|^{-\alpha} \d ^n \v x. \end{align*} $$

Substitute $\v x' = |\v \lambda|\v x$ to get $$ \begin{align*} \hat f_\alpha(\v \lambda) ={}& \int e^{-i\v m\cdot \v x'} \left(\frac{|\v x'|}{|\v \lambda|}\right)^{-\alpha} |\v \lambda|^{-n} \d ^n \v x' \\ ={}& |\v \lambda|^{\alpha-n} \underbrace{\int e^{-i\v m\cdot \v x} |\v x|^{-\alpha} \d ^n \v x}_{C_{n,\alpha}}. \end{align*} $$
