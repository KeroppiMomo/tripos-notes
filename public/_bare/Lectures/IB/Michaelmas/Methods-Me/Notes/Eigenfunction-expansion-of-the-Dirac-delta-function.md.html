Let $L$ be a Sturm-Liouville operator on $V = \sb{y\in C^2[a,b]}{y \text{ satisfies real and homogenous boundary conditions at non-singular endpoints}}$. Further let $\set{Y_k}_{k=1}^\infty$ be the normalised eigenfunctions of $L$.

For $\xi\in (a,b)$, $V$ contains $\delta(x-\xi)$, so we can write it as a sum of eigenfunctions: $$ \begin{align*} \delta(x-\xi) = w(\xi) \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} Y_k(\xi) Y_k(x). \end{align*} $$
