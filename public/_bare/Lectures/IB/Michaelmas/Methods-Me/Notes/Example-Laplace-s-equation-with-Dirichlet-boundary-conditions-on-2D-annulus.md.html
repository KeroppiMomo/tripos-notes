Solve $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \Delta\phi = 0, &r_1 < r < r_2, \\ \phi = 0, &r=r_1, \\ \phi = f, &r=r_2. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

After separation of variables, we obtain $$ \begin{align*} \Theta_n(\theta) &= C_n \cos(n\theta) + D_n \sin(n\theta) \\ R_n(r) &= A_n r^n + \frac{B_n}{r^n}, \quad n\geq 1, \\ R_0(r) &= A + B\log r. \end{align*} $$ Applying $\phi=0$ on $r=r_1$ to $R_n$, we have, up to a constant factor, $$ \begin{align*} R_n(r) &\propto \left(\frac{r}{r_1}\right)^n - \left(\frac{r_1}{r}\right)^n, \\ R_0(r) &\propto \log \left(\frac{r}{r_1}\right). \end{align*} $$ Hence $$ \begin{align*} \phi(r,\theta) = C_0 \log\left(\frac{r}{r_1}\right) + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left[\left(\left(\frac{r}{r_1}\right)^n - \left(\frac{r_1}{r}\right)^n\right)\left(C_n\cos(n\theta) + D_n \sin(n\theta)\right)\right]. \end{align*} $$

To have $\phi=f$ when $r=r_2$, set the constants to be $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{2}a_0 &= C_0 \log \left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right) \\ a_n &= \left(\left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^n - \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^n\right) C_n \\ b_n &= \left(\left(\frac{r_2}{r_1}\right)^n - \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^n\right) D_n \\ \end{align*} $$ and then $$ \begin{align*} f(\theta) = \frac{1}{2}a_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (a_n\cos(n\theta) + b_n\sin(n\theta)). \end{align*} $$ Given that $f$ is nice enough, we have $$ \begin{align*} a_n &= \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(\theta) \cos(n\theta) \d \theta, \\ b_n &= \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(\theta) \sin(n\theta) \d \theta. \end{align*} $$
