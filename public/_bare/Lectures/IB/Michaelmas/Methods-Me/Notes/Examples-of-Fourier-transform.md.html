• Exponential: $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}[H(x) \, e^{-\sigma x}] = \dfrac{1}{\sigma + i\lambda}$ where $H$ is the Heaviside function and $\sigma\in \C$ where $\Re(\sigma) > 0$. (Derivation)

  • Gaussian: $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-x^2/2}\right] = e^{-\lambda^2/2}$. (Derivation)

  • Dirac delta: $\mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}[\delta(x)] = 1$. By the inversion formula, $\delta(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int e^{i\lambda x} \d \lambda$(!) (Derivation)
