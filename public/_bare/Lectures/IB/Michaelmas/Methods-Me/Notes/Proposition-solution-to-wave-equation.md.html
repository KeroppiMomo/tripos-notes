Consider the initial value problem of the wave equation $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} u_{tt} - c^2 \Delta u = F(\v x, t), &\R^n \times (0,\infty), \\ u(\v x, 0) = f(\v x), &\R^n, \\ u_t(\v x, 0) = g(\v x), &\R^n. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$

  • For zero forcing $F=0$, the solution is $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x,t) = \pdd{t}(\Phi_t * f)(\v x) + (\Phi_t * g)(\v x). \end{align*} $$

  • For zero initial conditions $f=g=0$, the solution is $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x, t) = \int_{0}^{t} \int \Phi_{t-s}(\v x-\v y) \, F(\v y,s) \d^n \v y \d s. \end{align*} $$ Equivalently, if we set $F(\v x,t) = 0$ for $t<0$, then $$ \begin{align*} u(\v x,t) = \iint G(\v x, t; \v y, s) \, F(\v y, s) \d^n \v y \d s \end{align*} $$ where $$ \begin{align*} G(\v x,t; \v y, s) = H(t-s)\, \Phi_{t-s}(\v x-\v y) \end{align*} $$ is the Green's function for the wave equation on $\R^n$.
