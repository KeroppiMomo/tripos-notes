Define the free space Green's function for the Laplacian on $\R^n$ as $$ \begin{align*} G(\v x; \v y) = \begin{cases} -\dfrac{1}{(n-2) |S^{n-1}|} \cdot \dfrac{1}{|\v x-\v y|^{n-2}}, &n>2, \\ \dfrac{1}{2\pi} \log |\v x-\v y|, &n=2 \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ where $|S^{n-1}|$ is the area of a unit $(n-1)$-sphere.

Then $G$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} \Delta G(\v x; \v y) = \delta(\v x-\v y). \end{align*} $$ Moreover for $n>2$, $G$ is the unique solution where $|G(\v x;\v y)| \to 0$ as $|\v x|\to \infty$.
