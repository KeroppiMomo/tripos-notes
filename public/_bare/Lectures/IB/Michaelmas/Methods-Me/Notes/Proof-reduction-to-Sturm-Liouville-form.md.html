Define $p,q,w,I$ as stated. Note that $I' = (\beta/\alpha) I$. Then we compute $$ \begin{align*} & -\dd{x}\left(p \dfd{y}{x}\right) + qy - \lambda wy \\ ={} & -py'' - p'y' + qy - \lambda wy \\ ={} & I\left(-y'' - \frac{\beta}{\alpha} y' - \frac{\gamma}{\alpha}y - \frac{\lambda}{\alpha}y\right) \\ ={} & \frac{I}{\alpha}(-\alpha y'' -\beta y' - \gamma y - \lambda y) &\alpha>0. \end{align*} $$ Since $I>0$, this equals 0 iff $ay'' + \beta y' + \gamma y + \lambda y = 0$.
