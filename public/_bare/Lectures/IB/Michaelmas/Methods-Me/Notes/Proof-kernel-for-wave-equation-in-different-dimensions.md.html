$n=1$

Note that $$ \begin{align*} H(x + ct) - H(x - ct) = \begin{cases} 0, & x<-ct, \\ 1, &-ct<x<ct, \\ 0, &x>ct. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ So $$ \begin{align*} \hat \Phi_t(\lambda) ={}& \frac{1}{2c} \int_{-ct}^{ct} e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \frac{1}{-2ic\lambda} (e^{-ict\lambda} - e^{ict\lambda}) \\ ={}&\frac{\sin(ct\lambda)}{c\lambda} \\ ={}& \frac{\sin(ct|\lambda|)}{c|\lambda|}. \end{align*} $$

$n=3$

Adopt spherical polars $(r,\theta,\phi)$ to compute the inverse Fourier transform. WLOG, align the $\lambda_3$ axis in the same direction as $\v x$ such that $\v x\cdot \v \lambda = |\v x||\v \lambda| \cos\theta$. $$ \begin{align*} \Phi_t(\v x) ={}& \frac{1}{(2\pi)^3} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{|\v \lambda|<R} e^{i\v \lambda\cdot \v x} \frac{\sin(ct|\v \lambda|)}{c|\v \lambda|} \d^3 \v \lambda \\ ={}& \frac{1}{8\pi^3} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{R} \int_{0}^{\pi} e^{ir|\v x|\cos \theta} \frac{\sin(ctr)}{cr} r^2 \sin \theta \d \theta \d r \d \phi \\ ={}& \frac{1}{4\pi^2} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{0}^{R} \frac{\sin(ctr)}{-ic|\v x|} \left[e^{ir|\v x|\cos\theta}\right]_0^\pi \d r \\ ={}& \frac{1}{2\pi^2 c|\v x|} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{0}^{R} \sin(ctr) \sin(r|\v x|) \d r \\ ={}& \frac{1}{4\pi^2 c|\v x|} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{-R}^{R} \sin(ctr) \sin(r|\v x|) \d r &&\text{even integrand} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{8\pi^2 c|\v x|} \lim_{R\to \infty} \int_{-R}^{R} \cos(r(|\v x| - ct)) - \cos(r(|\v x| + ct)) \d r \\ ={}& \frac{1}{8\pi^2 c|\v x|} \int e^{ir(|\v x|-ct)} - e^{ir(|\v x|+ct)} \d r &&\text{$\sin$ is odd} \\ ={}& \frac{1}{4\pi c|\v x|} \big(\delta(|\v x| - ct) - \delta(|\v x| + ct)\big) \end{align*} $$ where the last step uses the oscillatory integral $\delta(\lambda) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int e^{i\lambda x} \d x$.
