1. Integrate by parts. Boundary terms vanish, as $f$ is assumed to decay sufficiently quickly. $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[\Big(\dd{x}\Big)^k f(x)\right] ={}& \int f^{(k)}(x)\, e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& -(-i\lambda)\int f^{(k-1)}(x)\, e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \ldots \\ ={}& (i\lambda)^k \int f(x)\, e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& (i\lambda)^k \hat f(\lambda). \\ \left(i \dd{\lambda}\right)^k \hat f(\lambda) ={}& \left(i \dd{\lambda}\right)^k \int f(x) \, e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \int x^kf(x)\, e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[x^k f(x)\right]. \end{align*} $$

Integrate by substitution. $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}\left[f(x-a)\right] ={}& \int f(x-a)\,e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \int f(y) \, e^{-i\lambda(y+a)} \d y &y=x-a \\ ={}& e^{-i\lambda a} \int f(y) \, e^{-i\lambda y} \d y \\ ={}& e^{-i\lambda a} \hat f(\lambda). \\ \mathcal{F}_{x\mapsto \lambda}[e^{-iax} f(x)] ={}& \int f(x)\, e^{-iax} e^{-i\lambda x} \d x \\ ={}& \int f(x) \,e^{-i(\lambda+a)x} \d x \\ ={}& \hat f(\lambda + a). \end{align*} $$
