Consider the Green's function $G(x;\xi)$ for a second order linear differential operator $$ \begin{align*} L = \alpha \dd[2]{x} + \beta \dd{x} + \gamma \end{align*} $$ where $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ are smooth and $\alpha(x)\neq 0$ on $a<x<b$.

$\dfd{G}{x}$ looks like the Heaviside function $H(x-\xi)$ and has a jump discontinuity $$ \begin{align*} \dfd{G}{x}(\xi^+ ; \xi) - \dfd{G}{x}(\xi^-; \xi) = \frac{1}{\alpha(\xi)}. \end{align*} $$
