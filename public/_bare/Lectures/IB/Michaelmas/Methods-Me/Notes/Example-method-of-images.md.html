In $\R^3$, we want to find $\mathcal{G}(\v x; \v y)$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \Delta \mathcal{G}(\v x ; \v y) = \delta(\v x - \v y), &x_3 > 0, \\ \mathcal{G}(\v x; \v y) = 0, &x_3 = 0. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ Here $\Omega = \sb{\v x = (x_1, x_2, x_3)}{x_3>0}$.

Imagine $\mathcal{G}$ as the electric potential at $\v x$ due to a point charge at $\v y = (y_1, y_2, y_3)$. We want to add an image charge external to $\Omega$ to cancel the effect of the charge $\v y$ at $\partial \Omega$.

We choose to add a negative charge at $\v y_0 = (y_1, y_2, -y_3)\not\in \Omega$. Take $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{G}(\v x; \v y) = G(\v x; \v y) - G(\v x; \v y_0). \end{align*} $$

For all $\v x\in \Omega$, $\v x-\v y_0\neq \v 0$, so $$ \begin{align*} \Delta\mathcal{G}(\v x ; \v y) = \delta(\v x- \v y) - \underbrace{\delta(\v x - \v y_0)}_{0} = \delta(\v x- \v y). \end{align*} $$

Also on $\Omega$, $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{G}|_{x_3=0} ={}& -\frac{1}{4\pi}\bigg(\frac{1}{\sqrt{(x_1-y_1)^2 + (x_2-y_2)^2 + (0 - y_3)^2}} \\ & \qquad \qquad -\frac{1}{\sqrt{(x_1-y_1)^2 + (x_2-y_2)^2 + (0+y_3)^2}}\bigg) \\ ={}& 0. \end{align*} $$
