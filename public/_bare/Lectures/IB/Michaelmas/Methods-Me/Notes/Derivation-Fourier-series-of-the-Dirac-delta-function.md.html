The Fourier coefficients are $$ \begin{align*} \frac{1}{L} \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} \delta(x) e^{-2\pi i n x/L} \d x = \frac{1}{L}. \end{align*} $$

Let's check: $$ \begin{align*} f(0) ={}& \int_{-L/2}^{L/2} \delta^L(x) f(x) \d x \\ ={}& \sum_{n} \frac{1}{L}\int_{-L/2}^{L/2} f(x) e^{2\pi inx/L} \d x \\ ={}& \sum_{n} \hat f_{-n} \\ ={}& \sum_{n} \hat f_n \\ ={}& \sum_{n} \hat f_n e^{2\pi inx/L}\Big|_{x=0}. \end{align*} $$
