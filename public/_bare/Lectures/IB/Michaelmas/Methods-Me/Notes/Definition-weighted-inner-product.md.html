In the vector space $V$ of functions $f: [a,b]\to \C$, we can equip $V$ with an inner product $$ \begin{align*} \langle f,g\rangle_w = \int_{a}^{b} f(x)\overline{g(x)} w(x) \d x \end{align*} $$ where $w: (a,b)\to \R_{>0}$ is called the weight function.

We can also define the norm $$ \begin{align*} \|f\|_w = \sqrt{\langle f,f\rangle_w}. \end{align*} $$
