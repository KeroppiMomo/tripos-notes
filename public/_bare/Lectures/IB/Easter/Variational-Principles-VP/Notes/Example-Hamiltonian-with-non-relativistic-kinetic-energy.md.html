Take $$ \begin{align*} T = \frac{1}{2}m|\dv q|^2, \qquad V=V(\v q), \qquad L = T-V. \end{align*} $$ Need to solve $$ \begin{align*} p_i = \pdfd{L}{\dot q_i} = mq_i \implies \dv q = \frac{\v p}{m} \end{align*} $$ so $\v p$ is indeed the momentum. The Hamiltonian is $$ \begin{align*} H(\v q, \v p, t) &= \v p\cdot\dv q - L(\v q, \dv q, t) \\ &= \v p\cdot \frac{\v p}{m} - \frac{1}{2}\frac{|\v p|^2}{m} + V(\v q) \\ &= T + V \end{align*} $$ which is the total energy.
