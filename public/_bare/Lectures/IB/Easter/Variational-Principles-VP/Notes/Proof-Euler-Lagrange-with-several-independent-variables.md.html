Perturb $\phi(x_1, x_2 ,\ldots, x_n) \to \phi(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n) + \epsilon \eta(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)$ with $\eta = 0$ on $\partial V$.

$$ \begin{align*} F[\phi + \epsilon\eta] &= \iiint_V f(x_1,\ldots,x_n, \phi+\epsilon\eta, \phi_{x_1} + \epsilon\eta_{x_1}, \ldots, \phi_{x_n} + \epsilon\eta_{x_n})\,dV \\ &= F[\phi] + \epsilon\iiint_V \eta \pdfd{f}{\phi} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} \eta_{x_i} \pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\, dV + O(\epsilon^2) \\ &= F[\phi] + \epsilon\iiint_V \eta \pdfd{f}{\phi} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdd{x_i}\left(\eta \pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\right) - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \eta\pdd{x_i}\left(\pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\right)\, dV + O(\epsilon^2) \\ &= F[\phi] + \epsilon\iint_{\partial V} \underbrace{\eta}_{0} \pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\, dS + \epsilon\iiint_V \left(\pdfd{f}{\phi} - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdd{x_i}\left(\pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\right)\right)\eta\, dV + O(\epsilon^2) &&\text{divergence theorem} \\ &= F[\phi] + \epsilon\iiint_V \left(\pdfd{f}{\phi} - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdd{x_i}\left(\pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\right)\right)\eta\, dV + O(\epsilon^2) \end{align*} $$ so we require $$ \begin{align*} \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \pdd{x_i}\left(\pdfd{f}{\phi_{x_i}}\right)\right) - \pdfd{f}{\phi} = 0. \end{align*} $$
