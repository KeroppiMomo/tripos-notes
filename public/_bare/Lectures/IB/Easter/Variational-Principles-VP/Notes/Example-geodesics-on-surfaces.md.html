A geodesic is the shortest path on a surface $\Sigma$ between two points $\v A$ and $\v B$. Suppose $\Sigma \subset \R^3$ is given by $g(x,y,z)=0$.

Parametrise the curve by $s\in[0,1]$ with $\v x(s) = (x(s),y(s),z(s))$.

We want to minimise $\int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{(x'(s))^2 + (y'(s))^2 + (z'(s))^2}\, ds$ subject to $g(\v x(s)) = 0$.

We introduce the Lagrange multiplier $\lambda(s)$ as a function of $s$, since we want $g(\v x(s)) = 0$ to hold for all $s$: $$ \begin{align*} \Phi[\v x; \lambda] = \int_{0}^{1} \underbrace{ \sqrt{(x')^2 + (y')^2 + (z')^2} - \lambda(s) g(x,y,z)}_{f(s,x,y,z,x',y',z')}\, ds. \end{align*} $$

Apply Euler-Lagrange: $$ \begin{align*} \dd{s}\left(\pdfd{f}{x'}\right) - \pdfd{f}{x} &= 0 &&\iff & \dd{s}\left(\frac{x'}{\sqrt{(x')^2 + (y')^2 + (z')^2}}\right) - \lambda \pdfd{g}{x} &= 0 \\ \dd{s}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right) - \pdfd{f}{y} &= 0 &&\iff & \dd{s}\left(\frac{y'}{\sqrt{(x')^2 + (y')^2 + (z')^2}}\right) - \lambda \pdfd{g}{y} &= 0 \\ \dd{s}\left(\pdfd{f}{z'}\right) - \pdfd{f}{z} &= 0 &&\iff & \dd{s}\left(\frac{z'}{\sqrt{(x')^2 + (y')^2 + (z')^2}}\right) - \lambda \pdfd{g}{z} &= 0 \\ \end{align*} $$
