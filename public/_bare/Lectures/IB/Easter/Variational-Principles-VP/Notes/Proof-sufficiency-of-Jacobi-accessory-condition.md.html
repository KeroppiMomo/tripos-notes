We use two algebraic tricks.

Let $\eta$ be a pertubation. Note that for any (smooth) function $w(x)$, since $\eta(\alpha)=\eta(\beta)=0$, $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} (\phi\eta^2)' dx = \left[\phi\eta^2\right]_{\alpha}^{\beta} = 0, \end{align*} $$ so we can add that to our second variation and complete the square: $$ \begin{align*} \delta^2 F &= \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \left(P(\eta')^2 + Q\eta^2 + (\phi\eta^2)'\right)\, dx \\ &= \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \left(P(\eta')^2 + Q\eta^2 + \phi'\eta^2 + 2\phi\eta\eta'\right) \, dx \\ &= \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \left(P\left(\eta' + \frac{\phi}{P}\eta\right)^2 + \left(Q + \phi' - \frac{\phi^2}{P}\right)\eta^2\right)\, dx. \end{align*} $$

If we can choose some $\phi$ satisfying $$ \begin{align*} Q + \phi' - \frac{\phi^2}{P} = 0, \end{align*} $$ then $\delta^2 F\geq 0$ for any $\eta$.

Substitute, for $u\neq 0$ anywhere in $[\alpha,\beta]$, $$ \begin{align*} \phi = -\frac{Pu'}{u}, \end{align*} $$ to give $$ \begin{align*} 0 &= Q - \frac{(Pu')'}{u} + \frac{P(u')^2}{u^2} - \frac{P(u')^2}{u^2} \\ 0 &= (Pu')' - Qu. \end{align*} $$

If there is a solution to $u$ with $u\neq 0$ anywhere in $[\alpha,\beta]$, then we can construct $\phi$ and deduce $\delta^2 F\geq 0$.
