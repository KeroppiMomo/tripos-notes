We want to extremise $$ \begin{align*} F[y] = \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} f(x,y,y',y'',\ldots,y^{(n)})\, dx \end{align*} $$ subject to $y,y',\ldots,y^{(n-1)}$ being fixed at $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

The necessary condition for an extremum is $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd{f}{y} - \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right) + \dd[2]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y''}\right) + \ldots+(-1)^n \dd[n]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y^{(n)}}\right) \equiv \sum_{i=0}^{n} (-1)^i\dd[i]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y^{(i)}}\right) = 0 \end{align*} $$

Proof

If $f$ does not depend on $y$, we have a first integral $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd{f}{y'} - \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y''}\right) + \ldots + (-1)^{n-1} \dd[n-1]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y^{(n)}}\right) = \text{const.} \end{align*} $$
