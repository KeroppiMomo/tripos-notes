Consider central force (again). Take $$ \begin{align*} L = \frac{1}{2}m(\dot r^2 + r^2\dot\theta^2) - V(r), \qquad (\Theta, R) = (\theta+s, r). \end{align*} $$ We have rotational invariance $dL/ds = 0$, so by Noether's theorem, $$ \begin{align*} \text{const.} = \pdfd{L}{\dot r} \dfd{R}{s} + \pdfd{L}{\dot\theta} \dfd{\Theta}{s} = mr^2\dot\theta \end{align*} $$ which is the conservation of angular momentum.
