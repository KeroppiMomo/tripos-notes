We follow the proof of the regular Euler-Lagrange equation. Perturb $y_i \to y_i + \epsilon \eta_i$ for each $i$, $$ \begin{align*} F[\v y + \epsilon \v \eta] &= F[\v y] + \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \sum_{i=1}^n \left(\epsilon\eta_i \pdfd{f}{y_i} + \epsilon\eta_i' \pdfd{f}{y_i'}\right) \,dx + O(\epsilon^2) \\ &= F[\v y] + \epsilon \sum_{i=1}^{n} \Big[\underbrace{ \eta_i }_{0}\pdfd{f}{y_i'}\Big]_{\alpha}^{\beta} + \epsilon \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(\pdfd{f}{y_i} - \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y_i'}\right)\right)\eta_i\, dx + O(\epsilon^2) \\ \end{align*} $$ Since $\eta_i$ is free to choose, we require $$ \begin{align*} \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y_i'}\right) - \pdfd{f}{y_i} = 0, \quad \forall i=1,2,\ldots,n. \end{align*} $$
