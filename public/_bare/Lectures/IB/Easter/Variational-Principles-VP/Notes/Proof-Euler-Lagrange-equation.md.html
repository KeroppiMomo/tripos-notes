Consider a small pertubation $y\to y+\epsilon\eta(x)$, where $\eta(x_1) = \eta(x_2) = 0$. Computing $F[y+\epsilon\eta]$: $$ \begin{align*} F[y+\epsilon\eta] &= \int_{x_1}^{x_2} f(x,y+\epsilon\eta, y'+\epsilon\eta')\, dx \\ &= \int_{x_1}^{x_2} \left(f(x,y,y') + \pdfd{f}{y}\epsilon\eta + \pdfd{f}{y'} \epsilon\eta' + O(\epsilon^2)\right) \, dx &&\text{Taylor expansion}\\ &= F[y] + \epsilon \left(\int_{x_1}^{x_2} \pdfd{f}{y}\eta\, dx + \int_{x_1}^{x_2} \pdfd{f}{y'}\eta'\, dx\right) + O(\epsilon^2) \\ &= F[y] + \epsilon \bigg(\int_{x_1}^{x_2} \pdfd{f}{y}\eta\, dx + \left[\pdfd{f}{y'} \eta\right]_{x_1}^{x_2} - \int_{x_1}^{x_2} \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right)\eta\, dx\bigg) + O(\epsilon^2) && \text{by parts} \\ &= F[y] + \epsilon\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\underbrace{ \left(\pdfd{f}{y} + \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right)\right)}_{(*)}\eta \, dx + O(\epsilon^2) &&\eta(x_1) = \eta(x_2) = 0. \end{align*} $$

For $y$ to extremise $F[y]$, the coefficient of $\epsilon$ must be zero for any pertubation $\eta$.

Therefore we must have $(*) = 0$.
