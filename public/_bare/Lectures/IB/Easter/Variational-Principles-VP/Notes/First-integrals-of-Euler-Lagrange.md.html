The Euler-Lagrange equation is typically a second-order ODE in $y$.

  1. If $f$ does not depend explicitly on $y$, i.e. $f(x,y')$, then Euler-Lagrange gives $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd{f}{y'} = \text{const}. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

  2. If $f$ does not depend explicitly on $x$, i.e. $f(y,y')$, then Euler-Lagrange gives $$ \begin{align*} f - y'\pdfd{f}{y'} = \text{const}. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)
