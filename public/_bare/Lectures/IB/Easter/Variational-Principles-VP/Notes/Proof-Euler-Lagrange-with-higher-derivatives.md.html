Perturb $y\to y+\epsilon\eta$ with $\eta,\eta',\ldots,\eta^{(n-1)} = 0$ at ends. $$ \begin{align*} F[y+\epsilon\eta] &= F[y] + \epsilon \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \pdfd{f}{y}\eta + \underbrace{ \pdfd{f}{y'}\eta'}_\text{by parts once} + \underbrace{ \pdfd{f}{y''}\eta''}_\text{twice} + \ldots + \underbrace{ \pdfd{f}{y^{(n)}} \eta^{(n)}}_\text{$n$ times}\, dx + O(\epsilon^2) \\ &= F[y] + \epsilon \int_{\alpha}^{\beta} \left(\pdfd{f}{y} - \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right) + \dd[2]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y''}\right) + \ldots + (-1)^n \dd[n]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y^{(n)}}\right)\right)\eta\,dx. \end{align*} $$ All boundary terms vanish as $\eta,\eta',\ldots,\eta^{(n-1)}$ all vanish at ends. This yields $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd{f}{y} - \dd{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y'}\right) + \dd[2]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y''}\right) + \ldots + (-1)^n \dd[n]{x}\left(\pdfd{f}{y^{(n)}}\right) = 0. \end{align*} $$
