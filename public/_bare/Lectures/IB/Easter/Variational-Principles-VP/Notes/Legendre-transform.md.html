Definition (Legendre transform) open_in_new

The Legendre transform of $f(\v x): \R^n\to\R$ is defined as $$ \begin{align*} f^\ast(\v p) = \sup_{\v x} \left[\v p\cdot \v x - f(\v x)\right] \end{align*} $$ whose domain is all $\v p\in\R^n$ for which the right hand side is finite.

Theorem (Legendre transform is convex) open_in_new

Let $f(\v x): \R^n\to\R$ be a function and $f^\ast$ be its Legendre transform.

The domain of $f^\ast$ is a convex set, and $f^\ast$ is a convex function.

Theorem (Legendre transform is involutive) open_in_new

If $f$ is convex, then $f^{\ast \ast} = f$.

Lemma (Legendre transform of convex and differentiable function) open_in_new
