A system of many particles is modelled by:

  • intensive variables: pressure $P$, temperature $T$;

  • extensive variables: entropy $S$, temperature $V$.

Different versions of energy:

  • Internal energy $U(S,V)$;

  • Helmhotz free energy $F(T,V)$: performing Legendre transform $S\to T$ $$ \begin{align*} F(T,V) = \min_{S} \left[U(S,V) - TS\right] = -U^\ast(T,V) \end{align*} $$ and the minimiser $S$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} T = \left.\pdfd{U}{S}\right|_V; \end{align*} $$

  • Enthalpy $H(S,P)$: performing Legendre transform $V \to P$ $$ \begin{align*} H(S,P) = \min_{V} \left[U(S,V) + PV\right] = -U^\ast(S, -P) \end{align*} $$ and the minimiser $V$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} P = -\left.\pdfd{U}{V}\right|_S; \end{align*} $$

  • Gibbs free energy $G(T,P)$: performing both Legendre transforms $$ \begin{align*} G(T,P) = \min_{S,V}\left[U(S,V) + PV - TS\right]. \end{align*} $$
