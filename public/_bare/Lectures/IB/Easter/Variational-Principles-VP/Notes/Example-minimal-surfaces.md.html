Minimise the area of a surface $\Sigma\subset \R^3$ subject to boundary conditions, e.g. soap film between two loops of wire.

Suppose $\Sigma$ can be written as $(x,y,z)$ where $z=\phi(x,y)$. (This might be possible locally on most of the surface, so restrict to some region $\mathcal{D}$ of $(x,y)$ where this is true.)

The area is $$ \begin{align*} A[\phi] = \iint_\mathcal{D} \left|\pdfd{\v x}{x}\times \pdfd{\v x}{y} \right| \,dx\,dy = \iint_D \underbrace{ \sqrt{1 + \phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2}}_{h(x,y,\phi,\phi_x, \phi_y)}\,dx\,dy. \end{align*} $$ Apply Euler-Lagrange to $h$: $$ \begin{align*} 0 &= \pdd{x}\left(\pdfd{h}{\phi_x}\right) + \pdd{y}\left(\pdfd{h}{\phi_y}\right) - \pdfd{h}{\phi} \\ &= \pdd{x}\left(\frac{\phi_x}{\sqrt{1+\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2}}\right) + \pdd{y}\left(\frac{\phi_y}{\sqrt{1+\phi_x^2+\phi_y^2}}\right) - 0 \\ &= \frac{\phi_{xx}}{\sqrt{1+\phi_x^2+\phi_y^2}} - \frac{\phi_x(\phi_x\phi_{xx} + \phi_y \phi_{xy})}{(1+\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2)^{3/2}} + \frac{\phi_{yy}}{\sqrt{1+\phi_x^2+\phi_y^2}} - \frac{\phi_y(\phi_x\phi_{xy} + \phi_y \phi_{yy})}{(1+\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2)^{3/2}} \\ 0 &= \phi_{xx}(1+\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2) - \phi_x^2 \phi_{xx} - \phi_x \phi_y \phi_{xy} + \phi_{yy}(1+\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2) - \phi_x\phi_y\phi_{xy} - \phi_y^2\phi_{yy} \\ \end{align*} $$ and we get the minimal surface equation $$ \begin{align*} 0 = (1+\phi_y^2)\phi_{xx} + (1+\phi_x^2) \phi_{yy} - 2\phi_x\phi_y\phi_{xy}. \end{align*} $$

Let us assume rotational symmetry, i.e. $\phi=\phi(r)$ where $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. Using $$\phi_x = \phi' \pdfd{r}{x} = \frac{x}{r}\phi', \qquad \phi_y = \frac{y}{r}\phi',$$ $$\phi_{xx} = \frac{1}{r}\phi' + \frac{x^2}{r^2}\phi'' - \frac{x^2}{r^3}\phi' = \frac{y^2}{r^3}\phi' + \frac{x^2}{r^2}\phi'', \qquad \phi_{yy} =\frac{x^2}{r^3}\phi' + \frac{y^2}{r^2}\phi'',$$ $$\phi_{xy} = \frac{xy}{r^2}\phi'' - \frac{xy}{r^3}\phi',$$ the equation becomes $$ \begin{align*} 0 &= \left(1+\frac{y^2}{r^2}(\phi')^2\right)\left(\frac{y^2}{r^3}\phi' + \frac{x^2}{r^2}\phi''\right) + \left(1+\frac{x^2}{r^2}(\phi')^2\right)\left(\frac{x^2}{r^3}\phi' + \frac{y^2}{r^2}\phi''\right) - 2\frac{xy}{r^2}(\phi')^2\left(\frac{xy}{r^2}\phi'' - \frac{xy}{r^3}\phi'\right) \\ &= \frac{x^2+y^2}{r^2}\phi'' + \frac{x^2+y^2}{r^3} \phi' + \frac{y^4 + x^4 + 2x^2y^2}{r^5}(\phi')^3 \\ &= \phi'' + \frac{1}{r}\phi' + \frac{1}{r}(\phi')^3 \end{align*} $$ and finally we get $$ \begin{align*} r\phi'' + \phi' + (\phi')^3 = 0. \end{align*} $$

An easier method is that we can just assume rotational symmetry from the start: set $r=r(z)$. Then $$ \begin{align*} A[r] = 2\pi\int \underbrace{ r\sqrt{1+(r')^2}}_{f(z,r,r')}\,dz. \end{align*} $$ $f$ does not depend on $z$, so using the first integral, $$ \begin{align*} A &= f - r'\pdfd{f}{r'} \\ &= r\sqrt{1+(r')^2} - \frac{r(r')^2}{\sqrt{1+(r')^2}} \\ &= \frac{r}{\sqrt{1+(r')^2}} \\ 1+(r')^2 &= \frac{r^2}{A^2} \\ r' &= \pm\sqrt{\frac{r^2}{A^2} - 1} \\ z &= \pm\int \frac{dr}{\sqrt{\frac{r^2}{A^2} - 1}} \\ &= A\cosh\inv\left(\frac{r}{A}\right) + z_0 \\ r(z) &= A\cosh\left(\frac{z-z_0}{A}\right). \end{align*} $$

Suppose the boundary is two circles with radius $R$ at $z=\pm 1$. By symmetry $z_0 = 0$, and we need to set $A$ so that $$ \begin{align*} R = A\cosh \frac{1}{A}. \end{align*} $$

  • If $R<R^\ast\approx 1.509$, there are no solutions.

  • If $R>R^\ast$, there are two solutions. It turns out the solution with a smaller $A$ is not stable.

If we slowly let $R$ shrink (or equivalently pull the rings further apart), the solutions becomes less and less stable, and around $R=R^\ast$ it will collapse.
