Denote $\mathcal{X}(\v c)$ as here. It suffices to show that the value function $\phi(\v c) = \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v c)} f(\v c)$ is convex. Then there exists a supporting hyperplane at $\v b$, so strong duality holds.

Let $t\in[0,1]$ and $\v c,\v d$ be such that $\mathcal{X}(\v c),\mathcal{X}(\v d)\neq\emptyset$. To show that $\phi$ is convex, we need to show that $\phi(t\v c + (1-t)\v d) \leq t\phi(\v c) + (1-t)\phi(\v d)$, i.e. $$ \begin{align*} \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(t\v c + (1-t)\v d)} f(\v x) \leq t \left(\min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v c)} f(\v x)\right) + (1-t)\left(\min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v d)} f(\v x)\right). \end{align*} $$

For any $\v y\in\mathcal{X}(\v c)$ and $\v z\in\mathcal{X}(\v d)$, $$ \begin{align*} &&h_j(t\v y + (1-t)\v z) &\leq t\, h_j(\v y) + (1-t)h_j(\v z) &&\text{convexity of $h_j$} \\ && &\leq tc_j + (1-t) d_j &&\v y\in\mathcal{X}(\v c), \v z\in\mathcal{X}(\v d) \\ \end{align*} $$ so $h(t\v y + (1-t)\v z) \leq t\v c + (1-t)\v d$. Hence $t\v y + (1-t)\v z \in \mathcal{X}(t\v c + (1-t)\v d)$.

Therefore we have $$ \begin{align*} \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(t\v c + (1-t)\v d)} f(\v x)&\leq f(t\v y + (1-t)\v z) \\ &\leq tf(\v y) + (1-t)f(\v z) &&\text{convexity of $f$}. \end{align*} $$ Taking the minimum over $\v y$ and $\v z$, $$ \begin{align*} \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(t\v c + (1-t)\v d)} f(\v x) &\leq t\left(\min_{\v y\in\mathcal{X}(\v c)} f(\v y)\right) + (1-t)\left(\min_{\v z\in\mathcal{X}(\v d)} f(\v z)\right) \end{align*} $$ as required.
