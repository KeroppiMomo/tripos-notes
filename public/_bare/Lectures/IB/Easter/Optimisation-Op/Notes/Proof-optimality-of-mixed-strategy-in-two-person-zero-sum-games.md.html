Both players' problems are linear programs and are dual of each other.

Linear programs satisfy strong duality, which implies the existence of a dual feasible $\v q$.

Moreover we use the fact that a primal feasible solution and a dual feasible solution are optimal iff complementary slackness holds. In this problem, $(p,v)$ is primal optimal and $(q,w)$ is dual optimal iff $$ \begin{align*} &\v p^T (A\v q - w \v e) = 0 \quad \text{and}\quad (A^T \v p - v \v e)^T \v q = 0 \\ \iff & v \v e^T \v q = \v p^T A \v q = w \v e^T \v p \\ \iff & v=w=\v p^T A\v q \qquad\text{since $\v p$ and $\v q$ are feasible}. \end{align*} $$
