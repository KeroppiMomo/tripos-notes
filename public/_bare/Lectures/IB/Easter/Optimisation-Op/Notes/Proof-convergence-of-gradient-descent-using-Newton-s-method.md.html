Let $\v \Delta_k = \v x_{k+1} - \v x_k$. Using Newton's update rule, $$ \begin{align*} \nabla f(\v x_k) = -[\nabla^2 f(\v x_k)](\v x_{k+1} - \v x_{k}) = -[\nabla^2 f(\v x_k)] \v \Delta_k. \end{align*} $$ We have $$ \begin{align*} \nabla f(\v x_{k+1}) &= \nabla f(\v x_{k+1}) - \nabla f(\v x_k) + \nabla f(\v x_k) \\ &= \nabla f(\v x_{k+1}) - \nabla f(\v x_k) - \nabla^2 f(\v x_k) \v \Delta_k \\ &= \int_{0}^{1} \big(\nabla^2 f(\v x_k + t\v \Delta_k) - \nabla^2 f(\v x_k)\big) \v \Delta_k\, dt. \end{align*} $$ Taking norm on both sides, $$ \begin{align*} |\nabla f(\v x_{k+1})| &\leq \int_{0}^{1} |\big(\nabla^2 f(\v x_k + t\v \Delta_k) - \nabla^2 f(\v x_k)\big) \v \Delta_k|\, dt &&\text{triangle inequality} \\ &\leq \int_{0}^{1} \|\nabla^2 f(\v x_k + t\Delta_k) - \nabla^2 f(\v x_k)\|\, |\v \Delta_k|\, dt &&\text{definition of $\|\cdot\|$}\\ &\leq \int_{0}^{1} lt|\v \Delta_k|^2\, dt &&\text{$\nabla^2 f$ is $l$-Lipschitz} \\ &= \frac{l}{2} |\v \Delta_k|^2 \\ &= \frac{l}{2} \big|(\nabla^2 f(\v x_k))\inv \nabla f(\v x_k)\big|^2 \\ &\leq \frac{l}{2} \|(\nabla^2 f(\v x_k))\inv\|^2 \,|\nabla f(\v x_k)|^2 &&\text{definition of $\|\cdot\|$} \\ &= \frac{l}{2\alpha^2} |\nabla f(\v x_k)|^2 &&\text{$f$ is $\alpha$-strongly convex}. \end{align*} $$

By induction $$ \begin{align*} |\nabla f(\v x_k)| \leq \frac{2\alpha^2}{l}\left(\frac{l}{2\alpha^2}|\nabla f(\v x_0)|\right)^{2^k}. \end{align*} $$

Then using bound on minimum value, $$ \begin{align*} f(\v x_k) - f(\v x^\ast) \leq \frac{1}{2\alpha}|\nabla f(\v x_k)|^2 \leq \frac{2\alpha^3}{l^2}\left(\frac{l}{2\alpha^2}|\nabla f(\v x_0)|\right)^{2^{k+1}}. \end{align*} $$
