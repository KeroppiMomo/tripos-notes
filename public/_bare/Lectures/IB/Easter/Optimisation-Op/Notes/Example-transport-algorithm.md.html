So we obtain the following equations: $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \lambda_1 + \mu_1 = 5, \\ \lambda_1 + \mu_2 = 3, \\ \lambda_2 + \mu_2 = 7, \\ \lambda_2 + \mu_3 = 4, \\ \lambda_3 + \mu_3 = 2, \\ \lambda_3 + \mu_4 = 4 \end{cases} \implies \begin{cases} \lambda_1 = 0, \\ \lambda_2 = 4, \\ \lambda_3 = 2, \\ \mu_1 = 5, \\ \mu_2 = 3, \\ \mu_3 = 0, \\ \mu_4 = 2. \end{cases} \end{align*} $$
