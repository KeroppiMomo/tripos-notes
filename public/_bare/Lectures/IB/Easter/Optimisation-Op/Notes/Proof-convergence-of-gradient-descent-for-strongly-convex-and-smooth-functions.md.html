$$ \begin{align*} f(\v x_{t+1}) - f(\v x^\ast) &= f\Big(\v x_{t} - \frac{1}{\beta}\nabla f(\v x_t)\Big) - f(\v x^\ast) \tag{1}\\ &\leq f(\v x_t) - f(\v x^\ast) - \frac{1}{2\beta}|\nabla f(\v x_t)|^2 \tag{2} \\ &\leq f(\v x_t) - f(\v x^\ast) - \frac{2\alpha}{2\beta}\big(f(\v x_t) - f(\v x^\ast)\big)^2 \tag{3} \\ &= \left(1-\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\right)(f(\v x_t) - f(\v x^\ast)) \end{align*} $$ then the result follows from induction.
