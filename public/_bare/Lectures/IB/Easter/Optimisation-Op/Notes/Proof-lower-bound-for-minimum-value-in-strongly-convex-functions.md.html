Using the bound on $\alpha$-strongly convex functions, $$ \begin{align*} f(\v y) \geq f(\v x) + \nabla f(\v x)^T (\v y - \v x) + \frac{\alpha}{2}|\v y-\v x|^2. \end{align*} $$ Take infimum on $\v y$ on both sides, $$ \begin{align*} p^\ast \geq f(\v x) + \inf_\v y \left[\nabla f(\v x)^T (\v y - \v x) + \frac{\alpha}{2}|\v y-\v x|^2\right]. \end{align*} $$

To minimise the right hand side, set derivative to zero: $$ \begin{align*} 0 = \nabla f(\v x) + \alpha(\v y - \v x) \implies \v y = \v x - \frac{\nabla f(\v x)}{\alpha}. \end{align*} $$ Substituting this $\v y$ gives the result $$ \begin{align*} p^\ast \geq f(\v x) - \frac{1}{2\alpha} |\nabla f(\v x)|^2. \end{align*} $$
