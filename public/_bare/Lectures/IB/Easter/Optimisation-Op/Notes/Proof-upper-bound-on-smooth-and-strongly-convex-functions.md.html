Use Taylor's expansion: $$ \begin{align*} f(\v y) = f(\v x) + \nabla f(\v x)^T (\v y-\v x) + \frac{1}{2}(\v y-\v x)^T \nabla^2f(\v z)(\v y-\v x) \end{align*} $$ where $\v z$ is a point between $\v x$ and $\v y$.

Using the $\alpha$-strongly convex or $\beta$-smooth condition on the function, $$ \begin{align*} (\v y-\v x)^T(\alpha I)(\v y-\v x) \leq (\v y - \v x)^T \nabla^2 f(\v z) (\v y-\v x) \leq (\v y-\v x)^T (\beta I)(\v y - \v x), \end{align*} $$ which gives the result.
