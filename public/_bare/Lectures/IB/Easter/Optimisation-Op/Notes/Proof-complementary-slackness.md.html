$-\lambda_i s_i$ is one of the terms in $L(\v x,\v s,\v \lambda)$ and $s_i\geq 0$.

Firstly we must have $\lambda_i\leq 0$ for $\v \lambda\in\Lambda$, as otherwise we can let $s_i\to\infty$ to drive $L(\v x,\v s,\v \lambda)\to-\infty$.

Then because $\lambda_i\leq 0$ and $s_i\geq 0$, we have $-\lambda_i s_i\geq 0$. Taking $\lambda_i = s_i = 0$ gives a minimum of $\lambda_i s_i$ of 0. Hence the minimiser $\v s^\ast$ satisfies $\lambda_i s^\ast_i = 0$.

Therefore complementary slackness follows.
