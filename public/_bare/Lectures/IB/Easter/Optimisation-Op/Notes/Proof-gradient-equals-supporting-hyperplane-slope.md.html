Fix $\v a\in\R^m$ and $t>0$. Since $\phi$ has a supporting hyperplane $\v \lambda$ at $\v b$, $$ \begin{align*} \phi(\v b + t\v a) &\geq \phi(\v b) + \v \lambda^T(t\v a) \\ \frac{\phi(\v b+t\v a) - \phi(\v b)}{t} &\geq \v \lambda^T\v a. \end{align*} $$ As $\phi$ is differentiable at $\v b$, taking the limit as $t\to 0$ gives $$ \begin{align*} \nabla \phi(\v b)^T \v a &\geq \v \lambda^T \v a\\ (\nabla\phi(\v b) - \v \lambda)^T \v a &\geq 0. \end{align*} $$ Take $\v a = -(\nabla\phi(\v b) - \v \lambda)$ and we obtain $\nabla\phi(\v b) = \v \lambda$.
