This is the same idea as the proof for Lagrange sufficiency theorem.

Let $\v \lambda\in\Lambda$ and $\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)$. $$ \begin{align*} f(\v x) &\geq \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)} f(\v x) &&\text{definition of $\min$}\\ &= \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)} \left[f(\v x) - \v \lambda^T(h(\v x) - \v b)\right] &&h(\v x) = \v b \text{ since } \v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b) \\ &\geq \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}} \left[f(\v x) - \v \lambda^T (h(\v x) - \v b)\right] &&\mathcal{X}(\v b) \subseteq \mathcal{X} \\ &= g(\v \lambda). \end{align*} $$
