Let $\v x\in C$. If $\v x = t\v y +(1-t)\v z$ where $t\in(0,1), \v y,\v z\in C$, then by the convexity of $f$, $$ \begin{align*} f(\v x) \leq t f(\v y) + (1-t) f(\v z) \leq \max\set{f(\v y), f(\v z)} \end{align*} $$ so the only points we need to consider are those that cannot be expressed as a convex combination, which are the extreme points.
