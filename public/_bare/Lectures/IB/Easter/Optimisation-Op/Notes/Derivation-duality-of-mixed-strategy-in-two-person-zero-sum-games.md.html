Consider the Lagrangian of P2's problem $$ \begin{align*} L(w,\v q,\v s,\v \lambda_1,\lambda_2) &= w + \v\lambda_1^T(A\v q + \v s - w\v e) - \lambda_2(\v e^T \v q - 1) \\ &=(1-\v \lambda_1^T \v e) w + (A^T \v \lambda_1 - \lambda_2 \v e)^T \v q + \v \lambda_1^T \v s + \lambda_2. \end{align*} $$ For $L$ to have a finite minimum over $w\in\R$, $\v q\in\R^n$ and $\v s\in\R^m$ with $\v s\geq \v 0$ and $\v q\geq \v 0$, we need $\v \lambda_1^T \v e = 1$, $A^T \v \lambda_1 - \lambda_2 \v e \geq \v 0$ and $\v \lambda_1\geq \v 0$. When these conditions are satisfied, the minimum of $L$ is $\lambda_2$ when $w,\v q,\v s$ are all zero. So the dual problem is

Problem

$$ \begin{align*} \text{Maximise }& \lambda_2 \\ \text{subject to }& A^T \v \lambda_1 \geq \lambda_2\v e, \\ & \v \lambda_1^T \v e = 1, \\ & \v \lambda_1 \geq \v 0, \end{align*} $$

which is P1's problem.
