Since $f_i$ are linear, let $$ \begin{align*} f_i(\v x) = \v a_i^T \v x + b. \end{align*} $$

We compute the Hessian of $\Phi(\v x)$: $$ \begin{align*} \nabla \Phi(\v x) &= -\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{-\nabla f_i(\v x)}{-f_i(\v x)} \\ &= -\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{\nabla f_i(\v x)}{f_i(\v x)}, \\ \nabla^2 \Phi(\v x) &= -\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{f_i(\v x) \nabla^2 f_i(\v x) - \nabla f_i(\v x)\, \nabla f_i(\v x)^T}{f_i(\v x)^2} \\ &= -\sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{f_i(\v x)\, 0 - \v a_i \v a_i^T}{f_i(\v x)^2} \\ &= \sum_{i=1}^{m} \frac{\v a_i \v a_i^T}{f_i(\v x)^2} \end{align*} $$ which is positive semidefinite as $\v v^T \v a_i \v a_i^T \v v = |\v a_i^T \v v|^2 \geq 0$.

As $f$ is convex and $t>0$, we have $t f(\v x) + \Phi(\v x)$ being convex.
