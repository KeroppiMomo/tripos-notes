Let $\v x^\ast$ be a minimiser of the Lagrangian $L(\v x, \v \lambda^\ast)$ for some $\v \lambda^\ast$ and is feasible i.e. $h(\v x) = \v b$.

By the definition of $\min$ $$ \begin{align*} f(\v x^\ast) \geq \min_{\v x\in\mathcal{X}(b)} f(\v x). \end{align*} $$

But also, $$ \begin{align*} \min_{x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)} f(\v x) &= \min_{x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)} \left(f(\v x) - (\lambda^\ast)^T(h(\v x) - \v b)\right) &&\text{since } h(\v x) - \v b = \v 0\quad\forall \v x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b) \\ &\geq \min_{x\in\mathcal{X}} \left(f(\v x) - (\v \lambda^\ast)^T(h(\v x) - \v b)\right) &&\text{since } \mathcal{X}(\v b) \subseteq \mathcal{X}\\ &= \min_{x\in\mathcal{X}} L(\v x,\v \lambda^\ast) \\ &= L(\v x^\ast,\lambda^\ast) \\ &= f(\v x) - (\v \lambda^\ast)^T \underbrace{(h(\v x^\ast) - \v b)}_{\v 0} \\ &=f(\v x) \end{align*} $$ so $f(\v x^\ast) = \min_{x\in\mathcal{X}(\v b)} f(\v x)$.
