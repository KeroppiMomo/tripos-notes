For any sets $X,Y\subseteq V$ (not necessarily disjoint), define $$ \begin{align*} f_x(X,Y) = \sum_{(i,j)\in E:\ i\in X,\ j\in Y} x_{ij}. \end{align*} $$

Let $S\subseteq V$ satisfy $1\in S$ and $n\in V\setminus S$. $$ \begin{align*} \delta &= \sum_{i\in S} \left(\sum_{j:\ (i,j)\in E} x_{ij} - \sum_{j:\ (j,i)\in E} x_{ji}\right) &&\text{$x$ is feasible}\\ &= f_x(S,V) - f_x(V,S) \\ &= \left(f_x(S,V\setminus S) + f_x(S,S)\right) - \left(f_x(S,S) + f_x(V\setminus S, S)\right) \\ &= f_x(S, V\setminus S) - f_x(V\setminus S, S)\\ &\leq f_x(S,V\setminus S) &&x\geq 0\\ &\leq c(S) &&x_{ij} \leq c_{ij}. \end{align*} $$
