Problem

$$ \begin{align*} \text{Minimise } & \v c^T \v x \\ \text{subject to } & A\v x = \v b, \\ &\v x \geq \v 0. \end{align*} $$

The Lagrangian is $$ \begin{align*} L(\v x, \v s, \v \lambda) &= \v c^T\v x - \v \lambda^T(A\v x - \v b) \\ &= (\v c - A^T \v \lambda)^T \v x + \v \lambda^T \v b. \end{align*} $$ If $(A^T \v \lambda)_i < c_i$, then we can set $x_i \to\infty$ to drive $L\to-\infty$. Hence $$ \begin{align*} \Lambda \equiv \sb{\v \lambda}{\min_{\v x\geq \v 0} L(\v x,\v \lambda) > -\infty} = \sb{\v \lambda}{A^T \v \lambda \leq \v c}. \end{align*} $$ If $\v \lambda\in\Lambda$, then $$ \begin{align*} g(\v \lambda) = \min_{\v x\geq 0} L(\v x,\v \lambda) = \v \lambda^T\v b. \end{align*} $$ Therefore the dual problem is

Problem

$$ \begin{align*} \text{Maximise } & \v b^T \v \lambda \\ \text{subject to } & A^T \v \lambda \leq \v c. \end{align*} $$
