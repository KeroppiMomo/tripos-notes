Applying small angle approximation, $$ \begin{align*} \sin\theta_1 \approx \tan\theta_1 = \frac{y}{u}, \quad \sin\theta_2 \approx \tan\theta_2 = \frac{y}{-v}. \end{align*} $$ By Snell's law, $$ \begin{align*} \frac{n_1 y}{u} = \frac{n_2 y}{-v} \implies \frac{-v}{u} = \frac{n_2}{n_1}. \end{align*} $$
