Superposition of two waves arriving at screen: $$ \begin{align*} A &= A_0\cos(kr_1 - \omega t) + A_0\cos(kr_2 - \omega t) \\ &= 2A_0 \cos\left(\frac{k(r_2-r_1)}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{k(r_1+r_2)}{2} - \omega t\right) \\ \end{align*} $$

Intensity $I\propto A^2$, so $$ \begin{align*} \langle I\rangle \propto \cos^2\left(\frac{k(r_2-r_1)}{2}\right) = \cos^2\left(\frac{kd\sin\theta}{2}\right). \end{align*} $$
