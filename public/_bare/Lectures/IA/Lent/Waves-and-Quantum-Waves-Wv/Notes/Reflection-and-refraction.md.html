A plane wave travels from a medium with refractive index $n_1 = c/v_1$ to one with $n_2 = c/v_2$.

(Usually larger $\lambda$ means smaller $n$.)

Law of reflection open_in_new

Angle of incidence $\theta_i = \theta_r$ angle of reflection. (Derivation)

If $n_2 > n_1$, there is a $\pi$ phase change upon reflection. If $n_2<n_1$, there is no phase change.

Snell's law open_in_new

Angle of incidence $\theta_1$ and angle of refraction $\theta_2$ are related by $$ \begin{align*} n_1 \sin \theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

Total internal refraction occurs when $\sin\theta_2 > 1$.

Image-object relationship of refraction open_in_new

Refraction at a spherical surface of radius $R$: for small angles, $$ \begin{align*} \frac{n_1}{u} + \frac{n_2}{v} = \frac{n_2-n_1}{R} \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

Take $R = \infty$ for planar surface. (Derivation)
