If $V$ is constant in some region, then we have

  • for $E>V$, $$ \begin{align*} \hat\psi(x) = Ae^{ikx} + Be^{-ikx}, \quad k = \frac{\sqrt{2m(E-V)}}{\hbar}, \end{align*} $$

  • for $E<V$,$$ \begin{align*} \hat\psi(x) = Ce^{\kappa x} + De^{-\kappa x}, \quad \kappa = \frac{\sqrt{2m(V-E)}}{\hbar}. \end{align*} $$
