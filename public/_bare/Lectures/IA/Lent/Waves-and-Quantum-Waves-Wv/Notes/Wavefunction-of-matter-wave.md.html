Matter wave has a complex wavefunction $\hat\psi(x,t)$, where $|\hat\psi(x)|^2$ is the probability density of observing a particle at $x$.

Therefore we have to normalise $$ \begin{align*} \int_{\R} |\hat\psi|^2 dx = 1. \end{align*} $$

De Broglie wave has wavefunction $$ \begin{align*} \hat\psi\propto \exp i(kx-\omega t). \end{align*} $$
