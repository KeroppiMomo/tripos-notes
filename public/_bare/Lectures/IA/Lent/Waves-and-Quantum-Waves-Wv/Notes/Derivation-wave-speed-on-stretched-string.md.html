Force along tranverse direction is $$ \begin{align*} \Delta F_y = T \sin(\theta+\Delta\theta) - T\sin\theta \approx T\Delta\theta \end{align*} $$ where small angle approximation is used.

The angle is the along the string, so $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd{\psi}{x} = \tan\theta \approx \theta \implies \Delta\theta \approx \pdfd[2]{\psi}{x} \Delta x. \end{align*} $$

Hence applying Newton's second law, $$ \begin{align*} \rho\Delta x \pdfd[2]{\psi}{t} = T \pdfd[2]{\psi}{x} \Delta x \end{align*} $$ so $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd[2]{\psi}{t} = \frac{T}{\rho}\pdfd[2]{\psi}{x}. \end{align*} $$
