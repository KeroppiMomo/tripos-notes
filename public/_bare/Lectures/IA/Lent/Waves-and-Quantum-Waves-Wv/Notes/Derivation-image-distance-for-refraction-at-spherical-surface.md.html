Using small angle approximation, $$ \begin{align*} \alpha \approx \sin\alpha \approx \tan\alpha = \frac{y}{u}, \quad \beta\approx\sin\beta\approx\tan\beta = \frac{y}{v}, \quad \theta\approx\sin\theta\approx\tan\theta = \frac{y}{R}. \end{align*} $$

By Snell's law, $$ \begin{align*} n_1 \sin(\theta+\alpha) &= n_2\sin(\theta-\beta) \\ n_1 \left(\frac{1}{R} + \frac{1}{u}\right) &= n_2\left(\frac{1}{R} - \frac{1}{v}\right) \\ \frac{n_1}{u} + \frac{n_2}{v} &= \frac{n_2-n_1}{R}. \end{align*} $$
