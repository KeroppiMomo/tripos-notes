We want to combine the two characteristic times of decay time $\tau=1/2\gamma$ and oscillation period $T = 2\pi/\omega_0$.

Logarithmic decrement open_in_new

If the logarithmic decrement is $\Delta$, the _amplitude_ of successive oscillations decays by a factor of $e^{-\Delta}$. The formula is $$ \Delta = \frac{2\pi\gamma}{\omega_d}. $$ (Derivation)

The energy of successive oscillations decays by a factor of $e^{-2\Delta}$.

Quality factor open_in_new

The qualify factor $Q$ is defined as $$ Q = \frac{\omega_0}{2\gamma}. $$

For good oscillators, $Q$ represents the number of radians of oscillation required for the _energy_ to fall by a factor of $e$. (Derivation) Such number for the amplitude is $2Q$.

For good oscillators, $$ Q = \frac\pi\Delta. $$
