Characteristic time for charge decay/gain $\tau = RC$, and so the characteristic time for energy stored is $RC/2$.

  • Discharge: $$ \begin{align*} \dot QR + \frac{Q}{C} = 0 \implies Q(t) = Q(0) e^{-t/RC}. \end{align*} $$

  • Charge with battery (emf $\mathcal{E})$ from $Q(0)=0$: $$ \begin{align*} \mathcal{E} - \dot Q R - \frac{Q}{C} = 0 \implies Q(t) = C\mathcal{E}(1-e^{-t/RC}). \end{align*} $$

    • $\Delta E_{\text{battery}} = -Q\mathcal{E}$, $\Delta E_{\text{capacitor}} = Q\mathcal{E}/2$, $\Delta E_{\text{resistor}} = Q\mathcal{E}/2$.
