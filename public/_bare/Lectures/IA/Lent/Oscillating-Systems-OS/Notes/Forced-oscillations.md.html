We investigate the equation $$ \ddot x + 2\gamma \dot x + \omega_0^2 x = f\cos\omega t. $$

Solutions to forced oscillations open_in_new

(Derivation) The solution is $x(t) = a_0 \cos(\omega t + \phi)$ where the complex amplitude is $$ A = \frac{f}{\omega_0^2 - \omega^2 + 2i\gamma\omega} \implies \begin{cases} a_0 &= \dfrac{f}{\sqrt{(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + 4\gamma^2 \omega^2}}, \\ \tan\phi&= \dfrac{2\gamma\omega}{\omega^2 - \omega_0^2}. \end{cases}$$

Power lost in forced oscillations open_in_new

In steady state, power given by driving force = power dissipated by damping: $$ \langle P\rangle = b\langle \dot x^2\rangle = \frac b2\frac{f^2\omega^2}{(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + 4\gamma^2\omega^2} = \frac b2 \frac{f^2}{\big((\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)/\omega\big)^2 + 4\gamma^2} $$ which peaks when $\omega=\omega_0$.

Lorentzian distribution (non-examinable): for $\omega \approx \omega_0$, we have the approximation $$ \begin{align*} \langle P\rangle = \frac{b}{2} \frac{f^2}{\left((\omega_0 - \omega)(\omega_0+\omega)/\omega\right)^2 + 4\gamma^2} \approx \frac{b}{8}\frac{f^2}{(\omega_0-\omega)^2+\gamma^2 .} \end{align*} $$

Resonance width open_in_new

Quantify the width of the resonance peak by how far from resonant frequency $\omega$ can be before the power absorbed falls by half.

Resonance peak width $$ \delta\omega = 2\gamma \implies \frac{\delta\omega}{\omega_0} = \frac1Q. $$
