We have characteristic equation $$ \lambda^2 + 2\gamma\lambda + \omega_0^2 = 0 \implies \lambda = -\gamma \pm \sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega_0^2}. $$

  • Heavy damping $\gamma>\omega_0$ is non-oscillatory. $$ x(t) = Ae^{-\big(\gamma+\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega_0^2}\big)t} + Be^{-\big(\gamma-\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega_0^2}\big)t}. $$

  • Light damping $\gamma<\omega_0$ oscillates with decaying amplitude. $$ x(t) = a_0 e^{-\gamma t}\cos(\omega_d t+\phi),\quad \omega_d = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \gamma^2} < \omega_0. $$ Note that the oscillation frequency is smaller with damping. When $\gamma\ll\omega_0$, $\omega_d \approx \omega_0$.

  • Critical damping $\gamma = \omega_0$ has the quickest decay without oscillation. $$ x(t) = Ae^{-\gamma t} + Bte^{-\gamma t}. $$
