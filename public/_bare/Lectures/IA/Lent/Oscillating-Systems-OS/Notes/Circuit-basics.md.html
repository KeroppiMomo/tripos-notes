• Current: unit $\mathrm{A} = \mathrm{C\ s\inv}$. In a material, $$ \begin{align*} I = qnA\langle v\rangle \end{align*} $$ where $q$ is charge of each charge carrier, $n$ is number density (number of charges per unit volume), $A$ is cross-sectional area, $\langle v\rangle$ is the time-average drift velocity.

  • Voltage: unit $\mathrm{V} = \mathrm{J\ C\inv}$. $$ \begin{align*} qV = W = \int_{a}^{b} q\v E\cdot d\v x. \end{align*} $$

  • Emf: unit $\mathrm{V}$.

  • Resistance: unit $\mathrm{\Omega} = \mathrm{V\ A\inv}$. Experimentally we have Ohm's law $$ \begin{align*} V = IR. \end{align*} $$ Equivalently, $$ \begin{align*} R = \frac{l}{\sigma A} \end{align*} $$ where $\sigma$ is the conductivity of the material.

  • Power: $$ \begin{align*} W = QV \implies P = \dot W = IV = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}. \end{align*} $$
