Energy decays as $e^{-2\gamma t}$. So after $n$ cycles, $$ E=E_0 e^{-2\gamma nT} = E_0 e^{-4n\pi\gamma/\omega_d}. $$ When energy falls by a factor of $e$, $n=Q/2\pi$. $$ \frac{4(Q/2\pi)\pi \gamma}{\omega_d} = 1 \implies Q=\frac{\omega_d}{2\gamma} \approx \frac{\omega_0}{2\gamma}$$ as $\omega_d \approx \omega_0$ for good oscillators.
