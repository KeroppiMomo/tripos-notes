Connect an AC source with voltage $V = V_0 e^{i\omega t}$ to a resistor $R$ and capacitor $C$, and measure the voltage across the capacitor.

Then using potential divider formula, $$ \begin{align*} V_C = V \frac{Z_C}{Z_C + Z_R} = \frac{1}{i\omega RC + 1}V \implies |V_C| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(\omega RC)^2 + 1}} V_0. \end{align*} $$ So this is a low-pass filter, i.e. large $|V_C / V|$ for small $\omega$.
