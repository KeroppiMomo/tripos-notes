For a system characterised by $x(t)$ which varies in time,

  • period $T$ is the shortest time-period for which $x(t+T) = x(t)$;

  • frequency $\nu$ is how many cycles the system go through per unit time: $$ \nu = \frac1T. $$

  • Angular frequency $\omega$ appears sinusoidal function $\cos \omega t$: $$ \omega = \frac\nu{2\pi}, \quad T = \frac{2\pi}\omega. $$
