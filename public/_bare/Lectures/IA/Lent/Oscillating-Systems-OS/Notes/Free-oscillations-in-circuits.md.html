• LC circuit: $$ \begin{align*} \frac{q}{C} + L\ddot q = 0 \iff \frac{1}{2}\frac{q^2}{C} + \frac{1}{2}L\dot q^2 = \text{const.} \\ \end{align*} $$ which has simple harmonic motion as the solution $$ \begin{align*} \omega_0^2 = \frac{1}{LC}. \end{align*} $$

  • RLC circuit: $$ \begin{align*} L\ddot q + R\dot q + \frac{q}{C} = 0 \end{align*} $$ which is a damped oscillation with $\gamma = R/2L$ and $\omega_0^2 = 1/LC$. Quality factor $Q = (1/R)\sqrt{L/C}$.
