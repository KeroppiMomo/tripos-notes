For a particular origin $O$, if a force $\v F$ is acted at a point $\v r$ from $O$, then its moment vector is given by $$ \v G = \v r \times \v F. $$

Couple moment open_in_new

Two forces $\v F$ and $-\v F$ act at two different positions separated by a displacement $\v a$ (points from where $-\v F$ is to where $\v F$ is). Then about any origin, the moment of the couple is $$ \v G = \v a \times \v F. $$ (Derivation)

Equilibrium of rigid body open_in_new

For a rigid body to be in equilibrium, two conditions have to be satisfied:

  1. net external force = 0;

  2. net external moment about any axis = 0.
