The source emits a pulse every $T' = 1/f'$ time. So the measured time interval between two pulse emissions in frame $S$ is $$ \Delta t_{AB} = \gamma T' = \frac\gamma{f'}, $$ and the space interval is $$ \Delta x_{AB} = v\Delta t_{AB} = \frac{\gamma v}{f'}. $$

Now $$ \Delta t_{AC} = \frac{\Delta x_{AC}}{c} $$ and $$ \Delta t_{BD} = \frac{\Delta x_{BD}}{c}. $$

Combining, $$\begin{align*} \Delta t_{CD} &= -\Delta t_{AC} + \Delta t_{AB} + \Delta t_{BD} \\ &= -\frac{\Delta x_{AC}}{c} + \frac\gamma{f'} + \frac{\Delta x_{AB} + \Delta x_{AD}}{c} \\ &= -\frac{\Delta x_{AC}}{c} + \frac{\gamma}{f'} + \frac{\gamma}{f'}\frac vc + \frac{\Delta x_{AC}}{c} \\ \frac{1}{f} &= \frac{\gamma}{f'}\Big(1 + \frac vc\Big) \\ f &= \frac{f'}{\gamma(1+v/c)} \\ &= \frac{\sqrt{(1+v/c)(1-v/c)}}{1+v/c} f' \\ &= \sqrt{\frac{1-v/c}{1+v/c}} f' \\ &= \sqrt\frac{c-v}{c+v} f'. \end{align*}$$
