The 4-momentum of a particle with mass $m$ and speed $v$ is $$ \v P \equiv m\v U = \Big(\frac Ec, \v p\Big) = \pmat{E/c \\ p_x \\ p_y \\ p_z}.$$ (Derivation)

4-momentum of a system is the sum of the 4-momenta of the particles: $$ \v P = \sum_i \v P_i $$ so 4-momentum conserves in an inertial frame.
