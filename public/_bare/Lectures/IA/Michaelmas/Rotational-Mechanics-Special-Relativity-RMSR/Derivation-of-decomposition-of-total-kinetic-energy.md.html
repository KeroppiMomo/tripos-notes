For each particle, we can write its velocity $\v v_i$ as the sum of the velocity of the centre of mass $\v V$ and the velocity of the particle relative to the centre of mass $\v v_i'$.

Then $$\begin{align*} K &= \sum_i \frac12 m_i |\v v_i|^2 \\ &= \sum_i \frac12 m_i (|\v V|^2 + |\v v_i'|^2 + 2\v v_i'\cdot\v V) \\ &= \frac12 \Big(\sum_i m_i\Big) V^2 + \sum_i\frac12 m_iv_i'^2 + \underbrace{\Big(\sum_i m_i \v v_i'\Big)}_0\cdot \v V \\ &= \frac12 MV^2 + \frac12 I_{CM}\omega^2. \end{align*}$$
