For two events $A,B$, if in $S'$, $A,B$ happen at the same point in space (rest frame) and the time interval between the two events is $\Delta t'_{AB}$ (proper time), then the time interval between the two events measured in $S$ is $$ \Delta t_{AB} = \gamma\Delta t'_{AB} $$ where $\gamma$ is the Lorentz factor $$ \gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}. $$
