We impose that $\v Y(t) \in \R^2$, so in particular for $t=0$, $$ \begin{align*} \v v_1 + C\v v_1^* \in \R^2 \implies C=1. \end{align*} $$ Hence $$ \begin{align*} \v Y(t) &= \v v_1 e^{\lambda_1 t} + \v v_1^* e^{\lambda_1^* t} \\ &= e^{\Re(\lambda_1) t} \left(\v v_1 e^{\Im(\lambda_1) t} + \v v_1^\ast e^{-\Im(\lambda_1)t}\right) \\ &= e^{\Re(\lambda_1) t} \Re\left(2\v v_1 e^{\Im(\lambda_1)t}\right) \end{align*} $$
