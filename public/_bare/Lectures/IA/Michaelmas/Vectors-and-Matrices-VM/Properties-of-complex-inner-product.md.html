• $\v u\cdot\v v = \overline{\v v\cdot\v u}$;

  • $\v u\cdot\v u\geq 0$, and $\v u\cdot\v u=0\implies\v u=\v 0$.

  • $\v u\cdot(\lambda\v v+\mu\v w) = \lambda\v u\cdot\v v + \mu\v u\cdot\v w$ (linearity of the second argument);

  • $(\lambda\v u+\mu\v v)\cdot\v w = \overline\lambda\v u\cdot\v w + \overline\mu\v v\cdot\v w$ (Proof).
