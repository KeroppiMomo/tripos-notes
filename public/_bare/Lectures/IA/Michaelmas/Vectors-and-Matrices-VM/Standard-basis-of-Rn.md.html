The standard basis of $\mathbb R^n$ is $\{\v e_1,\v e_2,\ldots, \v e_n\}$ where $$ \v e_1 = (1,0,\ldots,0), \quad\v e_2 = (0,1,\ldots,0),\quad \ldots\quad\v e_n = (0,\ldots,0,1). $$

This basis is orthonormal, i.e. $$ \v e_i\cdot\v e_j = \begin{cases} 0, &i\neq j &\text{(orthogonal)}\\ 1, &i=j. \end{cases}$$
