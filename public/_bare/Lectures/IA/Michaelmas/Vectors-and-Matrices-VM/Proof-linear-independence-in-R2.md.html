Let $$ \v 0 = \lambda\v a + \mu\v b.$$ Taking the cross product with $\v b$, since $\v b\times\v b=\v 0$, $$ \v 0 = \lambda(\v a\times\v b).$$ And as $\v a\times\v b\neq 0$, $\lambda = 0$. Similarly taking the cross product with $\v a$ gives $\mu = 0$. Therefore, $\v a$ and $\v b$ are linearly independent.
