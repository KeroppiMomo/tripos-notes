Let $V,W$ be vector spaces. $T: V\to W$ is a linear map if

  • $T(\v a + \v b) = T(\v a) + T(\v b)$ for all $\v a,\v b\in V$; and

  • $T(\lambda\v a) = \lambda T(\v a)$ for all scalar $\lambda$ and $\v a\in V$.

Or equivalently, for all scalars $\lambda,\mu$ and vectors $\v a,\v b\in V$, $$ T(\lambda\v a + \mu\v b) = \lambda T(\v a) + \mu T(\v b). $$
