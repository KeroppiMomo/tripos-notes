A vector space $V$ is a set of vectors equipped with a scalar field, closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication, and

  • forms a commutative group under vector addition, i.e.

    • commutativity: $\v a+\v b = \v b+\v a$;

    • associativity: $(\v a + \v b) + \v c = \v a + (\v b + \v c)$;

    • identity: there exists a unique $\v 0\in V$ such that $\v a + \v 0 = \v a$ for all $\v a\in V$;

    • inverses: for all $a\in V$, there exists a unique additive inverse $-\v a$ such that $\v a+(-\v a) = \v 0$,

  • and scalar multiplication satisfies

    • distributive properties:

      • $(\lambda+\mu)\v v = \lambda\v v+\mu\v v$;

      • $\lambda(\v u+\v v) = \lambda\v u + \lambda\v v$;

    • Associativity: $\lambda(\mu\v v) = (\lambda\mu)\v v$;

    • Identity: $1\v v = \v v$.
