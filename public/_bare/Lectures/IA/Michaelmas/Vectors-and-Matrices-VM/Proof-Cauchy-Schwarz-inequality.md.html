Consider $\v x-\lambda \v y$ for all $\lambda\in\mathbb R$.

$$\begin{align*} 0&\leq (\v x-\lambda\v y)\cdot (\v x-\lambda\v y) \\ &= \v x\cdot\v x - 2\lambda\v x\cdot \v y + \lambda^2\v y\cdot\v y \end{align*}$$

Suppose $\v x,\v y\neq 0$ (otherwise it's clear). Then this is a quadratic inequality on $\lambda$ which is true for all $\lambda$. Therefore $$0\geq \text{Discriminant} = 4(\v x\cdot\v y)^2 - 4(\v x\cdot\v x)(\v y\cdot\v y) $$ $$(\v x\cdot \v y)^2 \leq (\v x\cdot\v x)(\v y\cdot\v y)$$ hence the result.

Equality occurs iff $|\v x-\lambda\v y|^2 = 0$, i.e. $\v x=\lambda\v y$.
