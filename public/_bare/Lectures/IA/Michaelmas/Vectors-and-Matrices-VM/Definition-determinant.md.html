For an $n\times n$ matrix $A$, define its determinant $$ \det A = \sum_{\sigma\in S_n} \epsilon(\sigma) A_{\sigma(1) 1} A_{\sigma(2) 2} \ldots A_{\sigma(n) n}. $$

Alternatively, in suffix notation, $$ \det A = \epsilon_{j_1j_2\ldots j_n} A_{j_1 1} A_{j_2 2} \ldots A_{j_n n} $$ or equivalently, $$ \det A = \frac{1}{n!}\epsilon_{i_1i_2\ldots i_n}\epsilon_{j_1j_2\ldots j_n} A_{i_1 j_1} A_{i_2 j_2} \ldots A_{i_n j_n} $$
