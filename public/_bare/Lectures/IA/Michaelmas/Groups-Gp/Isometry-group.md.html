Let $\mathbb C$ be the (complex) plane equipped with the usual notation of distance.

Definition (isometry on C) open_in_new

For any subset $X\subseteq\mathbb C$, an isometry $f: X\to X$ is a bijection that preserves distance, i.e. for all $x,y\in X$, $$ |f(x) - f(y)| = |x-y|. $$

Proposition (isometry group of C) open_in_new

Let $X\subseteq\mathbb C$. The set of all isometries of $X$, $$ \mathrm{Isom}(X) = \{f:X\to X \mid f \text{ is an isometry}\}, $$ is a subgroup of $\mathrm{Sym}(X)$.
