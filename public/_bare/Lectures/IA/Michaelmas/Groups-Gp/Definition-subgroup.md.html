Let $(G, \cdot_G, e_G)$ and $(H, \cdot_H, e_H)$ be groups. If

  • $H\subseteq G$;

  • $e_H = e_G$; and

  • $a\cdot_H b = a\cdot_G b$ for all $a,b\in H$,

then $H$ is a subgroup of $G$.

Equivalently: if $G$ is a group, then a subset $H\subseteq G$ is called a subgroup if

  • $e\in H$;

  • for all $a,b\in H$, $a\cdot b\in H$; and

  • $(H, \cdot, e)$ forms a group.

We write $H\leq G$.
