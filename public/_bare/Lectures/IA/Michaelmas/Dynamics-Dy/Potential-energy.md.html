The potential energy $U$ of a system is the work done by an external force $\vec F_{ext}$ on the system, which is the same as $-\vec F_{int}$, negative internal force inside the system. $$ U = \int_{\vec r_1}^{\vec r_2} \vec F_{ext}\cdot d\vec r = -\int_{\vec r_1}^{\vec r_2} \vec F_{int}\cdot d\vec r $$ $$ \frac{dU}{dx} = F_{ext} = -F_{int} $$ $$ \vec\nabla U = \vec F_{ext} = -\vec F_{int} $$
