Suppose we have $N$ particles. For particle $i$, applying Newton's second law, $$ \frac{d\vec p_i}{dt} = \vec F_{1,net} = \vec F_{1,ext} + \sum_{j\neq i} F_{j\to i}. $$

The total momentum of the system is $$ \vec P = \sum_i \vec p_i $$ so $$\begin{align*} \frac{d\vec P}{dt} &= \sum_i \frac{d\vec p_i}{dt} \\ &= \Big(\sum_i \vec F_{i,ext}\Big) + \Big(\sum_{i\neq j} \vec F_{i\to j}\Big) \end{align*}$$ But then for each pair $(i,j)$, Newton's third law states that $$ \vec F_{i\to j} + \vec F_{j\to i} = 0, $$ so $$ \frac{d\vec P}{dt} = \vec F_{ext}. $$
