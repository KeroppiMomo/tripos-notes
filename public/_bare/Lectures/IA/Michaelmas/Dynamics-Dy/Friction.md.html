If the two surfaces are not moving, static friction is in play, which has a maximum frictional force $$ f_s \leq \mu_s N $$ where $\mu_s$ is the coefficient of static friction, and $N$ is the normal force.

If sliding occurs, dynamic friction is in play, and has a magnitude of $$ f_d = \mu_d N. $$

Usually $\mu_s > \mu_d$.
