We specify position of a particle by its displacement $\vec r$ from a reference point O.

The velocity $\vec v$ and acceleration $\vec a$ are $$ \vec v = \frac{d\vec r}{dt} = \dot{\vec r}, $$ $$ \vec a = \frac{d^2\vec r}{dt} = \ddot{\vec r}. $$

In 1D motion, integrating gives $$\begin{align*} v &= u + at \\ s &= ut + \frac12at^2 \\ v^2 &= u^2 + 2as. \end{align*}$$
