Conservation of momentum and energy gives $$\begin{cases} m_1v_1 + m_2v_2 &= m_1u \\ \frac12 m_1v_1^2 + \frac12 m_2v_2^2 &= \frac12 m_1 u^2 \end{cases}$$

First equation gives $$ v_1 = u - \frac{m_2}{m_1}v_2. $$

Second equation gives $$\begin{align*} m_1\Big(u-\frac{m_2}{m_1}v_2\Big)^2 + m_2 v_2^2 &= m_1 u^2 \\ m_1u^2 - 2m_2uv_2 + \frac{m_2^2}{m_1}v_2^2 + m_2v_2^2 &= m_1u^2 \\ v_2(-2u + \frac{m_2+m_1}{m_1}v_2) &= 0 \\ v_2 &= \frac{2m_1}{m_1+m_2}u \\ v_1 &= \frac{m_1-m_2}{m_1+m_2}u \end{align*}$$
