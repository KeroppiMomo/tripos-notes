Known systematic errors can be removed from final results.

  • Difference method: taking the difference between two measurements eliminate effects of unknown offsets.

  • Zero offsets: swap around things and take averages.

  • Variations with time: instead of measuring in the order 1,2,3,4,5,6, measure in the order 1,3,5,6,4,2.

  • Calibrate equipment: compare against more accurate standard equipment.
