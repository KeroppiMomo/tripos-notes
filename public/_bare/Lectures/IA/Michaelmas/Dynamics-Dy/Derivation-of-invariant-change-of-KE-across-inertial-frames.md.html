Suppose in frame $S$, inertial frame $S'$ is travelling at velocity $\v w$.

In frame $S$, the change in total KE is $$ \Delta K = \sum_{i}\Big(\frac12m_i(v_i^2 - u_i^2)\Big). $$

In frame $S'$, the change in total KE is $$\begin{align*} \Delta K' &= \sum_i \Big(\frac12m_i(|\v v_i-\v w|^2 - |\v u_i - \v w|^2)\Big) \\ &= \sum_i \Big(\frac12m_i (v_i^2 -2\v v_i\cdot\v w + w^2 - u_i^2 + 2\v u_i\cdot\v w - w^2)\Big) \\ &= \sum_i \Big(\frac12 m_i(v_i^2 - u_i^2)\Big) - \vec w\cdot\sum_i m_i(\v v_i - \v u_i) \\ &= \Delta K - \vec w\cdot\underbrace{\Delta\vec p}_0 \\ &= \Delta K. \end{align*}$$
