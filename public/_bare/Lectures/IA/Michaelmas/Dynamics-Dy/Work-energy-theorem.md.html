$$\begin{align*} W &= \int_i^f \vec F\cdot d\vec x &&\text{definition of work} \\ &= \int_i^f m\frac{d\vec v}{dt}\cdot d\vec x &&\text{Newton's second law} \\ &= \int_i^f m\,d\vec v\cdot \frac{d\vec x}{dt} \\ &= \int_i^f m\vec v\cdot d\vec v &&\text{definition of velocity} \\ &= \int_i^f mv\,dv \\ &= \frac12 mv_f^2 - \frac12 mv_i^2 \\ &= \Delta K &&\text{definition of kinetic energy} \end{align*}$$
