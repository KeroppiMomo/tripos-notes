By Kepler's second law, $$ \begin{align*} T &= \oint dt \\ &= \iint\frac{1}{\dfd{A}{t}}\,dA \\ &= \frac{2m}{L}\iint dA \\ &= \frac{2m}{L}\pi ab \\ &= \frac{2\pi m}{L} a^2 \sqrt{1-e^2} \\ &= \frac{2\pi m}{L} a^{3/2} r_0^{1/2} \\ &= \frac{2\pi m}{L} a^{3/2} \sqrt{\frac{L^2}{GMm^2}} \\ &= \frac{2\pi a^{3/2}}{\sqrt{GM}} \end{align*} $$
