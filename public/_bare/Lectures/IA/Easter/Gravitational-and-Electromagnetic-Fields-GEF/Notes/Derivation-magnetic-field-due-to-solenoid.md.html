Outside a long solenoid:

  • Consider the green surface. Since $\nabla\cdot\v B = 0$, radial field = 0.

  • Consider the green loop with the further side pulled to infinity. Using Ampere's law, we have longitudinal field = 0.

  • Consider the black loop. Using Ampere's law, we have azimuthal field = 0.

Inside a long solenoid: $$ \begin{align*} B = \mu_0 I \frac{N}{l} \end{align*} $$ where $N/l$ is the number of windings per unit length.
