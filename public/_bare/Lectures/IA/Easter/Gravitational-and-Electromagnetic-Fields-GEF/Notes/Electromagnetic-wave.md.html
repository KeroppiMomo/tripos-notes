In free space ($\rho=0$, $\v J = \v 0$), the fields follow the 3D wave equation $$ \begin{align*} \pdfd[2]{\v E}{t} = \frac{1}{\mu_0\epsilon_0}\nabla^2 \v E \end{align*} $$ so electromagnetic wave travels at speed $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$.

A plane wave solution $$ \begin{align*} \begin{cases} \v E(\v r, t) &= \v E_0 \cos(\v k\cdot\v r - \omega t), \\ \v B(\v r, t) &= \v B_0 \cos(\v k\cdot\v r - \omega t) \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ has $$ \begin{align*} E_0 &= cB_0, & \v B &\perp \v E, & \v k \parallel \v E\times \v B. \end{align*} $$ (Derivation)

Energy density is $$ \begin{align*} U = \frac{1}{2}\epsilon_0 E^2 + \frac{1}{2\mu_0}B^2 = \epsilon_0 E^2 = \frac{1}{\mu_0} B^2. \end{align*} $$
