For two point masses $m_1, m_2$ with separation $\v r$, the gravitational force is an attractive force: $$ \begin{align*} \v F = -G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}\vhat r \end{align*} $$ where $G$ is the universal gravitational constant.

The gravitational field is given by $$ \begin{align*} \v g = -\frac{Gm}{r^2} \vhat r. \end{align*} $$

This uses the gravitational mass, which differs conceptually from the inertial mass used in $\v F = m\v a$. But experimentally we found the two masses equal.
