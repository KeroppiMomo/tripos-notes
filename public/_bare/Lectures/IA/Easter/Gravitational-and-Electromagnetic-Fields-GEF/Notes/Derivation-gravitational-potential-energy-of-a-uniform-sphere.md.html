We build up the sphere by adding shells: $$ \begin{align*} \text{Mass inside } M(r) &= \frac{r^3}{a^3}M \\ \text{Shell mass } dM &= \frac{4\pi r^2M}{\frac{4}{3}\pi a^3}\,dr \\ &= \frac{3r^2M}{a^3}\,dr \\ dU &= -\frac{GM(r)\,dM}{r} \\ &= -3G\frac{r^4}{a^6} \\ U &= -\frac{3}{5}\frac{GM^2}{a}. \end{align*} $$

Alternatively, we compute the gravitational field using Gauss' law: $$ \begin{align*} g(r) &= \begin{cases} -\frac{GM}{r^2}, &r\geq a \\ -\frac{GMr}{a^3}, &r<a \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ and the gravitational potential: $$ \begin{align*} U(r) = -\int_{\infty}^{r} g(r')\, dr = \begin{cases} -\frac{GM}{r}, &r\geq a\\ \frac{GMr^2}{2a^3} - \frac{3GM}{2a}, &r<a \end{cases} \end{align*} $$ so using the formula, $$ \begin{align*} U &= \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{a} \frac{4\pi r^2M}{\frac{4}{3}\pi a^3}\, U(r)\, dr \\ &= \frac{3M}{2a^3}\int_{0}^{a} \left(\frac{GM}{2a^3} r^4 - \frac{3GM}{2a}r^2\right)\, dr \\ &= \frac{3GM^2}{4a^3}\left(\frac{1}{5}a^2 - a^2\right) \\ &= -\frac{3}{5}\frac{GM^2}{a}. \end{align*} $$
