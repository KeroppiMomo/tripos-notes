Define the Runge-Lenz vector $$ \begin{align*} \v R = \dv r \times \v L - GMm \vhat r. \end{align*} $$

Note that $\v R$ is constant: $$ \begin{align*} \dv R &= \ddv r\times \v L - \dv r \times \underbrace{\dv L}_{\v 0} - GMm\dd{t}\frac{\v r}{r}\\ &= -\frac{GM}{r^2}\vhat r\times (m\v r\times \dv r) - GMm\left(\frac{\dv r}{r} - \frac{\dot r\v r}{r^2}\right) \\ &=-\frac{GMm}{r^2}\left(\underbrace{(\vhat r\cdot\dv r)}_{\dot r} \v r - \underbrace{(\vhat r\cdot \v r)}_{r} \dv r - r\dv r - \dot r \v r\right) \\ &= 0. \end{align*} $$

The angle $\theta$ satisfies $$ \begin{align*} r R \cos\theta &= \v R\cdot \v r\\ &= (\v r\times\dv r)\cdot\v L - GMm(\vhat r\cdot \v r) \\ &= \frac{L^2}{m} - GMmr \\ r\left(R\cos\theta + GMm\right) &= \frac{L^2}{m} \\ r &= \frac{L^2/GMm^2}{1+(R/GMm)\cos\theta}. \end{align*} $$

$R$ has magnitude $$ \begin{align*} \v R\cdot \v R &= (\dv r\times \v L)^2 - 2GMm(\dv r\times \v L)\cdot \vhat r + G^2M^2m^2 \\ &= v^2 L^2 - \frac{2GM}{r}(m\v r\times\dv r)\cdot L + G^2M^2m^2 \\ &= v^2 L^2 - \frac{2GM}{r}L^2 + G^2M^2m^2 \\ &= \frac{2L^2}{m}\left(\frac{1}{2}mv^2 -\frac{GMm}{r}\right) + G^2M^2m^2 \\ &=\frac{2L^2E}{m} + G^2M^2m^2. \end{align*} $$

Therefore $$ \begin{align*} r_0 &= \frac{L^2}{GMm^2}, &\quad e &= \sqrt{1+\frac{2L^2E}{G^2M^2m^3}}. \end{align*} $$
