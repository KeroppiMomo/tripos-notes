This is a historic model.

Oersted observed that a magnetic field wraps around an electric current using the right-hand rule.

A magnet has two poles. Ampere stuck a bar magnet to a rotatable platform around an electric current and observed that there is no torque on the platform.

Therefore magnetic force on the poles $F\propto 1/r$. This means that the work done $W=2\pi r F$ to move a pole around the wire does not depend on $r$.

Define the pole strength $$ \begin{align*} p = \frac{W}{I} \end{align*} $$ where $W$ is the work done to move the pole around a current $I$. Then $$ \begin{align*} F = \frac{pI}{2\pi r} \end{align*} $$

Define the magnetic field $$ \begin{align*} \v H = \frac{\v F}{p} \end{align*} $$ and magnetic flux density $$ \begin{align*} \v B = \mu_0 \v H. \end{align*} $$
